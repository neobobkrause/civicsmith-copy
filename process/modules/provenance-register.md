# provenance-register: requirements

Next number: 82

## Public

### Purpose

The register's pure rules: its tables and their read contract (`schema.mjs`), the C-18 checks over a promoted register (`register-checks.mjs`), the refusal rows (`checks.mjs`) and the one helper that composes a releasable bundle (`releasable.mjs`). It writes nothing; `provenance` registers the checks, runs the schema and answers through the rows.

### Provides

**declareOrigin({bundleId, system, by, viewer}) → record or refusal; originOf(bundleId) → `{system, by, at}` or null**
- **R81** [met] (T52 R1; W5, the 0.87.0 walk's B-K1; `ratification` R22's entry requirements; `screens-intake` R23's shape) **One helper files a releasable bundle.** `releasableBundle({md, doc, locator, snapshots, indent})`, exported (`md` the filer's own `bundle.md`; `doc` its row of `data/provenance.json` with `capture.sha256`, `capture.bytes` and `provenance_chain`; `locator` defaulting to `doc.locator`; `snapshots` its files under `snapshots/`, as `promote` takes them; `indent` of `data/provenance.json`, default 2), answering `{ok: true, files, contentHash, dataset}` or `{ok: false, reason, code, detail}` with `NO_DOCUMENT_DIGEST`, `NO_CHAIN`, `NO_SNAPSHOT` or `NO_FRONT_MATTER` (a filer's own fault, never a member's act, so no catalogue row), composes an information bundle at `collected` that `ratification`'s `release` does not refuse `ENTRY_REQUIREMENTS`: `bundle.md` carrying `content_hash: sha256:<hex>`, the digest of `data/dataset.json`; `data/dataset.json` the canonical dataset `{documents: [{bytes, locator, sha256}]}` in record-grammar's canonical JSON (keys sorted, no space), one row per whole document as `screens-intake` R23 writes it; `data/provenance.json` with the document's chain; and the document's own file under `snapshots/`. **The register's read contract** (K72)
- **R48** [met] The tables `register` (its `capture_sha`, `bundle_id`, `path`, `registered`, `authored` and `observed_at` columns, `observed_at` the instant the member states they observed what an authored entry holds, R28, as they gave it (K2115, wording); `authored` 1 when a member authored the observation the entry holds, K182) and `captured_locators` (its `address_norm`, `address`, `retrieval_locator`, `capture_sha` and `first_retrieved` columns, the last as R13 states it; K243) are a stated read contract: a later module may join them in its own SQL, and this module changes none of those columns' names or meaning without a change to this requirement. `registered` is this module's clock at the register write (R1), an ISO instant, never a caller's time; `address_norm` is the document address as the acquisition that wrote the receipt normalised it (R13), the key a later module seeks a document address on. Every write to them stays this module's. *(widened by N111, K173)* The columns `register.bytes` (the registered capture's size in bytes, as the entry that registers it states it) and `register.author` (the member who authored an `authored` entry, stamped by this module, NULL on every other entry), and `captured_locators.via` (the receipt's source, R13, part of its key) and `last_retrieved` (R13's latest), are part of it too, on the same terms (N213, N484: `corpus-export` reads `register`'s `bundle_id`, `path`, `capture_sha` and `bytes` for the working-corpus export, its R1, moved from `publication` by K1024; N227, K276: `monitoring` R26 reads `via`; N532, K1332: `case-carriage` reads `register`'s `capture_sha`, `bundle_id`, `path` and `bytes`, the evidence-held document's byte count, for its R1, as `publication` R57 did before it). `first_retrieved` and `last_retrieved` are spelled whole-second UTC on every row, `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ` (record-core R47's `"second"`), so a later module compares and brackets them as text in its own SQL (N133). The table `provenance_route_marks`' contract is `provenance-routes`' (its R8; N512). (N688) `captured_locators.via` also takes `unpacked` (R15, R59): such a row's `address_norm` is the archive's normalised document address followed by `#zip:<index>`, and its `retrieval_locator` is `zip:<archiveSha>!<index>`, so a later module seeking a document address never finds a file at its archive's address, and one reading `via` reads `unpacked` as a file cut out of an archive the record holds. (T48; N1009; K3065) `register.registered` is the instant the capture was first registered (R1), never moved by a re-registration under the same bundle, so a reader asking which capture was first registered under a document (`capture` R77) reads it here; `register.last_registered`, the instant of the latest register write, joins the contract on the same terms. Test: after a re-registration, a join on `registered` orders the capture by its first registration; control: `last_registered` is later. Since R72 (K4505): `register.whole` is 1 for the row of the whole of a capture held in parts (no file at its `path`; a reader wanting files skips it), 0 otherwise; `registeredFor(bundle)` (R11) and `capturesOf` include that row.

## Private

### Uses

- `setup-words`: `word`, `localize` (each row's translation by key).

### Invariants

*The register's checks at the gate (C-18, K49).* Registered with `promotion.registerStep` as checks on the promoted package's `data/provenance.json`, for an information bundle whose register is present; each keeps its catalogue id and severity. At the write, a creation is refused on every error finding; a revision only on an error finding its held version's register does not already carry, so an inherited violation never blocks the correction that repairs it; a replay is exempt; a warning never refuses. The gate and the audit still report every finding (K121).
- **R42** [met] C-18.1 (error): the register is `{documents: [...]}`; each document is an object naming `file` (present in the bundle), `locator` and `retrieved`; `authority`, or `authority_state` `undetermined`, with an `authority_basis` in both states; a `capture` block with `method`, a `grade` in `CAPTURE_GRADES` (none for an authored observation; none for a document received through the doorbell, which states R51's `grade_basis` instead) and a declared `actor_class`; an `origin.kind` in `ORIGIN_KINDS` (`sweep` with `matched_sweep` and `deeming_actor`; `member` for an authored observation; `doorbell` for a document capture R65's pull writes). A `collected → verified` transition is authored by a named member, never a machine identity, and a sweep-origin bundle reaches `verified` only through such a transition. (N688) A document whose `capture.method` is `unpacked` (a file cut out of a captured archive, `acquisition` R39) carries a `container` block: `archive_sha256` (64 lowercase hex, a capture the record registers or holds a receipt for, R5), `index`, `compressed`, `uncompressed` and `local_offset` (whole numbers, at least 0), `path` (the entry's name as the archive states it, or null), `name_raw` (lowercase hex), `method` (0 or 8), `crc32` (a whole number), `member_sha256` (equal to `capture.sha256`) and `dos_time_stated` (the archive's stated time, or null); its `capture.grade` is present exactly when `captureGrade` answers a letter for its capture (R59) and is that letter, and otherwise its `grade_basis` is the archive's; its `origin` is that of the archive's own document. A `container` block on a document of any other method, or an `unpacked` document whose block is missing, malformed or names an archive the record does not hold, is an error finding.
- **R43** [met] C-18.3 (error): one capture appears once in a register: two documents with the same `capture.sha256`, or with the same determined evidentiary digest and different raw bytes, are refused as corroboration missed; an undetermined evidentiary digest is never compared.
- **R44** [met] C-18.4 (warn): a `crucial` document whose entry carries neither `co_archive` nor `timestamp` is flagged for a member to verify co-attestation before release.
- **R45** [met] C-18.6 (error): every registered capture's stored bytes (whole, or its parts streamed in order) hash to the recorded `capture.sha256`; bytes that cannot be decoded are refused with the reason.
- **R46** [met] C-18.9 (error): a document at or past `verified` records a non-empty `provenance_chain` whose every hop names its attestor, and states its authority or, when undetermined, its `authority_basis`; no chain, a non-array chain and an empty chain are three distinct findings.
