# store-services: requirements

Next number: 101

## Public

### Purpose

The record store's own services, split from `store-assembly` (P6): the ask, draft and transcription doors and the archive unpack, the owed-work ledger, job table and alarm, the assembly of every module's op declarations, and the owner map. `store-assembly` composes each at construction; none imports a platform module.

### Provides

**The ask, draft and transcription doors** (`ask.mjs`, `transcribe.mjs`, `unpack.mjs`)
- **R49** [met] (was `plane` R33) The composition root hands `answers` no `ceilingRefusal`: `answers` judges a limit through `ai-use.useCheck` on its own host. The assembly's ask path (`askOnObject`) and draft path (`draftOnObject`) take the account that serves the member's act, and its limit, from `answers.askAccount({member, project?, at?, kind?})` (an ask's `project` when it names one; `kind: "draft"` on the draft path), in place of their own `credentials.accountFor` call and any ceiling; a refusal it answers is answered as given, before any call to `agent-worker`, and nothing is posted. With no `AGENT_WORKER` bound, an ask and a draft answer 503 `AGENT_WORKER_UNBOUND`. Every read made under a grant the draft path mints is recorded in that grant's read log with `use: "draft"` (`answers.logRead`, its R2 as R30 widens it; `store-door` R11), so no row of a project `credentials.projectsKeptAway({use: "draft"})` names reaches a draft; an ask's grant reads under `use: "ask"`. The assembly owns a grant's `use`: `draftOnObject` notes each grant it mints (in memory, dropped when its TTL passes; no table), and the `logRead` the assembly hands `store-door` records `use: "draft"` for a grant so noted, else the entry's own `use` or `ask`. The suggestions switch sent with an ask or a draft is the one of the account that serves: the sign-in's own (`accountReferenceState().subscription.uses.suggestions`) when `answers.askAccount` answers `signin`, the member's reference's for `apikey` at level `member`, the group key's for `group`; a grant is minted by it. Controls: a sign-in with suggestions on sends `suggestions: true`; an API-key reference's switch is the member's own.
- **R50** [met] (was `plane` R40) `askOnObject` and `draftOnObject` refuse, before the keep-away read, any account read or any grant is minted, unless the part is deployed and admitted: `run-rules`' deploy flag for their mode (`ask`; for a draft, its kind's flag: `own_words` for `writinghelp` and `groupdescriptiondraft`, `translation` for `translationdraft`, `action_drafts` for `textcheck`, `infractiondraft`, `bearingdraft`) or D14's bar for that part not admitting them refuses. A flag that does not deploy the part is refused `AI_RUN_MODE_NOT_DEPLOYED` with `run-rules`' row (check, translation) and `mode`, status 403; a part whose flag deploys it but which `ai-runs.partAdmitted(part)` does not admit (part `ask`; `draft:<kind>` for a draft; `transcribe`; asked through `partAdmitted` alone, so the test-instance exception admits where the deployer set it and nowhere else) is refused `AI_PART_NOT_DEPLOYED` (`run-rules` R30's `partRefusal(part)`, relayed unchanged); nothing is posted to the assistant. Admitted with a label, the ask's request to `agent-worker` carries `untested` (`agent-worker` R79), and the draft's answer carries `untested` beside `grant`, `suggestions` and `read`; admitted by the bar, neither carries it. The draft path takes the status report's draft (`statusreportdraft`, handed `plan` and the items `plan-work` answered): `draft`'s flag and `partAdmitted("draft:statusreport")`, then the keep-away read (`credentials.aiKeptAway` with the plan's project), all before any account is read; it mints no grant and sends no `told`; its use is counted as `draft`. Tests: an ask and a writing-help draft on Civicsmith's empty set with no test instance are refused `AI_PART_NOT_DEPLOYED` with nothing posted; control: with `CIVICSMITH_TEST_INSTANCE` equal to `INSTANCE_NAME` the same post is made and carries the label; a status-report draft for a plan whose project is kept away is refused before any account is read.
- **R51** [met] (was `plane` R41) The draft door takes `op` `textcheck` (`{text}`), `infractiondraft` (`{matter, audience}`) and `bearingdraft` (`{action, reply}`), each gated by R50, and assembles from the asking member's own reads only, never wider than the member sees: for `infractiondraft`, `record` = `{plan, actions, correspondence, findings}` (the matter's plan when it names one, through `action-plans`; the plan's actions the member sees and their recorded entries, through `actions`; the findings those actions' legs cite, as `inquiry` reads them for that member); for `bearingdraft`, `action_record` (the action as `actions` reads it for the member, with its claims) and `reply_record` (the received entry `reply` names, with its text). The answer is labelled and writes nothing.
- **R52** [met] (was `plane` R47) A `signin` account answered by `answers.askAccount` carries its held token (`ai-accounts` R19's `key`) as `secret` in `agent-worker` R6's wire shape, for ask, draft and transcription; an answer `SIGNIN_TOKEN_REFUSED` calls `credentials.subscriptionTokenRefused`. Test: the secret reaches only the binding's request; control: an API key as before.
- **R53** [met] (was `plane` R50) The draft door takes `readtranslate` (`op=readtranslate`), `{subject: {kind, id, project?}, language?}`, `kind` one of `note`, `question`, `finding`, `notice`, for the asking member: 1. gated by R50: `draft`'s flag, then `ai-runs.partAdmitted("draft:reading")`; 2. it reads the subject only as she may: a note through `hypotheses.notesOf` for her (her own only: notes are never shared, `hypotheses` R12, R14); a question's or finding's text through `inquiry` for her; a notice's wording through `network-notices.noticesOf({project})` for her. An absent or unseen subject answers its owner's own refusal for an absent object, as given (`NO_SUCH_NOTE`; `inquiry`'s `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE`; `network-notices`' `noSuchProject`), before any account is read; this module mints no row; 3. the language is `translations.readingLanguage` (its R10), whose refusals pass through; 4. a passage over 20,000 characters is refused before any account is read, with the owner's text shown (a protective limit, K1881); 5. then the keep-away read for the subject's project, when it has one (`ai-accounts.aiKeptAway`, its R7), then `answers.askAccount({member, kind: "draft"})` (R49); each refusal is answered as given, nothing posted; 6. it posts `agent-worker`'s `POST /draft` with `task` `{op: "readtranslate", language, text, from_language?}` (its R86), minting no grant and sending no `told`.

  It answers `{translation, language, from_language, label: {kind: "machine"}, original: {kind, id, text}}`, with `untested` as R50 states; it writes nothing, and counts only the usage to her day (`ai-use.countAskUsage`, mode `draft`). Controls: another member's note answers `NO_SUCH_NOTE`, as an absent one does; her own note is translated.
- **R54** [met] (was `plane` R67) **The ask reads the reading language from `translations`.** The ask calls `translationsOf(ctx).readingLanguage(...)` (its refusals passing through), never `instance-setup`'s delegate. Test: the ask's language read answers as before through `translations`; control: an unknown member is refused as before.
- **R55** [met] (was `plane` R55) The assembly's ask, run, draft and transcribe calls to `agent-worker` (`/ask`, `/run`, `/draft`, `/transcribe`) carry the `trace` and member ref the door handed (`control-plane` R93; in process the strings `trace` and `memberRef`, K3293; the Durable Object class passes them through its RPC methods, `plane` R55) in the `x-civicsmith-trace` and `x-civicsmith-member-ref` headers, in `agent-worker` R83's shapes, and in no body or address; a call with none handed sends neither header. - **R56** [met] (was `plane` R59) **The ask stream passes through as written.** The stream `op=ask` answers reaches the caller chunk by chunk, unbuffered: the first chunk is written before the last is made, through the door and the Store's RPC. Where any buffering is, if anywhere, is not known: the job diagnoses it by a test with a slow producer and reports it to BOB before changing anything. Test: a producer that writes its first line then waits 2 s delivers that line to the reader before the wait ends.
- **R57** [met] (was `plane` R36) At start the composition root registers one transcriber with `extraction` (`registerTranscriber("plane", t)`), built on the object's own instances, so `extraction` reaches nothing above its layer: - `deployable()` is `ai-runs.partAdmitted("transcribe").admitted` (R50); anything that throws answers `false`; - `keptAway({project, use})` is `ai-accounts.aiKeptAway({project, use})` (its R7, R22), relayed as given; - `transcription({member, project, at})` answers `{member, project, at, act, credentials, useCheck, transcribe, untested}`, `untested` the label when the part runs on R50's exception, for `extraction` to record (its R74): `credentials` the object's own instance (its `accountFor`), `useCheck` `ai-use.useCheck`, `act` an id minted for this act (`transcribe:<captureSha prefix>:<instant>`, the prefix being the capture the AI path is asked for, so the id is read after that call), and `transcribe` the AI path below.

  The AI path, `transcribe({account, capture_sha, store, pages, use})`: with no `AGENT_WORKER` binding it answers `{ok: false, reason: "AGENT_WORKER_UNBOUND"}` and does nothing else; when the capture's bytes are absent from the evidence store, `EVIDENCE_ABSENT`; when no asked page is rendered within the limits below, `NO_PAGE_RENDERED`; neither posts to `agent-worker`. Otherwise it reads the capture's pages from the evidence store by ranges (R58) and renders the asked pages in ascending order, at most `TRANSCRIBE_PAGES_MAX` of them (`agent-worker`'s), each by `pdf-pixels.renderPageToPixels(bytes, page, {decodeDct: true})` or, for a page that answers `NOT_A_PDF`, `renderPictureToPixels(bytes, page, {maxEdge: TRANSCRIBE_PICTURE_EDGE, grey: true})` (`TRANSCRIBE_PICTURE_EDGE` exported, 1,568 px). A page the renderer refuses, or whose PNG is over `TRANSCRIBE_IMAGE_MAX_BYTES`, or beyond the maximum, is not sent and stays unread, named in the answer (R58). It posts `{capture_sha, pages, account}` to `agent-worker`'s `POST /transcribe` (its R72), `account` in its R6 wire shape (`accountFor`'s `key` named `secret`, `member` the acting member, `suggestions: false`), the secret leaving the object only in that one call. A binding that throws or answers no JSON answers `AGENT_WORKER_SILENT`; a refusal `agent-worker` answers (its R72 codes: `BAD_BODY`, `BAD_SHA`, `BAD_PAGES`, `NO_ACCOUNT`, `BAD_ACCOUNT`, `TRANSCRIBE_NEEDS_API_KEY`) is relayed with its own HTTP status, never read from `res.json()` alone, so `extraction` answers that refusal with that status. A `NO_PAGE_RENDERED` answer carries `refused`: one `{page, reason, process?}` for each asked page not sent, `reason` the renderer's refusal (`pdf-pixels` R39's key), `IMAGE_TOO_LARGE` or `DECODE_FAILED`, `process` the JPEG process when the renderer's detail names one; nothing is posted. Whenever the answer carries `usage`, whatever else it says, it is counted in one transaction through `ai-use.countUsage({owner, member, use: "transcribe", mode: "transcribe", model: engine, usage, calls, at, act})`, `owner` spelled as `ai-use` R1 from the account, so `ai-use.actualOf({act})` answers its cost after. It answers `agent-worker`'s answer, `{ok: true, engine, version, pages}` for `tier4Extend`. Control: a stub `agent-worker` answering 400 `BAD_PAGES` is answered 400 with that code.
- **R58** [met] (was `plane` R106) **The transcriber reads by ranges and names what it left.** `transcribe.mjs` reads the pages it renders from ranges of the capture (`acquisition`'s `readRange`, by the whole sha), and loads no PDF over the inline ceiling (`READ_BOUNDS`, quoted by `boundOf`) into a request; the pages it asked and the pages it sent are both stated, an asked page beyond `TRANSCRIBE_PAGES_MAX` or refused by the renderer is named unread (R57's `refused`) and never dropped silently, and what is left is owed as a job row (R74), not read in the request. Test: the press and `pagetranscribe` on a 24 MiB PDF and a 300-page scan do not load the object in the request, and `owedwork` lists what is left. Control: a 3-page file is read in the request. A posted answer names the pages left unread in `unread`, and the `NO_PAGE_RENDERED` answer in `refused`.
- **R59** [met] (was `plane` R25) The archive unpack's drain (`unpack.mjs`) reads `archive-unpack` events in pages, passing `after`, the `cursor` of the page's last event, while a page comes back full, so events at the retry limit, which stay queued, never hide newer events behind them; no event is read twice in one drain.

**The owed-work ledger, the job table and the object's alarm** (`pending.mjs`, `casesweep.mjs`, `acquire-timings.mjs`)
- **R60** [met] (was `plane` R73) **An acquisition's stage times are readable while it runs and after, by its trace.** The door hands its `trace` to `captureOp`; the object reads capture's `x-civicsmith-trace` header (the Store sets it in-process for a POST `acquire` and strips a caller's own); `acquiretimings?trace=` answers `{ok, stageTimes}`: the stage running and the stages that ran, held per trace in a bounded holder (64 entries, 60 s; an ended acquire's entry stays until it expires, so a late ask still reads it). Test: the whole hop (door → header → the object's timings under the trace → the read), while running and after; controls: another trace, none, an address trace, the bound.
- **R61** [met] (was `plane` R74) The door's owner getters (`ownerOps`' `of`, R86) also hand `extraction` (`extractionOf(ctx)`, for `readHeld`, extraction R80) and `projectRoster` (`projectRosterOf(ctx)`, for `projectList`'s `sharedDraftsOf`, project-roster R42), each asked only when its op is served. Test: through the real store, the door's `readheld` arm reaches extraction's one instance and `projectlist` answers `shared_drafts` from review; control: neither getter is called for another op.
- **R62** [met] (was `plane` R75) **The object's alarm finishes what was left pending, and the stage record survives a restart.** The object keeps `keeps_pending` (captures kept in parts whose whole is not yet put, `acquisition` R96) and the digests whose reading was deferred (`control-plane` R120); `alarm()` (`plane` R4) finishes them, each once, within a minute of being registered, and writes the reading by `extraction.readHeld`; a pending entry is removed only when its work has ended, and a failure leaves it for the next alarm up to a stated attempt count. The stage record of an acquisition (R60) is held in the object's storage and not only in memory, so `acquiretimings?trace=` answers the stages that ran after the object restarted. Test: a keep left pending is finished by the alarm and its reading follows; after the object is restarted `acquiretimings?trace=` still answers the stages. Control: with nothing pending the alarm does nothing; R60's bound still holds.
- **R63** [met] (was `plane` R76, in part) `acquiretimings?trace=` answers `restarted: true` beside `stageTimes` when the stage record survived an object restart (R62 holds the record in the object's storage). Test: after the object is restarted over the same storage the read answers `restarted: true`; control: a record made by the running instance answers none.
- **R64** [met] (was `plane` R77) **The owed-work ledger never forgets.** `keeps_pending`, keyed by digest and kind, holds kinds `keep`, `reading`, `ocr` (pages left), `subresources` (keyed by capture session), `unpack`, `fetch` (R67), `scan`, `safe_view`, `grade` and `ingest`; R74 is its table. The lower modules register through the hooks handed at composition: `captureOf(ctx).keepLater({sha, parts})` and `captureOf(ctx).owedLater({kind, sha, session?, parts?, detail?})`. No count and no clock ends an entry: retries back off from 15 s, doubling to at most one hour, and continue; `NO_HOME`, a missing owner or a refusal leave it owed; a second registration re-arms it (attempts 0, due now, a final reason cleared). An entry ends only when its owner answers done (`read`, `done`, an `ocr` answer without `owed`) or a `final` reason, which stays on the row; `owed: true` (an `ocr` cursor, a transient reading) continues. A keep that succeeds becomes a `reading` entry read in the same tick; `reading` and `ocr` call extraction's `readHeld({sha, storeName, env, kind})`; `subresources` calls acquisition's `owedRun` with `{storeName, env, deliver}`, `deliver` being `control-plane`'s `deliverOwedRender` (handed in, R1); `unpack` runs the archive-unpack drain as the daemon, a tree or depth stop ending it stated, a daily budget leaving it owed. Op `owedwork` (read; administrator and member; `?sha=` lists a digest's entries, else counts) is declared. A ledger of the old shape is carried over at the next start, every entry re-armed. A row is stamped started and its try counted before its owner runs; a started row found by a later alarm, of an isolate no longer running it, is an interrupted run, counted in `interrupted` (kept across a re-registration), which does not end the row (R76's poison rule judges the unit that was running, and its no-progress rule ends a step that advances nothing). A tick runs the light kinds (`subresources`, `unpack`) and then at most one of `keep`, `reading`, `ocr`; the consumer's wake is the entry's own due instant, never raised to now; `owedwork&sha=` also answers a `subresources` row by its session key; each entry states `interrupted`, `running`, `last_ms`, `last_mem`. Test: a reading with no home for 20 minutes of alarms is then read; a 40 MB keep promoted at 90 s; re-arm after exhaustion and after a final stop; the owed render delivered through `deliver`.
- **R65** [met] (was `plane` R80) **`owedwork` shows the watchdog's fields to an administrator.** The `owedwork` read answers an administrator `last_run`, `alarm_at`, `stale`, `oldest_overdue`, `watchdog_seen` and, since fix round 9 (G94; `plane` R103), `watchdog_ran` and `running_since` beside its counts, from the scheduler's `health()`, and answers a member none of them; the administrator is judged by the `viewer` stamp through membership (`class:admin` or an active admin member), never by a caller's `cls`. `oldest_overdue` is the scheduler's value (the earliest wake of any consumer), not a ledger-only minimum. The read writes nothing. Test: an administrator's answer carries the seven fields; a member's carries none.
- **R66** [met] (was `plane` R90) **The founder's session is an administrator for `owedwork`.** `administersOf` judges the viewer the door stamps: `class:admin`, the founder's bare `admin`, `member:<id>` naming an active administrator; any other spelling or none is not one. An administrator whose `health` throws or answers no object is shown the seven fields (R65) as `null` and a `watchdog_why`; a member none. Test: the founder's session, through the door, is shown the seven fields.
- **R67** [met] (was `plane` R81) **A parked fetch is owed work.** The ledger holds the kind `fetch`, keyed by the capture session, heavy (one heavy row a tick, R64), run by `acquisition`'s `owedFetch(store, {kind: "fetch", session}, {deliver})` with the control-plane's `deliver`; the row ends when `owedFetch` answers `done` or a `final` reason, and continues on `done: false` with a transient `reason`; `owedwork&sha=` answers a `fetch` row by its session key. Without an owner the row stays owed (`NO_OWNER`). Test: the real `acquire` parks a 9 MiB declared source as a `fetch` row with no body byte pulled; the tick runs the real `owedFetch`, which pulls the body.
- **R68** [met] (was `plane` R82) **A reading row goes on by the cursor it was left.** A `reading` row whose owner answers `more` with a cursor in `detail` is not ended: `readHeld` is handed the row's `detail`, one heavy piece runs a tick, the row is due one millisecond on, and `interrupted` is cleared when the cursor moved. Test: a text read in pieces over successive ticks, each piece once, the row ended when `more` is false; control: a row without a cursor ends as before.
- **R69** [met] (was `plane` R83) **Extraction is handed the owed-work ledger.** At construction the composition hands `extraction` the ledger's `owedLater` once (`registerOwe`, `extraction` R89), as it hands `capture` its `owedLater`. Test: a reading `extraction` defers is registered in the ledger; control: an `extraction` without `registerOwe` builds as before.
- **R70** [met] (was `plane` R87) **The case-file sweep is a scheduler consumer.** The assembly registers `casefile-sweep` with the scheduler (`casesweep.mjs`, beside the other consumers in the assembly), `finished` counting the editions built; the object's own alarm builds a complete edition that has no case file, retries a failed one after its backoff, re-derives its alarm at a restart, and heals a manifest recorded over bytes never stored. Test: the alarm builds a complete edition that has no case file and heals a manifest recorded over bytes never stored (workerd for the restart).
- **R71** [met] (was `plane` R91) **A row's time is its own.** `started_at` is the row's own start; the row states `last_ms`, `own_ms` (the part that is not awaiting a worker; the 10 s bar's) and `wait_ms` (the union of its owner's calls through a service binding). Test: a row states `last_ms`, `own_ms` and `wait_ms` (workerd, under contention).
- **R72** [met] (was `plane` R92) **`last_mem` is NULL when unmeasured and a finished row leaves its last run.** `keeps_last` (the assembly's table, declared to purge with `keeps_pending`; R97 names it beside `keeps_pending` and `acquire_stages`) holds the last run of each digest-or-session and kind: `ledgerLast` and `owedwork&sha=`'s `last`. Test: a row never measured states `last_mem` NULL; a finished row leaves its last run.
- **R73** [met] (was `plane` R94) **A keep with no home owes nothing.** After a keep (the `keep` owner, `pending.mjs`), the reading is asked in the same tick; an answer `NO_HOME` ends the keep owing nothing (the entered `reading` row is deleted; the filing's promotion owes the reading through control-plane's `unreadDocuments`); a digest with a home and no reading owes its reading; every other answer settles as before, so a transient failure still leaves it owed. Test: a keep with no home ends owing nothing; a digest with a home and no reading owes its reading; a transient failure stays owed.
- **R74** [met] (was `plane` R97) **`keeps_pending` is the job table for the reading kinds.** One row for each (whole sha or capture session, kind), additive to R64's columns: `state`, one of `queued`, `fetching`, `reading`, `partial`, `refused`, `finished`, `final`; `verdict` (JSON `{ok, size, limit, bound_key}`, the plan step's verdict on the bound); `cursor` (JSON); the counters `written`, `skipped`, `owed`, `failed` (integers) with `failed_why` (JSON, by cause; a refusal's facts, `kind`, `capture_sha` and `holder`, are carried as the refusing module answered them, `holder` present only where `provenance` names it to that caller, else null); `last_progress_at`, `rate_per_s`, `carrier` (the R2 key or `snapshots/<name>`), `reader_version` and `priority` (`raised` is "Read it"). Beside it `job_unit_attempts(sha, kind, unit, tries, stamped_at)` (a unit's attempt is stamped before its reader runs, R76) and `job_batches(sha, kind, batch, landed_at)` (a batch lands once, idempotent by (sha, kind, batch)). The kinds are R64's. `PENDING_RENDERS`, a module-level map lost at a restart, moves into the table. A row ends only when the job's counters say nothing is owed (`owed` zero and no unit pending), never because a hook was called, and a picture's `ocr` row does not end `finished` while the picture is unread. A `reading` row whose owner answers that the capture has no home yet (`NO_HOME`) waits as the `ocr` row does and counts no failure; the counters the owner's finishing answer carries (`counters: {written, skipped}`) are kept as the larger of the row's and the answer's, so a scan read to its end by the door's read and the OCR pass ends `finished` with the units written. The table is read by `control-plane`'s `op=reading` and `owedwork` (its R135), by `extraction`'s `completeness` and by the screens. It is declared to `record-core`'s purge as R97 says; a ledger of the old shape is carried over at the next start (the columns are additive), each row keeping what it had. Test: a row registered for each kind reads back every column; a batch landed twice writes once; a ledger of the old shape opens with every row kept. Control: a row whose `owed` is above zero is not ended by the owner's hook being called; a picture whose OCR failed transiently keeps its `ocr` row; an owner's real failure is still counted.
- **R75** [met] (was `plane` R98) **The composition root wires one registrar, `registerJob`.** The assembly sets `capture.registerJob = (e) => pending.registerJob(e)` beside `capture.keepLater = (a) => pending.keepLater(a)`; `acquisition`, `capture` and `doorbell` read it as they read `keepLater` (the function on the capture, else `opts.registerJob`, else the caller proceeds without a row and says so in its answer, never silently); `extraction` is handed it as `opts.registerJob` and its `owe` is `registerJob` bound to a kind (R69's hook is the same hand-off and is retired as a separate one). `registerJob({kind, sha, session?, cursor?, owed?, carrier?, parts?, detail?, verdict?, priority?})` answers `{ok: true, existed, state}` or `{ok: false, reason}`; it is synchronous and never throws. Its key is (the whole sha or, for `fetch` and `subresources`, the capture session; kind); a second registration re-arms, updates `cursor`, `owed`, `detail` and `parts` only when given, and never resets a counter. It is a synchronous write on the object's own SQL storage, so a caller makes it inside the synchronous function it gives `core.transact(fn)` and the row commits or rolls back with the filing; arming the alarm is a separate asynchronous `armJobs()` after the commit. A caller whose `registerJob` answers `{ok: false}` throws inside its transaction or answers a stated refusal, never swallows it. The root also hands `notice-producers` its `deps.readingsEnded({after, limit})`, a read of the job table (a callback, as `deps.updateNotice`, R39: a lower module reaches a higher one only so), answering the rows that ended (`finished`, ended in part with the reason, `refused`, `final`) with the member the filing was made for. Test: a kill between the put and the transaction finds one job row or none, never a row without its filing, for each way in (upload, `acquire`, the doorbell's pull, the archive); a second registration re-arms and changes no counter.
- **R76** [met] (was `plane` R101) **A step that advances nothing ends the row stated; a unit that kills its reader is stated for itself.** (1) No-progress: a row whose owner answers a step that moved neither its cursor forward nor any counter is ended `final` with that reason stated, on that tick, and never retried; a file that needs a user password ends `final` "needs a password" in one tick, never "being read" after. (2) Poison: a unit's attempt is stamped (`job_unit_attempts`) before its reader runs; a unit found stamped by a run that did not finish counts one kill, and after three kills of one unit that unit is stated unread alone (`PAGE_KILLED_READER`) after one retry at half the budget while the row goes on with the rest; "three interruptions end the whole read" (`PENDING_INTERRUPTED_MAX`) is retired. A member's kill is not counted `transient`. (3) A failure on the file's own bytes is deterministic: the row ends `final` with its reason; a transient failure at tick two or later is retried and never ends the read `partial` with no reason. Test: a stub reader answering `count: 0` ends stated; the user-password file ends in one tick; a page killed three times is stated and the other 299 pages of a 300-page scan are read. Control: a row that advances by one unit each tick is never ended by this rule.
