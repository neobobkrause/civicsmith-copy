# query-language: requirements

Next number: 36

**Size (P6).** 2,941 lines (`query.mjs`, T41). `viewerPredicate` and `GATE_MARK` are re-exports of `membership`'s (N37, done). Under the 4,000 at which BOB reports a module.

## Public

### Purpose

The query language: the one grammar a member types to search the record, and the one compiler that turns it into the statements `retrieval` runs. It parses a string into a tree, resolves fields and meaning arms from registries driven off the catalogue, and compiles every statement shape (page, count, select-all, snapshot, facets, meaning-grain rows, the level counts and the content-axis inputs) with the viewer's visibility predicate as the only gate. It is pure: it holds no database handle, no clock and no network, and it builds SQL without running it.

### Provides

Terms. A **query** `q` is a string. A **plan** is `compile`'s answer: `{ast, warnings, gate, viewer, sort, limit, offset, match, terms, widenable, meaningArms, meaning, facetFields, facetCols, cached, restricted, statements}`. A **statement** is `{sql, args}`. The **gate** is `viewerPredicate(viewer)` (`membership` R43). A **meaning arm** is one of `leg`, `resolves`, `concerns`, `content`, `passage` (`MEANING`); a **field** is a key of `FIELDS`. A warning is a sentence in `plan.warnings`; nothing here throws.

**compile({q, viewer, sort, dir, limit, offset, ids, facets, implicitOp, snippetChars, rows, rowLimit, rowOffset}) → plan** Never throws, for any input.
- **R1** [met] The grammar: bare words join by AND (`implicitOp: "or"` joins them by OR); `OR`, `AND` and `NOT` are operators only in capitals; a leading `-` negates; parentheses nest, and an unclosed one is read to the end with a warning; `"a b"` is one phrase; `term*` is a prefix; `field:value`, with a quoted value read to its end in as many pieces as it takes (`state:"open"`, `fm:"a.b"="c"`); `field:>v`, `>=`, `<`, `<=`, and `field:a..b` on a time or number field; `field:` or `field:*` asks presence; `has:field` and `has:<arm>` ask presence; `fm:path` and `fm:path=value` reach frontmatter, the path bounded to `[A-Za-z0-9_.[\]]{1,120}` or dropped with a warning; `text:` forces free text; `sort:field`, `sort:-field`, `sort:field:asc|desc`. A term with no letter or digit is dropped.
- **R2** [met] An unknown field is read as free text with the warning `unknown field "<f>"; read as free text`; an unknown `has:` field or `sort:` field is dropped with a warning.
- **R3** [met] `FIELDS` is the vocabulary of the bundle projection (`retrieval` R2): `id type group title state prior created updated criticality sha schema mode tier locator authority retrieved status hash monitored frequency checked annotations reeval since reevalsource capture connection legs actionkind risk addressee resolution due overdue`, and since T33 the fields of R28 (`standard cites person holder post kind phase stage basis period fund party event occurred obligor owed_to`), read as R29 says. `title`, `locator` and `authority` are free text (a column-scoped match); every other field is equality, its argument lower- or upper-cased where the field says so, and `type:` normalised through the catalogue's type map (a legacy spelling finds the canonical type).
- **R4** [met] `capture:` and `connection:` are two fields over two columns, never one composed strength (DEC-21); both are marked cached (R17).
- **R5** [met] A meaning selector takes a bare vocabulary word (`leg:hunch`), a qualified sub-field (`leg:role=cuts_against`, `leg:ground=*`), a comparison on the bare field (`resolves:>=B`) or on a named sub-field (`leg:grade>=B`, `content:cap<C`). Bare words are indexed from the catalogue's own vocabularies, never a copy. A bare word two sub-fields claim, an unknown sub-field, a comparison against nothing, a comparison on `passage:`, and a `passage:` term with no word are each DROPPED with a warning naming why (and, for an ambiguous word, both spellings to use): a dropped arm widens the answer; no spelling compiles to a predicate that matches nothing. `meaningArms` lists exactly the arms that compiled.
- **R6** [met] The `content:` arm's sub-fields: `kind` (the content extent kinds, `content` R1; `dom` is not a word), `stale`/`current`, `minted` (`member`, `plane`, `machine` as classes; any other value is equality on the minter), `cap` (a letter; `undetermined` is a null cap over text rows; `does-not-apply` is the rows cited as bytes; a comparison never matches either), `chain` (the last step kinds of `text-chain`; `undetermined` a null chain over text rows; `does-not-apply` the bytes rows; presence; a machine reading (`text-chain`'s `MACHINE_READ_KINDS`) also selects the units read in more than one kind (`mixed`)), `cited`/`uncited` (whether any live or version leg names the row). The `passage:` arm matches the indexed text of captures (`extraction`'s units); `text:` keeps meaning the group's own notes.
- **R7** [met] No member input enters a statement's text: for every value a member can type, the SQL is byte-identical and only `args` move. Column names come from the registries, and an ORDER BY only from `SORTABLE`.
- **R8** [met] Every statement every shape returns carries the gate's mark and takes its visibility from one call of the gate; none mints a second gate. An absent or unrecognised viewer compiles to the deny predicate, so the answer is empty, never unfiltered.
- **R9** [met] `viewerPredicate` and `GATE_MARK` are `membership`'s (R43), re-exported unchanged.
- **R10** [met] Order: relevance when the query has a positive text term, else `updated` descending; every order ends `bundle_id` ascending; a field sort puts nulls last in both directions; an explicit `sort` parameter outranks a `sort:` token.
- **R11** [met] Relevance is computed over the rows the viewer may see, and only the order is published, never a score: revising or creating a bundle the viewer cannot see changes neither the order nor the snippets of the viewer's answer (D-447). More than `RANK_ATOMS_MAX` (8) distinct terms are weighed as one, with a warning. *(K102)*
- **R12** [met] Bounds, each published as applied after clamping: `limit` 50 by default, 1–500; `offset` at least 0; select-all at most `IDS_MAX` (50,000) ids; meaning rows 200 by default, 1–1,000; snippet length 4–64 tokens, 12 by default.
- **R13** [met] `widenable` is true only for an implicit conjunction of more than one atom; the same query compiled with `implicitOp: "or"` is its wider reading, in which the positive atoms are ORed and each `NOT` atom stays a restriction (`(a OR b) AND NOT c`; K5267).
- **R14** [met] The bundle-grain shapes: `page` (the provenance columns `PROVENANCE_COLS` and a `snippet`, null when there is no term, and `snippet_from`, R33), `count`, `ids`, `snapshot` (id and sha), `facets` and `facetScan` (the requested facets or `DEFAULT_FACETS`, fields only, nulls excluded; both forms count alike). An `ids` list is an arm intersected with the query, never a filter after it. A query with any number of arms compiles. *(K75 (5))* An `ids` list of any length is bound as one statement argument (a JSON array read through `json_each`), so every statement stays within the plane's bound-variable ceiling (D-36, about 100) however many ids a scope holds (K3872, K3874).
- **R15** [met] `rows=<arm>` (`meaning`): every meaning row of every bundle in scope, the whole set of each bundle and not only the rows the arm matched; a row whose bundle the viewer cannot see is absent (never redacted), and so is a row naming another bundle the viewer cannot see; a row naming a bundle that does not exist is returned with `<col>_present` false; the count is over the same joins, so it never exceeds what paging reaches; the order is the bundle, then the arm's identity. `rows=passage` returns only the units matching the query's `passage:` terms (their OR), each with the `content_id` of the current row at that extent or null; with no term it returns every unit in scope, `snippet` null, and `meaning.matched` false.
- **R16** [met] `meaning({mode: "levels"})` counts the documents in scope and those holding any row of the arm's table; `mode: "axis"` lists, per capture of the documents in scope, the raw extraction and index observations and whether a reading exists, at most `MEANING_AXIS_CAP` (500) plus one. Both take their scope from the query's OTHER arms (the row arm stripped), so a miss never empties its own denominator.
- **R17** [met] `plan.cached` names each cached column (a field marked `asOf`) the plan reaches, and by which route: a filter anywhere in the tree, a default facet, or the sort. `cachedNotes(routes, {facets, ordered})` publishes only the routes that ran, in `FIELDS` order, each with `as_of`, the authority that derives the current value and a sentence; `[]` when none. (C-5a; §7 item 5) `due` and `overdue` are cached columns (the action's clock as of its last write, `actions`' own read the authority), so a plan that reaches either names it with its `as_of` note, and `overdue:` filters the cached flag and says so, never reading it silently as "late now".
- **R18** [met] `meaningVocabulary()` answers, per arm: `table`, `key`, `grain`, `bare`, `fields` (column, values, `selects` where a selector reads a column the row does not publish verbatim), `words`, `ambiguous`, `level`, and `rows` `{grain, identity, columns, refs}`, where `columns` is exactly what a row carries. `ambiguousBareWords()` lists each arm's contested words.
- **R24** [met] Proximity (K105): `NEAR(a b …)` or `NEAR(a b …, n)`, in free text and inside `text:` and `passage:`, matches where every one of its terms (a word, a quoted phrase or a prefix, as R1) occurs within `n` tokens of the others, `n` being 10 when absent and clamped to 0–100 with a warning. `NEAR` is an operator only in capitals. A `NEAR` with fewer than two terms, with no closing parenthesis, or with an `n` that is not a whole number is read as its terms joined by AND, with a warning naming why. It compiles to the full-text engine's own proximity operator, the terms moving only in `args` (R7).
- **R25** [met] (N106, K144) The compiler reads the per-bundle projection through a relation its caller names: `compile(query, {projection: {table, key}})` joins `table` on `key` = `bundles.bundle_id`, and keys the text index through `table.fts_id`. This module names no later module's table. With no `projection`, the columns are read from `bundles` (the arrangement before N106).

- **R26** [met] (N136, N137; K75 (2), K649 (6)) A field whose column a later module holds in a table of its own is read through the relation its caller names: `compile(query, {fields: {<field>: {table, key, col}}})` joins `table` on `key` = `bundles.bundle_id` and reads `col` for that field wherever the plan filters, facets or sorts by it, the names taken only from what the caller names (R7) and the gate unchanged (R8). `retrieval` names the relations its registrations hold (its R62): `legs` from `inquiry`'s table, `capture` and `connection` from `strength`'s. A field named with no relation is read from the column R3's registry names on `bundles`, as before. The grammar, every answer, R4's two axes and R17's cached marks (`capture` and `connection` stay cached, `legs` exact) are unchanged; a relation that is not three identifiers is ignored with a warning and the field read as before. This module holds no registration of its own (R20).

**textOf(bundleId, files) → `{title, body, meta, locator, authority}`** What the text index holds for a bundle.
- **R19** [met] `title` from the frontmatter; `body` the prose after the frontmatter, then every other `.md` and `.txt` file by path (JSON files never); `meta` the bundle id and every frontmatter key and scalar value; `locator` and `authority` from `source`. Each is cut at 128 KiB. Frontmatter that does not parse leaves `body` the whole text and the rest empty; never throws.

**T33: local-day ranges, new fields, the saved-query form** (T33-39; K1444 (iii), K1481)
- **R27** [met] (C-5b; §7 item 5) `compile` takes `zone`, the profile's time zone its caller governs (`local-facts`). On a time field (`created`, `updated`, `due`, `occurred` and the others R3 types `time`), a value written as a date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) is a local day in `zone`, and a range `a..b` of dates is inclusive of both days: it compiles to the instants from the start of local day `a` to before the start of the local day after `b`, each bound computed by `civil-time` (its local-day and calendar validation), so `created:2026-03-01..2026-03-31` holds every instant of 31 March in that zone. A comparison with a date (`>`, `>=`, `<`, `<=`) is on whole local days the same way. An impossible date (2026-02-31) and a date term with no `zone` given are each dropped with a warning naming why (a dropped term widens the answer, R5's rule), never read as UTC days. A value written as an instant compiles as before.
- **R28** [met] The T33 fields (R3), each an equality on the value a member types (lower-cased where the owner's words are lower-case), `occurred` a time field (R27): `standard` (a held standard, `STD-`, that a bundle rests on or cites; `standards`), `cites` (an id or instrument key a bundle's document cites); `person` (a `person` entity a bundle concerns), `holder` (a person holding, on the bundle's date, an office the bundle names; `lines.holderAt`), `post` (an office entity a bundle names); the money fields `kind`, `phase`, `stage`, `basis`, `period`, `fund` and `party` (of the money facts a bundle states; `money`'s closed words); `event` (an `EVT-` a bundle attests) and `occurred` (that event's `when`; `events`); `obligor` and `owed_to` (the parties of a duty a bundle sources or records; `duties`). A word outside an owner's closed list is dropped with a warning naming the list.
- **R29** [met] A T33 field is read only through the relation its caller names for it (R26's `fields`: `{table, key, col}`, the owner's read contract as `retrieval` registers it); with no relation named it is dropped with a warning (`"<f>" is not available here; read as nothing`), never read from a column of `bundles`, and the rest of the query compiles as before. The gate (R8) and R7's rule hold for every such join.
- **R30** [met] (K1481; `answers` R15) `savedForm({q, implicitOp?, sort?, dir?})` answers the saved-query form a standing question keeps, `{ok: true, form: {v: 1, q, implicitOp, sort, dir}}`, or a refusal: `SAVED_QUERY_EMPTY` (no term that compiles), `SAVED_QUERY_DROPS` (compiling it drops any term or field, R2, R5, R27, R29, naming each warning, so what is saved is what runs), `SAVED_QUERY_SELECTION` (it carries `ids`, a selection rather than a search). The form holds no viewer, no limit and no offset: it is run later under its owner's sight at that moment (`retrieval`), and compiles to the same plan for the same viewer every time. Pure; never throws.
- **R31** [met] (R085-21; K3510; amends R15; `retrieval` R15) Under `rows=passage`, a query with no `passage:` term reads its positive bare words, phrases, prefixes and `NEAR` groups (R1, R24) as `passage:` terms (their AND, as `passage:` would join them), and compiles with the warning `words read as passage words (rows=passage); use text: for the record's own text`; a `text:` term, a negated word and every selector keep their meaning. The scope is then what `passage:` gives (not narrowed to bundles whose own text holds the words), intersected with `ids` when given. Test: `rows=passage`, `q=pothole`, `ids` naming a captured document whose record text lacks the word but whose unit has it, answers that unit and the warning; control: `rows=passage&q=text:pothole` reads record text and answers no unit there; with `passage:pothole` no warning.
- **R32** [met] (R085-27; K3528; `walk-085-r5` D4; amends R15; extends R31) Under `rows=leg`, a query with no `text:` term reads its positive bare words as R31 reads them under `rows=passage`: they do not narrow the scope to bundles whose own text holds every word, and the query compiles with the warning `bare words ignored (rows=leg); use text: for the record's own text`; a `text:` term, a negated word and every selector keep their meaning. The rows are those of the bundles in scope, intersected with `ids` when given. Test: `rows=leg`, `q=crosswalk repaired`, `ids` naming a question whose own text lacks `repaired` but which holds a leg, answers the leg and the warning; control: `rows=leg&q=text:repaired` answers no row there.
- **R33** [met] (T50; N1166; K3501; WALK-085 F4; JOURNEYS V4 B-9; amends R14) In the `page` shape every hit carries `snippet_from`, always present as `snippet` is: `"passage"`, `"text"` or `null`. A hit whose bundle is matched through a `passage:` arm and has no text snippet carries as `snippet` the words of the first matching unit of its captures (capture sha, then reading order), cut as R12 cuts (same brackets, ellipsis, clamp), with `snippet_from: "passage"`. A hit matched through its own text carries the text snippet with `"text"`, and a text snippet is cut from the record's `title` and `body` only (R19), never from `meta` (the bundle id and the frontmatter keys and values flattened); a hit whose text term matches only `meta` and has no matching passage carries `snippet: null`, `snippet_from: null`. With no term, `null`/`null`. `rows=passage`'s own snippet is unchanged. Test: a bundle matched only by `passage:420` answers a snippet holding the matched words of its passage with `"passage"`; a question matched by a word in its body answers that body's words with `"text"`. Control: a question whose id and frontmatter alone hold the word answers no snippet and no frontmatter.
- **R34** [met] (fix round 9, G134d, G135f; K4922; `retrieval` R95, R96) **A passage snippet names its unit.** In the `page` shape every hit carries `snippet_unit`, always present: for a hit whose `snippet_from` is `"passage"`, the `rowid` of the unit row the snippet was taken from (the same row R33's choice takes: least `capture_sha`, `seq`, `extent_kind`, `extent`), else `null`; the caller reads that unit by key for its kind and extent, and no second compile of the query is needed. Test: a passage hit's `snippet_unit` is the row whose text holds the snippet's words, with two matching units in one bundle choosing the first by that order; controls: a text hit and an unmatched hit carry `null`.
- **R35** [met] (T52 R1; W10, the 0.87.0 walk's B-K5 marker; amends R12, R14, R33) **A snippet marks a match with two marks the stored text cannot hold.** Every snippet this module cuts (the page's text and passage snippets, and `rows=passage`'s) opens a match with `MARK_OPEN` (U+0001) and closes it with `MARK_CLOSE` (U+0002), both exported, in place of the brackets; the ellipsis and the clamp (R12) are unchanged. `textOf` removes both characters from what it indexes (R19). A stored text that holds either (a unit, or a record indexed before this rule) is cut with no mark and the characters removed. Every reader of a snippet reads these marks, never brackets (`screens-daily`'s draw, W28). Test: a stored "[placed nowhere]" and "[object Object]" stay text in the snippet with the match marked by the two marks; a stored text holding U+0001 is cut with no mark.

### Suggestions

- **Later layers' vocabulary.** `leg:` and `content:cited` read layer-6 tables, and the fields `capture`, `connection`, `legs`, `actionkind` … `overdue` read projection columns written by `strength`, `inquiry` and `actions`. A registry this module offers (the K31 pattern: an arm, a sub-field predicate or a field registered by its owner, `legacy-store` registering them until each extraction) would keep the grammar unchanged and the order intact. BOB decides (P17); see `build/extraction/retrieval.md` §5.
- **The `axis` statement** joins `register`, `readings` and `observation_log`; it could move to `retrieval`, which already reads them, leaving the compiler only the shapes of the query itself.
- **T33 (T33-39).** The field names `kind`, `phase`, `stage`, `basis`, `period`, `fund` and `party` are the entries' (B1b.7); `money`'s own words may ask a prefix (`money.kind:`) if a member's `kind:` would read ambiguously in the design stream's view, a naming question for BOB. R27's `zone` is passed by `retrieval`, which reads it from `local-facts` (governed) or `jurisdictions`. `savedForm`'s refusal codes are this fold's.
- Tests: R7 by compiling hostile values; R8 by walking every shape of a plan; R11 by revising a bundle the viewer cannot see; R15 by a basis of five legs of which one matches; R16 by a miss over a partly indexed scope.
