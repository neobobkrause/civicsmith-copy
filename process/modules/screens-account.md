# screens-account: requirements

## Public

### Purpose

The Settings screens of accounts: a member's own account, recovery codes and notes, how the assistant is connected for her (`connect`), the team's assistant (`teamai`), and the members roster with its invitations, grants and signing keys (`account.mjs`, `recovery.mjs`, `notes.mjs`, `connect.mjs`, `teamai.mjs`, `members.mjs`). Taken from `screens-settings` with their requirements and numbers; pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

Numbers are `screens-settings`'s, kept when ARCH split it (22 Nov 2026); a bare R<n> not here is that number in `screens-settings`. The tests that prove these requirements boot the whole `SCREENS` through `screens-settings`'s test kit, so they are `screens-settings`'s until this module has a kit of its own (code work for its job).
