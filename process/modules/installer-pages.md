### Purpose

What the installer shows and says: its first page and update page, the guide for a founder with no Cloudflare account, the progress page, the finished panels and the stops, every sentence read by key, and the invitation page (`ui.mjs`, `words.mjs`, `guide.mjs`, `held.mjs`, `images.mjs`, `release.mjs`, `scripts/invite-page.mjs`, `bio-plane/public/newgroup/`). Taken from `installer` with their requirements and numbers.

Numbers are `installer`'s, kept when ARCH split it (22 Nov 2026); a bare R<n> not here is that number in `installer`. Its tests run through `installer`'s test world and barrel, so they are `installer`'s until this module has a world of its own (code work for its job).

**The words members and founders see** (DEC-149; N664)

**The short name and the description** (T35; DEC-143, DEC-146)
- **R41** [met] (N657; DEC-143, DEC-87, DEC-88) The install page's act that chooses the group's short name (the slug, R2) shows the Irreversible weight on its button, the weight's word always beside its mark, because the choice can never be undone. Only the founder's confirming act in the dialog sends `/begin`; closing the dialog sends nothing. The `update` mode chooses no short name and shows neither. It is not a publication and opens no ceremony (DEC-143). The words are the design stream's; until they are given, the page says this meaning plainly.
- **R42** [met] (N660; DEC-146 (1)–(3)) Where the installer's pages and the invitation page (R1, R22) say in one line what Civicsmith is, the line is exactly \"Free software for groups that check whether government keeps its own rules and promises.\", and, where there is room for a second line, exactly \"Neighbourhood and issue groups, newsrooms, professional associations, and public offices checking their own work.\" (the same two lines as `public-read` R30's credit page).
