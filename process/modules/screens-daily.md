**Size (P6).** Estimated 2,200–2,800 lines with comments, its own keyed sentences included, measured at its START (M-T46-1, K617).

## Public

### Purpose

A member's daily screens: Home, the queue and "Where else to go", with the helpers Home and the queue share, and the module's `SCREENS`, which composes these with `screens-find`'s. Pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

**`SCREENS`** (the screen contract, `interface-shell` R21; each sentence read by key, R34)

**home** (Home; the registry's acts `promote`, `startfrom`)
- **R1** [met] Reads `queue` (the first items waiting on the member, each by its kind mark, under "Your queue (<n>)") and `projectdirectory` (each project the member may see, its question count and its stage ladder Forming, Investigating, Matured, Closed). Heading: the group's name and its description, from the public `groupidentity` read. Act: `promote` "Start a project" (a new project, as `store-request` R11 and `promotion` take it: its title, the member its owner), opening `project` on success. `startfrom` is not drawn in T46 (no runner can yet start what it lists).

**queue** (Queue; the registry's 11 acts; DEC-110, DEC-131)
- **R2** [met] Reads `queue` with the member's chosen sort (time added, time due, case, kind; `queue` R49) and grouping by case, drawing each item's kind by `mark.kind.<k>`, its summary and detail (the `queue.<kind>.*` words where keyed, else the item's own `summary` and `detail`), due dates with `mark.due`, and hints with `mark.hint` opening in place (what was matched, how, the false-alarm rate, what it is not). The three-kinds note is shown once per member on the device.
- **R3** [met] An item's acts are the item's own decorated `options` (`queue` R17), drawn when the op is one of the screen's registered acts (`taskresolve`, `taskforward`, `proposedispose` with a reason, `queuesnooze` with a date, `queuemute`, `adoptversion`, `keepversion`, `reminderanswer`, `promote`, `projectkeyset`, `exploreapprove`); any other option is drawn as "Open", a link to its subject's screen. An item resolved or muted leaves the list on the answer. (T51 K4265) `release` and `filingapprove` are registered acts of Home and the queue; a letter's item draws `filingapprove` ("Read and approve it"): `filingsfor {action}`, then `filingapprove {filing}`.

**Common**
- **R7** [met] `interface-shell` R8–R13 hold on every screen; after an act, the screen re-reads what it changed and draws it.
