## Public

### Purpose

A progression is a group's declared flow for one kind of happening: its ordered stages, what each presupposes, how many documents it may hold, how soon it must follow, and whether it is required. An instance threads real captured documents through those stages by following one entity. From the declared flow and the documents threaded, the record derives, on every read and never stored, the instance's grade (its weakest link), each required stage that is missing and not lawfully discharged, and each missing stage that is overdue. The derived questions are aggregated one per (progression, stage), and a member's recorded decision ages a question without hiding it.

### Provides

Terms. A **stage** is `{stage_key, stage_no, label, after_stage, cardinality, within_interval, required}`; `required` is one of `always`, `usually`, `sometimes`, `never`, `unless_exception`. A **version** is one declaration of a definition, numbered from 1. An **instance** is (progression key, entity id). A **grade** is the connection grade A–D; `established` is A or B (both read from `entities`, K76). A viewer is the control plane's stamp, read through `membership`. A decision is `deferred` or `dismissed`.

**defineProgression({progressionKey, label, note?, stages, declaredBy, basis, citation?}) → definition or refusal** (`op=progressiondefine`)
- **R1** [met] Refusals, in order: `NO_KEY`, `PROGRESSION_NO_LABEL` (C-100.2; N285), `NO_STAGES`; then for each stage in order `NO_STAGE_KEY`, `DUPLICATE_STAGE`, `NO_CARDINALITY`, `BAD_REQUIRED` (naming the five words); then `UNKNOWN_AFTER` (C-33.26) for an `after` naming no stage of this definition. A refusal writes nothing: a bad stage refuses the whole definition.
- **R2** [met] A first declaration writes version 1 with its basis: one without a basis statement is refused `NO_BASIS` (the shared row C-33.40, `record-grammar` R29), judged as R4 judges a revision's, after every stage check; a citation stays optional at version 1 (DEC-88; K1025). The declarer is the control plane's stamp. Bounds: note 1,000, basis statement 4,000, citation 2,000 characters.
- **R3** [met] A declaration identical to the current version (label, note, and every stage field, in order) writes nothing and answers `unchanged: true` with the current version.
- **R4** [met] A declaration that differs is a revision: refused `NO_BASIS` without a statement, then `NO_CITATION` without a citation (both judged after every stage check); otherwise written as version N+1 with its declarer, instant and basis, `prior_version: N`. Every earlier version stands unchanged. A definition declared before versions were kept is first written as version 1, basis not recorded.

**readProgression({progressionKey, version?}) → definition or refusal** (`op=progression`)
- **R5** [met] `NO_KEY`; a key never declared answers `found: false`, not a refusal. Otherwise the requested version (default the current) with its label, note, declarer, instant, basis and stages in order, `current`, `current_version`, and every version held with its declarer, instant and basis. A version not held is `PROGRESSION_VERSION_NOT_HELD` (C-100.8; N285) naming the versions held.

**threadInstance({progressionKey, entityId, placements, threadedBy, viewer}) → instance or refusal** (`op=thread`)
- **R6** [met] Refusals, in order: `NO_KEY`, `NO_ENTITY`, `NO_PLACEMENTS`, `NO_SUCH_PROGRESSION`, `NO_SUCH_ENTITY` (`entities.noSuchEntity`, its R36); then for each placement `NO_STAGE`, `BAD_STAGE`, `NO_CAPTURE`, `DUPLICATE_PLACEMENT` (one document at one stage twice), `NOT_CONCERNED` (the document does not resolve to the entity). A refusal writes nothing.
- **R7** [met] A placement's grade and bundle are the record's: the document's strongest resolution to the entity, never the caller's. The threading member is the control plane's stamp.
- **R8** [met] A thread records the instance's placements as a new dated version (K102): the current version is what the instance is read against (R10), every earlier one reads back with who threaded it and when, and nothing is overwritten. It answers R10's read with `threaded`, `threaded_by` and `at`. It then tells R33's listeners, so a scheduler registered there can wake at R17's next deadline.

**onThreaded(module, fn)** (K31's pattern; `scheduler`, layer 10, registers its `arm`, K90 (6))
- **R33** [met] A later module registers once at start; a malformed registration, or a second by the same module, is refused through `membership`'s `listenerRefusal` (its R81: `LISTENER_MALFORMED`, `LISTENER_DECLARED`), and the listeners run in the modules' total order (`membership`'s `MODULE_ORDER`, R83). After each successful `threadInstance` has written, every registered listener is called once, in the modules' total order, with `{progressionKey, entityId, nextDeadline}`, `nextDeadline` being R17's `next_deadline` at the thread's instant (null when none). A listener that throws or rejects never changes the thread or its answer.

**readInstance({progressionKey, entityId, viewer}) → instance or refusal** (`op=instance`)
- **R9** [met] `NO_KEY`, `NO_ENTITY`. No such definition answers `found: false, defined: false`; nothing threaded answers `found: false, defined: true` with no grade and no findings.
- **R10** [met] Derived on read against the current definition: the stages in order, each with its documents and grade (its strongest document); a `chain` of consecutive placed stages, each link graded the weaker end; the instance `grade`, the weakest link, and `established`; with fewer than two placed stages the grade is null and `grade_determined: false`, never invented. The instance, each finding and each discharge name `definition_version`.
- **R11** [met] A stage with no document whose `required` is `always`, `usually` or `unless_exception` is a `missing_predecessor` finding carrying the instance grade (or `undetermined`), `dischargeable: true`, unless an exception names that stage, when it is a `discharged_skip` carrying the exceptions. A missing `sometimes` or `never` stage is neither. An exception naming a present stage discharges nothing and is shown on its stage. *(K102)*
- **R12** [met] Each finding carries `disposition`: the decision recorded for its (progression, stage) with state, reason, decider, instant, the version it judged (or `not recorded`) and whether it applies (R20), or null. `open_finding_count` counts the findings no applying decision governs; `finding_count` counts all of them.
- **R13** [met] A document's bundle id is null for a viewer who may not see that bundle; capture shas, grades and findings are the same for every reader.

**dischargeStage({progressionKey, entityId, stageKey, captureSha, reason, citation, declaredBy, viewer})** (`op=discharge`); **readExceptions({progressionKey, entityId, viewer})** (`op=exceptions`)
- **R14** [met] Refusals, in order: `NO_KEY`, `NO_ENTITY`, `NO_STAGE`, `NO_CAPTURE`, `NO_REASON`, `NO_CITATION` (C-33.41), `NO_SUCH_PROGRESSION`, `NO_SUCH_ENTITY` (`entities.noSuchEntity`, its R36), `BAD_STAGE`, `NOT_CONCERNED`. Success records the exception document against that stage (reason at most 4,000, citation at most 2,000 characters, declarer stamped); recording the same document at the same stage again writes a new dated version of that exception, the current one applying and every earlier one reading back through R15 (K102). It answers R10's read with the discharge's fields.
- **R15** [met] `readExceptions`: `NO_KEY`, `NO_ENTITY`; every exception recorded for the instance, applied or not, ordered by stage then capture, bundle ids withheld as in R13.

**Overdue; overdueScan(now) → {overdue_count, next_deadline, next_deadline_at}**
- **R16** [met] A `missing_predecessor` stage is also an `overdue_successor` finding, with its deadline, predecessor date and how late it is, when its `within` reads `<n> day|week|month|year` (plural allowed), its `after` stage is placed, a placed document of that stage has its own date (R37), and now is past the latest such date plus the interval. The interval is counted by `civil-time` in its units on the local day of the profile's time zone as `local-facts` governs it (days and weeks as local days, months and years by the calendar), never as fixed 86,400,000 ms days (§7 item 3; R-1 T-E4); the deadline is a local day and `overdue_by` is stated in the interval's units. It runs from the event's own date, never from when the group captured, read or registered the document (K1444 (ii)): a placed predecessor with no own date makes the finding `overdue: undetermined`, its why naming the capture date as a bound only, never as the date. In every other case it is not overdue, and no deadline is invented. Now is the caller's instant (`now=`, milliseconds), else the instance's configured clock, else the wall clock.
- **R17** [met] `overdueScan` writes nothing; `next_deadline` is the earliest deadline strictly after now, or null, so a consumer never re-arms to now.

**proposalsFeed(now) → {instances, proposals, dispositions, counts}** (`op=proposals`)
- **R18** [met] One walk over every threaded instance. `instances[]`: each instance with an open finding, missing findings then overdue. `proposals[]`: one per (progression, stage) carrying its instances; the weakest grade across them, null with `grade_determined: false` if any is undetermined; `overdue`, `overdue_count`, `kinds`, `surfaced_by: machine`, `definition_version` and `prior_disposition` (a decision about an earlier version); ordered by instance count, most first, then key. `dispositions[]`: every decision recorded, with its version view. A finding governed by an applying decision leaves `instances` and `proposals` and stays in `dispositions`.

- **R43** [met] (T47; N983; K3027; amends R16, R18) `op=proposals` and `op=captureprogressions` are evaluated at the server's instant, since its answer (which stage is overdue, `overdue_count`) may gate an act: the route takes no instant from the request, a `now` in the query or the body being ignored, never read, so a request carrying one answers exactly as the same request without it. Its overdue findings are judged at R16's configured clock (the instance's clock, which tests set through `progressionsOf`'s `nowMs` option, else `BIO_NOW_MS`, else the wall clock). An in-plane caller (`queue-producers`, `calculations`, `intent`) may still pass its own instant to `proposalsFeed`, as R16 states; no request does.
- **R44** [met] (T50; N1102; K3326; DEC-243; amends R9, R10, R24) `instancesFor({set, limit?, after?, viewer})` (`op=instances`) answers the matters followed through a progression, for a set of entity ids the caller resolves (a project's, as `events` R28 leaves a project to its caller): one item for each (progression, entity) threaded for an entity of `set`, in entity id then progression key order, each a summary of R10's read taken now: `{progression_key, label, definition_version, entity_id, thread_version, stage_count, placed_count, grade, grade_determined, established, finding_count, open_finding_count, next_deadline}`, `next_deadline` the earliest deadline day (`YYYY-MM-DD`) of a stage not yet placed whose predecessor is placed (R16), `null` when none or undetermined, so the timeline's state b draws "by {date}" for a step not yet due (DEC-243; K3630). `NO_SET` for an empty set; `SET_TOO_LARGE` over 200 ids. `limit` 1-200 (default 50), `truncated` by reading one past, `next`. An entity of the set with nothing threaded contributes nothing and is no error. No bundle id is answered (R13); findings are derived on this read (R24) at the clock R43 names. It writes nothing and never throws. Test: two entities, one threaded under two progressions: two items with their counts. Control: an unthreaded entity yields none; a set of 201 is refused.
- **R45** [met] (T50; N1102; DEC-243, DEC-239; amends R16, R25) An `overdue_successor` finding carries `says: {key, vars}`, the members' words read through `setup-words` `word(key, vars)`: the sentence is `progression.nodoc` ("No document threaded.") then `progression.expects` with `{interval, n, date}` (`interval` the stage's `within` as declared, `n` the `after` stage's `stage_no`, `date` the deadline day `YYYY-MM-DD`), or, where R16 leaves the deadline undetermined (the predecessor has no own date), `progression.expects.undetermined` with `{interval, n}`. No member-facing field of the finding says "overdue", "late" or "missing"; `kind: "overdue_successor"`, `overdue`, `overdue_by` and `deadline` are identifiers and stay. The English `detail` is a record-keeping field no screen draws. Test: a stage due in 10 days after step 1, past its deadline, answers `progression.expects` with `interval` `10 days`, `n` 1 and the deadline; with no own date on step 1, `progression.expects.undetermined`. Control: no value of `says` or its words contains "overdue" or "late".
- **R46** [met] (T51; DEC-329 (3); N1233, K3806; amends R12, R19) Each finding (R10, R12, R19, and R18's `instances[]`) carries `state`, one of `open`, `dismissed`, `deferred`: the state of the decision that applies to it (R20) when one does, else `open` (no decision, or one judging an earlier version of the declared flow, which `disposition.applies` already says). So `screens-investigate` draws `progression.finding.state.<state>` (explained by `progression.finding.state.help`) and not a state it works out itself; the member-facing words are keys it holds. `open_finding_count` (R12) counts exactly the findings whose `state` is `open`. The words of the Define and Revise step fields (`progression.steps.label.*`, six, `progression.steps.help`, `progression.revise.help`) label the stage fields R1 and R5 already answer (`stage_key`, `label`, `after_stage`, `within_interval`, `required`, `cardinality`) and are `screens-investigate`'s, with no read here. Test: a finding with no decision answers `open`, one dismissed at the current version `dismissed`, one deferred at an earlier version `open`; control: `open_finding_count` equals the count of `open`.

**captureProgressions({captureSha, now}) → {capture_sha, count, instances}** (`op=captureprogressions`)

- **R21** [met] Refusals, in order: `NO_KEY`, `NO_STAGE`, `NOT_A_DISPOSITION` (R35), `NO_REASON`, `BAD_REASON` (over 160 characters, or a quotation mark, backslash or line break), `NO_DECIDER` (empty stamp), `NO_SUCH_PROGRESSION`, `BAD_STAGE`, `NO_DEFINITION_VERSION` (C-33.42: absent, or not a positive integer; a boolean is no version), `DEFINITION_MOVED` (C-33.43: not the current version, earlier or never held).
