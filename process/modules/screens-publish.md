## Public

### Purpose

A team's own publishing screens: the case editor, the review copy, the signing ceremony, the docket and imported cases, with the module's `SCREENS`, which composes these with `screens-public`'s. Pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

**`SCREENS`** (the screen contract, `interface-shell` R21; each sentence read by key)

**case-editor** (Projects › <project> › Case; the registry's 5 acts)
- **R1** [met] Reads the project's case drafts (`casedrafts`), the draft's document (`casedocument`), its check (`casecheck`) and the "what changed" drafts (`whatchangeddrafts`). Draws the readiness ladder (Draft, Internally checked, Externally compliant, Distributed, each "not yet evaluated" until checked), the scope, what it leaves out with its acknowledgements, the people it names each with its reason (or that the reason is missing), and what changed since the last edition. Acts: `casedraft` "Prepare the draft"; `whatchangedpropose` "Draft what changed" (only while the assistant is reachable, drawn labelled machine work, `ai.label.untested` where its answer carries `untested`, kept only by the member's own words); `statementack` "Acknowledge what it leaves out", with the acknowledger's own reason (`case-inputs` R1, `STATEMENT_ACK_NO_REASON`); `attribute` "Name with the reason" (its consequence statement in the dialog, `op-grades` R4); `writinghelp` under the scope and "what changed" fields only (`interface-shell` R20; never under a reason). (T51 fix round 2; K4320) The statement box is labelled `review.statement.title`; a never-prepared edition is no failed read.

**review-copy** (Projects › <project> › Review copy; the registry's 3 acts; DEC-100)
- **R2** [met] Reads the draft's review grants and their comments (`reviewcomments`). Each named reader is a row with what they may read and until when, offering `reviewrevoke` "Stop sharing". `reviewgrant` "Share with a named reader" (a name, a role, and an end date) carries `mark.outward.means` and answers the grant's secret once: the screen shows it once as the reader's link `<origin>/app/review#grant=<secret>`, with "Civicsmith sends no email" (K3137; journey 21), for the member to hand over, and never keeps it (no storage, address or later request). `reviewcomment` "Reply" answers a reader's comment. (T51 fix round 2; K4320) A session that may not share is drawn no share boxes.

**ceremony** (Projects › <project> › Publish; the registry's 7 acts; DEC-147, DEC-180, DEC-183; N683, N757, N788)
- **R3** [met] The ceremony draws its steps in order (What, Strength, Leaves out, People, Photos, Disclosures, Bias, Preview, Ties, Sign), the current one marked, and carries the required "Publication ceremony" wizard (`screens-wizards` R7). Reads `publishpreflight` (each finding against the bar, every refusal that holds before signing, by its words), `publishtensions` (what will be disclosed with the case: unresolved contradictions, the timeline frozen at signing), `photomarks` for each photo the case carries, and `publishschedule` for an edition waiting to publish. Acts: `publishpreflight` "Check again"; `publishtensions` "See everything that will be disclosed".
- **R4** [met] The Photos step (N757, N788; DEC-180, DEC-183): each photo the case carries (relied on or supporting) is shown with its marks; the member draws rectangles on the photo and marks each a person not part of a finding, a number plate, or a city staff member at work (a staff area with a reason), and sends `obscuremark` with those areas, or "nothing to obscure" (`areas: []`); a mark is withdrawn by `obscuremarkwithdraw` with a reason, never erased. The step says what the public copy carries (areas obscured and labelled, camera details removed) and that every photo must be marked or "nothing to obscure" before signing; a photo whose format cannot be covered stops the signing, naming it, by its refusal's words.
- **R5** [met] amended (K1490, K3041; `case-authoring` R76; DEC-208 (2)): the Ties step's "Confirm, and prepare Edition <n> for signing" sends `publish` with `tieAttested: true`; Sign is offered only after it answers. Test: `publish` carries `tieAttested`; control: no Sign before it.

**imported** (Projects › Imported › Another group's case; the registry's 4 acts; DEC-96, DEC-112)
- **R7** [met] Reads `importedcases` and `importedcase` (each finding with whether it recreated, in part, or not), drawn in the imported band (read-only). Acts: `caseimport` "Import a case"; `importaccept` "Accept what recreated, with a reason"; `importflag` "Flag an issue"; `importwatch` "Watch the publisher's docket".

**docket** (Projects › <project> › Docket; the registry's 2 acts; DEC-116, DEC-100)
- **R8** [met] Reads `docket` (the case's entries: editions, corrections, withdrawals, court orders, each drafted or posted) and `docketprepare` for a draft entry. Acts: `docketfile` "File a docket entry" (with the member's reason for the record or public choice); `docketpost` "Post it, signed" (signed with her own key, as R5's signing). *(T51, K4253: `docketfile` sends `edition` (a ratified edition of the case, the latest first) and `capture` (a document the project's captures hold) beside the rest; either missing, nothing is sent; `heldcaptures` and `publishedcase`'s `edition_index` give the choices; `screen.docket.capture.add` is a reference to `capture`. `writinghelp` on the summary waits on wizard-scripts' registry re-take.)* (T51 K4260) `writinghelp` on the docket's summary is drawn only while the assistant is reachable and sends `op: docketfile`, `field: summary` and what was typed.
