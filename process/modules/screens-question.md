# screens-question: requirements

## Public

### Purpose

The `question` screen (one question with what supports and cuts against it, its readings, the assistant's run on it and its conclusion) and the assistant's dock beside the working screens (`question.mjs`, `assistant.mjs`), with `common.mjs`, the readers every investigation screen shares. Taken from `screens-inquiry` with their requirements and numbers; pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

Numbers are `screens-inquiry`'s, kept when ARCH split it (22 Nov 2026); a bare R<n> not here is that number in `screens-inquiry`, `screens-document`. The tests that prove these requirements boot the whole `SCREENS` through `screens-inquiry`'s test kit, so they are `screens-inquiry`'s until this module has a kit of its own (code work for its job).
