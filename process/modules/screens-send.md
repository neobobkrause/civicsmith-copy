## Public

### Purpose

Starting and sending an action: start and send, a records request and its help, a new action of any kind, a litigation hold and its notice, and counsel's packet (`start-send.mjs`, `request.mjs`, `help-records.mjs`, `actionnew.mjs`, `hold.mjs`, `counsel.mjs`), with `send-steps.mjs`, the send steps every action screen shares. Taken from `screens-action` with their requirements and numbers; pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

Numbers are `screens-action`'s, kept when ARCH split it (22 Nov 2026); a bare R<n> not here is that number in `screens-action`. The tests that prove these requirements boot the whole `SCREENS` through `screens-action`'s test kit, so they are `screens-action`'s until this module has a kit of its own (code work for its job).
