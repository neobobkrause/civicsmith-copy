## Public

### Purpose

The fleet member that performs an AI run for the plane: called over a service binding with a run and its `ai` credential, it walks a deterministic control-flow table, writes nothing itself (every change is the plane's, at an op the credential's member-authored scope admits), and states which account serves the run without a secret. Its Worker shell (`src/index.mjs`, with the cascade) hands `/ask`, `/draft`, `/transcribe` and `/signin` to `agent-worker-routes`' handlers, and it never attests, concludes, accepts or publishes.

### Provides

Terms. A **refusal** is `{ok:false, reason, code, detail, worker:"agent-worker", …extra}` with `reason` equal to `code`. The **plane** is `env.PLANE`. A plane answer is **silent** when the binding throws or its body is not JSON, **refused** when the status is not 200, the envelope's `ok` is not `true`, or the innermost `result` that states an `ok` says `false`; otherwise it is the result. A plane refusal is passed on with its code, its C-number and the plane's body unchanged; this member rewords none.

**`POST /run`** (path `run` or the empty path) — body `{run_id, store, credential, claude_accounts?, turns?, judgements?, max_steps?}`. Checked in this order, and no plane call is made before R1–R8 pass:
- **R1** [met] When `env.PLANE.fetch` is not a function: 503 `PLANE_NOT_CONFIGURED`, before the body is read.
- **R2** [met] A body that is not JSON: 400 `BAD_BODY`.
- **R3** [met] `run_id` not a non-empty string of at most 200 characters: 400 `BAD_RUN_ID`. This member mints no run id.
- **R4** [met] `store` not a string: 400 `BAD_STORE` (no namespace is assumed). A string not exactly `bio` or `scratch` (case-sensitive; `""` included): 400 `NAMESPACE_UNKNOWN` with `asked` (at most 80 characters) and `namespaces` (the two, in order). The set equals the plane's.
- **R5** [met] `credential` absent or empty: 401 `NO_CREDENTIAL`. Not `aik-` followed by 64 lowercase hex: 400 `BAD_CREDENTIAL_SHAPE`. This is a shape test only; liveness and scope are the plane's.
