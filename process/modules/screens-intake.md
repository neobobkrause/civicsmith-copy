**Size (P6).** Estimated 2,000–2,600 lines with comments, its own keyed sentences included, measured at its START (M-T46-1, K617).

## Public

### Purpose

Everything after a capture comes in: the held captures and the set-aside list, the archive, opening an original file safely, the inbox of knocks and a knocker's page, leads and watched addresses, and the module's `SCREENS`, which composes these with `screens-capture`'s Add screen. Pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides
