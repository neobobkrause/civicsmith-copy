# screens-document: requirements

Next number: 140

## Public

### Purpose

The `document` screen: one captured document with its reading, passages, versions, names, connections and citations, and the acts on it (`document.mjs`), with `admit.mjs`, the reading of a held copy that `question` shares. Taken from `screens-inquiry` with its requirements and numbers; pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

Numbers are `screens-inquiry`'s, kept when ARCH split it (22 Nov 2026); a bare R<n> not here is that number in `screens-inquiry`, `screens-question`. The tests that prove these requirements boot the whole `SCREENS` through `screens-inquiry`'s test kit, so they are `screens-inquiry`'s until this module has a kit of its own (code work for its job).

**document** (Projects › <project> › Document; the registry's 10 acts)
- **R3** [met] Reads the capture (`image`), its reading's passages (`reading`), `gradenote` ("Why B?"), `backlinks` (what cites it), `versionchain` (its versions; a newer version marked changed), and the pages its reading could not make out (`document.unread.pages`). Acts: `findin`, `cite` (per passage), `gradenote`, `release` (by its weight), `attest` (signed: `op-grades` R1 grades it attested), `retire` (terminal), `monitor`, `identityclaim` (a person the record follows, from a name in it), `transcribe` (the member types a page's words), and `pagetranscribe` only while the assistant is reachable (`interface-shell` R20; DEC-190). Opening the original goes through `open-file` (`screens-intake`). Fix round 6 (G19): the supporting files kept, of their total, with the reasons the others were skipped (`supporting`). (G38, fix round 6; K4617) The reads that use a reading's units (the words a leg cites, R69; the passages a contradiction flag is set beside, R127; whether a newer copy has a text to move citations to, R124) follow `next` of `op=reading` until each has what it needs and never stop at the first page; a page that cannot be read is said, never taken for the unit's absence; a page kept while the reading was being read is read again once its totals change. Test: `fr6-pages.test.mjs`, `fr6-seam.test.mjs` (a reading of three pages whose cited and flagged units sit on page 3; control: a one-page reading makes one read). **Amended (G41, fix round 6; K4636, K4645, K4672):** a reading that names `cells_held` is drawn with one quiet line, `screen.document.cells_held` (the sheet's text read whole and searchable, its cells interpreted for a range when opened or cited); not drawn while the text is being read, nor for a reading naming none, once whatever the sheets held. A reading stated too large (`failure: "final"`, `too_large: {bytes, key}`) is drawn `screen.document.reading.too_large` with that size and key, for a table document too, never "Nothing here". Test: `g41-document.test.mjs`.
