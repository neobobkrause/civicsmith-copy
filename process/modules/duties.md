# duties: requirements

Next number: 35

**Size (P6).** About 2,200–2,700 lines (constructs-2 §4.1). Under 4,000.

## Public

### Purpose

The one obligation object: who owes what, to whom, by when, under which authority. A duty `DUT-` is a duty, a prohibition or a power, held from its source at its version and adopted by a member's act. Its occurrences are derived on read from its trigger, its source in force and the events that meet it, and each change of an occurrence's state is recorded, append-only, so a plan step can wait on "overdue as known on that day" (K1466). "Overdue" raises a question, never a violation. Powers are read as the instruments that grant them, never as a verdict that an act was within them. Members see "obligation" and "power" (DEC-107).

### Provides

Terms.
- A **duty** is `{duty_id, modality, obligor, obligee?, performance, source, trigger, time, exceptions, enforcer?, observed_by?, arising_in?, reported_status, adopted_by, at}`.
- **Modality** (closed): `duty`, `prohibition`, `power`.
- **obligor**: an entity of kind office or body, or an organisation acting for a public body under a public law, contract, franchise or grant (K1440), or a `person` where a law binds them by name or role (K1453; a filer, a registrant).
- **source** (closed union): `standard` (a held standard and portion at its version, `standards`), `court` (a court standard's paragraph or a decree, order, grand jury or audit recommendation, held as a standard), `practice` (labelled measured practice), `dependency` (labelled: the event it must precede, the lead and why, K1431).
- **trigger** (closed union): `event` (an event kind concerning a named entity), `recurrence` (a `civil-time` RRULE subset), `source` (a registered trigger source, R16), `date` (a stated date).
- **time**: the due date's basis kind (`rule`, `commitment`, `dependency`, `window`; K1431) and the rule or lead it is computed from; the due date itself is computed by `civil-time` on each read and never stored. Only `rule` is answered as a deadline the law sets.
- **performance**: the act owed, in words, and for a `pay` duty the money facts it cites (a duty never holds an amount, INT C-7).
- **Occurrence states** (closed): `met`, `met_late`, `overdue`, `pending`, `discharged`, `undetermined`.
- **reported_status** is a list of `{status, extent}`, each quoted from a cited extent, `status` one of the active profiles' `response_statuses` (`jurisdictions` R58); this module holds no list of its own.
- The **viewer**, the refusal shape and `by` are as in `entities` (the control plane's stamps; DEC-52).

**propose({…fields, by}), adopt({proposalId, clause, by}), declare({…fields, clause, by})**
- **R1** [met] Refusals for a duty's fields, in order: `UNKNOWN_MODALITY`; `NO_OBLIGOR`, `NO_SUCH_ENTITY` (`entities.noSuchEntity`); `PERSON_OBLIGOR_NEEDS_LAW` (a `person` obligor whose source is not a `standard` or `court` source binding them by name or role); `NOT_ACTING_FOR_PUBLIC` (an organisation of a sector other than government whose source or a held `acts_for`/`contracts_with` line names no public body it acts for); `NO_ENFORCER` (a non-government obligor's duty names no enforcing office, K1440); `UNKNOWN_SOURCE_KIND`, `NO_SUCH_STANDARD` / `NO_PORTION` / `VERSION_NOT_HELD` (`standards`); `UNKNOWN_TRIGGER`, `BAD_RECURRENCE` (`civil-time`); `UNKNOWN_BASIS_KIND`; `HOLDS_AMOUNT` (any amount field: a `pay` duty cites money facts), `NO_SUCH_FACT` for a cited money fact; `UNKNOWN_REPORTED_STATUS` (a status outside the active profiles' `response_statuses`, naming them; while they hold none, every reported status is refused so, saying the profiles hold none, never checked against a default list, K1563 (8)).
- **R2** [met] A duty computed from a profile rule or proposed by the machine is held by `propose`, stored apart and labelled, and is never tracked until a member adopts it in one act with `adopt`, naming the clause (K1440, K1443, DEC-54; `NO_CLAUSE`); `adopt` naming no proposal this module holds is refused `DUTY_NO_SUCH_PROPOSAL`. The machine never declares or adopts (`DUTY_MEMBER_ACT_ONLY`). These two codes, and R6's `DUTY_NO_REASON`, are this module's own, each with its row in its own family (C-133), because another family holds the bare codes `NO_SUCH_PROPOSAL`, `NO_REASON` and `MEMBER_ACT_ONLY` for other conditions (DEC-49 arm A: one code, one condition; K1679).
- **R3** [met] `declare` is a member's own declaration, which is its adoption in the same act. Adoption allocates `DUT-<year>-NNNN` and records who, when and the clause. From adoption a duty is tracked and told once, whatever its basis kind (K1440).
- **R4** [met] A duty arising in a proceeding or report (an order, a decree paragraph, a grand jury or audit recommendation) names it in `arising_in` (a `proceeding` entity or a held capture) and may carry `reported_status` entries, each a quote of a cited extent; the record never states the status in its own words.
- **R5** [met] A profile deadline is held as a generic duty of the office the rule names ("the agency asked"), so one records request's clock is one occurrence of the addressed office's duty, triggered through R16. (T45; N858; R-reconciliation R7) The profile deadlines R5 reads are `local-facts.governingView`'s (its R12), in place of `combine`'s view, so a group's confirmed rule governs here as it does in `action-clocks`; with no group rule the view equals `combine`'s.

**revise({dutyId, …changes, reason, by}), withdraw({dutyId, reason, by}), readDuty({dutyId, viewer}), dutiesOf({entity, as?: obligor | obligee | enforcer, at?, limit, viewer})**
- **R6** [met] `revise` writes a new version with who, when and why, every earlier version kept; it re-checks R1 and is a member's act. `withdraw` refuses `DUTY_NO_REASON` (R2); a withdrawn duty remains, shown withdrawn, and derives no further occurrences.
- **R7** [met] `readDuty` answers every field, its versions, its proposal and adoption, and `in_force` at the read's date (R8). `dutiesOf` answers the duties by the entity's place in them, bounded 1–500 with `truncated`, from the index `duties (obligor)`.
- **R8** [met] `in_force` is derived, never stored: for a `standard` or `court` source, `standards.inForceAt` of its portion on the date; for a `practice` or `dependency` source, "held as practice" or "held as a dependency", never a law's in-force answer. An undetermined in-force answer carries its reason.

**occurrencesOf({dutyId, from, to, asOf, viewer})** (derived on read)
- **R9** [met] Answers each occurrence in the window: its key (deterministic from the duty, its version and its trigger instance), its trigger (the triggering event, recurrence instance or source item, with its date from `events`, never a capture time; K1444 (ii)), its due date computed by `civil-time.due` (recurrences by `expandRecurrence`) with its basis kind and trace, its state as of `asOf`, why, its evidence, and the recorded transitions (R13). `asOf` is required (`NO_AS_OF`); nothing reads "now" itself.
- **R10** [met] State: `met` when a matched event's `when` is on or before the due date, `met_late` when after, `discharged` when a held exception applies, `pending` before the due date with no match, `overdue` after it with no match, and `undetermined` with why wherever `civil-time` does not settle the comparison or the trigger has no date. With an uncertain due date (`civil-time.overdueOn`, side `body`) a body is `overdue` only after the latest candidate, "possibly overdue: undetermined, because …" between (K1444 (i)). A dependency date missed is answered as a fact about sequence, never as a legal deadline.
- **R11** [met] `overdue` is answered as a question with its derivation (the source in force, the trigger date, the due date, the level searched), never as a violation; `met` occurrences are answered with the same derivation.

- **R33** [met] (T48; N1044; K3150; amends R9, R10) Beside `why`, which stays as it is for the record, every occurrence `occurrencesOf` answers carries `reason`: `null` unless its state is `undetermined` or the "possibly overdue" band (R10), and then `{code, says}`. `code` is one of the exported, frozen `OCCURRENCE_REASONS`: `trigger_undated` (the trigger is placed nowhere, or its date is undetermined), `deadline_not_held` (the active profiles hold no rule for a `rule` basis), `due_not_computed` (the due date could not be computed, or its basis is unknown), `due_between_days` (the due date falls between candidate days, K1444 (i)), `match_undated` (the matched event is placed nowhere, or its date is undetermined or open-ended), `order_unsettled` (the precision held does not settle whether the match falls on or before the due date). `says` is `words.json`'s `duty.reason.<code>`, read by key, a member-facing sentence that names no ISO date, no zone id and no ruling or requirement number; while the file lacks a key, this module's interim word in DEC-149's form, held in its own word list and read by key as the file's would be (`extraction` R75's pattern), until the design stream gives it. Each undetermined path of R10 maps to exactly one code; a path none fits is reported to BOB, never given a code of the job's own. A code adds no catalogue row: it is no refusal. `notice-producers` reads `reason.says` for its `{reason}` (its T48 entry). Test: each code's occurrence on the test profile answers its code and a `says` matching none of `/\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}/`, a zone id or `/\bK\d+|\bR\d+/`; control: a `met` occurrence answers `reason: null` and its `why` unchanged.
**matchEvent({dutyId, occurrenceKey, eventId, reason, by}), registerOccurrenceEvidence(module, fn)**
- **R12** [met] `matchEvent` is a member's act (`DUTY_MEMBER_ACT_ONLY`, R2; `NO_SUCH_EVENT`; `NO_SUCH_OCCURRENCE`), recorded with who, when and why, and correctable by a later act that keeps the earlier one. `registerOccurrenceEvidence`: later modules (`calculations`) register once each (`membership.listenerRefusal`); `fn({duty, occurrence})` answers measured evidence (a calculation output) that R10 reads as a match, cited as such.

**recordTransitions({asOf, budgetMs, cursor?}), recordTransition({dutyId, occurrenceKey, state, asOf, cause, evidence, by}), transitionsOf({dutyId?, occurrenceKey?, viewer})**
- **R13** [met] A transition is `{occurrence_key, state, as_of, at, cause, evidence, by}`, append-only. `recordTransitions` (the scheduler's consumer, registered by `scheduler`, T33-80) derives each tracked occurrence's state as of `asOf` and appends a transition only where it differs from the last recorded, in slices within `budgetMs` with a cursor. `recordTransition` is a member's own recording, refusing `UNKNOWN_STATE` and `NO_CAUSE`.
- **R14** [met] A recorded transition is never rewritten by a later capture, correction or rule change: "overdue as known on 30 March" stays readable as recorded, beside the occurrence's current derivation. `transitionsOf` answers them in order of `at`, for `action-plans` to condition a step on (K1466).

**powersOf({office, at, viewer})**
- **R15** [met] Answers the duties of modality `power` whose obligor is the office, in force at `at` (R8), each with its instrument and any delegation instrument held, and undetermined ones apart with why. It never answers whether an act was within or outside a power, and never walks reporting lines as delegation (K1442).

**registerTriggerSource(module, fn)**
- **R16** [met] Later modules (`actions`) register once each (`membership.listenerRefusal`); `fn({duty, from, to})` answers the source's items (the group's sent request, with its date) that trigger an occurrence, so a body's response duty is triggered by the group's own request. A source that throws is answered beside the occurrences as `{source, error}`.

**setAgainst({dutyId, period, viewer})** (restrictions, thresholds, transfer authority; stage 2b)
- **R17** [met] For a `prohibition` or threshold duty whose performance cites money facts and names funds or entities, answers the money facts in that scope and period (`money.moneyOf`), each with its `calc-grammar` comparison to the cited term as a labelled computed fact and a question, never "breach" or "unauthorised" (D275).

**neighbours({node, kinds, at, page})** (the connection owner)
- **R18** [met] The module registers once through `connection-grammar.registerOwner`, owner `duties`, kinds "owes" (obligor and obligee), a power held by an office, and "met by" (an occurrence met by an event, derived with its derivation). `neighbours` answers them as of `at` in `connection-grammar`'s shape, paged by its bounds, and passes `connection-grammar.ownerConformance`.

**OCCURRENCE_KEY_RE, noSuchDuty(dutyId, extra?), onDutyTracked(module, fn)** (T34; N583, N601, N605)
- **R24** [met] `OCCURRENCE_KEY_RE` is exported, frozen: the form of R9's occurrence key, `OCC-` followed by 32 lowercase hexadecimal digits, anchored at both ends. Every key R9 answers matches it, and a later module that checks an occurrence key's form (`inquiry-grammar` R15) imports it rather than spelling its own. (T35; N675, K1799) It is stated in `duties/vocab.mjs`, a file that imports nothing of the record (no module's `index.mjs`, no store, no table), and `index.mjs` re-exports that same value unchanged, so a caller importing it from `vocab.mjs` loads no record code (the case checker stays small); both exports are one object (`===`).
- **R25** [met] `noSuchDuty(dutyId, extra?)`, a module-level function, is the one answer to one condition: no duty the caller may see answers to `dutyId` (absent, or not visible to the viewer, answered alike, R22). It answers `{ok: false, reason: "NO_SUCH_DUTY", code: "NO_SUCH_DUTY", check, translation, duty_id, detail}`: `duty_id` the id as asked (null when none), `detail` one fixed sentence, the same for every caller, `check` and `translation` its row in this module's family; `extra` adds a caller's own fields and never replaces these. Every read and act of this module that answers this condition answers through it, and a later module that answers it without reading a duty (`action-plans`) calls it, so the code is minted at one site (`entities.noSuchEntity`'s pattern, K1569). It writes nothing and never throws.
- **R26** [met] `onDutyTracked(module, fn)`: one registration per module, refused through `membership.listenerRefusal`; after a duty is adopted or declared (R2, R3), revised (R6) or withdrawn, `fn({duty})` is called once, after the act's transaction, with the duty's id, so `scheduler` re-arms its wake for R13's consumer (`scheduler` R9). A throwing `fn` never undoes the act, and the notice writes nothing (`following` R19's pattern, K1666).

**usesOfPower({dutyId, from?, to?, after?, limit, viewer}), linkUse({dutyId, eventId, reason, by}), unlinkUse({dutyId, eventId, reason, by})** (T35; N644, N642; POLICIES L3 PO6; Capability Ladders §6B.4; K1713 (2), K1442)
- **R27** [met] A power held links to the events that use it. `usesOfPower` refuses `NO_SUCH_DUTY` (R25) and `NOT_A_POWER` (a duty whose modality is not `power`). It answers the events of kinds `discretion`, `waiver` and `assessment` (`events` R43–R46, read through `events.usesOf` or its read contract, R47) that use the power, each with how it is linked: `provision`, when the event's provision key names the standard of the power's source (R1's `standard` or `court` source) and, where the source names a portion, that portion; or `member`, when a member linked it with `linkUse`. Each item carries the event as `events` answers it and whether its decider participant is the power's obligor (`decider_is_obligor`: `true`, `false`, or `undetermined` with why, as when the decider is a person and no `lines` holding of the office at the event's `when` is held), stated as a fact about the record, never as a judgment that the act was within or outside the power (R15). `from`/`to`, `after`, `limit` and `truncated` are as `events.usesOf`; sight is R22's and `events`' own. `linkUse` and `unlinkUse` are a member's acts (`DUTY_MEMBER_ACT_ONLY`), each refusing `NO_SUCH_DUTY`, `NOT_A_POWER`, `NO_SUCH_EVENT` (an event not of those kinds answering so), `DUTY_NO_REASON`; a repeat answers `already: true`; an unlink keeps the link, shown unlinked with who, when and why. `readDuty` (R7) and `powersOf` (R15) answer each power's count of uses (the count `usesOfPower` would answer the viewer), and R18's owner gains the kind "used in" (a power to an event that uses it), derived with how it is linked.

**proposeReview({standard, reviewDue, cycle?, owner, by})** (T35; N644; POLICIES L3 PO9; Capability Ladders §6B.4; K1431, K1440, K1443)
- **R28** [met] A policy's own review date is held as the body's own commitment. `proposeReview` composes a proposal (R2: stored apart, labelled, tracked only once a member adopts it with `adopt`) of one duty: modality `duty`; obligor `owner`, the office or body that owns the policy (the policy's issuer or the coordinator its header names, a registered entity: `NO_SUCH_ENTITY`); performance "review the policy", its source the held standard `standard` (a `standard` source, R1; `NO_SUCH_STANDARD`); time basis `commitment`, never `rule`; trigger `date`, the date stated by `reviewDue`, an extent of the standard's text (`{captureSha, extent}`, a found match passing as it is, K1941's one shape; `content`'s extent refusals, and `REVIEW_EXTENT_NOT_HELD` for an extent of a capture that is not the standard's held text, K1965; `REVIEW_DATE_UNREAD` when its words are not a whole calendar date, such as a placeholder, which is kept as written and never completed), and, when `cycle` cites an extent stating a revision cycle in whole years, months or weeks (a cycle in days only when it is whole weeks, `civil-time` holding no daily recurrence; K1965), a `recurrence` from that date (`BAD_RECURRENCE` otherwise). It is refused under R29 as any duty is. The machine may call it (R2's `propose`); a member's own call is still a proposal until adopted. Once adopted, an occurrence whose review date has passed with no match (R10, R12: a member matches the event of the review, such as the revised policy's issuance) is answered with `label: "Noticed"` and the why "the body's own review date, a commitment it stated, has passed and no review is recorded", never called overdue against a deadline the law sets, never a violation (K1431, D241); its queue item is `notice-producers`'.

**An organisation's own policy** (T35; N644; POLICIES L3 PO10; Capability Ladders §6B.4; K1713 (3), K1440 as amended by K1453)
- **R29** [met] A policy or standard whose issuer is an organisation of a sector other than government (`entities` R42, `standards`' issuer) is held and measured on its own terms in `standards`; it becomes a duty here only where R1 admits it under K1440: its obligor acts for a public body under a public law, contract, franchise or grant (a held `acts_for` or `contracts_with` line, or a source naming the body), and the duty names its enforcing office. Every other duty, adoption or review proposal (R28) resting on such a source is refused `NOT_ACTING_FOR_PUBLIC` or `NO_ENFORCER` (R1), and nothing is written; the refusal says the organisation's policy stays held as a standard and may be compared with what the organisation does (`calculations`), never tracked here as an obligation.

**proposeFromResponse({kind, arisingIn, quote, obligor, performance, standard?, standardProposal?, by}), dutiesArisingIn({captures, asOf, viewer})** (T45; N855, N882; Actions D6d, D7; v0.2 rules 16, 17; K2781 (2))
- **R30** [met] (T45; Actions D7; v0.2 rule 16; COURTS L3) `proposeFromResponse({kind, arisingIn, quote, obligor, performance, standard?, standardProposal?, by})` composes a proposal (R2: stored apart, labelled, tracked only once a member adopts it with `adopt`) of one duty from a body's reply or a report. `kind` is `promise` or `required_response` (`RESPONSE_KIND_UNKNOWN`); `arisingIn` the held capture of the reply or report (R4); `quote` an extent of that capture (`content`'s extent refusals; `RESPONSE_EXTENT_NOT_IN` for an extent of another capture), held as the duty's quote, never paraphrased. R1's and R29's refusals apply. (K2806) A proposal's source standard proposal is read through `standards` R54; an unreadable or unadopted one is `DUTY_SOURCE_NOT_HELD` at adopt. A required response's due date is the governing view's `deadlines` entry with `owed_by: "body"` whose `starter` names the report's issuer: one such entry is used; several leave the date `undetermined` naming them unless the caller names one by an optional `rule`; none leaves it `undetermined`, never defaulted. - **R31** [met] (T45; N855; Actions D6d; v0.2 rule 17) `dutiesArisingIn({captures, asOf, viewer})` answers the adopted, unwithdrawn duties whose `arising_in` is one of `captures` (1–200 ids; `NO_AS_OF` without `asOf`), each with its occurrences' states as R9 derives them as of `asOf`, and `all_met`, true exactly when there is at least one such duty and every one has at least one occurrence and every occurrence reads `met` or `met_late` (a promise kept late is kept). - **R32** [met] (T46; DEC-194; N913; amends R30) The rows C-133.41 `RESPONSE_KIND_UNKNOWN`, C-133.42 `RESPONSE_EXTENT_NOT_IN` and C-133.44 `DUTY_SOURCE_NOT_HELD` take their translations from `words.json`'s `refusal.<CODE>`, read by key (as `membership`'s `HANDLE_WORDS` and `extraction`'s `TRANSCRIBE_WORDS` are), never a copy of the sentence; codes, numbers, `where`s and behaviour are unmoved.
