# escalation: requirements

Next number: 40

**Size (P6).** New. Estimated 800–1,200 lines of code with its tables; one session reads it with the public parts of its uses.

## Public

### Purpose

The escalation protocol (Design Requirement 7 as amended): the stages a group works through to bring a government act back into conformance, from a live noncompliant determination to its end, each stage with its entry condition, its defined acts and the trigger for the next. The next stage is proposed when its trigger is met, from the record at the time of reading, and a member advances it. It ends only when compliance is restored and the consequences are addressed (Operational Principle 6). It decides nothing about significance and takes no position on what policy should be (Operational Principle 1).

### Provides

Terms. The **stages**, in order: `1 documentation` (discovery and documentation), `2 notification`, `3 clock`, `4 response_evaluation`, `5 legal_tools`, `6 sustained_attention`, `7 political_accountability`. The **stage table** (edges a member may advance along): 1→2; 2→3; 3→4; 4→5, 4→7; 5→6, 5→7; 6→4; 7→4. An escalation's **state** is `open`, `suspended` or `ended`. A **breach action** is an action whose document states `breach: true` and rests on the escalation's determination (`actions` R8). A refusal is `{ok: false, reason, ...}`.

**escalationOpen({determination, reason, author, viewer}) → `{ok: true, id, stage: 1, proposed}` or refusal**
- **R1** [met] Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_OPEN` (an empty or machine author); `ESCALATION_NO_REASON` (R24: the author's reason why this breach is worth pursuing, absent, blank or over 2,000 characters); `NO_SUCH_DETERMINATION` (absent or invisible, one answer; `conformance.noSuchDetermination`, its R19); `DETERMINATION_SUPERSEDED` (`conformance.determinationSuperseded`, its R20); `DETERMINATION_VOID` (`conformance.determinationVoided`, its R33; K2850); `NOT_NONCOMPLIANT` (no standard's outcome is `noncompliant`, `conformance` R4); `ESCALATION_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` (the author is not joined in the determination's project; its row C-116.6, K275); `ALREADY_OPEN` (an open or suspended escalation of this determination, named). Otherwise it opens at stage 1, recording the noncompliant standards it pursues, the reason, who and when. Any joined member may open one (Design Requirement 7: "any group or individual can initiate"). (DEC-89 (1); DEC-88; K1019)

**escalationRead({id, nowMs?, viewer}) → `{id, state, stage, history, standards, actions, triggers, proposed, exit}`**
- **R2** [met] The read derives, at `nowMs` (the caller's, else the instance clock), for each edge out of the current stage whether its trigger (R4–R10) is met, naming the record ids that meet it or what is missing; `proposed` lists the met ones, each with the instant its trigger was first met by those ids and its age. That instant is the latest of the dates of the ids that meet the trigger (a `sent` entry's `at`; for a clock, the day after its date), never the read time, so the age is a fact of the record (K171). Nothing is stored as the answer: the same record read later can propose more. `NO_SUCH_ESCALATION` for an absent or invisible one.
- **R3** [met] `exit` answers R14's two conditions separately, each `met`, `not_met` or `undetermined` with its ids and why, and never composes them into a score.

**The stages: entry, defined acts and trigger** (each trigger is a fact about the record, read through the module named)
- **R4** [met] Stage 1 (documentation) is entered by R1. Its acts: the published findings and the determination. Trigger to 2: the determination is live, and the act's actor is an office (`conformance` R1, R25), so a notice has an addressee. The actor is resolved to its `office` entity (`conformance` R25's `entity_id`, read through `entities`), which stage 2's notification and stage 7's targets (R12) read; an actor with no entity held still triggers, and the read states it unresolved.
- **R5** [met] Stage 2 (notification). Its act: a breach action addressed to the actor's office, attached at this stage (R9). Trigger to 3: that action's ledger holds a `sent` entry (`actions` R16).
- **R6** [met] Stage 3 (clock). Its act: a clock entry, with its basis, on the notification action, stated by a member (`action-clocks` R2 may propose it; K617). Trigger to 4: after the `sent` entry, the ledger holds a `received` or `no_response` entry, or the clock's earliest pending entry is past (`actions` R12's rule at `nowMs`). A stage-3 action with no clock entry never triggers by time, and the read says so. (T45; v0.2 rule 6; S1 §C 8) A stage-3 action stating `reply_owed: false` (`actions` R82) never triggers by time or by the lack of a reply; the read says "no reply owed". Control: the same action with `reply_owed` absent triggers when its clock passes.
- **R7** [met] Stage 4 (response evaluation). Its act: a member's evaluation (R10). Triggers: to 5 and to 7, the latest evaluation reads `denied`, `partial` or `none`; a `complied` evaluation proposes no stage and points at R14. Partial compliance is recorded and does not stop the clock or end the escalation (Functional Architecture Layer 3 Function 5). The trigger names the evaluation by `<id>/evaluation#<n>`, `n` its place (1, 2, …) among this escalation's evaluations, an id that counts no other entry (N462; DEC-36, K913).
- **R8** [met] Stage 5 (legal tools). Its acts: breach actions attached at this stage, with their filings or counsel packets (`filings.filingsFor`); what is available is listed by tier (the profile's kinds for the actor's office, read through `filings.availableActions`, its R21, never from the published case; K171). Trigger to 6: an attached action's ledger holds a `sent` entry. Trigger to 7: as R7's, from the evaluation in force, named by R7's id (N462).
- **R9** [met] `escalationAttach({id, action, purpose?, standards?, reason, author, viewer})` attaches a breach action to the current stage, which must be 2, 5 or 7: `MACHINE_CANNOT_ATTACH`; `ESCALATION_NO_REASON` (R24: the author's reason for attaching this action); `NO_SUCH_ESCALATION`; `NO_SUCH_ACTION`; `NOT_A_BREACH_ACTION` (no `breach: true`, or no `rests_on` leg naming this escalation's determination); `STAGE_TAKES_NO_ACTION` (stages 1, 3, 4, 6); `ALREADY_ATTACHED`. An action is attached to one escalation at one stage, the reason recorded with the attachment. At stage 7 the attachment records R12's `purpose` and `standards`, checked here; `actions`' document is unchanged (K171). (DEC-88; K1025)
- **R10** [met] `escalationEvaluate({id, response?, reading, reason, author, viewer})`: a member's reading of a response, `complied`, `partial`, `denied` or `none` (nothing came back by the clock), naming the response (`{action, ord}` of a `received` entry, or none for `none`) and a reason of 1–2,000 characters. Refusals: `MACHINE_CANNOT_EVALUATE`; `NO_SUCH_ESCALATION`; `NOT_IN_EVALUATION` (the stage is not 4); `READING_UNKNOWN`; `NO_SUCH_RESPONSE` (not a `received` entry of an attached action); `RESPONSE_FOR_NONE` (a response with `none`); `ESCALATION_NO_REASON` (R24). Evaluations are append-only; the latest is in force.
- **R11** [met] Stage 6 (sustained attention). Entered from 5. Its act: none of its own; `monitoring` watches the attached actions' clocks and the counterparty's responses (R15). Trigger to 4: a `received` entry on an attached action later than the latest evaluation.
- **R12** [met] Stage 7 (political accountability), entered from 4 or 5 (K14). Its acts are breach actions attached here, each attachment (R9) stating one `accountability` purpose of `official_request` (asking an elected office to act on the breach), `oversight_request`, `audit_request`, `testimony` or `enforcing_legislation` (legislation that restores or enforces an existing requirement), and naming at least one of the escalation's noncompliant standards as the requirement it seeks enforced. Refused at R9: `NOT_ACCOUNTABILITY` (no purpose, or one outside the five: policy advocacy and candidate support have none); `NOT_THE_BREACH` (no standard, or one the escalation does not pursue); `COUNTERPARTY_NOT_ELECTED` for `official_request` addressed to an office the profile marks `elected: false` (`jurisdictions` R24; where the profile says nothing, it lands and the read states the office's election undetermined); `COUNTERPARTY_NOT_OVERSIGHT` for `oversight_request` or `audit_request` addressed to an office that holds no `oversees` or `appoints` line to the actor's office entity (R4), or to an organisation the actor's office is `part_of`, valid on the attachment's date (`lines.structureAt`), and that the profile, as the fallback, marks `oversight: false` (`jurisdictions` R24, R27). A line so held governs over the profile's flag, and the read names it with its grades; with no line and no flag the attachment lands and the read states the office's oversight undetermined; a line undetermined on that date is stated so, never read as held (K108 (5), N72; K1442). The read (R2) offers, at stage 7, the offices that hold such a line as the targets the record supports, each with its line; it offers and never chooses. Trigger to 4: as R11's.

**escalationAdvance({id, to, reason, author, viewer}); escalationDecline({id, to, reason, author, viewer})**
- **R13** [met] `escalationAdvance`: `MACHINE_CANNOT_ADVANCE`; `NO_SUCH_ESCALATION`; `NOT_OPEN` (suspended or ended); `ESCALATION_NO_REASON` (empty or over 2,000 characters; R24); `ILLEGAL_STAGE` (not an edge of the table from the current stage, with the legal ones); `TRIGGER_NOT_MET` (R2 does not propose `to`; it names what is missing). Otherwise it appends `{from, to, reason, author, at, trigger ids}` to the history. Compatibility (N462): an evaluation id recorded in an advance before R7's id, `<id>/evaluation/<seq>` with `seq` the escalation log's number, is read as naming the evaluation that log entry records and is answered in R7's form; one naming no evaluation leaves the ids; the recorded entry is not edited (R18). `escalationDecline` records, with the same refusals and `EDGE_NOT_PROPOSED` (its row C-116.30; K275, membership's `NOT_PROPOSED` naming another condition) in place of `TRIGGER_NOT_MET`, that a member chose not to advance along a proposed edge now, with a reason; the proposal stays in the read, with its age and the declines.

**escalationEnd({id, author, viewer}); escalationSuspend({id, reason, author, viewer}) / escalationResume**
- **R14** [met] `escalationEnd` refuses `MACHINE_CANNOT_END`, `NO_SUCH_ESCALATION`, and, naming the ids, `COMPLIANCE_NOT_RESTORED` unless, for every standard the escalation pursues, a live `compliant` determination of the same act (its act id, `conformance` Terms) recorded after it opened exists (`conformance.determinationsFor`), and `CONSEQUENCES_NOT_ADDRESSED` or `CONSEQUENCES_UNDETERMINED` unless `consequences.addressed` for the escalation's determination, superseded or not, is `addressed` (`consequences` R9). A determination with no consequence recorded reads `undetermined`, so it answers `CONSEQUENCES_UNDETERMINED`; a group that judges the breach had none records an assessed part saying so and addresses it (K172). Otherwise the state is `ended`, with who and when; an ended escalation is never reopened (a new breach is a new determination). (T45; N855 (D5); Actions D5; v0.2 rule 17; S1 §D 7) Compliance is restored, for each standard pursued, by a live `compliant` determination recorded after the escalation opened either of the same act (as now) or of a curing act: a later act of the same office linked by a member as the cure of this escalation's determination for that standard (`conformance.curesOf`, its R37, `cure_standing: true`). `COMPLIANCE_NOT_RESTORED` names, per standard, what is missing. Harm is read through `consequences.addressed` unchanged. Control: a compliant determination of another office's later act, or one not linked as the cure, does not restore compliance.
- **R15** [met] `escalationSuspend` (a member, with a reason) stops proposals being reported as due; the escalation stays open to reading, its clocks keep running in the actions, and the read says it is suspended and since when. `escalationResume` restores it at the same stage. Neither ends it: an escalation is never ended, withdrawn or resolved without R14's two conditions, so the record never reads a stopped escalation as a finished one.

**declineToEscalate({determination, reason, author, viewer}) → `{ok: true, id}` or refusal** (`op=declinetoescalate`; DEC-89 (2))
- **R27** [met] A member records, in their own words, why the group is not pursuing a live noncompliant determination now. Refused exactly as R1 refuses an opening, in R1's order, with `MACHINE_CANNOT_DECLINE_TO_ESCALATE` in place of `MACHINE_CANNOT_OPEN`; so no decline is recorded while an escalation of the determination is open or suspended (`ALREADY_OPEN`). Otherwise it records the decline with its reason, who and when. A decline is never edited: a later decline of the same determination, or an escalation opened later with its own reason, supersedes it, and every decline stays readable. Any joined member who may open an escalation may decline one. (DEC-89 (2); K1019)

**escalationStatus({determination, viewer}) → `{determination, status, escalations, declines}` or refusal** (`op=escalationstatus`)
- **R28** [met] Answers whether the determination was `escalated`, `declined` or `neither`: `escalated` when the latest of its openings and declines is an opening, `declined` when it is a decline, `neither` when it has none. It also answers every escalation of it (as R22) and every decline (reason, who, when, and what superseded it), oldest first. An absent or invisible determination answers `NO_SUCH_DETERMINATION` (`conformance.noSuchDetermination`). Writes nothing. (DEC-89; placed here, not in `conformance`, which comes earlier and cannot read escalation, P4; K1019)

**escalationReasonDraft({determination, nowMs?, viewer}) → `{determination, text, label, parts}` or refusal** (`op=escalationreasondraft`; DEC-89, K1019)
- **R29** [met] Offers the member the opening reason (R1) pre-assembled from the determination's record, so the member can document why the breach is worth escalating: each noncompliant standard it pursues with that standard's basis (`conformance.determinationRead`); the act determined; each action resting on the determination that the viewer may see (`actions.actionsFor`), with its clock entries and every date passed without a response at `nowMs` (the caller's, else the instance clock; `actions` R12's rule); and the consequences recorded on it (`consequences.consequencesOf`). `parts` names the record id each sentence was assembled from; what could not be read is stated undetermined, never filled. The text states only what the record holds and carries no significance, severity, priority, urgency or score (R19). It is labelled machine work (`record-grammar`'s `proposalLabel` for the subject `escalation_reason`, its R44, in the state `machine_proposed`), as DEC-101's draft of an edition's statement is, and is never a reason until a member sends it, as offered or edited, as R1's `reason`; the reason then recorded is the member's own, attributed to them. Refusals, as R1 asks them: `NO_SUCH_DETERMINATION`, `DETERMINATION_SUPERSEDED`, `NOT_NONCOMPLIANT`. Writes nothing. (DEC-89; K1019)

**For `monitoring`: escalationsDue({nowMs, limit?, viewer}) → `{items, truncated}`**
- **R16** [met] Lists every open escalation with at least one proposed edge not advanced or declined since its trigger was met, with the edge, the trigger's instant and its age, the escalation's project and the member who opened it, oldest first, at most 500 (`truncated` stated). This is what `monitoring` asks and `queue-producers` (its R17) notifies on. (T45; N855 (D6a)) An escalation `stopped_pursuing` (R31) is never listed.

**For `action-plans`: escalationsFor({determination, viewer}) → `{items}` or refusal**
- **R22** [met] `escalationsFor({determination, viewer})` answers every escalation of that determination the viewer may see, oldest first, each with its id, state and stage (at most one is open or suspended, R1's `ALREADY_OPEN`); an absent or invisible determination answers `NO_SUCH_DETERMINATION` (`conformance.noSuchDetermination`), and a determination with none answers `items: []`. Writes nothing.

**An overridden action (`actions` R8)**
- **R23** [met] R9 refuses an action carrying a `premise_override` (`actions` R8) `ACTION_PREMISE_OVERRIDDEN`, after `NO_SUCH_ACTION` and before `NOT_A_BREACH_ACTION`: an escalation pursues a determined breach only (K600 (a)). (T45; N884; Actions D3; v0.2 rule 2; K2780 (K600 (a) revised)) `ACTION_PREMISE_OVERRIDDEN` applies only while the action has no `rests_on` leg naming this escalation's determination; once a member's revision adds one (`actions` R8 (d)), R9 attaches it, and the attachment and R2's read show the override (its ground, reason, who and when) in the history. Control: the same overridden action without the leg is still refused.

**"What we did" in the timeline** (EVENTS 2a; K1494)
- **R30** [met] At start this module registers once with `events.registerEventSource` (its R30). For a set of entity or event ids, its source answers, for the escalations the viewer may see whose determination's act event or actor office entity is in the set, each opening, stage move, attachment, evaluation, decline, suspension and end as `{at, label, ref, kind}` within `from`–`to`, bounded by `limit`. It writes nothing. (T45; N855 (D6a); Actions D4, D6a; v0.2 rules 15, 17) The source also answers each stop and reopening (R31), `kind: "stopped_pursuing"` or `"reopened"`, with its date and never its reason. An opening, stage move, attachment, end, stop or reopening carries `public: true` (`case-authoring` R57); an evaluation, decline or suspension is the group's deliberation and carries `public: false` (v0.2 rule 15: a published record of actions holds outward acts only; K2850). An escalation every attached action of which is confidential (`action-records` R5, was `actions` R87; T47, N961) is not answered at all, and a confidential attached action's attachment is never answered. Control: the stop is answered dated, with no reason.

**Its own reason code** (N433, K730, K766)
- **R24** [met] (BOB's choice: own codes, as `intent` R30) This module's refusal of a reason that is absent, blank or over 2,000 characters, wherever an act takes one (R1's opening, R9's attachment, R10's evaluation, R13's advance and decline, R15's suspension, R27's decline to escalate), is `ESCALATION_NO_REASON` (C-116.24), its row's number and translation unchanged, so no code is held with two rows (DEC-49): it never answers `NO_REASON`, which is `progressions`' (C-100.18). (DEC-89; K1019; R9: DEC-88, K1025)

**The ops map: escalationOps(escalation, url, body)** (`build/extraction/legacy-store.md` §4.2 (6), §4.4 (8); `build/plan/current.md` rule 5; K760's pattern)
- **R25** [met] The module publishes `escalationOps(escalation, url, body)`, an object of route arms keyed by op name, each a function of no arguments that answers what the named service answers, with today's behaviour (`store.mjs`' ten explicit arms, N216, K262): `escalationopen` (R1), `escalationattach` (R9), `escalationevaluate` (R10), `escalationadvance`, `escalationdecline` (R13), `escalationend`, `escalationsuspend` and `escalationresume` (R14, R15), each with the body's fields and the query's `author` and `viewer` set after them; `escalation` (the read), with the query's `id` and `viewer` (no `now`: R37); `escalationsdue` (R16), with the query's `limit` and `viewer` (no `now`: R37); `declinetoescalate` (R27), with the body's fields and the query's `author` and `viewer` set after them; `escalationstatus` (R28), with the query's `determination` and `viewer`; `escalationreasondraft` (R29), with the query's `determination` and `viewer` (no `now`: R37) (K1025, N485; met in T23). `limit` is a number when the query states it and absent when it does not or states it empty. `author` and `viewer` are the control plane's stamps, read from the query, never from the body; which credential reaches each op is `op-declarations`' and `control-plane`'s, never this map's. `legacy-store`'s own job replaces its ten explicit arms by one spread of the map (K671). (The arms for R27–R29: DEC-89; K1019) (T45) The map gains `escalationstoppursuing` and `escalationreopen` (R31), as its other acts.

**Stopped pursuing; the evidence-seeking context** (T45; N855 (D6a); Actions D6a; v0.2 rule 17; S1 §C 13)
- **R31** [met] (T45; N855 (D6a); Actions D6a; v0.2 rule 17, State Rules §4.4 `stopped_pursuing`) `escalationStopPursuing({id, reason, author, viewer})` (`op=escalationstoppursuing`) sets the state `stopped_pursuing` (a state of the `ESC-` type beside `open`, `suspended`, `ended`), recording the stage, reason, who and when. Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_STOP`; `NO_SUCH_ESCALATION`; `NOT_OPEN` (ended or already stopped; a suspended one may be stopped); `ESCALATION_NO_REASON` (R24). A stopped escalation proposes nothing (R2 answers `proposed: []` and its triggers as facts), is never `ended` and never answered as success; R1 answers `ALREADY_OPEN` for its determination, naming it. `escalationReopen({id, reason, author, viewer})` (`op=escalationreopen`) returns it to `open` at the stage where it stopped (`NOT_STOPPED` otherwise; the other refusals as stopping's, `MACHINE_CANNOT_STOP` first). Both are append-only (R18). Control: an open escalation at the same stage still proposes its met edges.
- **R32** [met] (T45; S1 §C 13) R2's read also answers `context`: the actions the viewer may see that rest on the determination or its findings and are not breach actions (records requests, requests for comment and other evidence-seeking acts), each with kind, first `sent` date and resolution. They are never attachments and never meet a trigger. Control: an attached breach action is in `actions`, never in `context`.

**The stop as the document's state; attaching within sight and at stage 7; its words** (T46)
- **R33** [met] (T46; N910; amends R31; `record-grammar` R57) R31 is built whole: `escalationStopPursuing` writes `stopped_pursuing` as the escalation's state in its document, along `record-grammar`'s edge from `open` or `suspended`, promoted with its history (R21), and `escalationReopen` writes `open` along `stopped_pursuing` → `open`; the state every read answers is the document's, never one held only in this module's rows. Its two rows take C-116's next numbers, minted with their translations from `words.json`, read by key (as `membership`'s `HANDLE_WORDS` and `extraction`'s `TRANSCRIBE_WORDS` are): `MACHINE_CANNOT_STOP` (`refusal.MACHINE_CANNOT_STOP`) and `NOT_STOPPED` (`refusal.NOT_STOPPED`). Control: a stopped escalation's document reads `stopped_pursuing`; reopened, `open` at the stage where it stopped.
- **R34** [met] (T46; N931; amends R9, R12; `affordances` R51) R9's one-attachment refusal `ALREADY_ATTACHED` stands for every attachment, whatever the viewer may see; it is answered within the viewer's sight as `escalationRead(...).actions` is (R26): it names the escalation and stage already attached only where the viewer may see that escalation, and otherwise names nothing of it (no escalation id, stage or count), one answer. `attachConditions({id, action, viewer})` (a read) answers, for an action the viewer may see and an escalation at stage 7, R12's counterparty conditions for the action's addressee, per accountability purpose: `official_request` admitted, refused `COUNTERPARTY_NOT_ELECTED`, or the office's election undetermined (R9 then lands it); `oversight_request` and `audit_request` admitted (naming the `oversees`, `appoints` or `part_of` line valid on the day read, with its grades, or the profile's flag), refused `COUNTERPARTY_NOT_OVERSIGHT`, or the office's oversight undetermined; `testimony` and `enforcing_legislation` asking no counterparty condition. Each answer is what R9 would judge at that day; it writes nothing, chooses nothing, and refuses as R9 does for an absent or invisible escalation or action. So `affordances` offers attachment at stage 7 as R9 would admit it. Control: an office the profile marks `elected: false` answers `official_request` refused, `testimony` admitted.
- **R35** [met] (T46; DEC-194; N913; amends R13, R31) C-116.27 `NOT_OPEN` keeps its present translation in T46: `words.json`'s `refusal.NOT_OPEN` says "a suspended one is resumed first", while R31 refuses a stop `NOT_OPEN` only for an ended or already stopped escalation and lets a suspended one be stopped directly; the sentence does not match what that arm does, so it is not adopted, is returned to BOB, and is read by key once the sentence and R31 agree. This module's other rows, which `words.json` does not hold, keep theirs (R33's two new rows apart). Answer-envelope's `catalogue-end` pin of C-116.27 is not opened by this module in T46.

**Its words; the instant a read is evaluated at** (T47)
- **R36** [met] (T47; N962, N992; DEC-198, DEC-199; amends R9, R34 and the catalogue's C-116.41) Two rows take their translations from `words.json`, read by key (as `membership`'s `HANDLE_WORDS` and `extraction`'s `TRANSCRIBE_WORDS` are), never a copy: C-116.41 `ESCALATION_BY_ACT_ONLY` from `refusal.ESCALATION_BY_ACT_ONLY`, whose sentence names every act an escalation changes through, stop pursuing and reopen (R31) among them, as its `detail` already does; and C-116.14 `ALREADY_ATTACHED` from `refusal.ALREADY_ATTACHED`, a sentence that names no escalation, so R34's one answer to a viewer who may not see the escalation holds in its translation as in its fields. Controls: each row's translation equals its key's sentence; an `ALREADY_ATTACHED` answered to a viewer who may not see the escalation carries no escalation id, stage or count, translation included.
- **R37** [met] (T47; N983; K3027; amends R2, R16, R25, R29) The reads routed through R25's map, `escalation` (R2), `escalationsdue` (R16) and `escalationreasondraft` (R29), are evaluated at the server's instant, since their answers (a proposed edge and its age, a date passed without a response) may lead a member to an act: the route passes no instant from the request, a `now` in the query or the body being ignored, never read, so a request carrying one answers exactly as the same request without it. The instant is this module's own clock, `escalationOf`'s `now` (`deps.now`, record-core's `stampInstant` by default), which tests set there. An in-plane caller (`monitoring`'s tick, `queue-producers`) may still pass `nowMs` to the service, as today; no request does. The services' answers are otherwise unchanged. Controls: a read with `now` in the query answers byte-identically to the same read without it; a test moving the module's clock across a deadline moves the proposal.

Rows (T45), C-116's next numbers at the job's stamp, translations BOB's drafts and the UX stream's to revise: `MACHINE_CANNOT_STOP`, `NOT_STOPPED`; `NOT_OPEN`'s translation widened for a stopped escalation.

### Invariants

- **R17** [met] Nothing a machine writes opens, declines to open, attaches to, evaluates, advances, declines, suspends or ends an escalation; a proposal, and R29's pre-assembled reason, is the protocol's derivation over the record, stated as such, never an act (Design Requirement 12; DEC-24). (DEC-89; K1019)
- **R18** [met] Every opening reason, decline to escalate, stage move, evaluation, decline, suspension and end is append-only with who, when and why; the history is never edited. (DEC-89; K1019)
- **R19** [met] No input or answer carries a significance, severity, priority, urgency or score (K12); no stage act has a purpose outside enforcing a standard the escalation pursues (Operational Principle 1, K14).
- **R20** [met] Every read answers an escalation in a project the viewer may not see as absent. Tables are declared to purge (K23). No place, office or law is named in this module's behaviour or outward text.
- **R21** [met] An escalation is a record object of its own type: promoted through `promotion`, with history, audit and export like a determination; R18's rule holds.
- **R26** [met] (K903 (4), DEC-36) An attached action the viewer may not see (`actions.actionRead` refuses it) is withheld whole from every read of the escalation (R2): - it leaves `actions`, with its stage, attacher, time, purpose, standards and filings; - R6's note naming it leaves `notes`; - an id naming it or one of its ledger entries leaves a trigger's `ids` and a history entry's trigger ids.

  No id, state, placeholder or count, and no gap in a sequence number that would count one: where an entry was withheld from a list numbered by `seq` (`actions`, the history's `attach` entries), the entries left are answered in their order without `seq` (K913). The escalation states `out_of_view: true`, which says only that something was withheld. The escalation's own stage, history acts, evaluations and proposals stand, an evaluation's trigger id with them: it counts no other entry (R7, R13's compatibility; N462). A viewer who may see everything is answered as before, with no `out_of_view` key. A withheld action meets no trigger for that viewer: R7 and R8 count only the actions the viewer may see (N460, K933).
