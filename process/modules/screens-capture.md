# screens-capture: requirements

Next number: 72

## Public

### Purpose

The Add screen (`capture`, `capture.mjs`): a capture from an address, a file or photo, or what a member saw or heard, through to its result, its reading's state and the document it made. Taken from `screens-intake` with its requirements and numbers; pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

Numbers are `screens-intake`'s, kept when ARCH split it (22 Nov 2026); a bare R<n> not here is that number in `screens-intake`. The tests that prove these requirements boot the whole `SCREENS` through `screens-intake`'s test kit, so they are `screens-intake`'s until this module has a kit of its own (code work for its job).

**capture** (Add; three tabs, each its own; the registry's 6 acts, `capture` re-pointed to `captureupload`)
- **R1** [met] "From an address": `acquire` with the address; `capturerequest` ("Ask for this later"); `monitor` ("Watch this address"). `acquire`'s refusals `BAD_LOCATOR`, `SOURCE_REFUSED`, `FETCH_FAILED`, `EMPTY` and `TOO_LARGE` are drawn each in its own sentence (as `setup-page` R10), any other by `interface-shell` R13. (N747; `control-plane` R58) An acquire whose answer carries `promoted` (an archive the door already promoted) is not promoted again by the screen; one without it is promoted as the screen's flow does.
- **R2** [met] (fix round 3, C17; K4356) "A file or photo" (`captureupload`, `capture` R86; `control-plane` R72): the member chooses a file (on a phone the camera may open, through the input's `accept` and `capture` attributes); the screen asks "How you got it" (four choices, none preselected), "Where and when" and "What it shows, in your own words", and composes `statement` from the three in that order, each with its label, at most 2,000 characters, refusing to send while any is empty and saying which; it sends `POST /api/?op=captureupload&statement=<s>&name=<the file's name>` with the file's bytes as the raw body and the session header. An answer with `existed: true` says the record already held the bytes; otherwise the screen says the capture waits in Held captures. For an upload filed under no project (R22) the result line also says whether the document was read and, when it was not, that no reading is held and what to do next. `UPLOAD_NO_STATEMENT`, `EMPTY`, `TOO_LARGE` and `MEMBER_SESSION_REQUIRED` are drawn each in its own sentence. "Downloaded from a website?" offers the address tab instead. The photo reminder is drawn by its key (`photo.reminder`). (fix round 9; capture R113; K4954) A file above 64 MiB, or one a `TOO_LARGE` with `in_parts` names, is sent in parts: the whole file's SHA-256 is hashed in the page first; `captureuploadbegin` declares it; the parts are sent in order, each under its own digest in the address; `captureuploadcomplete` names the parts' digests; the answer is filed as an upload's (R21). The screen draws its progress, begins again once on `UPLOAD_LOST`/`UPLOAD_EXPIRED`, aborts what it began when it stops the upload itself, and a member who leaves the screen ends the loop.
- **R3** [met] "What I saw or heard": `testify` with the member's words, where and when; `writinghelp` under the words field only while the assistant is reachable (`interface-shell` R20), helping word only what the member saw (DEC-153 (2)). (T51 fix round 2; K4328) The testimony's result links the document (C29).
