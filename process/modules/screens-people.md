# screens-people: requirements

Next number: 67

## Public

### Purpose

The people and organisations the record follows: `people`, a `person` (or an organisation's) page, and `explore`, the graph of connections (`people.mjs`, `person.mjs`, `explore.mjs`), with the parts and words every investigate screen shares (`parts.mjs`, `graphwords.mjs`, `whywords.mjs`, `rereads.mjs`). Taken from `screens-investigate` with their requirements and numbers; pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

Numbers are `screens-investigate`'s, kept when ARCH split it (22 Nov 2026); a bare R<n> not here is that number in `screens-investigate`. The tests that prove these requirements boot the whole `SCREENS` through `screens-investigate`'s test kit, so they are `screens-investigate`'s until this module has a kit of its own (code work for its job).

**person** (People › Person; the registry's 6 acts; N719; DEC-142, DEC-170, DEC-181)
- **R1** [met] Reads `person` (positions with their dates and sources, career, credentials, memberships, interests, statements, each with its grade), `samepersoncandidates`, and the identity claims as `person` answers them. Acts: `person` "Open the person" (the read again); `recordpersonfact` (a fact read from a passage the member chooses, citing it); `identityclaim` "Say another record is this person…" (with its basis); `withdrawidentityclaim` (with a reason); `followregister` (a register the person appears in, `following`); `personexpunge` "Remove it for good, with this reason": the dialog states `op-grades`' consequence statement (its R21, filled with the date and the member), takes the reason naming the law or order that requires it, and is drawn with the Irreversible weight; at the phone width it is read and not done (`interface-shell` R11, DEC-181). Each position and fact is a reference to its source passage; "Explore connections" opens `explore` from this person. (K5148; WALK-FINAL-05 R149, R59) A person's page draws each `removed` row as "Removed where the law requires, {date}, by {member}" where the fact stood; an office's page offers recording who holds it (`recordline` kind `holds`, with an optional end) and stating a holder current through a day (`linecurrentthrough`), each with the reason the shell asks.

**explore** (People › Person › Explore connections; the registry's 5 acts; K1469, K1487)
- **R2** [met] The member ticks what to follow (the connection kinds `VOCABULARIES.connection_kinds` names, by their words) and an end (optional), and the date it is explored as of (never "now"); `explore` answers the chains, each drawn hop by hop with each hop a reference and its cited source, and the chain's grade the weakest hop's (`mark.scale.connection.*`); a step declared and not evidenced is drawn as a lead, never a finding; a walk stopped at its depth says so (`mark.gap.undetermined`). Acts: `explore`; `explorepreset` (a preset by its name); `exploreverify` "Check this chain"; `connectionassert` "Attach a source to this step" (a passage chosen, on a declared step); `promote` "Open a question from this chain" (a new inquiry naming the chain, opening `question`). No score, rank or "most connected" is drawn (K1486).
