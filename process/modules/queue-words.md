# queue-words: requirements

Next number: 56

## Public

### Purpose

The words of the feed's producers, split from `queue-producers` (P6): every sentence an item says, and every date or time inside one, read by key through `setup-words`, with facts filled into placeholders and nothing composed here. It holds the words members see for the feed's classes and matters (a "to do", a "status", a "matter").

### Provides

**The words members see** (DEC-107, DEC-110, DEC-114; H15, H19)
- **R24** [met] Every member-facing sentence this module answers (an item's `summary` and `detail`, and the words of its options) calls an OBLIGATION item a "to do" and a CONDITION item a "status", never an "obligation", a "condition" or a "signal" ("signal" leaves member text; DEC-131); "obligation" names only a public body's duty (DEC-107). The internal codes, kinds and item ids are unchanged (`OBLIGATION`, `CONDITION`, `OBLIGATION::…`). (K1038; DEC-131, K1536)
- **R28** [met] Every member-facing sentence this module answers (an item's `summary` and `detail`, and the words of its options) calls what an action plan addresses a "matter" ("matters"), never a "subject". The item key `subject` (what an item is about, `queue`'s term) and every code and kind are unchanged. (DEC-114; as R24 for "to do" and "signal") - Measured: no item says "subject" for a plan's matters today (R16's checkpoint item names none). R28 holds that line as items are added.

#### T51: the sentences by key; the inviter named (N1231, N1209)

- **R43** [met] (T51; N1231; DEC-329 (10); K3766, K3806; `setup-words` R11–R13; amends R1, R2, R4–R7, R9, R14, R23, R30–R35, R37–R40) **Every older producer's sentences are read by key.** Each item's `summary` and `detail`, and the sentences that join them, are read through `setup-words`' `word(key, vars)` and never composed here; the item answers the key beside its text (`summary_key`, `detail_key`, as `notice-producers` R20), and a quotation below identifies a key and binds no wording. **Facts, not English:** a producer passes facts into `{placeholders}`: a name, a title, a count, a code, a local day (`date.daymonth`, or `date.daymonthyear` when not in the current local year, `civil-time`, as `notice-producers` R16's `{date}`) or a set time as a plain date and time (`time.at`, its `{date}, {time}`, never ISO text: `{date}` as above, `{time}` through `time.clock` ("{hour}:{minute} {ampm}", the 12-hour clock, DEC-335, K3870; `publish-schedule` R4's stored `HH:MM` mapped to it) (DEC-299, DEC-333 changes only the order of the day and month); in the group's zone as `publish-schedule` R4 carries it, R37; an instant the plane stamped, such as `{published}`, is read in that zone, its day through `civil-time.localDay` and its clock with `Intl.DateTimeFormat` on the 24-hour cycle, mapped to `time.clock`'s fills, until `civil-time` offers a local clock (N1249; K3864, as `notice-producers` R25)). A fill that is another module's sentence (`{statement}`, `{says}`, `{scope}`, `{grade}`, `{affects}`, `{checks}`, `{reason}`, `{reasons}`, and a new reading's `{state}`) is that module's own keyed word, read from the fact it states (its key and vars, or its code through the key that module holds for it), never English written here. An **optional fact** (a date, an author, a reason, a note, a count) is its own sentence key, appended only when the fact exists: the item without it is the item's base sentence, never a sentence with a blank. A count that branches is its `.one` or `.other` key (one when the count is 1). A key the word list lacks, a fill a source states in English or as a bare code it holds no key for, fails its test and is named in the job's REPORT (BOB carries it to the design stream); the fill is then left empty and the item is still raised, never filled with inline English, and nothing is written as a fallback sentence.
