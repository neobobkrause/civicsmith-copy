## Public

### Purpose

The screens of a project's action: its plan, a matter, an action and its dashboard (`dash-action`), with the module's words and `SCREENS`, which composes these with `screens-send`'s. Pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

**`SCREENS`** (the screen contract, `interface-shell` R21; each sentence read by key)

**plan** (Projects › <project> › Action plan; the registry's 10 acts; K608 (4); `views/plan-page.html`)
- **R1** [met] Reads the plan (`plan`: its subjects, called matters in member words, each with its determination and escalation stage; its options with category, regulated end date, subject and disposition; its scenarios and their phases; what each started option became) and its checks (`planproposals`, the assistant's suggestions, drawn only while the assistant is reachable, labelled machine work "not yet in the plan"). The checks strip states each check, such as a scenario with no branch for a hostile response. Options sort by category, regulated end date, matter or disposition, and are picked as rows (`interface-shell` R19). Acts: `plansubjectadd` "Add a matter"; `optionadd`; `optionpropose` "Ask the assistant for options" (reachable only); `optionadopt` (a suggestion, by the member's own act); `optiondispose` "Choose" or "Decline" (with a reason where declining); `scenarioset` "Lay out a scenario"; `checkpointrecord` "Judge" a checkpoint; `optionstartpreview` "Start…" (opening `start-send` for the option); `itemtakeup` "Take this up" on an item nobody has taken up (routing its to-dos to the member; nobody assigns anybody); `planclose` "Close plan…" with a reason.

**action** (Projects › <project> › Action; the registry's 6 acts; DEC-113; v0.2 rules 6, 15)
- **R5** [met] Reads the action (`action`: what it is, to whom, when and by whom it was sent, its clock entries with their due dates and state, its replies and their entries, its scope, its holds and pressure) and draws "Waiting on <office>'s reply" while one is owed, each due date with `mark.due` (a passed one as canon's question, "no reply recorded by <date>", never "overdue" until a member determines it, `action-producers` R1). Acts: `actioncorrespond` "Record a reply"; `actionpressure` "Mark a reply as pressure"; `actionmove` "Move the action"; `reminderset` "Remind me"; `actionhold` "Record a litigation hold" (with a reason); `actionscope` "Make the action confidential, or not" (with a reason; a published case keeps what it said). Each due date opens `due-date`. (fix round 4, K4473) C64: a clock entry that is a profile rule is worded by the rule's `label` (jurisdictions R97), else by the entry's own description, never by its code. (T51, K4569) An invited, not joined member is offered none of this screen's weighty acts, as R3's join-first rule.

**matter** (Projects › <project> › Matter; the registry's 6 acts; DEC-89; `views/matter-page.html`)
- **R6** [met] Reads the matter's determination (`determination`: the act, the standard it is tested against, what it requires and what the act did, its outcome, compliant or not, recorded with the same care either way), its consequences (`consequencesof`: who or what, the measure and how it is known, an undetermined measure never read as zero, people counted as a class), and its escalation (`escalation`, `escalationstatus`: the stages Documentation, Notification, Clock starts, Public, Formal, Political, Legal, the proposed next stage and its trigger, what ends it, and the actions attached). Acts: `escalationopen`; `escalationadvance` "Advance to stage <n>"; `declinetoescalate` "Decline to escalate, with a reason"; `escalationend` "End" (terminal, its dialog stating what ending does); `escalationstoppursuing` "Stop pursuing, with a reason"; `escalationreopen` "Reopen where it stopped" (with a reason). The legal tools a stage makes available are listed as facts from the profile, never as advice.
