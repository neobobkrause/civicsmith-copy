# filing-texts: requirements

Next number: 71

## Public

### Purpose

What `filings` reads in a text it prepares, split from `filings` (P6): the sentence split, the record check's verdicts, the disclosures printed first, the asking sentences of a notice, and the guards on assertions, recognition, junior staff and legal facts, each pure and handed what `filings` read from the record. It also holds the module's one door to its words (`words.mjs`), every sentence read by key through `setup-words`.

### Provides

**The Action layer at T45: the record check, disclosures, joint texts, documenting and the chance to fix, notices, quoting, templates after the act, second approval** (T45; K2780; N828, N858, N861, N880, N883, N884, N887, N890, N892 (b)–(d))
- **R34** [met] (T45; Actions D2; v0.2 rule 3; reconciliation R7) **The record check.** Every filing draft, counsel packet `briefing` section and communication is checked sentence by sentence (one split, R43 reading the same) by `case-account`'s sentence judgment (as its R13 judges), against the findings of R35's three kinds and the group's own dated correspondence: `supported` (naming what supports it), `unsupported`, `contradicted` (`ACCOUNT_CONTRADICTED_BY_RECORD`, naming what contradicts it); this module classes `question` itself (a sentence that asks) and `own_doing` (a sentence the member marks as about the group's own doing). An unsupported sentence is pointed out, never refused; a question is never flagged. `filingApprove` (R6) refuses a text holding a `contradicted` sentence `CONTRADICTED_HELD` unless the approval names it with a reason (`release: [{ord, reason}]`), recorded with the approval. The check is re-run on the approved text; a judgment that throws reads each sentence undetermined, never supported. Control: without a contradicted sentence no `release` is asked.
- **R35** [met] (T45; N828, N883; Actions D16a, D24, D28d; v0.2 rules 3, 21; reconciliation R7) Everything prepared from an action resting on findings checked for publication and held carries "Rests on findings checked for publication on {date}, not yet published." (`prepared.rests.held`); on another group's accepted findings, "Rests on findings of {group} (edition of {date}), accepted by us on {date}." (`prepared.rests.accepted`), with that group's public identity and declared stake (canon's words), each read from `actions` R75's `disclosures` (one source). One resting only on published findings carries neither. Control: each of the three kinds.
- **R36** [met] (T45; N883; Actions D16; v0.2 rule 14) **Joint texts.** For an action with `partners` (`actions` R78), the approved text has a body fingerprint, SHA-256 over the text without R22's per-group fields. `jointSignatory({filing, organisation, body_sha, account, author, viewer})` (`op=jointsignatory`): a member records that a partner approved the same text in its own copy, by its own act (`account`, how it is known). Refusals in order: `MACHINE_CANNOT_APPROVE`; `NO_SUCH_FILING`; `NOT_APPROVED`; `NOT_A_PARTNER`; `JOINT_FINGERPRINT_MISMATCH`. `filingsFor` (R13) lists each signatory; no shared plan is made. Control: a matching fingerprint from a listed partner lands; one character changed mismatches.
- **R37** [met] (T45; N887; Actions D25; v0.2 rule 19) **Documenting an infraction.** `communicationPrepare` takes `purpose: "document_infraction"` with `audience` (`violating_party`, `members`, `oversight`, `public` or `broad`). Without AI its text is assembled from the record: the act and its office, the standard, the evidence for each point with its grade, and what is still uncertain, each part naming its source and an undetermined part said so; the assistant's draft (`agent-worker` R76's `infractiondraft`), where enabled, is a labelled proposal (R5). Each version is approved per R6. A version for `violating_party` is a request for comment: it carries R40's sentence and a time for review and response (a date with its basis, `action-clocks` R7). Control: assembled with AI off, each part sourced.
- **R38** [met] (T45; N887, N892 (b); Actions D26, D28b; v0.2 rules 19, 21; K2781 (5)) **The chance to fix.** `purpose: "chance_to_fix"` drafts a notice stating what correcting the act and remedying its harm would look like under the standard, and a time: the law's period (a profile deadline, cited) or the group's window (`window` basis). Each sentence matching `action-grammar.pointOut` for `own_benefit_ask` (its R18) is pointed out, and R6 refuses approval `SELF_BENEFIT_ASK` until each is rewritten (no override; the list holds no remedy, K2781 (5)); the same holds for any demand. Other asking sentences are pointed out unless they ask for what the standard requires, for remedy of a recorded consequence (`consequences`) or for a route the profile names. Control: a notice asking only for the standard's act and remedy is approved.
- **R39** [met] (T45; N861; Actions D11; v0.2 rule 9; reconciliation R7) **Election notice and position sentences.** When the member marks at preparation that a draft names a pending measure or a candidate (no list of measures exists), the governing view's `legal_facts` of trigger `pending_measure_or_candidate` (`jurisdictions` R79) is answered once per draft with its citation (`notices: [{kind: "election", citation}]`, keyed `filings.election.notice`), never refusing. In a public comment or testimony prepared as the group's act (R44), each sentence `action-grammar.pointOut` answers for `position` is pointed out for the member to remove or resolve, as R43's are. Control: an unmarked draft carries no notice.
- **R40** [met] (T45; N880; Actions D13; v0.2 rule 8; K2780 (K1483/K1484 row 14 read with rule 8)) Every draft or communication for a `request_for_comment` carries the sentence that the reply may be quoted unless it says otherwise (`rfc.quoting`); R6 refuses an approved text without it `RFC_QUOTING_MISSING`. Control: the text with it is approved.

**The lines no draft wrote** (T45; reconciliation R1, R17; Actions D1, D9, D10, D12, D28c; v0.2 rules 2, 5, 8, 9, 12)
- **R43** [met] (T45; N884; Actions D1; v0.2 rule 2; reconciliation R1; K2781 (5)) **The assertion gate.** Every prepared outward text (filing draft, communication) is read by `action-grammar.pointOut` for `assertion` (its R18) over the draft's sentences (R34's split) in the language the member states for the draft, with the view's `phrasings`; where enabled, `agent-worker`'s `textcheck` sentences with `reads_as: assertion` are unioned with it, labelled machine work, matched to R34's sentences by their verbatim `quote`, never by `index` (K2824). `filingApprove` (R6) takes `resolutions` (`action-grammar` R19's shape), recorded with the approval, refused `ASSERTION_RESOLUTION_REFUSED` for a malformed one and `ASSERTION_UNRESOLVED` while `assertionsUnresolved` answers any sentence unresolved, each naming the sentences, after `TEXT_UNWRITABLE`. A machine never resolves (R6's `MACHINE_CANNOT_APPROVE`). A `breach_claim` resolution makes the action a breach action through `actions`' revision (`breach: true`, its R8's gate, whose refusals pass through). A list `undetermined` (no list for the language) is never a refusal: the approval shows `phrasing.no_list` and lands. Controls: a text with no pointed sentence needs no resolution; an `only_asks` with words lands; a Spanish draft with no Spanish list is approved with `phrasing.no_list`.
- **R44** [met] (T45; Actions D12; v0.2 rules 8, 9) **For the group, or as myself.** A public comment or testimony is prepared `voice: "group"` (approved by R6 and stamped, R22, an action's act) or `voice: "self"`: the member's own notes, answered with the sentence that it is not the group's act, never approved, stamped or recorded as an action or correspondence (`filingRecordSent` refuses it `NOT_THE_GROUPS_ACT`), and never published. Absent reads `group`. Control: a `self` text cannot be recorded sent.
- **R45** [met] (T45; Actions D12; v0.2 rule 5) **The recognition reminder.** A text prepared for an action whose kind the view marks `shape: recognition` (`actions` R93) carries, once per draft, the reminder that thanks names staff publicly, keyed `recognition.staff_public`, shown before approval and never refusing. Control: a non-recognition draft carries none.
- **R46** [met] (T45; N879; Actions D12; v0.2 rule 8) **The review copy.** A review copy of a draft prepared for a press action (`actions` R79) carries the press terms' `embargo_until` and `release_at` on its face and in its stamp's `subject` content (R22; K2832), and `filingRecordSent` before `release_at` records the sending as the review copy, saying so. With no terms stated, the copy carries the sentence that no embargo is stated. Control: a press draft with terms prints both times.
- **R47** [met] (T45; N858 D10; Actions D10; v0.2 rules 10, 12) **The hold notice's text.** `holdNoticePrepare({place, viewer, preparer})` prepares, for a hold in place (`action-holds` R2), a plain notice telling members what to keep outside Civicsmith: the place, the reason as stated, the date, and what kinds of material to keep, assembled from the record and labelled as R5's drafts; a member sends it by her own hand and records it (`action-holds` R11). It names no member as a recipient and records nothing of any member's compliance. `HOLD_NOT_IN_PLACE` passed through. Control: a released hold prepares nothing.
- **R48** [met] (T45; Actions D28c; v0.2 rule 21) **Junior staff.** A sentence naming staff below the responsible official is pointed out only through `agent-worker`'s `textcheck` (`reads_as: staff_named`) where enabled, labelled machine work, never refusing; no phrasing list matches names. With the assistant off, nothing is pointed out and the draft says the check did not run. Control: with `textcheck` off, the draft carries the not-run sentence.
- **R49** [met] (T45; N858, N861; Actions D8, D9; v0.2 rules 9, 12) **Legal facts shown once.** At preparation, each `legal_facts` entry of the governing view (`jurisdictions` R79) whose `applies_to` names the action's kind is shown once per draft or packet with its citation (`notices: [{kind: "legal_fact", key, citation}]`), never refusing. Control: an entry for another kind is not shown.

**The second approval's read; its words** (T46)
- **R52** [met] (T46; DEC-194; N913; amends R10, R34, R35, R38–R40, R42–R45, R48) Each sentence this module writes or answers by a key is the words `words.json` holds for that key, read by key, never a copy, its placeholders as `words.json` names them and filled from the record as now: `prepared.rests.held` (`{date}`) and `prepared.rests.accepted` (`{group}`, `{date}`, `{date_accepted}`) (R35); `packet.privileged` and `packet.privileged.warning` (R10); `filings.election.notice` (`{fact}`, `{citation}`, from the governing view's legal fact) (R39); `recognition.staff_public` (R45); the pointers, each sentence pointed out or judged answered with its key: `pointer.record.supported` (`{findings}`), `pointer.record.unsupported`, `pointer.record.contradicted` (R34), `pointer.position` (R39), `pointer.selfbenefit` (R38), `pointer.staff` and, with the assistant off, `pointer.staff.notrun` in place of `filings.staff.not_run` (R48); `voice.group`, `voice.group.means`, `voice.self` and `voice.self.means`, the last in place of `filings.voice.self` (R44); `filings.secondapproval.given` (`{member}`, `{date}`) once a second approval is recorded (R42). Three keep their present sentences in T46, each returned to BOB and read by key once the sentence and the requirement agree: `phrasing.no_list` (R43: `words.json`'s names "Civicsmith" bare, where DEC-149 says "your group's Civicsmith"); `rfc.quoting` (R40: R40's refusal looks for the sentence in the approved text, so a new wording would refuse `RFC_QUOTING_MISSING` every request for comment prepared before the change); `filings.secondapproval.waiting` (R42: "Any member of this project other than {approver}", while R42 admits any member other than the approver who may see the draft). `filings.review_copy.no_embargo` and `filings.review_copy.terms` (R46), which `words.json` does not hold, keep theirs. (T50, N1132, K3751) The three kept sentences and the two review-copy sentences are now read by key too, `filings.secondapproval.waiting` drawn only where a requirement answers it. Where each sentence is answered is unmoved. Control: each keyed sentence of a draft reads as `words.json` holds it, its placeholders filled.
