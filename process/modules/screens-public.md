## Public

### Purpose

What the public frame draws with no session: a published case and its public docket, the doorbell, and the outside reader's review copy (`published.mjs` and its parts, `docket-public.mjs`, `doorbell.mjs`, `review-outside.mjs`), with `parts.mjs`, the parts every publish screen shares. Taken from `screens-publish` with their requirements and numbers; pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

Numbers are `screens-publish`'s, kept when ARCH split it (22 Nov 2026); a bare R<n> not here is that number in `screens-publish`. The tests that prove these requirements boot the whole `SCREENS` through `screens-publish`'s test kit, so they are `screens-publish`'s until this module has a kit of its own (code work for its job).
