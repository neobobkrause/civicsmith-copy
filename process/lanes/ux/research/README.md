# The UX lane's research: the index

The index of the UX lane's research (Process4 design part 4, section 3; part 5, section 3). Every session of the lane reads this index in full first, reads a research file, or gives it to a worker, when a question needs it, and adds one line here for each file it adds. Research is background, not governing text; the lane's decisions are in `../decisions.md`, its pages in `../walks/`.

## The files

- `01-founding.md` (40 KB, 22 Nov 2026): what the founding documents and the owner's rulings say about the user's experience, ranked as `process/ranking.md` ranks them: what Civicsmith is and for whom, its audiences and roles, the principles and design requirements that bind screens and wording, the constructs a member sees, the assistant, first use and sign-in, the owner's rulings that shape the experience, and the conflicts and gaps found.
- `02-earlier-screens-and-journeys.md` (53 KB, 22 Nov 2026): the earlier design material in `docs/development/ux-substrate/`, frozen since 27 Oct 2026: the 91 screens in 7 groups, the wizard library, the three journey lists (27, 27, 32) reconciled to 35 distinct journeys, how steps were recorded, earlier walks and questions still open, and what the material lacked for walkable pages.

Quotations checked by the lane.

- `07-ungraded-passages.md` (5 KB, 26 Nov 2026): the canon's answer to Q1, searched on the owner's instruction of 26 Nov 2026: DEC-18 (Case Making R1 amended): an ungraded leg contributes nothing, is always named, and does not hold up a conclusion with graded legs; an all-ungraded conclusion is UNRATED, refused at signing only where load-bearing on an axis the project declared (case-authoring R6); no rule refuses publication for ungraded passages.
