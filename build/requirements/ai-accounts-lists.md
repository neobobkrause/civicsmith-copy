# ai-accounts-lists — requirements

**Status** · Split from `ai-accounts` on 5 Nov 2026 (BOB, coordinator ruling 08:23; the op-grades-folds pattern), to bring `build/requirements/ai-accounts.md` under the requirements cap. The requirements below are copied from it verbatim, each line, sub-bullet and mark unchanged, under the same numbers, so a test that names "R41: ..." stays true. They are the account-list rules (each owner's ordered list, entries, the cross-owner opt-in and share flags, hops along the list); the account itself, its plans and the other rules stay in `ai-accounts`, whose terms these requirements use. Their change history is the one kept in `docs/requirements-notes/ai-accounts.md`. Until a job of this module moves the code they rule, it lives in `bio-plane/src/ai-accounts/`.

## Public

- **R41** (T53; AIR27, AIR44; BR18) Each owner has one ordered list of accounts, which defines the sequence R11 and R50 walk (each account in order, its entries in their order). `accountOrderSet({owner, accounts, by})` replaces it and is recorded. Who sets it: administrators for `group` (`NOT_AN_ADMIN`), a project's owner for `project:<id>` (`PROJECT_ACT_NOT_THE_OWNER`), each member for `member:<id>` (`NOT_YOUR_ACCOUNT`); a machine credential `MACHINE_CANNOT_HOLD_ACCOUNT`. An account of another owner is accepted only with its standing opt-in (R49), else `OPT_IN_MISSING` naming the account; a personal subscription only in its owner's own list, else `PERSONAL_SUBSCRIPTION_NOT_SHARED`; an unknown or repeated account `ACCOUNT_NOT_LISTED`. An account not in its owner's list serves no one. Test: each of the three authorities sets a list; a list naming another owner's account with opt-in is kept; control: a member ordering the team's accounts, an unconsented account and a personal subscription in the team's list are each refused and the old list stands.

- **R46** (T53; AIR10, AIR17) An account's `entries` is an ordered allow-list of catalogue entry ids, each in the plan's `plan_ids` (else `ENTRY_NOT_ALLOWED`), non-empty (else `ENTRIES_EMPTY`), changed by `accountEntriesSet({account_id, entries, by})` with the owner's authority. Above accounts, `offeredSet({owner, entries | null, by})` sets a ceiling of entries for work at that level (null: none): the team's by administrators, a project's by its owners, a member's own by that member. A lower level's set must lie inside every higher level's, else `NARROWING_ONLY` naming the entry. For an act, the entries allowed are the intersection of the account's allow-list, the team's ceiling, the project's when the act is directly in the project, and the acting member's own, so a later shrink above drops out below with no rewrite. Test: a project set outside the team's ceiling is refused `NARROWING_ONLY`; a set inside is kept and the act sees the intersection; control: the team shrinking its ceiling removes the entry from a project's effective set with no write.

- **R49** (T53; AD37; DEC-465; K5486; amends the draft R49 to DEC-465's two flags) `crossOwnerOptInSet({account_id, on, by})` (`op=aioptinset`) sets the one share flag the account's owner level carries, and nothing else: a **team** account carries `share_with_lists`, a **member's own API account** carries `share_with_projects`; a project's account carries neither (`OPT_IN_NOT_OFFERED` naming the account, `ai.refused.optinnotoffered`), and a **personal subscription** (R42) can carry neither: `PERSONAL_SUBSCRIPTION_NOT_SHARED` (R48), asked first. Both flags are off when the account is set. `share_with_lists` is turned on or off by an active administrator only (`NOT_AN_ADMIN` through `membership.notAnAdmin`; a machine credential refused the same); while on, a member may add the account to her own list and to the list of any project she has joined (`membership` R54; K5486: joined, not only led), as may that project's owners (R41). `share_with_projects` is set by the member alone: any other caller, an administrator and the founder included, is `NOT_YOUR_ACCOUNT`, a machine credential `MACHINE_CANNOT_HOLD_ACCOUNT`, and `by` is the door's stamp, never the body (R38); while on, it may be added to the list of any project she has joined (`membership` R54; K5486), by her or by that project's owners; it never reaches the team's list or another member's. R41 accepts an account of another owner only while the flag that account carries is on (else `OPT_IN_MISSING` naming the account), and R50 tests the same flag at each hop; no per-list consent is stored. `accountState` (R2) answers the flag as `opt_in: {flag, on}` to the account's owners and `opt_in: null` for a project's account and a personal subscription. Each change is recorded with who and when (R16). Test: an administrator turns `share_with_lists` on and a member who has only joined a project adds the team account to that project's list and to her own; a member turns `share_with_projects` on and a project she has only joined adds her account; `accountState` answers each flag. Control: a project she has not joined cannot take either account (`OPT_IN_MISSING`); both flags are off on a new account and the lists refuse it `OPT_IN_MISSING`; a plain member and a machine credential turning on `share_with_lists` are `NOT_AN_ADMIN`; an administrator turning on a member's `share_with_projects` is `NOT_YOUR_ACCOUNT`; a project's account is `OPT_IN_NOT_OFFERED`; a personal subscription is `PERSONAL_SUBSCRIPTION_NOT_SHARED`; a member's `share_with_projects` account offered to the team's list or to another member's list is `OPT_IN_MISSING`.

- **R50** (T53; BR18, BR19) `hopFor({member, act, from: {account_id, entry_id}, reason, at_step_boundary})`, in-plane, writes nothing, answers the next pair or a refusal. `reason` is one of `limit` (a Civicsmith-side or provider-side limit reached), `unavailable` (circuit open or a provider outage), `lapsed` (the entry lapsed or retired), `capability` (the entry cannot serve the part), `auth` (the credential was refused or resolves to nothing, R60), `member_switch` (the member's own deliberate move down her list); any other is `HOP_REASON_REFUSED`; a use its owner switched off is never a reason (`AI_USE_SWITCHED_OFF`, K2352 as excepted by Bob, K4443). `at_step_boundary` false is `HOP_NOT_AT_STEP_BOUNDARY`. The walk starts after `from` in the sequence R11 chose (R41) and returns the first pair that is usable (R11 step 3), is offered, meeting every check in force and passing Gate 1 for the part (R55), is the same owner's or an opted-in account's (R49), and has its free tier accepted (R56). A target whose recipient is not disclosed to the member waits (R51). Nothing found is `LIST_EXHAUSTED` (`ai.refused.listexhausted`, naming who can act: raise a limit, add an account, change the order). The answer carries `payer_change` (`{from_owner, to_owner}`, null when the target has the same owner) for `ai-runs`' hop record. Test: each reason advances; a target with a check open, a part not passed, another member's personal subscription, a missing opt-in or an unaccepted free tier is skipped; the list ends `LIST_EXHAUSTED`. Control: a switched-off use, mid-step, and an unknown reason are refused and move nothing.

- **R70** (T53; DEC-465; AIR44; BR18; amends R41, R61) **A member orders her own list.** `aiorderset` serves a member's own list as well as the team's (DEC-420): its body is `{owner: "group" | "member", account_id, position}` with `position` the 1-based place the account is to take; `owner: "member"` takes the member from the door's stamped `by`, never the body (R38), and `accountOrderSet({owner: "member:<id>", accounts, by})` (R41) is what it calls. The screen's "Move up" and "Move down" are an explicit position change (the account's place minus or plus one), never a toggle: the door removes the account from the list as read at that instant and inserts it at `position`, every other account keeping its relative order; a `position` outside 1 to the list's length is `ORDER_POSITION_INVALID` (`ai.refused.orderpositioninvalid`), an account not on the list `ACCOUNT_NOT_LISTED`, a machine credential `MACHINE_CANNOT_HOLD_ACCOUNT`, `owner: "group"` by a non-administrator `NOT_AN_ADMIN`. The list may hold the team's account only while its `share_with_lists` is on (R49), and her own accounts, a personal subscription among them (R48); shared accounts move like any other. The change is recorded with the new order (R16). Test: through the door, a member with three accounts moves the last to position 1 and the list reads in that order, the other two keeping theirs; `accountFor` for her act then walks that order (R11); a body naming another member acts on the caller's list only. Control: position 0 and 4 on a list of three are `ORDER_POSITION_INVALID` and the list stands; an account not on the list is `ACCOUNT_NOT_LISTED`; a plain member sending `owner: "group"` is `NOT_AN_ADMIN`, and the team's list is unchanged by her moving her own.

- **R72** (T53; DEC-465; AD37; K5457) **Turning a share flag off.** When `crossOwnerOptInSet` (R49) turns `share_with_lists` or `share_with_projects` off, in that same act the account is removed from every list it is on that is not its owner's own (R41), each removal a change (R16) recorded with the list's owner and the instant, and the owners of those lists (each owner of a project whose list held it, each member whose list held it) are owed one notice that the account has left their list, which this module offers and `notice-producers` raises (the notice is a read, never a call upward): `accountNoticesFor({viewer, after, limit})`, in-plane, answers for each removal row (R16) of a list the viewer owns, `{kind: 'left-list', key, account_id, provider, plan, list_owner, at}`, a stable key per removal row, naming the account by provider and plan and never carrying a secret, digest or key; it writes nothing and never throws (R77 (3) and R59 add their kinds to the same read). A run's segment already dispatched on the account ends as it ends; the next hop no longer finds the account (R50), a run that needed it takes `ai-runs` R93's refusal if its list is then exhausted. Turning the flag on again restores no place: each list's owner adds it afresh. Test: a team account on two projects' lists and one member's: turning `share_with_lists` off removes it from all three, writes three removal rows and the flag row, and `accountNoticesFor` answers one `left-list` entry to each of three owners, the same keys on every read; control: the team's own list keeps it, turning off an already-off flag writes and answers nothing, turning it on again leaves the three lists without it, and an entry carries no secret.

- **R77** (T53; K5501; AD37; K5486; R27's pattern; amends R41, R72, R27) **A member who can no longer place a shared account loses her placements.** (1) *Record.* An entry on a list that is not its owner's own (R41, R49) carries who placed it and when, `placed_by` (the door's stamp, R38) and `placed_at`, written in the act that puts it there and shown in R16's row for it (the list's change); an entry written before this line has `placed_by` null. (2) *Listener.* At start one listener on `membership.onProjectLeft` (its R158, refused through `listenerRefusal` as R26 is). When a member stops being a participant of a project (`why: "removed"`), in the same act, from that project's list (the owner `project:<id>`) are removed: every entry whose `placed_by` is her and whose account is not the project's own (the team's account, another member's account that she placed under R49, or her own that she placed), and her own member account (`member:<her id>`, R49's `share_with_projects`) whoever placed it, since she is no longer joined and R49 offers it only to a project she has joined; an entry placed by another member (the team's account placed by an owner, say) stays. Each removal is a change (R16) recorded with the project, the account, who had placed it, the act's `by` and the instant. (3) *Told.* The project's owners other than the removed member are owed one notice, per act and project, that the account(s) have left their project's list: each removal row (2) records is answered to them by R72's `accountNoticesFor` as `{kind: 'left-project-list', ...}`, naming the account by provider and plan (as R72 does) and never carrying a secret, digest or key, and `notice-producers` raises it; a removal that removes nothing answers nobody and records nothing. (4) *The run.* A run's segment already dispatched on a removed account ends as it ends; the next hop no longer finds the account (R50), and a run that needed it takes the refusal R72 names if its list is then exhausted. She is no longer a joined participant, so `accountFor` (R11) refuses her act naming the project `PROJECT_ACT_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT` as before. (5) *No notice to her.* The act is hers or an owner's to know by the removal itself; this line sends her nothing. Test: a team account placed by member A and a second placed by owner O on project P, and A's own account placed by O: an owner removes A (`projectRemove`): the team account A placed and A's own account leave P's list, the team account O placed stays, two removal rows are recorded with `by`; `accountNoticesFor` answers P's owners other than A one entry for each account, naming it by provider and plan; the team's own list and A's own list are untouched; a segment dispatched on the account ends as it ends. Control: an entry with `placed_by` null stays (except A's own account); a project's own account stays; a removal of a member with no placements writes and answers nothing; `projectLeave` (she is `leaving`) and `projectOwnerRemove` call this listener not at all and the placements stand; a throwing `accountNoticesFor` read changes nothing of the removal; an entry carries no secret (sentinel scan, R64).
