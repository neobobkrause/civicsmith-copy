# store-ask — requirements

**Status** · New 4 Nov 2026: split from `store-assembly` for size (its requirement lines alone are over the 70,000-byte cap; the split proposed by `store-assembly`'s job, attempt 1, in `docs/requirements-notes/store-assembly.md` on `job/store-assembly`), accepted by the coordinator (BOB) 4 Nov 2026; K617, K624. A new module, layer 11, directly before `store-assembly`, split by copy with no `from`. R1–R11 are `store-assembly`'s R49, R50, R51, R52, R53, R54, R55, R56, R57, R58, R59, copied verbatim with their marks and renumbered in the order of their old ids, meaning unchanged; each line says "moved from store-assembly:R<n>". `store-assembly`'s own restarted job removes and retires the moved lines there, naming their new ids, and never reuses them. A bare `R<n>` inside a moved line that names no module is a `store-assembly` id. The code this module owns today is `bio-plane/src/store-assembly/ask.mjs`, `transcribe.mjs` and `unpack.mjs`: it moves here only once `store-assembly`'s job has retired the lines; until then this module's job implements here and leaves `store-assembly`'s code to its job.

## Public

### Purpose

The ask, draft and transcription doors of the assembled store. It uses `store-jobs` (`transcribe.mjs` reads `pendingOf`). `store-assembly` re-exports what `plane` imports from it, so `plane` does not change.

### Provides

- **R1** (moved from store-assembly:R49) (was `plane` R33) The composition root hands `answers` no `ceilingRefusal`: `answers` judges a limit through `ai-use.useCheck` on its own host. The assembly's ask path (`askOnObject`) and draft path (`draftOnObject`) take the account that serves the member's act, and its limit, from `answers.askAccount({member, project?, at?, kind?})` (an ask's `project` when it names one; `kind: "draft"` on the draft path), in place of their own `credentials.accountFor` call and any ceiling; a refusal it answers is answered as given, before any call to `agent-worker`, and nothing is posted. With no `AGENT_WORKER` bound, an ask and a draft answer 503 `AGENT_WORKER_UNBOUND`. Every read made under a grant the draft path mints is recorded in that grant's read log with `use: "draft"` (`answers.logRead`, its R2 as R30 widens it; `store-door` R11), so no row of a project `credentials.projectsKeptAway({use: "draft"})` names reaches a draft; an ask's grant reads under `use: "ask"`. The assembly owns a grant's `use`: `draftOnObject` notes each grant it mints (in memory, dropped when its TTL passes; no table), and the `logRead` the assembly hands `store-door` records `use: "draft"` for a grant so noted, else the entry's own `use` or `ask`. The suggestions switch sent with an ask or a draft is the one of the account that serves: the sign-in's own (`accountReferenceState().subscription.uses.suggestions`) when `answers.askAccount` answers `signin`, the member's reference's for `apikey` at level `member`, the group key's for `group`; a grant is minted by it. Controls: a sign-in with suggestions on sends `suggestions: true`; an API-key reference's switch is the member's own.
- **R2** (moved from store-assembly:R50) (was `plane` R40) `askOnObject` and `draftOnObject` refuse, before the keep-away read, any account read or any grant is minted, unless the part is deployed and admitted: `run-rules`' deploy flag for their mode (`ask`; for a draft, its kind's flag: `own_words` for `writinghelp` and `groupdescriptiondraft`, `translation` for `translationdraft`, `action_drafts` for `textcheck`, `infractiondraft`, `bearingdraft`) or D14's bar for that part not admitting them refuses. A flag that does not deploy the part is refused `AI_RUN_MODE_NOT_DEPLOYED` with `run-rules`' row (check, translation) and `mode`, status 403; a part whose flag deploys it but which `ai-runs.partAdmitted(part)` does not admit (part `ask`; `draft:<kind>` for a draft; `transcribe`; asked through `partAdmitted` alone, so the test-instance exception admits where the deployer set it and nowhere else) is refused `AI_PART_NOT_DEPLOYED` (`run-rules` R30's `partRefusal(part)`, relayed unchanged); nothing is posted to the assistant. Admitted with a label, the ask's request to `agent-worker` carries `untested` (`agent-worker` R79), and the draft's answer carries `untested` beside `grant`, `suggestions` and `read`; admitted by the bar, neither carries it. The draft path takes the status report's draft (`statusreportdraft`, handed `plan` and the items `plan-work` answered): `draft`'s flag and `partAdmitted("draft:statusreport")`, then the keep-away read (`credentials.aiKeptAway` with the plan's project), all before any account is read; it mints no grant and sends no `told`; its use is counted as `draft`. Tests: an ask and a writing-help draft on Civicsmith's empty set with no test instance are refused `AI_PART_NOT_DEPLOYED` with nothing posted; control: with `CIVICSMITH_TEST_INSTANCE` equal to `INSTANCE_NAME` the same post is made and carries the label; a status-report draft for a plan whose project is kept away is refused before any account is read.
- **R3** (moved from store-assembly:R51) (was `plane` R41) The draft door takes `op` `textcheck` (`{text}`), `infractiondraft` (`{matter, audience}`) and `bearingdraft` (`{action, reply}`), each gated by R50, and assembles from the asking member's own reads only, never wider than the member sees: for `infractiondraft`, `record` = `{plan, actions, correspondence, findings}` (the matter's plan when it names one, through `action-plans`; the plan's actions the member sees and their recorded entries, through `actions`; the findings those actions' legs cite, as `inquiry` reads them for that member); for `bearingdraft`, `action_record` (the action as `actions` reads it for the member, with its claims) and `reply_record` (the received entry `reply` names, with its text). The answer is labelled and writes nothing.
- **R4** (moved from store-assembly:R52) (was `plane` R47) A `signin` account answered by `answers.askAccount` carries its held token (`ai-accounts` R19's `key`) as `secret` in `agent-worker` R6's wire shape, for ask, draft and transcription; an answer `SIGNIN_TOKEN_REFUSED` calls `credentials.subscriptionTokenRefused`. Test: the secret reaches only the binding's request; control: an API key as before.
- **R5** (moved from store-assembly:R53) (was `plane` R50) The draft door takes `readtranslate` (`op=readtranslate`), `{subject: {kind, id, project?}, language?}`, `kind` one of `note`, `question`, `finding`, `notice`, for the asking member:
  1. gated by R50: `draft`'s flag, then `ai-runs.partAdmitted("draft:reading")`;
  2. it reads the subject only as she may: a note through `hypotheses.notesOf` for her (her own only: notes are never shared, `hypotheses` R12, R14); a question's or finding's text through `inquiry` for her; a notice's wording through `network-notices.noticesOf({project})` for her. An absent or unseen subject answers its owner's own refusal for an absent object, as given (`NO_SUCH_NOTE`; `inquiry`'s `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE`; `network-notices`' `noSuchProject`), before any account is read; this module mints no row;
  3. the language is `translations.readingLanguage` (its R10), whose refusals pass through;
  4. a passage over 20,000 characters is refused before any account is read, with the owner's text shown (a protective limit, K1881);
  5. then the keep-away read for the subject's project, when it has one (`ai-accounts.aiKeptAway`, its R7), then `answers.askAccount({member, kind: "draft"})` (R49); each refusal is answered as given, nothing posted;
  6. it posts `agent-worker`'s `POST /draft` with `task` `{op: "readtranslate", language, text, from_language?}` (its R86), minting no grant and sending no `told`.

  It answers `{translation, language, from_language, label: {kind: "machine"}, original: {kind, id, text}}`, with `untested` as R50 states; it writes nothing, and counts only the usage to her day (`ai-use.countAskUsage`, mode `draft`). Controls: another member's note answers `NO_SUCH_NOTE`, as an absent one does; her own note is translated.
- **R6** (moved from store-assembly:R54) (was `plane` R67) **The ask reads the reading language from `translations`.** The ask calls `translationsOf(ctx).readingLanguage(...)` (its refusals passing through), never `instance-setup`'s delegate. Test: the ask's language read answers as before through `translations`; control: an unknown member is refused as before.
- **R7** (moved from store-assembly:R55) (was `plane` R55) The assembly's ask, run, draft and transcribe calls to `agent-worker` (`/ask`, `/run`, `/draft`, `/transcribe`) carry the `trace` and member ref the door handed (`control-plane` R93; in process the strings `trace` and `memberRef`, K3293; the Durable Object class passes them through its RPC methods, `plane` R55) in the `x-civicsmith-trace` and `x-civicsmith-member-ref` headers, in `agent-worker` R83's shapes, and in no body or address; a call with none handed sends neither header. Test: a stub binding receives both headers on an ask; control: neither appears in a body.
- **R8** (moved from store-assembly:R56) (was `plane` R59) **The ask stream passes through as written.** The stream `op=ask` answers reaches the caller chunk by chunk, unbuffered: the first chunk is written before the last is made, through the door and the Store's RPC. Where any buffering is, if anywhere, is not known: the job diagnoses it by a test with a slow producer and reports it to BOB before changing anything. Test: a producer that writes its first line then waits 2 s delivers that line to the reader before the wait ends.
- **R9** (moved from store-assembly:R57) (was `plane` R36) At start the composition root registers one transcriber with `extraction` (`registerTranscriber("plane", t)`), built on the object's own instances, so `extraction` reaches nothing above its layer:
  - `deployable()` is `ai-runs.partAdmitted("transcribe").admitted` (R50); anything that throws answers `false`;
  - `keptAway({project, use})` is `ai-accounts.aiKeptAway({project, use})` (its R7, R22), relayed as given;
  - `transcription({member, project, at})` answers `{member, project, at, act, credentials, useCheck, transcribe, untested}`, `untested` the label when the part runs on R50's exception, for `extraction` to record (its R74): `credentials` the object's own instance (its `accountFor`), `useCheck` `ai-use.useCheck`, `act` an id minted for this act (`transcribe:<captureSha prefix>:<instant>`, the prefix being the capture the AI path is asked for, so the id is read after that call), and `transcribe` the AI path below.

  The AI path, `transcribe({account, capture_sha, store, pages, use})`: with no `AGENT_WORKER` binding it answers `{ok: false, reason: "AGENT_WORKER_UNBOUND"}` and does nothing else; when the capture's bytes are absent from the evidence store, `EVIDENCE_ABSENT`; when no asked page is rendered within the limits below, `NO_PAGE_RENDERED`; neither posts to `agent-worker`. Otherwise it reads the capture's pages from the evidence store by ranges (R58) and renders the asked pages in ascending order, at most `TRANSCRIBE_PAGES_MAX` of them (`agent-worker`'s), each by `pdf-pixels.renderPageToPixels(bytes, page, {decodeDct: true})` or, for a page that answers `NOT_A_PDF`, `renderPictureToPixels(bytes, page, {maxEdge: TRANSCRIBE_PICTURE_EDGE, grey: true})` (`TRANSCRIBE_PICTURE_EDGE` exported, 1,568 px). A page the renderer refuses, or whose PNG is over `TRANSCRIBE_IMAGE_MAX_BYTES`, or beyond the maximum, is not sent and stays unread, named in the answer (R58). It posts `{capture_sha, pages, account}` to `agent-worker`'s `POST /transcribe` (its R72), `account` in its R6 wire shape (`accountFor`'s `key` named `secret`, `member` the acting member, `suggestions: false`), the secret leaving the object only in that one call. A binding that throws or answers no JSON answers `AGENT_WORKER_SILENT`; a refusal `agent-worker` answers (its R72 codes: `BAD_BODY`, `BAD_SHA`, `BAD_PAGES`, `NO_ACCOUNT`, `BAD_ACCOUNT`, `TRANSCRIBE_NEEDS_API_KEY`) is relayed with its own HTTP status, never read from `res.json()` alone, so `extraction` answers that refusal with that status. A `NO_PAGE_RENDERED` answer carries `refused`: one `{page, reason, process?}` for each asked page not sent, `reason` the renderer's refusal (`pdf-pixels` R39's key), `IMAGE_TOO_LARGE` or `DECODE_FAILED`, `process` the JPEG process when the renderer's detail names one; nothing is posted. Whenever the answer carries `usage`, whatever else it says, it is counted in one transaction through `ai-use.countUsage({owner, member, use: "transcribe", mode: "transcribe", model: engine, usage, calls, at, act})`, `owner` spelled as `ai-use` R1 from the account, so `ai-use.actualOf({act})` answers its cost after. It answers `agent-worker`'s answer, `{ok: true, engine, version, pages}` for `tier4Extend`. Control: a stub `agent-worker` answering 400 `BAD_PAGES` is answered 400 with that code.
- **R10** (moved from store-assembly:R58) (was `plane` R106) **The transcriber reads by ranges and names what it left.** `transcribe.mjs` reads the pages it renders from ranges of the capture (`acquisition`'s `readRange`, by the whole sha), and loads no PDF over the inline ceiling (`READ_BOUNDS`, quoted by `boundOf`) into a request; the pages it asked and the pages it sent are both stated, an asked page beyond `TRANSCRIBE_PAGES_MAX` or refused by the renderer is named unread (R57's `refused`) and never dropped silently, and what is left is owed as a job row (R74), not read in the request. Test: the press and `pagetranscribe` on a 24 MiB PDF and a 300-page scan do not load the object in the request, and `owedwork` lists what is left. Control: a 3-page file is read in the request. A posted answer names the pages left unread in `unread`, and the `NO_PAGE_RENDERED` answer in `refused`.
- **R11** (moved from store-assembly:R59) (was `plane` R25) The archive unpack's drain (`unpack.mjs`) reads `archive-unpack` events in pages, passing `after`, the `cursor` of the page's last event, while a page comes back full, so events at the retry limit, which stay queued, never hide newer events behind them; no event is read twice in one drain.
