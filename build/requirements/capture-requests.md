## Public

### Purpose

The AI does not capture: it requests, and the daemon captures with provenance preserved (INVESTIGATIVE-SESSION §4). This module is the request door and the drain. A run files a request for a public address under the question it is working; the request is a row that carries no bytes and fetches nothing. The drain alone turns a row into a fetch, through `capture`'s trusted in-process arm, after applying DEC-47's conduct (a legible agent, a purpose, rate) and composing the attribution that names both principals. It reports what became of every request, to the run, to members and to operators.

### Provides

Terms. A **request** is one row: `request` id, `run`, `target` (the inquiry it was asked under), `address`, `host`, `purpose`, `ua_mode`, `principal_plane`, `principal_claude`, `state`, `code`, `detail`, `capture_sha`, `attempts`, `requested_at`, `updated`, `expires`, `captured_at`, `lead_inquiry`, `run_woken_at`, `render`, `sweep` (null unless R45), `co_archive` (null unless R50; T35). Its **state** is `requested`, `draining`, `captured`, `refused` or `expired`; `captured`, `refused` and `expired` are **terminal**. The **caller** is the control plane's `principal` stamp; the **viewer** is its viewer stamp; neither is ever a body field. Every refusal is an answer `{ok: false, reason, code, check, translation, detail}`, never a throw.

**captureRequest(args, {viewer, caller}) → answer** The door (`op=capturerequest`). `args`: `run`, `address`, `target`, optional `lead_inquiry` (alias `lead`), `purpose`, `ua_mode` (default `civicsmith`; `civicos` accepted as the same mode, R14), `render`.
- **R1** The run: a run absent, or whose context the viewer cannot see (ai-runs' run sight), or not `running`, is `CAPTURE_REQUEST_NO_RUN` (C-28.1), the unseen and never-minted cases answering alike but for the id. A run the caller is not the principal of is `run-rules`' `runPrincipalGate` refusal (`AI_RUN_NOT_PRINCIPAL`, C-22.12), relayed field by field. Sight is asked before position, and position before status.
- **R2** The address must be a public locator (`isPublicHttpsLocator`) with a readable host, else `CAPTURE_REQUEST_NOT_PUBLIC` (C-28.2). The host is derived here, lower-cased, and stored. An address is public for this rule when it is fetched without credentials: no credentials are used for it unless a member supplied them for that scope (R41, K103). (T35; F2, K1880) A public address is then judged by R49, its refusals answered before anything is written.
- **R3** `target` must be an inquiry the viewer can see, else `CAPTURE_REQUEST_NOT_AN_INQUIRY` (C-28.3), answering alike for an unseen and an absent one. A `lead_inquiry`, when given, must be another inquiry the viewer can see (`CAPTURE_REQUEST_LEAD_NOT_AN_INQUIRY`, C-28.14) and not the target (`CAPTURE_REQUEST_LEAD_IS_THE_TARGET`, C-28.15).
- **R4** A request carrying any of `capture_sha`, `sha256`, `bytes`, `content`, `provenance_chain`, `via`, `retrieved` (non-empty) is `CAPTURE_REQUEST_CARRIES_A_CAPTURE` (C-28.4), naming the fields; nothing is dropped silently.
- **R5** `render` absent, `null` or `false` asks for the served document and `true` for the rendered page; any other value is `CAPTURE_REQUEST_RENDER_MALFORMED` (C-28.16) and nothing is queued.
- **R6** Idempotent on (run, address, render): with a row in `requested`, `draining` or `captured` for that key, the answer is that row's `request`, `target`, `purpose`, `ua_mode`, `lead_inquiry`, `render`, `state` and principals, with `requested: false, already: true`, and nothing is written. Otherwise one row is written in `requested` with `attempts` 0, `requested_at` and `updated` now, `expires` now + 24 h, and answered with `requested: true, already: false` and the values as written (never as sent). `now` is the caller's stated instant when an in-process caller gives `at` (a test, the scheduler's replay), else this module's clock; `expires` is that instant + 24 h, so each request's expiry follows its own `at`. The op never reads `at` from a body.
- **R7** The request id is minted here (`CR-<instant>-<random>`); `captureRequest` never throws.
- **R8** `principal_plane` is the caller stamp; `principal_claude` is the run's. Neither is taken from the body. `purpose` and `ua_mode` are recorded as sent and judged only at the drain (R14).
- **R9** The door makes no outbound request of any kind, and runs no conduct or attribution check.

**captureRequestAttribution(row) → `{ok: true, actor, machine_attributed: true, at_the_request_of: {run, inquiry}, principals: {plane, claude}, statement}` | `{ok: false, code: "CAPTURE_ATTRIBUTION_ONE_PRINCIPAL", plane, claude}`** The one composer.
- **R10** Both principals, trimmed, must be non-empty, else the refusal naming which is present. `actor` is `token:daemon` (machine-shaped by construction). `statement` is composed only from those fields: "the daemon captured this, at the investigative session's request (run <run>), under <plane>, paid by <claude>". Every surface that states a request's attribution (R15, R25, and the notifications built on R26) uses this composer.

**captureRequestDrain({limit, actor, now}) → `{configured, actor, at, drained, captured[], refused[], held[], expired[], remaining}`** The sole fetcher (`op=capturerequestdrain` and the scheduler's `capture-request-drain` consumer).
- **R11** Inert unless the instance's unattended capture is configured: unconfigured, it answers `configured: false` with nothing drained, and the pending count it offers the scheduler is 0, so no alarm is held. Not re-entrant: a drain called while one is running answers `busy: true` and does nothing.
- **R12** A tick acts on at most 10 rows (`limit` lowers it, never raises it). It first releases render rows past their `expires` (R20), before selecting, so an expired row never takes a slot; then takes `requested` rows oldest first (`requested_at`, then `request`). Given the scheduler's rank (`drain({…, rank})`, scheduler R10), the tick reads at most ten times its batch of `requested` rows, oldest first, and takes its batch in the rank's order, each offered as `{kind: "request", id, waitingSince: requested_at, cadenceMs}`. The rank itself puts work waiting longer than one cadence (`cadenceMs`) first. Without `rank`, it takes oldest first.
- **R13** Each selected row is judged by R14; a refusal writes `code`, `detail` and `attempts + 1`, and moves the row to `refused` when terminal or leaves it `requested` when not. Every refusal and hold is appended to the observation log as `LOOKED_INDETERMINATE` for the row's address, `governed` exactly when the reason is this instance's own pacing or renderer.
- **R14** Conduct, in this order, the first failing rule deciding: attribution (R10, terminal, C-28.11); `purpose` in `CAPTURE_PURPOSES` (`investigate`, `acquire`), else `CAPTURE_CONDUCT_NO_PURPOSE` (terminal, C-28.8); `ua_mode` in `CAPTURE_UA_MODES` (`civicsmith`, `member-browser`; `civicos`, the name written before T31, is accepted on input and read in a stored row as the same mode as `civicsmith`, every read and feed item answering it as `civicsmith`, and a request is always written with `civicsmith`; DEC-124, K1365 (4)), else `CAPTURE_CONDUCT_UA_ILLEGIBLE` (terminal, C-28.6); for `member-browser`, the agent recorded on the target inquiry (inquiry's `memberUserAgent`, R44), else `CAPTURE_CONDUCT_UA_UNRECORDED` (terminal, C-28.7); for `civicsmith`, `civicsmithUserAgent(version, instance, purpose)`, which must pass `userAgentIsLegible` (a `(+<url>` contact), else C-28.6; the host in cool-off (host-governor's non-consuming `isHeld`), else `CAPTURE_CONDUCT_HOST_HELD` (held, C-28.9, condition `governor-holding-host`); a host already fetched once this tick, else `CAPTURE_CONDUCT_TICK_SPENT` (held, C-28.10). No rule reads `robots.txt`, and a document under a `Disallow` path is captured (BOB-3). (T35; F2, K1880) Directly after attribution, R49 is judged again over the row's address, a failure terminal (`refused`) under R49's code, so a row filed before R49 held, or returned by R42, is never fetched for an address the record does not hold.
- **R15** A row passing conduct is set `draining` (`attempts + 1`) in the tick that then fetches, counts one fetch for its host, and is fired through `capture`'s trusted in-process arm with the row's address, purpose, agent (for `member-browser` only) and render; the credential R41 holds for its scope; the held capture's digest when R39 applies (`heldSha`, `acquisition` R22); and `origin` `{matched_sweep, deeming_actor}` (R38, `acquisition` R21); and (T35) the row's `co_archive` (R50), as `acquisition`'s per-capture co-archive choice (K1888; T35-21). Nothing else is passed. On a filed capture the row becomes `captured` with `code` null, `detail` the attribution statement, `capture_sha` and `captured_at`; the observation log gains `PRESENT` with `result_kind` `capture`, `result_ref` the digest and the statement as detail; the answer lists `{request, address, sha, grade, attribution}`.
- **R16** Nothing outside this drain can make the instance fetch for a request: `capture`'s arm is reachable only in process, and `op=acquire` refuses `via: "capture-request"` from any caller (`CAPTURE_NOT_DRAINING`, C-28.13, `acquisition` R1, R29).
- **R17** A render `acquisition` refuses before fetching (C-83.3, .4, .5, .8) holds the row `requested` under that code with its detail, gives the host's tick slot back, is appended `LOOKED_INDETERMINATE`, `governed`, condition `render-deferred`, and is answered in `held` with `render: {state, content: "undetermined"}`, `state` being `acquisition`'s own word (`waiting` for C-83.8) or else `deferred`. The served shell is never filed in its place.
- **R18** A render result decided after the page was fetched spends the host's slot: `RENDER_NOT_A_PAGE` (C-83.6) is terminal (`refused`), and `RENDER_FAILED` (C-83.7) holds the row.
- **R19** Any other failure holds the row `requested` under a code catalogued in C-28 with its check and translation, appended `LOOKED_INDETERMINATE`, not governed, and answered in `held`. Every code this module writes to a row is catalogued in C-28, C-83 or C-108.
- **R20** Every non-terminal row past its `expires`, plain or render, is released: state `expired`, its last code kept, `detail` saying what it was held under and that nothing was filed, appended `LOOKED_INDETERMINATE` (for a render, `governed`, condition `render-deferred`), answered in `expired` (a render with `render: {state: "expired", content: "undetermined"}`), and never fetched. At most one tick's batch is released per tick.
- **R21** A row left `draining` by a tick that did not finish is not left there: the next tick returns it to `requested`, or releases it by R20 when past `expires`.
- **R22** `remaining` is the number of `requested` rows after the tick.
- **R38** A capture filed for a request is promoted at `collected`, never higher, with `origin: sweep`, the run and both principals (R10) as the deeming actor, and the target inquiry as the matched scope, DEC-47 making the inquiry the authorisation (K102).
- **R45** (Intake Doctrine §4, the AI's "relevant nearby" arm; K1036, K1099) A run's request may name a sweep (`sweep: "<bundle>#<id>"`). The drain then files the request with `matched_sweep` set to that sweep and `deeming_actor` set to the run (R38). It does so only when a scope check, registered once at start by `link-sweep` (its R12; K31's pattern; this module is earlier, P4), answers that the sweep is ratified and not held, and that every locator of the request is in its scope. The check is `registerSweepScope(module, fn)`; `fn({sweep, locators, run, target})`, sync or async, admits only `{ok: true, scope: [prefix, …]}`, and any other answer refuses, carrying its optional `reason` (`unknown`, `unratified`, `held`, `out-of-scope`) and `detail`; with no check registered, a request naming a sweep is refused. An admitted request's fetch carries `scope` as `acquisition` R31's, with origin `{kind: "sweep", matched_sweep, deeming_actor}`. Otherwise the request is refused `CAPTURE_SWEEP_OUT_OF_SCOPE` (C-28.19, terminal), nothing filed. The request counts toward the sweep's `per_run` on the sweep's next run, read by `link-sweep` from the captures' `matched_sweep` (K1140).
- **R39** One fetch per version of an address (K103 (1)). A request for an address of which the record holds a successful capture is fetched conditionally, with the validators that capture's fetch recorded, where the source offers them, and an answer that the content has not changed files no new capture; where the source offers none, bytes identical to the held capture are recorded as that capture, never as a new one. Either way the row becomes `captured` with `capture_sha` the held capture's digest, the new request's details (run, target, purpose, principals, `captured_at`) are recorded against that capture, and the answer's entry says the capture was already held.
- **R49** (F2; K1880, BOB's final rule; K1881 for the bound) **An address the record already holds.** Every request is filed by a run (R1), so every request is judged so, at the door (R2) and again at the drain (R14), in this order:
  - an address longer than 2,048 characters is refused `CAPTURE_REQUEST_ADDRESS_TOO_LONG`, terminal, naming the bound and the length, never cut;
  - an address that is not one the record already holds is refused `CAPTURE_REQUEST_ADDRESS_NOT_HELD`, terminal. The address, query and fragment included, is held when it is equal, character for character once its scheme and host are lower-cased, to the address or retrieval locator of an acquisition receipt (`provenance`'s `captured_locators`, its R48), or to the raw address with its fragment of an outbound link of a capture (`capture` R27's links), in either case of a capture the viewer may see (`membership` R43, through the register's bundle; a capture filed in no bundle is visible, as capture R27 reads it).

  The refusal names the code and says that the assistant's reading may go anywhere but only an address the record already holds is captured for it. No other rule widens this one: a request naming a sweep (R45) is judged by it first, and a held capture (R39) holds its own address. The run's discovery is not limited here: the assistant reads any public site while it searches, and nothing it reads that way enters the record (K1880); only what a request files does, through the drain.
- **R40** A refusal or hold that arises from the source, not from this instance's conduct, states its reason as one of `login` (the source asks for a login), `paywall`, `user-agent` (the source refuses the agent sent) or `other`, on the row and in every read of it (R24, R26, R29), with the source's own answer in `detail` (K103 (3)).

**The co-archive choice** (K1888 (4); T35-21's per-capture choice)
- **R50** `captureRequest` takes an optional `co_archive`: `true` (ask the co-archive for this capture), `false` (ask none) or absent or null (the group's setting decides at the drain, `acquisition`'s, default on). It is the member's choice: a `true` or `false` is taken only when the caller stamp is a member's (`member:<id>`, or a credential that member minted, read through `run-rules`' `runPrincipalOf`), and under any other stamp it is refused `CAPTURE_REQUEST_CO_ARCHIVE_NOT_A_MEMBERS`; any other value is `CAPTURE_REQUEST_CO_ARCHIVE_MALFORMED`. Each refusal is answered before anything is written. The value is stored on the row (Terms), answered by R6 and by every read of the row (R24, R43), and passed to the fetch unchanged (R15); conduct (R14) does not read it. A request answered `already: true` (R6) keeps the stored row's value.

**drainPending() → count; drainIntervalMs() → milliseconds** (for `scheduler`'s `capture-request-drain`, K90 (3))
- **R37** `drainPending()` answers the number of `requested` rows, and 0 whenever unattended capture is not configured (R11), so an idle or unconfigured instance holds no alarm. `drainIntervalMs()` answers the cadence between drain ticks while work waits: 60,000 by default, or the instance binding `CAPTURE_REQUEST_TICK_MS` when it reads as a number ≥ 0. Both are synchronous, write nothing and never throw.

**captureRequests({run, target, state, limit, viewer}) → `{count, limit, truncated, requests[]}`** The read (`op=capturerequests`).
- **R23** Rows are those whose target the viewer can see (membership's predicate; an absent or unrecognised stamp sees none), filtered by `run`, `target`, `state` when given, oldest first. `limit` defaults to 200 and is clamped to 1–1,000; `limit` in the answer is the bound applied, `count` the rows returned and `truncated` whether more matched. Nothing says how many rows the gate withheld.
- **R24** Each row answers the request's fields (Terms, less the principals), `render` as a boolean, and `attribution` (R10's composer, its refusal when a principal is missing).
- **R25** Each row answers `render_deferral`: for a render row `expired`, or `requested` with a code, `{state: "expired" | "deferred", content: "undetermined", code, check, translation}` with check and translation from the family that minted the code (C-83, else C-28, else null); otherwise null.

**For later modules** (reached through this module's factory, K61):
- **R26** `completed({viewer, limit})` answers `captured` rows the viewer can see by target; `leads({viewer, limit})` answers `captured` rows with a `lead_inquiry` the viewer can see, by `lead_inquiry`; `rendersHeld({viewer, limit})` answers render rows `expired`, or `requested` with a code, by target. Each is bounded as R23 and says when it was cut. (The queue's `capture-completed-unattended`, `out-of-inquiry-lead` and `render-deferred` producers.)
- **R27** Terminal rows do not accumulate unboundedly in what R26 walks.
- **R28** `bundlesOf(request)` answers the request's `target` and `lead_inquiry` (either absent when not set), or `null` for an unknown id (observation-log's authority-to-bundle resolution for a look this module wrote).
- **R29** `waits({run, now})` answers the run's outstanding requests (`requested` or `draining`, not past `expires`) and its completions not yet told to it (`captured`, `refused` and `expired` with `run_woken_at` null), each bounded by 25; `markWoken({requests, at})` stamps `run_woken_at`. A run's wait is bounded by its requests' own expiry.
- **R43** (N169) `requestById({request, viewer})` answers one request as R24 answers a row (with R25's `render_deferral`), when its target is one the viewer can see. It answers null for a blank id, an unknown one or an unseen one alike. It is one read by key, writes nothing and never throws. (Read by `intent`'s `pursuitOf`, its R14.)
- **R44** (N223, K259) `onRequestFiled(module, fn)`: a later module (`scheduler`, its R9) registers once at start; a malformed registration, or a second by the same module, is refused through `membership`'s `listenerRefusal` (its R81: `LISTENER_MALFORMED`, `LISTENER_DECLARED`), and the listeners run in the modules' total order (`membership`'s `MODULE_ORDER`, R83). After each `captureRequest` that writes a row (R6), every listener is called once with `{request, run, expires}`. A request answered `already: true` notifies nobody. A listener that throws or rejects never changes the row or the answer.
- **R48** (N587; DEC-141, K1618, K1645) This module registers, once at start, `capture`'s `captured-for` reader (`capture.registerReader("captured-for", "capture-requests", fn)`, capture R83), the seam by which a held document's question and asker reach `capture`, which is earlier in the order. For `{document, captures, viewer}` (capture R83: the bundle id and its capture digests, K1770), `fn` answers synchronously, from the requests whose filed capture (`capture_sha`, R15, R39) is one of `captures`: one entry per (`target`, `principal_plane`), `question` the request's `target` (R3) and `asker` its `principal_plane` (R8; the member who asked, never `principal_claude`); `visible` whether `viewer` may see the target, by the same sight as R23; `title` the target inquiry's title when visible; `title` and `asker` null when not; `waiting` true when the target inquiry is open. A document no request was filed into answers `questions: []`. It writes nothing; a failure inside it answers nothing (capture R83), never a partial list. `lead_inquiry` is not a question the document was captured for. (T50; N1117; K3627; capture R83, R94; amends R48) The registered `fn({document, captures, viewer, asks})` also answers one entry for each (question, asker) of `asks` (capture R94's `captured_for` rows for those captures), by the same sight, title, `asker` and `waiting` rules as a request's, a pair that is both a request's and an ask's answered once. Test: a capture recorded for a question by `acquire`'s `for`, with no request, is answered for a viewer who may see the question with its title and asker, and for one who may not as `withheld` with no id, title or count. Control: with `asks` empty the answer is as before.

**Credentials and retry** (K103 (3); SOURCE-ACCESS DEC-47 extended beyond publicly available documents)
- **R41** A member who can see a request's target may supply what a retry needs: login credentials, a user-agent setting, or another authorisation, declaring it the member's own, the project's or the group's (its **scope**). It is held by `capture-sources` (its R55–R58: supply, the read for a fetch, withdrawal and the listing; K109 (3)). Credentials are secrets: held encrypted, never shown back, never exported, and used only for fetches made under their declared scope (a member's own for requests of runs that member is the plane principal of; a project's for requests whose target inquiry is in that project; the group's for any request). A capture fetched with them records in its provenance that it was, whose they were and at which scope, and is marked not reproducible by the public.
- **R42** `captureRequestRetry({request}, {viewer, caller})` (`op=capturerequestretry`; admin and member with `contribute`, mutating): a `refused` request whose reason (R40) is the source's, and whose target the viewer can see, returns to `requested` with `attempts` kept, `expires` now + 24 h and its last code and reason kept, and the drain judges it again under R14, fetching with what R41 holds for its scope. Any other request is refused `CAPTURE_REQUEST_NOT_RETRYABLE` (a C-28 row allocated with the job) and nothing is written. Never throws.

**Personal sites and login-gated platforms** (T33-51; K1492 (2), (4); K1449: never unattended)
- **R46** `captureRequest` takes an optional `site_kind`, the requesting run's judgement of the address under its skill's doctrine: `personal` (a private individual's own site) or `platform` (a login-gated platform, such as a social or professional network, on which a person's page sits). A request with either is refused `MEMBER_CAPTURE_ONLY` before anything is written, naming the kind and routing it to the members: the refusal carries `route: "member"` and a sentence saying that such a page is captured only by a member's own act in their own browser (`capture`'s member capture), never by the daemon. Any other `site_kind` is `CAPTURE_REQUEST_SITE_KIND_UNKNOWN`. At the drain, a source whose refusal reason is `login` (R40) on a host a member has marked a platform (R41's scope record, `kind: "platform"`) is not retried with a supplied login (R42 refuses `MEMBER_CAPTURE_ONLY`): it is routed to the member the same way. Nothing here collects from such a page automatically, deceives a platform or fetches against its terms (K1492 (4)).

**Records requests for a policy known only by citation** (T35; N646; POLICIES L2, PO3; K1724, K1740)

Terms. A **records request** is a row of this module's own records-request table, apart from the capture requests above: `request` (minted here, `RR-<instant>-<random>`, as R7 mints), `standard`, `citation` (who cited the standard and where, copied from the standard at the open), `addressee` (the standard's issuer as `standards` holds it), `opened_by`, `opened_at`, `state` (`open` or `answered`), `answer`, `answer_capture`, `answer_note`, `answered_by`, `answered_at`. It carries no address, is never drained and fetches nothing: R11–R22 take only capture requests, and nothing here sends the request to anyone. Every refusal is R1's answer shape, its code with a row.
- **R51** `recordsRequestOpen({standard, by}, {viewer})` (`op=recordsrequestopen`; admin and member with `contribute`, mutating, carrying the viewer stamp and `by` the control plane's stamp) records that the group is asking the issuer of a policy held only by citation for its text. Refusals in order, each writing nothing: a machine or empty `by` `MACHINE_CANNOT_REQUEST_RECORDS` (only a member opens one); a standard absent or that the viewer may not see `standards`' `noSuchStandard` answer (one answer); a standard whose `held` (T35-31) is not `cited` `RECORDS_REQUEST_NOT_CITED`, naming its `held`. With an `open` request for that standard, it answers that row with `already: true` and writes nothing; otherwise it writes one row `open` and answers it with `already: false`.
- **R52** `recordsRequestAnswer({request, answer, capture?, note?, by}, {viewer})` (`op=recordsrequestanswer`; R51's classes) records the issuer's answer, once, by a member's act. `answer` is `produced` (the issuer produced the text: `capture` names a capture the record holds and the viewer may see, else `RECORDS_ANSWER_NO_CAPTURE`), `none_exists` (the issuer says no such record exists), `withheld` (the issuer declined it: `note`, its stated ground in at most 1,000 characters, required, else `RECORDS_ANSWER_NO_GROUND`) or `no_answer` (none came by the time the member records it). Refusals in order, each writing nothing: `MACHINE_CANNOT_REQUEST_RECORDS`; `NO_SUCH_RECORDS_REQUEST` (absent, or its standard unseen by the viewer, one answer); `RECORDS_REQUEST_ANSWERED` (an answer is held; it is never replaced); `RECORDS_ANSWER_UNKNOWN` (naming the four); then the two above. It moves the row to `answered` and changes nothing else: what the standard then holds (its text, or a "not found" entry whose search is this request and its answer) is the member's own act in `standards`.
