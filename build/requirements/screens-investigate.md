**Status** · In force; R67–R93 not yet met (T53). Requirement lines only. Consolidated under the 70,000-byte cap (job screens-investigate, T53): the change history, Size, Uses, Invariants, Satisfies, Suggestions and each requirement's sources are in `docs/requirements-notes/screens-investigate.md`; superseded clauses were folded into the requirement that superseded them. No id moves and no mark changes.

## Public

### Purpose

The investigation's tools beside a question: a person the record follows and the connections from them, a project's timeline, a money trail, a calculation and its spot-check, a court or regulatory proceeding, how a due date was worked out, a standard the city set itself, and a member's own ties. Pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

**`SCREENS`** (the screen contract, `interface-shell` R21; each sentence read by key)

**person** (People › Person)
- **R1** Reads `person` (positions with their dates and sources, career, credentials, memberships, interests, statements, each with its grade), `samepersoncandidates`, and the identity claims as `person` answers them. Acts: `recordpersonfact` (a fact read from a passage the member chooses, citing it); `identityclaim` (with its basis); `withdrawidentityclaim` (with a reason); `followregister` (a register the person appears in, `following`); `personexpunge` "Remove it for good": the dialog states `op-grades`' consequence statement (its R21, filled with the date and the member), takes the reason naming the law or order that requires it, and is drawn with the Irreversible weight; at the phone width it is read, not done (`interface-shell` R11). Each position and fact is a reference to its source passage; "Explore connections" opens `explore` from this person. Each `removed` row is drawn as "Removed where the law requires, {date}, by {member}" where the fact stood; an office's page offers recording who holds it (`recordline` kind `holds`, an optional end) and stating a holder current through a day (`linecurrentthrough`), each with the reason the shell asks.

**explore** (People › Person › Explore connections)
- **R2** The member ticks what to follow (the kinds `VOCABULARIES.connection_kinds` names, by their words, grouped as R24 and R27), an optional end, and the date it is explored as of (never "now"); `explore` answers the chains, each drawn hop by hop, each hop a reference with its cited source, and the chain's grade the weakest hop's (`mark.scale.connection.*`); a step declared and not evidenced is a lead, never a finding; a walk stopped at its depth says so (`mark.gap.undetermined`). Acts: `explore`; `explorepreset` (by its name); `exploreverify` "Check this chain"; R17's step source; `promote` "Open a question from this chain" (a new inquiry naming the chain, opening `question`). No score, rank or "most connected" is drawn.

**timeline** (Projects › <project> › Timeline)
- **R3** Reads `timeline` over the project's set, drawn in two lanes, "What they did" and "What we did", each event with its date as precise as its source states it (a day, or a month marked "month known"), who decided or took part, and its grade; events whose order is undetermined are drawn with `mark.gap.undetermined` and the evidence that would order them, never guessed into order; undated events are listed apart. Acts: `createevent` (from a passage); `addparticipant`; `relate` (link events, and cite which came first); `recorddatedfact` (an exact date from a passage); `hypothesishold` (a suspected cause as a hunch, `mark.hunch`, never evidence); `findin` (opening `find-in` over the project). An event recorded on `timeline` is sent with its attestation alone and found again by the project's captures (events R53); no list is kept in the page.

**money** (Projects › <project> › Money trail)
- **R4** Reads the trail (`moneyset`) and its figures (`moneyof`), each a row (`interface-shell` R19) with its kind, from, to, when it moved (its tied event, never its accounting period), stage, period, amount, source grade, and whether it is included with its reason; a value its source does not state is drawn `mark.gap.undetermined` and opens the row's panel at that item with the evidence that would help; a filter keeps only rows with something undetermined. Each row's panel offers, by its kind: `reconcile` (a budgeted figure beside a paid one, naming every way they differ, nothing merged), `authoritychain`, `include` and `exclude` (with a reason), `gradenote`, `acquire` (its source's address), `findin`, `recordfact` (from another source), `actioncreate` (opening `request`), `createevent` (when it moved), `checkrequest` (a member with declared budget expertise), `hypothesishold`. The screen's own acts: `recordfact`, `createset`, `committedagainstpaid`. Picked rows bring the hold strip with `include`, `exclude` (one reason for all), `reconcile` (exactly two picked) and `checkrequest`. Two sources that disagree are both drawn, in tension, never one picked. A project's money page lists its trails by `moneysets` (money R28), newest first, each linked to its page; none says `screen.money.notrail`.

**calculation** (Projects › <project> › Calculation)
- **R5** Reads `calculation` (the computed fact, its method as written, its inputs with their grades, its checks) and, for a spot-check, `spotcheck` (the draw: the set, its size, the seed and method and that anyone can repeat it; the question; each drawn item with its visits and standing; the counts and the estimate with its exact range). Acts: `calculationcreate`; `tabledeclare`; `recordcheck` (a second member's check); `calculationaccept`; `addworkbook` and `bind` (an input tied to the record); `calculationdraw` (how many, the seed recorded); and, as two acts (R14), the member's testimony through `testify` (her words, a photo optional) and `spotcheckvisit` tying it to the drawn item with her finding (`yes`, `no`, `could_not_tell`; `calculations` R38). A disagreeing item's visits are drawn in tension, never one picked. "How spot-checks work" opens the `help` page.

**proceeding** (Projects › <project> › Proceeding)
- **R6** Reads the proceeding (`entity`, its caption, court and neutral label), its parties by role (`partiesof`), its register's entries as followed (`follows`, with the newest marked new), and the orders held as obligations (`dutiesof`), each with its due date. Acts: `entitycreate` (from its caption: court, number, a neutral label); `recordline` (a party by role); `followregister` (a register needing a login says new filings come only by a member's own capture); `declare` (an order held as obligations); `courtlink` (a decision linked to what it interprets). Procedural facts are drawn as facts, never as advice on what to file.

**due-date** (Queue › Due date)
- **R7** Reads the action's clock entry (`action`) and its standing proposals (`clockproposals`), and draws how the date was worked out: its basis (the law the profile names, cited), the day received, the days counted, each holiday or closed day skipped with its source, and the date and time it falls due, in the action's zone. Acts: `clockpropose` (a proposal, never the date until confirmed); `clockadopt` (only on a standing proposal, naming it); `reminderset`. The calendar download fetches `clocksics` for the entry by `cs.download`.

**standard** (Projects › <project> › Standard)
- **R8** Reads the standard (`standard`: its words as written, its source passage, its issuer, kind and when it is in force, and the plane's `says`, standards R45) and its relations to laws (`lawrelations`), and the project's proposed standards (R19). Acts: `standarddeclare` (from a passage); `standardadopt` (a proposal, by the member's own act); `lawrelate`. The kind choice offers every kind `standards` accepts (`court` included); a related law is named by its citation, with words for its direction; "Who set it" chooses among the registered bodies (sending the `ENT-` id) or takes a typed name; a fixed-list choice opens with nothing chosen and a press without one refuses in place; every act sends the standard's own id (the subject before any `/`); a member in a `why` is drawn by name.

**ties** (Settings › Your ties)
- **R9** Reads `memberties` (the member's own ties, seen only by her and the administrators), drawing why she is asked and who sees the list. Acts: `declaretie` (its kind, who, and whether a case discloses it naming her by her handle or "a member of the group", the disclosure stated either way); `withdrawtie` (from its row, with a reason).

**Common**
- **R10** `interface-shell` R8–R13 and R16 hold on every screen; every grade is drawn as one mark (the scale's hue, icon and letter) explaining itself; after an act, the screen re-reads what it changed and draws it. Every item the record holds that a screen names (a person, an event, a figure, a document, a proceeding, an order) is a reference with its card.
