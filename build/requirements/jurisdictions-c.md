## Public

- **R121** (T53; N1257; K5458, K5462; amends R99; `record-core` R26, R92) **One function resolves a stored name, for every module.** `nameResolve({section, name, layer?, asking?, layers})` (`section` one of `records_laws`, `deadlines`, `closures`, `holidays`, `counterparties`; `asking` the layer the caller is reading from, if any; `layers` the active layer set, which the caller passes: this function is pure, so it reads no setting and takes no edge from this layer to a higher one) answers `{ok: true, layer, entry}`, or `{ok: false, reason: "NAME_UNKNOWN"}`, or `{ok: false, reason: "NAME_AMBIGUOUS", layers: [{layer, level, place}]}`. With `layer` given, it answers that layer's entry or `NAME_UNKNOWN`. With a bare `name` and no `layer`, R99's order applies (the asking layer's own, then its branch, then the one active layer that gives it, else `NAME_AMBIGUOUS`), over `layers`. For a record with no `layer`, the caller passes the layers that were active at the record's own recorded date, which it reads as `record-core` R92's `getSettingAt('jurisdiction_profiles', at)` and expands (a held package id into its layers, R114); a record with neither `layer` nor a date is read over today's active layers. No module copies this order. Test: asked over the four layers of `oakland-alameda` (the set a caller passes for a day when only those were active), with a second city layer giving the same law name added later, the name resolves to the Oakland layer's law; the same bare name asked over today's layers is `NAME_AMBIGUOUS` naming both. Control: with one layer giving the name, the bare name resolves and `layer` is not asked.

- **R115** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; DEC-151 (2); K5452; row 15; amends R2, R44) **The team layer: one layer, placeless, holding the team's rules and its per-government facts.** A team holds exactly one layer of its own: `layer: {origin: "team"}`, id `team`, with no `level` and no `place`, `within` the federal layer (R101). It is THE one team layer: it holds the team's rules (`local-facts` R24) and the facts the team writes about governments. An entry of a section of R116 about a place carries `government: {level, place}` (`level` `county` or `city`; a team's own state layer is not ruled, DEC-404 B), so a team's work in any set of places, wherever the team is (K5452), is entries of this one layer, never several layers. A government's entries are read for that government's rules (the branch whose Civicsmith layer has the same level and place, else alone); entries about two governments never conflict. It is held in the team's record by `local-facts` (`team_layers`, its R30) and handed to `combine` with the Civicsmith layers; `instance-setup` holds none (its setting records only the choice of Civicsmith layers). Every entry carries `basis` of the form `team:<capture sha>` (the held capture it was read from) and `status: researched`; an entry without a source is not written (R44); a second member confirms it (`local-facts` R23). The module marks every fact it gives from the layer `origin: team`. **Exception: a stated fiscal year (R124) needs neither that basis nor that confirmation.** Test: entries about two counties and a city validate in the one layer and combine under a California layer; a second layer of `origin: team` is `LAYER_TEAM_TWICE`; an entry with no source is `BASIS_MISSING`; an entry with `government.level` `state` is `LAYER_INVALID`. Control: a team layer carrying `level` or `place` is `LAYER_INVALID`; a stated fiscal year with `how` and no basis validates.

- **R116** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; DEC-151 (2); amends R83) **What a team may write.** `TEAM_LAYER_SECTIONS` is exported, frozen: `records_laws` (with `stages`, `requesters`, `response`, `governs`), `deadlines`, `holidays` (with closure lists), `counterparties` (with `hours`), `action_kinds` (with `venue`: where requests go), `systems` (where records are published), `time_zone`, `fiscal_year` (R124), `weekend`. These are the matters DEC-151 (2) lists (records and open-meeting laws and their deadlines, holidays, office hours and time zone, offices, where records are published) and Bob's DEC-458 A2. Any other section in the team layer is `TEAM_SECTION_REFUSED`. A new section is a requirement change. Test: a team layer carrying `vocabulary` is refused. Control: each listed section validates.

- **R117** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; amends R12, R15) **A team entry never replaces a shipped one.** `combine` keeps the team layer's value for a key a Civicsmith layer also gives only when they are equal; otherwise it is R102's conflict (withheld). The team is told at entry (`local-facts` R23, `LAYER_ENTRY_IN_SHIPPED`), so the conflict arises only after an update brings a value for a key the team had written. Test: a team deadline `immediate_disclosure` with 5 days beside a shipped layer giving 3 is a conflict naming `origin`. Control: a team key no shipped layer gives is kept.

- **R123** (T53; N1257; DEC-458 A2; K5456; amends R49, R55) **A layer states the fiscal year of the one government it stands for, only where the whole government has one.** `fiscal_year` (R49) is one value per `body` of one government: an entry with `body` `*` is the government's own; a federal layer states the federal year, a state layer the state's, as `time_zone` is stated only where the whole state has one. It is addressed by layer and body and never conflicts across governments (R102). Where no layer and no stated year (R124) gives a government's year it is undetermined: no consumer offers a fiscal-year view for it and no July to June (or any) default is ever assumed. Two givers of one government's year with equal `start`, `named_by` and `label` agree whatever their citation or basis (as R103 does for the weekend; the check is made in F1 of the main file). Test: two city layers each with a `*` year of its own give no conflict; a government with none reads undetermined; equal values with different citations give one year and no conflict. Control: two different years for one government in one branch give R102's conflict.

- **R124** (T53; N1257; DEC-458 A2; K5456; row 15; amends R49, R115; `local-facts` R21) **A member may state a government's fiscal year, with how they checked it, as the team's time zone is stated.** Where the place's records (a layer) give none, a member states the year of a government in the team layer: `fiscal_year` with `government` (R115), `start`, `named_by` and `label` (R49), and `how` (1 to 500 characters saying how the member checked: the official page at an address, a call to the office), the member and the date, by `factConfirm` `correct` as `local-facts` R21 does for the zone. **R115's basis (`team:<capture sha>`) and `status: researched`, and `local-facts` R23's second-member confirmation, do not apply to it**; a machine cannot state it (`MACHINE_CANNOT_CONFIRM`). Refusals and the records-first rule: R134 and `local-facts` R31. It governs for the named government only. Test: a member states a city's year with `how`: it governs for that city and for no other government, with no second member; one without `how` is refused. Control: a stated year beside a layer's equal year is no conflict.

- **R125** (N858 R99; T53; N858; Action v0.2 rule 11; K2781 (6), K3935; B1; amends R80, R40) **A procedure guide carries the same attribution a template does,** in whichever layer it sits (guides are keyed by kind and layer, `reading-guides` R17). A profile guide has no opt-out and no team: the opt-out of K5508 is for a team's own guide only (`reading-guides` R20), and nothing a team does changes a profile guide's attribution (K5527 (3)). Each `procedure_guides` entry (R80) carries, beside its `kind`, `items`, `status` and `basis`, `notes` (text, possibly empty, as R40's), the optional `machine_draft` (boolean, R138), `authored_by` (a non-empty name), `reviews` (R40's shape, `reviewed_text_sha` included: `[{reviewer, kind, organisation?, credential?, scope, outcome, at, reviewed_text_sha?}]`), `approved_by` (a non-empty name different from `authored_by`) and `approved_at` (`YYYY-MM-DD`); its `basis` is a ruling `K<n>`, or `TEST` in a test profile, as R40 says of a template's (R80 states no form for it till now). No guide-level `sources` field is added: the sources are the items' `citation`s (R80), at least one item being required. A guide missing a field is `GUIDE_UNATTRIBUTED` naming the first missing field (the form of R40's `TEMPLATE_UNATTRIBUTED`); a value of the wrong form, or an approver equal to the author, is `VALUE_INVALID` naming the field, as R40 does for a template; a basis that is not a ruling is `BASIS_INVALID`. The review the guide needs to be offered is judged by `reading-guides` R19, not here. Test: a guide with all fields passes; one with no `approved_by`, or with `approved_by` equal to `authored_by`, is refused naming the field; a guide in the test profile with basis `TEST` passes (the test profile's existing guide, R84, gains the fields); a guide carrying `review_opt_out` or `not_reviewed` is `VALUE_INVALID` naming the field. Control: R80's other rules and a profile with no `procedure_guides` (the Oakland profile today) are unchanged.

- **R126** (N858 R100; T53; N858; v0.2 rule 11; K2781 (6); amends `filing-templates` R15, R35) **`attributionLacks(entry)` and `textsLack(profile)` list what each text lacks.** Both pure, exported by `jurisdictions` (layer 1, which `filing-templates` and `reading-guides` both use; the judge cannot sit in either of those two, since `jurisdictions` may use neither and `reading-guides` (layer 6) may not use `filing-templates` (layer 9)), never throwing. `attributionLacks(entry)` answers the subset of `writer`, `sources`, `professional_review`, `second_approval`, in that order (the words `filings` R65 uses; `filing-templates` maps nothing, K5528 T4), by the rules `filing-templates` R15 applies today in its private `#lacks`, with these: `writer` when `authored_by` is empty or a machine; `sources` when no source has a `citation` (for a guide, when it has no item); `professional_review` unless the entry holds a **counted review**: a review of `kind: professional`, `outcome: no_concerns`, a non-empty `reviewer` who is not a machine, a non-empty `organisation` and `credential`, a real `at`, and a `reviewed_text_sha` equal to the SHA-256 of the entry's text now (a template's `text` as UTF-8; a guide's `items` in `record-grammar`'s `canonicalJson`, route, expect, citation and after included, K5528 T2), so a changed text lacks `professional_review` until a new review is recorded (no new word); `second_approval` when `approved_by` is empty or equals the writer. "Machine" is judged by `record-grammar`'s `isMachineIdentity` and the hash by its `sha256HexSync`, both imported directly (`record-grammar` is already in this module's uses; K5530 F10). `countedReview(entry)` is exported beside the two functions and answers `{reviewer, organisation, credential, at}` of the latest counted review, or `null`; R137's answer key `reviewed` (renamed from `reviewed_by` so a team library guide's string `reviewed_by` keeps its meaning, K5528 T5) is built from it. `filing-templates` R15, R35 and `reading-guides` R19 call it, so there is one copy. `textsLack(profile)` (a layer is a profile) answers, for every template (R81, and R25's single `template` read as one of purpose `initial`) and every guide (R80) of the profile, `{kind, purpose|guide, lacks}` with a non-empty `lacks`; a profile with no text answers `[]`. The profile's tests print it and do not fail on it (a text lacking a part is allowed in data and never offered); the acceptance test (op-declarations R84) fails on a text offered that lacks one. Test: a profile with four texts lacking, respectively, the writer, the sources, the professional review and the second approver answers four entries with their one `lacks` each; a complete text answers none; `filing-templates` R35's `unofferedFor` answers the same words for the same template. Test also: the fixtures complete (none lacking), no `organisation`, no `credential`, a hash of other text, a machine reviewer and a machine `authored_by` each answer their one lack; `countedReview` answers the later of two counted reviews. Control: an empty profile answers `[]`; a text with no `reviewed_text_sha` on a `member` review is unchanged.

- **R127** (N1259-a R99; T53; N1259; K4403 (2), (5), (6); K1485, K1492; amends R4, R57) **A profile may hold `registers`: the definitions the file reader and the listing use.** `registers`: `[{key, label, publisher, system, url, pieces, delimiter?, header_from?, columns, row_key, row_key_basis, schemes?, use_limit?, terms_basis, gate, phase, listing, refresh?, count?, basis}]`. `key` is `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]*$` and unique. `label` and `publisher` are plain text (the listing's name and source). `system` is an R4 `origin`; `url` is the register's own public https page (the listing's URL) and its host is one of that system's `hosts`. `pieces` is a non-empty (but for `phase: beta` and `status: not_measured`, R133) `[{key, label, locator, member?, delimiter?, header_from?}]`: `locator` an https address, or a template with the named holes `{cycle}` or `{table}`; `member` the name of a zip member; `delimiter` one of `comma`, `tab`, `pipe` (a piece's overrides the register's); `header_from` absent (the first row is the header) or the `key` of another piece (a separate header file). `columns` is `[{name, role, keep, date_format?, currency?}]`; `role` is one of `name`, `organization`, `amount`, `date`, `filer_id`, `form`, `licence_no`, `docket_no`, `company_no`, `city`, `state`, `zip5`, `home_address`, `phone`, `other`; `keep` is a boolean. `row_key` is a non-empty list of column names; `row_key_basis` is `source` (the columns are the source's own key) or `content` (no source key: the key is the kept cells plus their occurrence number). `gate` is `none`, `account`, `fee` or `people_search` (always refused, R128); `status` is absent or `not_measured` (R133). `phase` is `alpha` or `beta`. `schemes` is a list of R52 scheme names the register issues. Malformed: `REGISTER_INVALID` (new), naming field and register. `SECTIONS` (`index.mjs:19`) gains `registers`, `use_limits`, `query_templates`; R4's allowed `systems` fields (`index.mjs:415`) gain R132's; `combine` unions `registers` by `key` (R14, R15). Test: a complete fixture register is accepted; each field absent, of the wrong type or outside its list is refused naming it; a duplicate key is refused; a `url` whose host is not among its system's hosts is `REGISTER_INVALID`. Control: a profile with no `registers` is valid and lists none.

- **R128** (N1259-a R100; T53; N1259; K1485 row 9, K4403 (5), DEC-408; K1492 (2), (3); amends R127) **A definition that would keep contact data, hide its terms, or reach a paid people-search is refused.** Exported `validateRegisterDefinition(definition, view)` answers `{ok: true, definition}` or `{ok: false, code, field, key}` and never throws. It refuses: `REGISTER_KEEPS_CONTACT` for any column whose `role` is `home_address` or `phone` with `keep` other than false; `REGISTER_NO_TERMS` for an absent or empty `terms_basis` (the source's stated terms, or "none stated" with the date read); `REGISTER_NO_USE_LIMIT` for a register whose `system` carries a `use_limit` (R131) and which does not name it; `REGISTER_PEOPLE_SEARCH` for a `gate` of `people_search` (a paid people-search is a member's own act on their own account, K1492 (3); results enter as cited sources, never as a register); `REGISTER_ROW_KEY_INVALID` for a `row_key` naming a column the definition does not list, or `row_key_basis: source` with a `row_key` of columns whose role is `other` only. A column the file holds and the definition does not list is not an error: the reader drops it (`register-readers` R3). The same function validates a team's own definition (`registers` R7): one rule. Test: a definition keeping a `phone` column, one with no `terms_basis`, one of an FEC-flagged system without its `use_limit`, one with `gate: people_search` and one with a `row_key` naming an unlisted column are each refused by name; every shipped definition passes. Control: the same definitions with the fault removed pass.

- **R129** (N1259-a R101; T53; N1259; K4403 (2); amends R127) **The listing facts each carry their basis.** `listing`: `{bytes, rows, source_date?, measured_at}`; `bytes` and `rows` are each `{value, basis}` with `basis` one of `stated` (the source says it), `counted` (this product counted it), `estimated` (computed, with the method in `how`) or `not_stated` (`value` null); `measured_at` is the civil date the figures were taken (`basis` is R2's form, a dated entry such as `2026-10-21 M-R1`; a bare `M-R1` is no basis). A figure with a basis other than `not_stated` and no `measured_at` is `REGISTER_INVALID`. `count`: `{kind, template, supports}` optional: `kind` `source_count_query` (the source can state the number of rows for a filter, e.g. a SoQL `count(*)`), `template` an https address template, `supports` the filter criteria (`register-readers` R6) the source's query can apply. `refresh`: `{cadence_hours, validators, append_only?}` optional: the least interval between looks, which of `etag`, `last_modified`, `content_length`, `count` tell the source has not changed, and `append_only` true when the source never changes or removes a row. Test: a figure with a basis and no date, and a `count` naming a criterion the filter lacks, are refused; a `refresh` with no validators means "always fetch". Control: `not_stated` with a null value is valid.

- **R130** (N1259-a R102; T53; N1259; K1484, K1492; amends R52) **Identifier schemes for filers, licences, dockets and companies, and the query that composes a register's own address from a number.** `identifier_schemes` (R52) may also hold the schemes `filer` (FPPC or NetFile filer id), `committee` (FEC committee id), `licence` (board plus number), `docket` (CourtListener docket id and a court's docket number) and `company` (a company number), each as R52 requires (`scheme`, `label`, `entity_kinds`, `space`, `form`, `systems`, `basis`) plus `registers`, the keys that issue it. R3 has no space for committees, licences or companies: `filer` and `licence` take `person` (R3 names filer ids and licence numbers), `docket` `proceeding`, `committee` and `company` `institution` (K5530 F1). The `company` scheme's `registers` include the officers register (R133); its `form` is the state's company (entity) number as the state writes it, no spaces, so that no scheme is a name; each needs a form in that space, else `SCHEME_INVALID` (`index.mjs:1280-1281`), basis `UNMEASURED` where none was read (R2). `query_templates`: `[{scheme, register, template, basis}]`: `template` is an https address with exactly one hole `{value}`, in the path or one named query parameter, on a host among the register's system hosts; the address a watch reads is composed from the template and a value that matches the scheme's `form`, percent-encoded, never typed by the member. The officers lookup (`registers` R46, `acquisition` R84, R130) composes its address the same way: one `query_templates` entry `{scheme: company, register: <officers key>, template, basis}`, the `template` an https address on the host of the officers service's `systems` entry (R132) with exactly one hole `{value}` for the company number; `basis` is `UNMEASURED` until the service has been read (R2). A lookup by a person's name, an officer's name or a partial number has no template and no hole, and is `QUERY_TEMPLATE_NOT_NUMBER` at validation. A template with a hole for anything but a value of a listed scheme, two holes, or a hole in the host is `QUERY_TEMPLATE_INVALID`; a template naming a field `name`, `first`, `last` or `person` is `QUERY_TEMPLATE_NOT_NUMBER` (K1484: a watch is by a number). Malformed schemes stay `SCHEME_INVALID`. Test: a docket template composes the address for a fixture id and refuses a value not of the form; a `{name}` hole is `QUERY_TEMPLATE_NOT_NUMBER`; a hole in the host is refused. The test profile's officers template composes the address for a fixture company number and refuses a value not of the scheme's form and a value with a path separator (percent-encoded, never a second path); an `{officer}` hole and a hole in the host are refused as above. Control: a scheme with no template validates and cannot be watched; the same officers entry with its template removed validates and cannot be looked up (`registers` R46 answers `NO_QUERY_TEMPLATE`, nothing sent).

- **R131** (N1259-a R103; T53; N1259; K4403 (3); K1492 (5); 11 CFR 104.15; 52 U.S.C. 30111(a)(4)) **`use_limits`: the data-use limit of a source, as data.** `use_limits`: `[{key, citation, text_key, export, on_publish, basis}]`. `citation` is the rule as cited (for the first profile's federal campaign-finance limit: 11 CFR 104.15 and 52 U.S.C. 30111(a)(4)); `text_key` a `setup-words` key whose words state the limit (the notice a row carries, drawn by the design); `export` is `never` (no export of the register's rows as a list; the only value this release accepts); `on_publish` is `warn`, `refuse` or `allow` (what a case citing a row of such a register meets; counsel's answer sets it, K4403 (3); this release ships `warn`); `basis` the measurement or ruling (the rule's text is in `measures-T52-DEC406.md`). A `systems` entry (R4) may name a `use_limit` key; every register of that system inherits it and may not clear it. A limit with an `export` other than `never`, or an `on_publish` outside the three, is `USE_LIMIT_INVALID`. Test: the first profile's federal system names the limit and its registers inherit it; a register omitting it is `REGISTER_NO_USE_LIMIT`; `on_publish: maybe` is refused. Control: a limit-free register carries none.
