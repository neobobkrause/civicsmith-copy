# screens-publish — requirements

**Status** · In force. Consolidated under the cap by job screens-publish, attempt 4 (7 Nov 2026); each requirement's sources and the folds of amended text are in `docs/requirements-notes/screens-publish.md`, with the history, size, Satisfies and Suggestions.

## Public

### Purpose

From a case's draft to its public page, and what comes in from outside: preparing the case, sharing a review copy, the publication ceremony, the published case page, another group's case imported, the case's docket, and the doorbell's page for someone with no account. Pure: every read and act is an op, through `interface-shell`.

### Provides

**`SCREENS`** (the screen contract, `interface-shell` R21; each sentence read by key, R24)

**case-editor** (Projects › <project> › Case; the registry's 5 acts)
- **R1** Reads `casedrafts`, the draft's `casedocument`, its `casecheck` and `whatchangeddrafts`. Draws the ladder (Draft, Internally checked, Externally compliant, Distributed, each "not yet evaluated" until checked), the scope, what it leaves out with its acknowledgements, the people named each with its reason (or its lack), and what changed since the last edition. Acts: `casedraft` "Prepare the draft"; `whatchangedpropose` "Draft what changed" (only while the assistant is reachable; labelled machine work, `ai.label.untested` where the answer carries `untested`; kept only in the member's own words); `statementack` "Acknowledge what it leaves out", with the acknowledger's own reason (`case-inputs` R1, `STATEMENT_ACK_NO_REASON`); `attribute` "Name with the reason" (its consequence statement in the dialog, `op-grades` R4); `writinghelp` under the scope and "what changed" only (`interface-shell` R20), never a reason. The statement box is labelled `review.statement.title`; a never-prepared edition is no failed read.

**review-copy** (Projects › <project> › Review copy; the registry's 3 acts)
- **R2** Reads the draft's review grants and their comments (`reviewcomments`). Each named reader is a row with what they may read and until when (R27), offering `reviewrevoke` "Stop sharing". `reviewgrant` "Share with a named reader" (name, role, end date) carries `mark.outward.means` and answers the grant's secret once: the screen shows it once as the link `<origin>/app/review#grant=<secret>`, with "Civicsmith sends no email", for the member to hand over, and never keeps it (no storage, address or later request). `reviewcomment` "Reply" answers a reader's comment. A session that may not share gets no share boxes.

**ceremony** (Projects › <project> › Publish; the registry's 7 acts)
- **R3** Steps in order (What, Strength, Leaves out, People, Photos, Disclosures, Bias, Preview, Ties, Sign), the current one marked, with the required "Publication ceremony" wizard (`screens-wizards` R7). Reads `publishpreflight` (each finding against the bar, every refusal holding before signing, by its words), `publishtensions` (what will be disclosed: unresolved contradictions, the timeline frozen at signing), `photomarks` per photo, and `publishschedule` for an edition waiting to publish. Acts: `publishpreflight` "Check again"; `publishtensions` "See everything that will be disclosed".
- **R4** The Photos step shows each photo (relied on or supporting) with its marks; the member draws rectangles, marks each a person not in a finding, a number plate, or city staff at work (with a reason), and sends `obscuremark` with the areas, or "nothing to obscure" (`areas: []`); a mark is withdrawn by `obscuremarkwithdraw` with a reason, never erased. The step says the public copy has the areas obscured and labelled and no camera details, and every photo must be marked or "nothing to obscure" before signing; a photo whose format cannot be covered stops the signing, named by its refusal's words.
- **R5** The Ties step's "Confirm, and prepare Edition <n> for signing" sends `publish` with `tieAttested: true`; Sign is offered only after it answers. Test: `publish` carries `tieAttested`. Control: no Sign before it.

**published** (the public frame; the registry's 2 acts)
- **R6** Public frame, no session (`interface-shell` R30), `/app/published/<case>`: reads `publishedcase` (findings with their two strengths and bar standing, timeline, people named, evidence, when signed and published) and `publishededitions`; says whose work it is and how to read a case. Acts: `verify` "Check the signature"; `publishedcase` "Read the case". No account needed. A finding's claim and falsifier come from the signed `case_words`, else `body.conclusion` / `body.falsifies`, else the authored words, a leading heading being no words. It draws an unrated axis ("not graded yet"), "no bar declared" when `bar.declared` is false, the newer edition's What-changed beside an older edition (`successor`), the lens's `closing`, the case id, manifest hash and basis-version name in the header, and `verification.detail`; the imported screens name a case by `scope` and a finding by `title`, never by id.

**imported** (Projects › Imported › Another group's case; the registry's 4 acts)
- **R7** Reads `importedcases` and `importedcase` (each finding with whether it recreated, in part, or not), drawn in the imported band (read-only). Acts: `caseimport` "Import a case"; `importaccept` "Accept what recreated, with a reason"; `importflag` "Flag an issue"; `importwatch` "Watch the publisher's docket".

**docket** (Projects › <project> › Docket; the registry's 2 acts)
- **R8** Reads `docket` (editions, corrections, withdrawals, court orders; drafted or posted) and `docketprepare` for a draft entry. Acts: `docketfile` "File a docket entry" (with the member's reason for the record or public choice), sending `edition` (a ratified edition, latest first) and `capture` (a document the project's captures hold) beside the rest, nothing with either missing; `heldcaptures` and `publishedcase`'s `edition_index` give the choices; `screen.docket.capture.add` is a reference to `capture`; `docketpost` "Post it, signed" (her own key, R16). `writinghelp` on the summary, only while the assistant is reachable, sends `op: docketfile`, `field: summary` and what was typed. The document picker (R82) lists every held document, reading on from `next`, and says how many it lists where it cannot.

**doorbell** (the public frame; the registry's 1 act)
- **R9** Public frame, no session, `/app/doorbell`, headed by the group's public name (`groupidentity`): what happens to what is sent (only this group's Civicsmith gets it; a member reads it and decides; an optional secret phrase marks later material as the same unknown sender; nothing is beyond a court's reach), files, an optional note and an optional secret phrase. `knock` "Hand it over" sends them with no credential, with each file's `fileName` and `mediaType`, so the Inbox row and detail show them (`inboxlist` `file_name`, `media_type`); refusals (`KNOCK_EMPTY`, `KNOCK_PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE`, `KNOCK_ENVELOPE_TOO_LARGE`, the rest) by their words; the answer says it was received. The page never keeps the phrase. Test: through the real page, a JPEG `notice.jpg` gives an `inboxlist` row with `file_name` and `media_type` `image/jpeg`. Control: a note-only knock sends neither.
