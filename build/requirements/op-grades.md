## Public

### Purpose

Every op's grade, stated or absent: the rung ladder and each writing op's rung, or the one ground on which it has none; the consequence statements published beside a rung; the codes that back a `reasoned` rung; the ops refused to a machine by name; every other gated op's reason; the advisory phone set; and the alias table that gives an alias its op's grade. It holds data only. It decides no op's behaviour: each grade is read from its owner's requirements, and `affordances` checks that every op is graded and every rung backed (its R12, R19, R20).

### Provides

Terms. A **rung** is one of `RUNG_LADDER`. A **ground** is one of `RUNG_ABSENCE_GROUNDS`. An **act**, a **decorated act** and a **gate** are `affordances`' (its Terms). "The control plane's table" is the op table `affordances` R12 checks.
