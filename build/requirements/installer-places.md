**Custody and the release**

- **R21** The install offers the jurisdiction profiles held (`jurisdictions.list`, test profiles excluded), each by name and coverage, with none preselected. The chosen ids, in order, are bound as `JURISDICTION_PROFILES` for the copy to record at its first boot (instance-setup R13). Choosing none is allowed, and the page says what that means. An update never changes them. (T53; K5452: where this and R94 differ, R94 governs: the places are a set of any level, only the federal layer preselected.) *(not yet met: T53)*

- **R83** (fix round 7 G83; K4746, K4747, K4752; `installer-steps` R41, R42) **The team's room is created near the founder.** `POST /begin` reads the founder's request's `cf` (continent, longitude), carries the hint `installer-steps.locationHintFor` answers through the install's cookie to `/callback`, and the install hands it to the plane's upload; a request with no `cf`, or a `null` hint, installs exactly as today. #### T53 (plan-T53-draft.md, installer; N1265 "Check my account" and the gate, the places in layers, the levels, the region, the move)

- **R94** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; K5452, K5497; amends R21) **The install asks where the team's work is focused, a set of places, and preselects nothing below what the team names.** The page asks "where does your work focus?" (not where the team is, K5452) and offers the held places of every level as one list grouped by state: whole states, counties, cities, any number checked, each by name; a team may check a state alone (state-wide), several counties, one city, or places in two states. The federal layer is the only one preselected and may be removed, and the page says what that means; no state, county or city is preselected. The page tells the team that Civicsmith ships United States places only (K5497). A place not held, or a place outside the United States, is named in the optional free-text place (`instance-setup` R60), one name for each; a team whose work is outside the United States adds those offices and rules itself, as its own research (DEC-150); the level names are unchanged. The chosen ids, with the layers each sits within added and shown (`instance-setup` R107), in order, are bound as `JURISDICTION_PROFILES`, the same binding; `instance-setup` R13 records it. The page says that a team may add facts about its counties and cities, with their sources, to its team layer after install. An update never changes the choices (R21 kept). Test: choosing California alone binds the federal and California layer ids, in that order, and no county or city; choosing Alameda County and Oakland binds federal, California, Alameda County and Oakland; choosing nothing and removing federal binds none and the page says so; the page states that only United States places are shipped. Control: a test layer is not offered, and a fresh page has only the federal layer checked. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R95** (T53; K5438, K5444, K5454, K5570; PL29; `runtime-limits` PL20, PL25; `service-levels-draft.md` §3; amends R70) **The install offers three levels and sets the design stage and the declared level.** The install offers Team, Institution and Enterprise (Level 1, 2 and 3), each stating: what it is for, in a plain sentence; its measured range (members at once, record, upload) or "not yet measured"; the Cloudflare plan it needs; and what it costs the team a month by Cloudflare's published prices (Enterprise by quote). A level whose design stage is not yet built is shown and not selectable, "not yet available" (Institution until N1271 is built, and Enterprise, K5454, until a team wants an enterprise-scale copy); only a built level is selectable. The chosen level sets the design stage the copy is created at (Level 1: one room per team) and is recorded as the copy's declared level, which `runtime-limits`' `levelOf` answers (a Level 1 copy that later declares a Cloudflare plan with higher guarantees still answers 1, PL25). R70's preflight checks the plan the chosen level needs, and an account that lacks it is refused before anything is created, naming the plan. The page says that a copy can move up later and not down, and how (the upgrade step; the whole-record export and import, R97). Words, ranges and the sentences are UX-DESIGN's, through BOB. Test: an unbuilt level is listed unselectable; a built one sets the stage and the declared level; Enterprise chosen on an account without that plan is refused before the room exists, naming the plan; the words say "not yet measured" while the level's `measured` is null. Control: a copy that declares a plan with higher guarantees at Level 1 answers level 1. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R96** (T53; N1273; K5440, K5441, K5443; PL32; `runtime-limits` PL30; R83 names this as N1273's) **The install shows the region the copy will use, and offers two honest choices.** The install page shows the region the copy will use and offers: **near me** (a location hint; `installer-steps` `locationHintFor` chooses it by default, a list lets the founder pick another) and **keep my data in this region** (the jurisdictions EU, US, and FedRAMP only where the account has it). Before the choice the page states that the choice cannot be changed later; that moving later is an export and an import (K5441); what "near me" is (faster for people near the place, not a guarantee where the data is) and what "keep my data in this region" is (the records and the stored files are stored there; it does not make everything that runs on the copy run there). Choosing sets `STORE_LOCATION` or `STORE_JURISDICTION`, never both, restated in every upload (`installer-steps` R46). Screen needs (the two-way choice, the lists, the sentence before the choice) are UX-DESIGN's through BOB. Test: an install carries its default hint; a picked region overrides it; a jurisdiction sets the jurisdiction and no hint; a missing `cf` installs as today (no hint). Control: the page states, before the choice, that it cannot be changed. This is the placement `screens-settings` R88 states afterwards. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R97** (T53; K5441, K5443, K5570 (2); PL54; `corpus-export` PL50 to PL53; `installer-steps` R48) **A new copy can be created from a whole-record export.** The install takes the export's manifest and parts (from where the administrator names them, by a link or a file; the parts check themselves) and the region or jurisdiction the founder chooses for the new copy (R96); it creates the room and, with a jurisdiction, the R2 bucket in it first (`installer-steps` R47), and then imports (`installer-steps` R48). The page offers the path ("move my copy") with the progress by part, resume, and what was not carried. Screen needs (the move path, "what was not carried", the sentence before choosing a region) are UX-DESIGN's through BOB. Test: an import into a fresh install with `eu` creates the bucket with `eu`; a missing part leaves the install paused and resumable, not partly done and live. Control: an install that names no export installs exactly as R10 describes. *(not yet met: T53)*
- **R98** (T53; K5441, K5443; PL56, PL57) **The new copy states what it received and what it did not; the old copy is untouched.** The new copy states what it received and what it did not (the export's `not_carried` list, in plain words: members' sign-in material is not carried), the export's instant, and the old copy's placement; the old copy is untouched and stays the rollback until its administrator ends it, and the import never deletes it. Importing into a copy in a different region or jurisdiction than the export's is allowed and is the point; the new copy's placement is the founder's choice (fixed at install, no later change), and the install says this before asking for it. The old copy's "ended by the administrator" act is a screen need for UX-DESIGN through BOB. Test: the new copy's first screen reads the `not_carried` list; the old copy's record is equal before and after; an export from a hinted copy imported into a jurisdiction copy keeps the new placement. Control: a missing `not_carried` entry is drawn as unknown, never as carried. *(not yet met: T53)*
