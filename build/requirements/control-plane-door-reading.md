## Public

- **R72** (`capture` R86, R111, R113; `op-declarations` R45) The door routes `captureupload` to the store, refused to any caller not arriving by a member's session, `by` stamped from the session (`store-request` R7), never a value the caller sends, and the raw request body streamed on as `bytes`; it holds no arm's behaviour (the arm is `store-assembly` R87). `declared`, the request's length, is the door's word alone: the door hands `store-request` the request's `Content-Length` as `length` (R136), and `store-request` sets `declared` from it for `captureupload` and `captureuploadpart` when it is a whole number and removes any a caller sends (its R8). A file past the request-body ceiling is sent in parts by four more ops, `captureuploadbegin`, `captureuploadpart`, `captureuploadcomplete` and `captureuploadabort`, which the door routes likewise: `by` is stamped from the session, so `capture`'s member-session fence refuses any stamp that is not a member's (`MEMBER_SESSION_REQUIRED`, 403); begin, complete and abort take their fields from the request body, and `captureuploadpart` takes the part's raw body streamed and unread, with `upload`, `index` and `sha256` from the address. An uploaded archive is opened by the plane's `unpack` job when a document holds it: when `op=unpack` names no `project`, the door asks the record (`heldat`, by the archive's digest, as the administrator sees it; nothing is fetched) and files the archive's members under the project of the document that holds it, as it files a fetched archive's under the project the request names; while no document holds the archive, the plane's job (the `daemon` class) is answered `{ok: true, result: {ok: true, complete: false, waiting_on: {code: "ARCHIVE_NOT_FILED"}}}`, nothing is unpacked, and the job is offered again. Test: another member's upload, a machine stamp and an `ai` stamp are refused; a part over the part size is refused before a byte is read; an archive uploaded and not yet held by a document is answered `ARCHIVE_NOT_FILED`, and once a document holds it its members are filed under that document's project.

- **R100** (R085-4; K3477; WALK-085 F1; amends R72; `capture` R92, `extraction` R1) The door passes `captureupload`'s `type` field (the declared media type, as the screen sends it beside `statement` and `name`) to `capture.uploadCapture` as `type`. After an upload `capture` answers as new (not `existed`, not refused), the door registers its `reading` job row (R130) and reads the uploaded document inline only when R131's gate admits it, exactly as it reads an acquired one on `op=acquire` (`extraction.read` over the answer's document); an admitted read's answer carries `reading` and `text_units` as `op=acquire`'s answer does, so the promotion the capture screen sends with that document stores the reading (`extraction` R20), and any other upload is answered `reading_pending: true` with its row and no reading (fix round 9, G126). A failed reading leaves the upload standing and is answered as the failed reading. Test, through the door: a member uploads a two-line text file with `type` `text/plain`, then promotes it as the capture screen does; `op=reading` by its digest answers `found: true` with both lines' text in its units, and the original opens by the warned path (`op=openwithwarning`), a received file being graded by `file-safety` R6 and R8 as any upload is. Controls: the same upload with no `type` stands, its reading failed or undetermined; bytes already held (`existed: true`) are not read again; a file the gate refuses is answered `reading_pending` with no reading.

- **R105** (T50; T51: N1234, DEC-329 (5); N1117, N1194; K3369, K3627; `acquisition` R47 `BAD_FOR`, C-139.21; capture R83) **`acquire`'s `for` is judged for sight, and the door answers within a bound.** A `for` naming a question the member may not see (or that does not exist) is refused with `acquisition`'s `BAD_FOR` (one code, one sentence), the same answer for both, before any fetch, nothing written or filed; a `for` naming one she may see is accepted. A large page's `acquire` ends within the stated bound in a document or a named refusal, never a silent stop. The bound is 25 seconds: past it the door answers the refusal `ACQUIRE_TIMED_OUT` (its words are the design's key `refusal.ACQUIRE_TIMED_OUT` (DEC-329 (5)), which the interface draws for the code as it draws every refusal (`interface-shell` R13); the answer carries the code, the op and the status 504 and, as R99 does for `AGENT_WORKER_UNBOUND`, `detail` read by `word("refusal.ACQUIRE_TIMED_OUT")`, no sentence of this module's; T51), never a silent stop (K3770: the timed live `acquire` was refused by the session's safety check; BOB's release walk on biosmoke8 times one, and a bound too short or too long becomes an entry). Test: a `for` naming an unseen question answers `BAD_FOR` with no fetch made; a large fixture ends in a document or a named refusal inside the bound. Control: a `for` the member may see is accepted and recorded; a daemon call carrying `for` is ignored (acquisition R47). T51 test: the timed-out answer's `detail` equals `word("refusal.ACQUIRE_TIMED_OUT")`, and `hasWord` holds the key; control: no other op's answer is bounded or carries it. (Fix round 2, C12; acquisition R20: the attestation is requested after the document is kept.) The answer states what the call had kept, `kept`, read off the stage times the object states for its trace (R115): `held` once the capture's receipt (acquisition R13) has ended, `nothing` before it, `undetermined` when the object states no stage (a call with no trace); `detail` is the design's `refusal.ACQUIRE_TIMED_OUT` ("kept nothing") only for `nothing`, and an answer in time carries no `kept`. The interface draws the state; the words for a capture that is kept are the design's (a row owed). Test: a call stopped while `attest` ran after its receipt is `kept: held` with no `detail`; one stopped in `fetch`, `read`, `render` or the receipt itself is `nothing` with the detail; none stated is `undetermined`.

- **R115** (T51; K4222; acquisition R95; amends R105) The door hands `acquire` the object its stages are kept in, `timings`, through the plane's hook (`gatedOp`), and a call the door stops waiting for (R105's bound) answers the refusal `ACQUIRE_TIMED_OUT` with `timings`: (K4234; B13) the object keeps the stages, not the door: a session's `acquire` is minted a trace, handed to the hook as `trace` beside `timings`, and on the bound the door asks the store-internal route `acquiretimings?trace=` and answers the `stageTimes` it gives as `timings`, `{}` when the request has no trace or the object answers none. An answer in time is the handler's, untouched, and no op other than `acquire` is handed the object. Test: the timed-out answer carries the stage times the object answers for the hook's trace, with the read naming that trace (`t51-acquire-timings.test.mjs`, R115). Control: an answer in time carries no door timings and asks the object nothing, another op is handed none, and a call with no trace asks nothing and states `{}`. The hand-off through the plane's `gatedOp` (PLANE #44) and capture's `acquire` arm (CAPTURE #39) is their end-to-end proof; the door's part is tested here.

- **R117** (fix round 3, C5, C17; K4356, K4358; `extraction` R80, `acquisition` R96; amends R100) **A held capture gets its reading.** After `op=acquire` or `op=captureupload` answers `existed: true` with a document, and after a capture is kept whole (`acquisition` R96), the door asks `extraction.readHeld` for the capture's digest, so a record whose home holds no reading for it gets one once its bytes are held whole; a reading already held is left as it is. When the digest has no home yet (an upload whose first answer was never promoted, so its reading was never kept), the door reads the stored bytes as R100 reads a new upload and answers the reading with the document, so it is kept when the document is promoted (K4360). The answer's other fields are unchanged, a failed reading is answered as R100 answers one, and a refusal of `readHeld` never turns a kept capture into a failed one. Test, through the door: a digest registered under a home with no reading is uploaded again (`existed: true`) and `op=reading` by the digest then answers `found: true` with its units; an upload of bytes held with no home and no reading, answered `existed: true`, carries its reading, and after the screen promotes it `op=reading` answers `found: true`; a capture of several parts, once kept whole, is read. Control: a digest that already has a reading is not read again (R100's control); `existed: false` is read as R100 reads it.

- **R121** (fix round 4, C5a, P9; K4429, K4428; amends R105, R115) **The door times each op and each phase of an acquire.** Every answer of the door carries a `Server-Timing` header with the whole milliseconds the door spent in that op (the op's name as the metric, and, where the op made calls to the object, the time of each); no header carries a credential, a name or an address of a member's. An `acquire` refused or timed out (502, 504, `ACQUIRE_TIMED_OUT`, `STORE_DID_NOT_ANSWER`, `CAPTURE_NOT_KEPT`) carries `timings.door`: `{acquire, reading, promote, cold}`, the first three each `{started_ms, elapsed_ms}` or `null` for a phase not reached, `cold` as R129 states, clocked in the door's own isolate so that it survives a stop or restart of the object, and `timings.stages` as R95 states them when the object answered them (`{}` only when the object answered none, and then `timings.door` is still present). The times change nothing a capture records or answers but the header and the `timings` key. Test: a call stopped in the reading states `timings.door.reading` as running and `acquire` as ended, with the object's stage record forced empty; an answered op carries `Server-Timing` naming the op. Control: an answer in time carries no `timings.door`; a read op carries a header and no body change.

- **R123** (fix round 4b, F1, F2, F6; K4487, K4503; D3; `store-assembly` R64, capture R103, store-door R18) **A reading follows every entry into the record.** After `op=promote`, `op=captureupload` and `op=inboxpull` (and `inboxresolve` at `pulled`) answer, the door reads each document the record now stores with no reading: for a promotion, each document of its `data/provenance.json` that states a capture digest and no reading (every one is followed, R133; none for a verified replay); for an upload, the document answered (new, or `existed` with no home); for a pull, the pulled capture (store-door R18's `sha`, else the doorbell's `capture`). A reading the gate admits (R131) is made at once through `readHeld`; any other, and any that `readHeld` could not make (no whole object, no home, a refusal, a silence), is registered as owed work (`registerJob`, kind `reading`, `store-assembly` R74, R75) before any inline read starts (R130), and the answer's `result` carries `reading_pending: true`; an upload the gate refuses is answered `reading_pending` with its row, and one over the alpha ceiling is stated at once (R125 (a), R132). A reading held or made changes the answer by nothing. A refused act asks nothing, and no refusal or silence of the reading changes what the act answered. Test: `r123-reading.test.mjs`; whole path `t51-reading-follows.test.mjs` (a pulled 3 MB PDF knock read without a press; a 40 MB upload and a 3 MB PDF filed after the alarm gave up, read by the alarm).

- **R128** (fix round 8, G113, G116, G118, G120; review G4; fix round 9, P24; K4817, K4822, K4850, K4855, K4870; amends R121) **The door carries the timings a module states.** The phase clock takes the entries a module states into the answer's `Server-Timing`: from the object's `server-timing` header (token-checked `name;dur=` and `name;desc=` entries, the first of a name, never one of the door's own, at most `METRIC_MAX` = 64) and from the hook `stateTiming(name, ms, desc)` (`desc` optional: written beside the `dur` as the entry's `desc` when it is a short token of letters, digits, `_`, `.`, `:` and `-`, at most 48, else dropped and the entry keeps its `dur` alone), handed to the plane's door context (gated and public) and to `instance-setup`'s `io`. For every answer the door also states the shape of the request, never its content, as `shape;dur=0;desc=f<n>:l<limit>`: `f<n>` when the address names `fields` (n the number of its non-blank comma-separated names) and `l<limit>` when it names a `limit` that is a whole number of at most nine digits; a part the address does not give is left out and a request giving neither states no `shape`. The Queue's `result.derivation` is lifted by the door into `queue-count;desc=hit|miss` and `rows-queue;dur` (the Queue runs in the object and cannot hold the hook).
