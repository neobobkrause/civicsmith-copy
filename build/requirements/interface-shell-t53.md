## Public

### Provides

- **R136** (T53; N1264, K4599 (a); `platform/combined-read-and-fold-draft.md` PL16; amends R113, R123) **A screen opens by one `screenread`.** A screen opens by one `screenread` (`op-declarations` PL11); an in-app move needs no session read, no HTML and no shell file (they are already absent); the shell asks again only the reads an act names as its dependants (R113). *(not yet met: T53)* Test: opening Project makes one API request; an act re-reads only its dependants.
- **R137** (T53; N1264, K4599 (a); `platform/combined-read-and-fold-draft.md` PL17) **The first screens through the combined read are Project, then Home and Queue, then each screen, in that order.** A screen not yet converted works as today. *(not yet met: T53)* Test: a converted and an unconverted screen on one copy, each drawn within its budget class.
- **R138** (T53; DEC-463; K5516, L2; amends R27, in the family of R91; the screens modules' pickers cite it) **A document picker with two scopes.** Every screen that asks the member to choose a held document draws one kit picker. It opens with the documents of the screen's own project, each by title and date, and beneath them the control "Show documents from other projects" (key owed by design) [decided: K5516, L2 as recommended; stands as written]. Pressing it adds those documents, each row naming its project (a group heading satisfies this; a document with no project says so with `screen.document.pick.noproject`'s word), the rows already listed not repeated. A document chosen stays chosen from either scope; typing narrows what is shown; when nothing shown matches, the none-found sentence is drawn and the control is still offered; an archive keeps its files with it, in its own scope; paging and any "N shown" statement hold per scope. A screen that knows no project for the choice has no first scope: its list is the second, each row labelled with its project, with no control; a member who sees one project's documents only is drawn no control. A document filed in this project from elsewhere counts as this project's, labelled with its home project (`capture` R77). A scope that fails to load is drawn failed (R10), never as empty, and the other scope stands. The control, the picker's search and its rows are the kit's own words and classes (R56, R5). Test: with documents in project A and B, the picker opens with A's only; pressing the control adds B's labelled B and does not repeat A's; choosing a B document is the choice; typing a name that matches nothing shown draws the none-found sentence and the control; a screen with no project draws one labelled list and no control. Controls: one project only: no control; a failed second scope is drawn failed and A's list stands.
- **R139** (T53; N1260; DEC-410 (1) `follow(kind)`; DEC-447 "in place" and `FOLLOW.fund`; amends R21, R90) *(not yet met: T53)* **The kit gains `cs.follow({item, onEntry})`: one "Follow from here" control, and its in-place fold.** It draws a closed fold `screen.follow.label`, explained by `screen.follow.tip` (R15), and reads `followfrom` for the item (no `entry`) only when it is first opened, never on load. The control is the same on every item kind: the menu it draws is whatever `followfrom` answers for the item (`affordances` R73), so a person, organization, fund, payment, donation, event or document differ only in the entries answered. Each menu entry is an act button labelled `screen.follow.<entry>`, drawn under R12 (absent, never greyed, when the session may not call its op); with no entry, the control is not drawn (the common line above: nothing to show draws nothing). Pressing an entry calls `onEntry(entry)`. **In place means a fold of that dimension's rows for the item.** An entry whose op is `followfrom` is sent as `followfrom {item, entry}` and its answer drawn by the screen in a fold under the item (`data-follow-result`), the page, its scroll and its other folds unchanged; pressing again closes it; a failed read is drawn `state.failed` in the fold (R10); a fold whose answer holds no row is not drawn. **Under the rows the fold draws `screen.follow.open.whole`, "Open all of it in {screen}"** (key `screen.follow.open.whole`, DEC-447): a link, by `cs.go`, to the whole screen of that dimension narrowed to the item, `{screen}` that screen's name by its registry word. The screen is chosen by the entry's dimension from a table in the screen module (money and donations: `money`; events: `timeline`; organizations: `person`; documents: `finder`; registers: `registers`), and the link is drawn only when the target screen takes the narrowing; for `law` the rows each open their `standard` and no whole-screen link is drawn. The control sends no write. Test: a fixture item draws the fold; opening it sends one `followfrom` read; a menu of three entries draws three buttons; an entry the session may not call is absent; an empty menu draws nothing; the result fold keeps a second fold's open state; a fold with rows draws "Open all of it in Money" for `who.money` and its link opens `money` narrowed to the item, and for `who.laws` draws no such link; a fold with no rows is not drawn. Control: an unopened control sends no request; the menu is drawn from the answer, not from a table in the kit.
- **R140** (T53; DEC-474 (1), (2); DEC-461, K5548) **The kit draws the record's ceiling on a scale of its own.** `cs.grade` and the chip tooltip gain a scale `ceiling` beside `capture`. The record's ceiling for a copy is `ceiling`, named "How well it could be captured" (`mark.scale.ceiling.name`, explained by `.means`), and every chip on a document or source is drawn on it; hover cards and code rows that grade a document read "could be captured: {grade}". The team's grade keeps the `capture` scale id with the short name "Copy" (`mark.scale.capture.name`, `.means`). The `ceiling` mark uses the capture icon until one is drawn. *(not yet met: T53)* Test: `cs.grade` given `ceiling` draws the chip with the ceiling's name and its tooltip reads "could be captured: {grade}"; given `capture` it reads "Copy". Control: a chip with no scale draws as before; no test holds the English.
- **R141** (T53; DEC-466 U1; N1220, K5457) **A machine result is labelled with the AI that made it.** Every label a screen draws on the assistant's work ("Draft · the assistant's, asked by {handle}") names the AI in parentheses after "the assistant" (`screen.machine.*.ai`, `{ai}` the model's display name from the result's label data, `ai-runs` R104, never the account), with the tooltip `screen.machine.ai.tip`. Test: a draft made by an AI named in the result's label data draws the label with that name and the tooltip. Control: no label holds an account's name.
- **R142** (T53; JOURNEYS; changes R108; answers R-2) **A list row keeps a readable text column at 390 px whatever the screen marks.** The phone rule that gives a row's text its width holds for every row a screen draws, not only rows a screen marks as needing it. With six published-case rows on `front` at 390 px, each row's text column is at least 150 px wide and no word is broken across lines. *(not yet met: T53)* Test: `front` with six published cases at 390 px: every row's text column measures at least 150 px, the "Edition 1 - date - signed by" line wraps at word boundaries only, and the sign-in form starts within one screenful of the list's end; control: one published case unchanged.
- **R143** (T53; JOURNEYS; new; answers R-10) **The assistant not being ready is said, where it would be offered.** When the assistant is not ready (the banner's own condition: its tests have not passed), each screen that would offer it draws one sentence saying so and what the member can do, in place of an offer that cannot work: Team's assistant ("Switch on", Connect a form), Ask (a search form only), the dock, and Plan's tray. - **R144** (T53; JOURNEYS; new; answers R-31) **A press answers at once.** Within 100 ms of a press the control shows its pending word (R10's loading state for acts) and cannot be pressed again until the act answers. *(not yet met: T53)* Test: a fake plane that answers after 3 s: the control draws the pending word before 100 ms and ignores a second press; control: it returns to its label when the act answers or refuses.
- **R145** (T53; JOURNEYS; new; answers R-33) **An explanation never covers what it explains.** The card an explanation opens (R15, R16), on hover or focus, does not overlap the text or field it explains. *(not yet met: T53)* Test: for a page title, the refusal line, the "As of" field and the wizard list, at 1280 and 390, the open card's box and the explained element's box do not intersect; control: the card still opens on the same triggers.
- **R146** (T53; JOURNEYS; new; answers R-34) **A way past the furniture, and every stop named.** A skip link is the first stop on every working screen and moves focus to the screen's main content; no stop is without an accessible name; the count of stops before the first control of the screen is at most the number the test fixes from the measure below.
