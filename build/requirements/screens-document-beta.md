The requirements below are copied from `build/requirements/screens-document.md` as it stands on `job/screens-document` verbatim, each line, sub-bullet and mark unchanged, under the same numbers, so a test that names "R58: ..." stays true. They are R58 to R75, "T53: the Document page in the beta", as the job proposed (`docs/requirements-notes/screens-document.md` on `job/screens-document`); R1 to R57 and the page itself stay in `screens-document`, whose terms these requirements use. ## Public

- **R58** (T53; DEC-459; amends R1, R4) **The Document page links the source to listed members.** The Document page of a handed-in capture draws the door "A source" (opening `about-knocker`, `screens-intake` R76) exactly when the same read answers a `source`; every other viewer who may see the document sees "Handed in through the doorbell"; a document that did not come through the doorbell draws neither. Neither shows a pseudonym, `KNOCK-` id, `knock:` reference or rung. A failed read is drawn failed (R49); a hidden project's document opened by a non-participant stays R54's state; a bare or unseen document stays "Nothing is here" (R50). The name by which the document is called stays `screens-intake` R54's. *(not yet met: T53)* Test: a listed viewer sees the door; an unlisted viewer who may see the document sees the sentence and no link; an uploaded document sees neither; a failed read is drawn failed. Control: the same document opened by the listed viewer on `held` and here shows the same door.

- **R59** (T53; DEC-464; K5524, K5525, K5549 (L24 = A); `content` A8-R63, A8-R64; `leg-earning` A8-R18, A8-R19; `citation` A8-R26, A8-R27) **A member's typed words can be cited, and are labelled as typed.** `document` draws a typing's words with a Cite act (the same `cite` act as a machine passage's, with the typing's `handle` and `content_id`); wherever typed words show (the document page, the cite picker, a question's legs) they carry the label by key `content.typed.unchecked` ("typed by {typist}, not yet checked") or `content.typed.checked` ("typed by {typist}, checked by {checkers}"), the member named as her credit level allows, and the grade with its plain reason: D when unchecked, C once a second member has checked it, never the standing of text Civicsmith read. Confirm (R24) says that a check raises the typing one step. The cite picker offers typings. No word calls typed words Civicsmith's reading or the original. *(not yet met: T53)* Test: a typed page draws Cite, citing it writes a leg at grade D with the label `content.typed.unchecked`; after a second member's Confirm the label is `content.typed.checked` and the grade C; a typing credited anonymously is "a member of the team". Control: a machine-read passage draws no typed label; the typist's own page offers no Confirm.

- **R60** (T53; N1260; DEC-410 `document`; DEC-447 (1)) *(not yet met: T53)* **Document: "Follow from here".** `document` draws `cs.follow` (`interface-shell` R139) for the document (item kind `document`) beside the head. `document.who` draws the names the document's capture resolves to as R11's rows (grade and how matched), `document.money` the money recorded from it (kind, from, to, amount, grade, its source), `document.events` the events recorded from it (date, kind, grade), and `document.laws` ("Laws and standards it cites", `screen.follow.document.laws`) the held standards its readings cite, each with how it matched and opening `standard`; each is a reference (DEC-160), is drawn only when it holds a row (the common line above), and a read that failed is drawn failed. `rows.match` and `conn` as `interface-shell` R139's; `conn` opens `explore` from the document. Nothing is composed from the document's text. Test: a document with two resolved names, one money fact, no event and one cited standard draws the names, money and laws folds and no empty heading for events; a document citing no standard draws no `document.laws` entry. Control: no "shorter path" is drawn on `question` (11.10).

- **R61** (T53; DEC-454) **The Document page draws its own words for each state, not a borrowed stand-in.** `document.mjs` draws the display rows DEC-454 minted in place of its stand-ins: `screen.document.text.partial.one`, `screen.document.reading.beingread`, `document.read.more` ("More passages", apart from the page bar's "Next page"), `screen.document.reading.firstn`, `screen.document.supporting.notwalked.pending|toolarge|unavailable`, and `screen.document.unsupported.<kind>` by the reading's kind for a kind not read (the twelve rows for kinds the beta reads are withdrawn by DEC-467; `ico` stays); the sentences a reading stored keep their own English. Test: each state draws its own key; a kind the beta reads draws no `unsupported` row; `ico` draws its row. Control: a stored reading sentence is drawn as stored.

- **R62** (T53; DEC-467, DEC-473; `files/UX-NEEDS.md` 1, 3, 7, 8, 9; `files/KIND-TABLE.md`) **The Document page says how each kind of file was kept and read.** `{kind}` is read from `file.kind.<key>` by `files/KIND-TABLE.md`'s key column (DEC-473). A kind kept and not read yet draws `screen.document.held.kind` ("{kind}: kept whole and findable by its name and details. It isn't read yet; it will be read once Civicsmith reads files of this kind."). A Word, Excel or PowerPoint file with macros draws "This file contains macros. Its text is read; the macros were never run." A page of a multi-page file draws "Page {n} of {m}, {file}". A text read from a converted older format draws "Read from a converted copy of {file}; the original is kept." and a citation names the page and the original. An encrypted file draws "Needs a password. Civicsmith never asks for it or keeps it." Never "non-textual or too large". *(not yet met: T53)* Test: a Visio drawing draws the held sentence with its plain name; a macro-enabled workbook draws the macro sentence; a page of a TIFF draws "Page {n} of {m}, {file}"; a converted `.doc` draws the converted sentence and its citation names the original; a locked file draws the password sentence. Control: a read PDF draws none of these.

- **R63** (T53; DEC-467, DEC-473; A4-R2, A4-R3, A4-R5, A4-R10; `screens-intake` R77) **A photograph's page draws the picture, when and where, and what was left out.** The Document page of a photograph draws its safe picture as `screens-intake` R77 draws it on `open-file`: "Taken {date}, near {place}." (or its no-place and none forms), "Shown beside the safe picture, read from the original; not carried in the picture.", and "Left out of the safe picture: {what}." with `{what}` joined from A4-R3's `left_out` kinds, each read by `screen.photo.dropped.<kind>`. *(not yet met: T53)* Test: a photograph with place and time draws the three sentences; a kind outside A4-R3's closed set is never drawn. Control: a document that is not a photograph draws none of them.

- **R64** (T53; N1272; K4571, K4636, K5495; DEC-476, DEC-478; amends R1, R4) *(not yet met: T53)* **`document` draws "Interpreted" as a fact row of its head: what was interpreted for this investigation, by whom and when, and what not yet.** Drawn, DEC-478 (over DEC-476): label `screen.document.interp.label` ("Interpreted"), tip `.tip`; the value lists each investigation the viewer can see under `.for` ("For {investigation}:"), then one line per member and date, `.group` ("{parts}, by {member}, {date}"), from `interpretationOf` (`investigation` R30–R32 / `interpretationOf`) `parts` grouped by `by` and day, then `.notyet` ("Not yet: {ranges}.") from `not_interpreted` or, when it is empty, `.all` ("Every part is interpreted."); before any part, `.none` ("Nothing yet for {investigation}."). `.other` is dropped: the same heading serves every investigation. `{parts}` and `{ranges}` are named by the units the reading holds (`part.*`, R143). This row never says how far the document is read ("Its text" does, DEC-476). Test: a read PDF with pages 3 to 5 interpreted by one member and 6 to 9 by another, on two days, draws "For {investigation}:", "pages 3 to 5, by {member}, {date}", "pages 6 to 9, by {member}, {date}" and "Not yet: pages 1 to 2, 10 to 12."; another visible project draws its own "For" heading and lines; every page interpreted draws `.all` and no "Not yet"; nothing interpreted draws `.none`. Control: no row omits what was not interpreted unless it draws `.all` (only when `not_interpreted` is empty); finding names for search draws no row and no mark (C21, K5496).

- **R65** (T53; N1272; K5445 A; K5495; DEC-476) *(not yet met: T53)* **A later investigation is offered "Interpret parts of this for {this investigation}".** Drawn, DEC-476: on `document`, when the viewer's project has a live interpretation for fewer parts than the document has, or others have interpreted it, the act `screen.document.interp.offer` ("Interpret parts of this for {investigation}"), op `interpretmark` (it sends `markForInterpretation`, R26), opens the chooser (R66); what others interpreted is the row's "For {investigation}:" lines (R64). Test: a viewer in Q on a document interpreted for P (visible to the viewer) sees the act and P's "For" lines; a viewer who cannot see P sees the act and no mention of P. Control: the act is absent for a viewer not in any project of the record's (R12 of the shell: absent, never greyed).

- **R66** (T53; N1272; K5445 B; DEC-476, DEC-478) *(not yet met: T53)* **The part chooser: the member names the parts to interpret, and sees what that will do.** Drawn, DEC-476, DEC-478: the chooser states `screen.document.interp.chooser.will` ("This reads the parts you choose for {investigation}."), offers `.chooser.whole` ("The whole document") and ranges in the unit the reading holds, none ticked; pressing sends `markForInterpretation` (`investigation` R26); a part chosen records basis `unaided` (R117). Not drawn: the count of parts and the blocks sentence for a range past R115's block. Nothing is preselected the member has not seen (DEC-77 (3c)). Test: choosing pages 3 to 9 (or rows 1 to 500 of a sheet) and pressing sends one `markForInterpretation` and R64's row follows; nothing is sent before the press. Control: the chooser draws no part pre-ticked.

- **R67** (T53; N1272; K4571, K5495; DEC-476, DEC-478) *(not yet met: T53)* **Citing or opening a part says it was interpreted, and for whom.** After an open or cite that records a row (`investigation` R115) the screen states `screen.document.interp.recorded` ("Interpreted for {investigation}: {part}.") under the text's legend (drawn, DEC-478), the page and its other folds unchanged; a re-open states it again, with no second record. - **R68** (T53; N1272; K5495; DEC-476, DEC-478; amends R1, R4) *(not yet met: T53)* **The document view marks, in the margin of the text, what is interpreted.** Drawn, DEC-476; owed words keys: none (`screen.document.interp.legend.this`, "Interpreted for {investigation}", once per bar, is also the text equivalent). - **R72** (T53; DEC-479 (51)) **A reading says how many words were hard to make out, and marks them.** "Read {read} of {total} words" stays, and is followed, only when the reader measured how clearly each character resolved (never a model's opinion of itself, `TEXT_CONFIDENCE_PSEUDO`), by `screen.document.text.unsure` ("{unsure} of them were hard to make out and are marked on the page. Check a marked word against the original before you cite it."); each such word is underlined dotted, with the card "Hard to make out · The reader could not make this word out clearly. Check it against the original." The count and the positions are the reading's own (`reading-pipeline`'s count of words below the reader's measured clarity). *(not yet met: T53)* Test: a reading with 7 of 4,000 words below the clarity draws the sentence with 7 and 7 dotted words with the card. Control: a reading with no measured clarity draws no sentence and no mark.
