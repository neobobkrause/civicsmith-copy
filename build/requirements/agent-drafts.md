# agent-drafts — requirements

**Status** · New 4 Nov 2026: split from `agent-worker` for size (its requirement lines alone are over the 70,000-byte cap; the split proposed by `agent-worker`'s job, attempt 1, in `docs/requirements-notes/agent-worker.md` on `job/agent-worker`), accepted by the coordinator (BOB) 4 Nov 2026; K617, K624. A new module, layer 6, directly after `agent-runner`, split by copy with no `from`. R1–R7 are `agent-worker`'s R59, R68, R69, R70, R76, R77, R86, copied verbatim with their marks and renumbered in the order of their old ids, meaning unchanged; each line says "moved from agent-worker:R<n>". `agent-worker`'s own restarted job removes and retires the moved lines there, naming their new ids, and never reuses them. A bare `R<n>` inside a moved line that names no module is a `agent-worker` id. The code this module owns today is `agent-worker/src/draft.mjs`, `action-drafts.mjs` and `untested.mjs`: it moves here only once `agent-worker`'s job has retired the lines; until then this module's job implements here and leaves `agent-worker`'s code to its job.

## Public

### Purpose

The agent's `POST /draft`: the drafts it makes for the plane's draft door, their answer and label.

### Provides

- **R1** (moved from agent-worker:R59) `POST /draft` takes `{task, told, account, grant?, pack?, firsthand?}` (`pack`, the rendered pack the door holds, `control-plane` R41, sent when no `grant` is: with a grant the pack is read by `op=agentpack` under it, R48, R60; without one it is the body's, held to R48's whole-pack test; neither answers 502 `PACK_UNDETERMINED` with no model call; K1983), sent by the plane's door when it routes `writinghelp` and `groupdescriptiondraft` here (`control-plane` R57 as T35 amends it): `task` is `{op: "writinghelp", act, field}` (the act and field `wizard-scripts` R24 offered help in) or `{op: "groupdescriptiondraft"}`; `told` is what the member typed (`wizard-scripts` R27's `told`, 1 to 4,000 characters) or, for the group's description, the administrator's `answers` (`instance-setup` R65: `[{question, text}]`, each `text` at most 1,000 characters, not all empty); `account` the account `ai-accounts.accountFor` answers for that member's act, in R6's wire shape, its `suggestions` that account's switch (`ai-accounts` R4, R9); `grant` the member's short-lived, read-only `ai` grant, sent only when the draft may read; `firsthand` true when the field records what the member saw. Refusals before any model call, in order: no `PLANE` binding as R1; a body not JSON as R2; a `task` of another op or shape 400 `BAD_TASK`; a `told` absent, of another shape or outside its bounds 400 `BAD_TOLD`; no account 409 `NO_ACCOUNT`; an account R6 refuses 400 `BAD_ACCOUNT`; a `grant` present while `suggestions` is not true or `firsthand` is true 400 `DRAFT_READ_NOT_ALLOWED` (K1841 (2); DEC-153 (2)). It then runs turns through `agent-model` (`converse`, mode `draft`, within `run-rules`' bounds for that mode) under that account, instructed by the published pack's `writing_help` layer (`skills` R36), with its `suggestions` layer loaded only when the switch is on (R56); the pack is read as R48 reads it, and with none published it answers 502 `PACK_UNDETERMINED` and calls no model. The model is offered a read tool only when `grant` arrived, reading through it only `answers`' `ASK_SCOPE` (its R1; R55's refusal of any other op, returned as the tool's result); with no grant it is offered no read tool, so the draft works only from `told`. It answers 200 `{ok: true, task, draft, label: {kind: "machine"}, usage, calls}`, `draft` `{text}` for `writinghelp` or `{focus, purpose}` for `groupdescriptiondraft`, `usage` and `calls` as `agent-model` R6 answers them; a conversation that ends without a draft (`agent-model` R6's `stopped`, `exhausted`, `silent` or `refused`) answers `{ok: false, code, detail}` naming that ending. It checks, stores and shows no draft: the door checks it with `wizard-scripts` R25 against the read log the plane holds for the grant (none when no grant was sent) before the member sees anything, and counts `usage` to the member's day (`ai-runs` R48, R52). It makes no write op, capture request or run row, and keeps nothing between calls (R36). (T37; N669; K2200) `POST /draft` also takes a translation draft, `task.op` `translationdraft` (R68–R70); this requirement's `writinghelp` and `groupdescriptiondraft` arms are unchanged by it. (T53; AIR5, BR29; amends R59) `POST /draft` takes `{task, told, account, grant?, pack?, firsthand?}`, `account` in R6's shape. Refusals before any model call, in order: as before through `BAD_ACCOUNT`, then 503 `AI_PATH_NOT_READY`, 409 `AI_CAPABILITY_MISSING`, 409 `PART_NOT_PASSED` naming the task's part, 409 `RECIPIENT_NOTICE_DUE`; the entry is R120's. It answers 200 `{ok: true, task, draft, label: {kind: "machine", ai}, usage, calls}`, `ai` the label (R122), `usage` the neutral record (`agent-model`). `groupdescriptiondraft` stays the op's code; words say team. Test: each refusal precedes any model call, in order; control: a valid draft answers 200 with `label.ai`.
- **R2** (moved from agent-worker:R68) `POST /draft` with `task` `{op: "translationdraft", direction, language, words}`, sent by the plane's door when it routes `translationdraft` (`store-door` R10; `control-plane` R57's third draft; `instance-setup`'s T37 requirement), takes `account` as R59 does and never a `grant`. `language` is a BCP 47 tag (`instance-setup` R64's form). `direction` is `to_language` or `to_english`. For `to_language`, `words` is a list of 1 to 100 entries `{key, en, note, means, protected}` (K2201), each a word the group's `language` lacks as the word list holds it (DEC-179 (3)): `en` its English, `note` its `note` or null, `means` the text of its sibling `.means` or `.does` entry or null, `protected` its mark. For `to_english`, `words` is exactly one `{key, en, text, protected}`, `text` the word kept in `language`. Refusals before any model call, in R59's order and with R59's codes: no `PLANE` binding as R1; a body not JSON as R2; a `task` of another shape (an unknown `direction`, a `words` list out of its bounds or with an entry missing `key` or `en`, a duplicate `key`) 400 `BAD_TASK`; no account 409 `NO_ACCOUNT`; an account R6 refuses 400 `BAD_ACCOUNT`; a `grant` present 400 `DRAFT_READ_NOT_ALLOWED` (`run-rules` R22: `draftMayRead` is false for a translation).
- **R3** (moved from agent-worker:R69) It runs turns through `agent-model` (`converse`, mode `draft`, within `run-rules`' bounds for that mode) under that account, instructed by the published pack's `interface_translation` layer (`skills` R39) and never its `suggestions` or `writing_help` layer. The pack is read from the body as R59 reads it when no grant arrived; with none it answers 502 `PACK_UNDETERMINED` and calls no model, and so it does while the pack renders R39 as a stated absence. The model is offered no tool (`run-rules` R22: a translation draft reads nothing). `words` reach it only in the user turn, as R61 places `told`; the system prompt carries the pack and this module's own words, never a word list.
- **R4** (moved from agent-worker:R70) It answers 200 `{ok: true, task, draft, label: {kind: "machine"}, usage, calls}`: for `to_language`, `draft` is `{words: [{key, text}]}`, one entry per key it drafted, and each asked key it could not draft is listed in `not_drafted`; a key not asked is never answered; for `to_english`, `draft` is `{key, english}`. A conversation that ends without a draft answers as R59's do. It checks, stores and shows no draft and adopts nothing: what is recorded, labelled and shown is `instance-setup`'s, and `usage` is counted to the member's day by the door (`ai-runs` R48, R52). It makes no plane call at all, no write op, capture request or run row, and keeps nothing between calls (R36). R61's sentinel test and R62's fixtures apply: a `words` entry whose English or `text` carries an instruction is drafted as words, and no call to the plane follows. (T53; BR29; amends R70) the answer's `label` is `{kind: "machine", ai}` (R122); the rest as before. Test: `label` is the stub adapter's; control: a request-body `label` is ignored.
- **R5** (moved from agent-worker:R76) (T45; N861, N887, N892; Actions D1, D11b, D25, D28; v0.2 rules 2, 9, 16, 19, 21) `POST /draft` takes three further `task.op`s, each the member's own act, answered as a labelled proposal and never stored here: `textcheck` (`{text}`, a prepared outward text: each sentence that reads as an assertion of breach, a policy position, an ask for the group's or its members' own benefit, or a name of staff below the responsible official, answered `{sentences: [{index, quote, reads_as, why}]}`, `reads_as` one of `assertion`, `position`, `own_benefit_ask`, `staff_named`; it never rewrites a sentence); `infractiondraft` (`{matter, audience}`, `audience` one of `violating_party`, `members`, `oversight`, `public`, `broad`: `{text, sources}`, a documented account of the act and office, the standard, the evidence per point with its grade and what is uncertain); `bearingdraft` (`{action, reply}`: `{claims: [{claim, bearing, quote}]}`, `bearing` one of `new_evidence`, `disputed_without_evidence`, `not_addressed`). Refusals before any model call, in R59's order and codes, plus `DRAFT_NEEDS_GRANT` for `infractiondraft` and `bearingdraft`, which read the record, without a `grant`, and R59's `DRAFT_READ_NOT_ALLOWED` for a `textcheck` that carries one (`run-rules` R27: it reads nothing of the record); reads go only through the grant and only to the reach `run-rules` R27 sets for these drafts. The records a draft is about arrive in the request from the door (K2824): `infractiondraft` takes `record` (`{plan?, actions, correspondence, findings}`, what the asking member may see of the matter), `bearingdraft` takes `action_record` and `reply_record` (the action with its claims, the reply's entry and text); each is given to the model as data, never as instruction (R77's injection rule), and an absent or malformed one is R59's `BAD_TASK`. Answers as R59's (`label: {kind: "machine"}`, `usage`, `calls`). Record-check assistance on a prepared filing is `/draft`'s `account_check` task over its text (N842's, not in T45), not a fourth task. (T46; N920; N881; `study-T46-assistant.md` §2; `run-rules` R27) A fourth task, `statusreportdraft`: `task` `{op: "statusreportdraft", plan, items}`, sent by the door when it routes `plan-work`'s `statusreportassist` (`control-plane` R57), `plan` the plan's id and `items` the items `plan-work` R8 assembled, handed in by the door as data; it answers `{text, sources}`, each sentence of `text` coming from a handed item and `sources` naming the items it rests on. It reads nothing (`run-rules` R27's reach `none`): a `grant` present is `DRAFT_READ_NOT_ALLOWED`, and an absent or malformed `plan` or `items` is R59's `BAD_TASK`, in R59's order. It is instructed by the `action_drafting` layer's status-report task (`skills` R48), and R77's injection rule and fixtures and R79's label apply. Tests: a stub model's report answered with `sources` among the handed items; an item telling the model to read the record or state a breach comes back only in `text`, with no plane call; control: a `grant` refused `DRAFT_READ_NOT_ALLOWED`.
- **R6** (moved from agent-worker:R77) (T45; N861; Actions D11b; v0.2 rules 1, 9, 12) R76's tasks are instructed by the published pack's `action_drafting` layer (`skills` R45) and never its `suggestions` or `writing_help` layer; while that layer is a stated absence they answer 502 `PACK_UNDETERMINED` and call no model. R60–R62 apply: R62's fixtures gain record text (a reply, a finding) telling the model to state a breach, a position, what to file or "you have a claim": driven through each task with a stub model that obeys, the text comes back only in the draft's fields, no write op or capture request is made, and nothing else is answered.
- **R7** (moved from agent-worker:R86) (T48; N670; `run-rules` R33; `skills` R49) `POST /draft` takes `task` `{op: "readtranslate", language, text, from_language?}`, sent by the plane's draft door.
