## Public

#### T51 (T51-128): the words by key, the foreign stop, the short name recorded (N1239, N1151, N1242; DEC-329 (7), (8), DEC-331 (1), (2), DEC-336 (1); K3806, K3818, K3864, K3868, K3878)

- **R84** (fix round 9, K4937; G144-G150; amends R4, R7, R14, R70) **The account made ready before anything is created.** Before `fresh` creates anything, every condition below is checked, and each one not met stops with a plain walk-through, says that nothing was created, and offers "Check again", which re-checks from the start with the founder's choices kept (`emit.retry`; never a dead end): (1) **storage**: R2's entitlement is read without writing (installer-steps R43); refused as not entitled, the founder is walked through Cloudflare's R2 checkout (Storage & databases, R2, Overview, the checkout), never told it is a payment method unless Cloudflare's answer says so, and any other refusal is told as itself; (2) **Containers**: decided by Cloudflare's measured answer code, never by matching words; where the cause is the plan, the plan's stop (R6) is shown; an answer for want of permission asks the founder to approve again (R71); a count that cannot be read is "could not tell", stated, never zero; (3) **limits**: Cloudflare's published ceilings of the account's plan (Workers 500, Durable Object classes 500, R2 buckets 1,000,000, cron triggers 250), each with its source in a comment, and the limit stop also re-checks; (4) **the address**: when the account has no workers.dev prefix, the founder is shown the proposed name, told it is permanent for the account, may change it, and chooses it with a button; it is registered (installer-steps R44) before any upload, and a taken name asks for another; nothing is registered without that click; (5) **the permission**: the granted scopes are compared with those every install needs before the first call, and a missing one asks to approve again (R71); (6) **the account**: when the sign-in lists more than one account, the founder chooses one by name, the choice kept with the others; (7) **size**: each member's bundle is compared with Cloudflare's 64 MiB uncompressed Worker limit before upload, and one over it is left out by name (R38's per-member rule). Cloudflare offers no permission to buy a plan or turn R2 on for the founder (its OAuth scope list, read 2026-10-24, holds no billing scope), so these stay walk-throughs; the address is provisioned for the founder, with their click. Test: for each condition, a fixture with Cloudflare's answer shape (each shape measured once on the rehearsal account, K4942, and the fixture naming where it came from) stops before any write with its walk-through and "Check again", and passes after the fixture changes; controls: a ready account passes all; a 500 from R2 is not told as the checkout. Containers on a Free account answer HTTP 401 with code 1000, read as the plan stop, not "could not tell" (measured, K4970).
- **R85** (fix round 9, K4951; amends R23, R84) **A founder with no Cloudflare account is walked through from the start.** Before the sign-in, the installer asks whether the founder already has a Cloudflare account, and walks a founder who has none, or one not yet ready, through every step in plain words, one step a screen, each saying why it is needed and what it costs: creating the account and confirming its email; choosing Workers Paid (about $5 a month, paid to Cloudflare, never to Civicsmith) and adding a payment method; turning on R2 storage; then signing in and the "Allow" page, naming what Civicsmith is asking to do and what it never does. Every step can be left and resumed, and "Check again" (R84) confirms it. Beside every step the founder's likely questions are answered in place (at least: why my own account; what it costs, who is paid, and how to stop paying; what Civicsmith can see and do in the account and how to remove it; is my card shared; what if I already use Cloudflare; what if I get stuck), their words the design stream's. Never asked of a founder: an API token or an account id (the sign-in grants the permission, R2). Test: a founder answering "no account" is shown each step in order, each with its answers and a resume point; one answering "yes" goes to the sign-in; no screen asks for a token or an account id.

#### T53 (plan-T53-draft.md, installer; N1265 "Check my account" and the gate, the places in layers, the levels, the region, the move)

- **R86** (T53; N1265; DEC-415, DEC-416; K4937, K5449, K5460; amends R2, R3, R4) **"Check my account" is a run that makes nothing that stays.** `POST /begin` takes `mode: check` beside `install`, `update` and the removal modes (R2, R65), with the slug validated as for an install (the name line needs it) and asking exactly the scopes R2 states (K5460 (2), K5473 (2)). `GET /callback` for a check runs `runChecks(token, account, slug)` (`ready.mjs`) against the account the sign-in lists (R84 (6) chooses one when there are several) and draws the list R87; it clears the cookie, stores nothing and names the account it read. It takes no lock (`installer-steps` R6's `takeLock` creates the Worker `bio-install-lock`), registers no workers.dev prefix (it reads `addressState`, never `addressReady` or `registerSubdomain`, installer-steps R44) and creates no bucket. Nothing is created that stays: the only write is the plan check's own probe upload (`establishPlan`, `installer-steps` R7, the Worker `bio-plan-probe`), deleted at once, or, when it cannot be, named (R4's rule); a failed delete is a line, not a silence. K5530 F7: one probe upload is accepted only where no read answers the same check (none answers whether the plan lets a Worker be made); the card line makes no probe of its own (R88; K5460 (1)). A refusal of permission before the list asks the founder to approve again (R71). Test: a check on the fake account (`newgroup/test/fixture.mjs`) draws the list and leaves the account's stored state equal to before it (the probe script deleted); the recorded calls hold no write other than the probe's upload and delete; a check whose probe delete fails names the script left. Control: an install and an update answer as before.
- **R87** (T53; N1265; DEC-416; amends R70, R84) **The list: every line at once, in a fixed order.** `CHECK_LINES` (exported, frozen from `ready.mjs`): `plan` (Workers Paid, `establishPlan`), `containers` (R84 (2), `containersState`), `limits` (R84 (3), `limitReached`), `name` (the short name free: R70's F7, today inline in the install's `fresh` step, `index.mjs:662-680`, and extracted into one function the install and the check both call, so there is no second copy), `card` (R88). Each line is drawn `ready`, `not_ready` or `unknown`, with its fix sentence and a button to the page that fixes it (R92's links, by key), and each is judged independently: a line that errors is `unknown` and never stops the others; a count that cannot be read is "could not tell", never zero or ready (R84 (2)). The state of the whole is `ready` only when every line is `ready`. A line answering `permission` asks for approval again (R71) instead of drawing. Test: a fake account on the free plan with a taken name and the limits reached draws three `not_ready` lines in one run, each with its own fix; an account whose Containers read fails draws that line `unknown` and the rest as they are; a ready account draws five `ready`. Control: R70's and R84's own stops, in the install, answer as before.
- **R88** (T53; N1265; DEC-415; K4937, K5449, K5460 (1)) **The card line is derived from the plan line and one read, and has no probe of its own.** The `card` line is `ready` when `plan` is `ready` and `installer-steps` R43 (`storageEntitlement`) answers `on`; when R43 answers `not_entitled` it is `not_ready` and says what was measured, never that a card is missing unless Cloudflare's answer says so (R84 (1)); on `permission` it asks to approve again; on `unknown` it is `unknown` with Cloudflare's words. No bucket is made and no write is added for this line: it uses the plan line's throwaway probe (R86; `installer-steps` R7), the only write the check makes, and the line says so in its words (K5460 (1) corrects K5449 (1)). Test: free plan: `card` not ready with the plan line's fix; paid plan with R2 not entitled: the storage fix; paid with R2 on: ready; paid with R2 unknown: unknown. Control: no create call is made in any of the four.
- **R89** (T53; N1265; DEC-415) **Re-runnable, and honest about Cloudflare's own limits.** "Check again" re-runs R86 from the start with the founder's choices kept (`emit.retry`'s way, R84; `CHECK_AGAIN`) and is offered with every list; there is no count limit, no state kept and no run depends on an earlier one. A Cloudflare answer that stays too-many-requests past `installer-steps` R1's bounded waiting (`Lasting`, `lasting: true`; `special(e)` is true of it) makes that line `unknown` with "try again in a minute", never `not_ready`; an `ApproveAgain` is not caught by the line but asks for approval (R71). Test: two runs on a fake account whose state changes between them draw the second's state; a 429 that never mends on one read (timers immediate, `test/steps/world.mjs`'s `immediateTimers`) draws that line `unknown` with the sentence and the other lines as they are. Control: a ready account's repeated runs all draw ready.
- **R90** (T53; N1265; DEC-415 "the gate"; K5449) **"Install now" opens only on a passed check, and the install's own stops stay.** The page after a check whose whole is `ready` offers "Install now", which posts `/begin` in install mode and says before it does that Cloudflare will ask once more (K5449); with any line not ready it is drawn disabled with `screen.install.check.closed`. A passed check sets one cookie `chk`, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax, 30 minutes, holding the slug and the instant and nothing else (no token, no account id, no result), unsigned: it is the first line only (the name `chk` is held by nothing today; the begin cookie is `bio_wiz`, `CFG.COOKIE`). `POST /begin` in install mode with no `chk` for the same slug, an expired one, or one for another slug is refused 400 `CHECK_NOT_PASSED` in the keyed words, naming "Check my account", and creates nothing. Update, removal (R65) and check modes are exempt. R84's checks at the start of the install stand unchanged as the second line; `screen.install.refused.rare` is drawn when the second line stops what the first passed. Test: install `/begin` without `chk` is refused and nothing is created; with `chk` it proceeds to the callback; a `chk` older than 30 minutes or for slug B while installing A is refused; an update and a removal need none; after a pass, a fake account changed to the free plan is stopped by R84 and draws the rare-case sentence. Control: the cookie holds only slug and instant, asserted by its decoded bytes. **Consequence for the existing tests:** every install-mode `begin(...)` in `newgroup/test/` (about 62 call sites through `fixture.mjs`'s `begin`) would be refused; the fixture's `begin` sends a passed `chk` for install mode by default, and one test sends none.
- **R91** (T53; N1265; DEC-415, DEC-416, DEC-430; K5449; amends R85, R23) **"Get ready" is R85's guide, with Cloudflare's pages as buttons.** R85's four steps (`GUIDE_STEPS`: account, plan, storage, signin) are the Get-ready; DEC-416's five-step page is not built (DEC-430 retired its rows). Each of the first three steps gains a button that opens its Cloudflare page in a new tab (`target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`), the page and its label read from R92's table by the step's id, with a line on what the founder will see there (a key); the last step's end reads "When these are done, check your account" and begins a check (R86), where R85 leads to the install's sign-in. The first page's primary action for an install begins a check; the install itself begins only from the list's "Install now" (R90). The page never asks for a token or an account id and never opens an account, enters a card or buys a plan: those are the founder's acts at Cloudflare (Distribution §5; sovereign model). The update page draws no Get-ready. Test: each of the three steps draws a button whose URL is R92's table's and which opens a new tab; no field asks for a token or an id; the page makes no outbound call; a founder answering "yes" (R85) still goes on without the guide. Control: the update page is unchanged.
- **R92** (T53; N1265; DEC-416) **Every link is data, in one place.** The Cloudflare addresses the Get-ready buttons and the lines' fix buttons open are held only in two frozen tables of the installer, `GET_READY_LINKS` (step id to `{key, url}`) and `FIX_LINKS` (each `{line id, state}` for `not_ready` and `unknown` to `{fix_key, url}`; a `ready` line has none); `ui.mjs`, `guide.mjs` and the pages hold no link to Cloudflare of their own. `ui.mjs:217` holds one today (`https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up`, the account-needs line): it moves into `GET_READY_LINKS` (step `account`). `CFG.AUTHORIZE`, `CFG.TOKEN` and `CF_API` are endpoints, not links the reader follows, and are not in the tables. A fix button opens the exact page named by the line's id and state (Workers Paid; storage; limits; the workers.dev name). Test: a scan of every `href` the served pages draw finds a `cloudflare.com` address only from the two tables; every `CHECK_LINES` id has an entry for both states. Control: the tables' URLs are what the pages draw.
- **R93** (T53; N1265; DEC-415, DEC-416, DEC-430; amends R72, R75-R77) **Words by key; no inline English.** The list, the gate and `CHECK_NOT_PASSED` are drawn from the table's `screen.install.check.*` and `screen.install.refused.rare` (held) and from the keys the design adds for section 3's needs; they join `KEYS` in `newgroup/src/words.mjs` and the `installer` entry of `words-reads.json`, which R72 holds equal to the keys the source names (a key the source names that the table lacks fails by name; the sentence is then reported, never shipped as English).
