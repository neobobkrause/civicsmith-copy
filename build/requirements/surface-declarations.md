**Status** · New 4 Nov 2026: split from `op-declarations` for size (its requirement lines alone are over the 70,000-byte cap; the split proposed by `op-declarations`'s job, attempt 1, in `docs/requirements-notes/op-declarations.md` on `job/op-declarations`), accepted by the coordinator (BOB) 4 Nov 2026; K617, K624. R1–R18 are `op-declarations`'s R76, R77, R78, R79, R80, R81, R82, R83, R84, R85, R86, R87, R88, R89, R90, R91, R92, R93, copied verbatim with their marks and renumbered in the order of their old ids, meaning unchanged; each line says "moved from op-declarations:R<n>". `op-declarations`'s own restarted job removes and retires the moved lines there, naming their new ids, and never reuses them. A bare `R<n>` inside a moved line that names no module is a `op-declarations` id. The code this module owns today is `bio-plane/src/op-declarations/t51.mjs`, `declared.mjs` and the T51/T53 parts of `index.mjs`, with `t51.test.mjs`, `declared.test.mjs`, `fix3.test.mjs` and `browser.mjs`: it moves here only once `op-declarations`'s job has retired the lines; until then this module's job implements here and leaves `op-declarations`'s code to its job.

## Public

### Purpose

T51's browser acts and reads, the T51 fix round, and T53's combined read, budget classes, founder's acts and assistant ops: `BROWSER_ACTS`, `T51_SHARES`/`T51_FAMILIES`, the owners' declarations (`declared.mjs`), the screens' read waves and `screenread`, the `budget_class` rows and the assistant's ops. Pure data, like `op-declarations` (K617, K624, K651). It exports them as rows that `op-declarations` composes, the way it composes `op-capabilities`' `needsRows()` into `NEEDS`.
