## Public

### Purpose

The matter screen, with its own `SCREENS` and the `screen.matter.*` words. It uses `screens-action` (`matter.mjs` imports `readOnly`, `WRITE_HELP`, `actName`, `citeOf` and `later` from its `send-steps.mjs`).

### Provides

- **R1** (moved from screens-action:R6) Reads the matter's determination (`determination`: the act, the standard it is tested against, what it requires and what the act did, its outcome, compliant or not, recorded with the same care either way), its consequences (`consequencesof`: who or what, the measure and how it is known, an undetermined measure never read as zero, people counted as a class), and its escalation (`escalation`, `escalationstatus`: the stages Documentation, Notification, Clock starts, Public, Formal, Political, Legal, the proposed next stage and its trigger, what ends it, and the actions attached). Acts: `escalationopen`; `escalationadvance` "Advance to stage <n>"; `declinetoescalate` "Decline to escalate, with a reason"; `escalationend` "End" (terminal, its dialog stating what ending does); `escalationstoppursuing` "Stop pursuing, with a reason"; `escalationreopen` "Reopen where it stopped" (with a reason). The legal tools a stage makes available are listed as facts from the profile, never as advice.
- **R2** (moved from screens-action:R36) (C45; amends R6) **The matter's "what actions are available by tier" draws one row for each kind.** The answer `filings` gives (`{kinds, determinations, says}`) is read as the object it is, never wrapped in a list, so a matter whose escalation answers three kinds draws three rows and not one blank row. Test: a fixture answering `{kinds, determinations, says}` draws one row per kind. Control: no answer draws no such section.
- **R3** (moved from screens-action:R45) (C41; DEC-374; NEW) **The matter screen draws a determination and its absence.** With no determination yet it says so and draws the escalation's acts (open, attach, hold, pause, record a response) as unavailable, each saying why; it offers "Record a determination" and, on a recorded one, a revision (`determine`: the act, what it rests on, the rule it is measured against and what it requires and what was done, compliant, noncompliant or unclear for each rule, an optional cause, and for a revision the reason it replaces the earlier one), each sent with the fields `conformance` declares. Test: each state; `determine` sends the declared fields; the acts shown unavailable say why (R20's style). Control: a recorded determination draws "Record a determination" as a revision, never an edit. (T51 K4259, wording) `matter` also opens on a project id: it reads the project's live determinations, draws their absence and offers "Record a determination" (`determine`). (T51 fix round 6, G20; S11; amends R45) **The matter's pickers over held documents say when they show part.** The matter's pickers over the project's held documents (the passage the act rests on, the event it was part of) read them page after page by `heldcaptures`' `next` (at most five pages of 200, each page once) and say, by the word `screen.held.more`, how many are shown when more are held than were read (the cap reached, or a later page failed); the event picker asks `timeline` about at most 200 captures (`events` R53's bound) and says so likewise. Test: 250 documents held, the 250th is offered; 1,200 held, at most five reads and the count said; a later page failing is said, not read as the end.
- **R4** (moved from screens-action:R50) (C42; DEC-374; own id, K874) **Consequences are recorded, revised and addressed, and a success recognised.** The matter screen draws `consequencerecord` (who or what was affected, what it is measured in, the amount and period, how it is known, the question showing the harm follows), `consequencerevise` (with a reason), `addressedrecord` (addressed or not, in what way, with evidence and a reason) and `successrecord` (what changed, when, evidence, the order of events, an optional cause), each sent with the fields its owner declares; `escalationend` refused while compliance is not restored or consequences are undetermined or not addressed is drawn by its words. Test: each act sends the declared fields; each refusal is drawn by its words. Control: no determination, no consequence form.
- **R5** (moved from screens-action:R51) (C43; DEC-374, DEC-376; own id, K874) **The escalation's attach, hold, pause, resume and evaluate are drawn.** `escalationattach` ("Attach an action to this stage", with the stage's purpose where the design draws one), `escalationdecline` ("Hold at this stage, with a reason", which keeps the proposal in view and is a different act from `declinetoescalate`, "Decline to escalate"), `escalationsuspend` ("Pause, with a reason"), `escalationresume` and `escalationevaluate` ("Record the government's response"); stages are named in words, never by number. Test: each act sends its declared fields; "Hold at this stage" and "Decline to escalate" are two acts. Control: a paused escalation draws "Resume" and no stage proposal.
- **R6** (moved from screens-action:R58) (T51 fix round 10; DEC-438 (UX-DESIGN U480); K5299) Matter draws `screen.matter.event.none` when the act has no dated event (with the way to record one on the Timeline), `screen.matter.determine.needscase` when no finding is held to rest a determination on, and `screen.matter.concluded.unpublished` (walk C C-M11); its body field is a choice among the team's office and body entities sending the entity id, helped by `screen.matter.f.body.help` (R56 item 2 and N5 already send the id: confirm and add the help). Test: each sentence in its case through the world's real reads; controls: absent when the condition does not hold.
- **R7** (moved from screens-action:R71) (T53; JOURNEYS; changes R45, R58; answers R-68, matter half, BLOCKS; the timeline half is T52-74) **A matter with a dated event and a determination offers the escalation.** Once the act has a dated event and a determination, the matter draws "Open an escalation" and "Decline to escalate" (reasoned) with what each does; "NOT AVAILABLE YET" gives its reason and the step that clears it; "This project has no dated event yet" is drawn only when none exists. Test: a project with a dated event and a noncompliant determination draws both acts and neither sentence; with none, the sentences and the way to record one; control: `escalation` R27's decline records and the matter draws "declined".
