# acquisition-owed — requirements

**Status** · Split from `acquisition` on 5 Nov 2026 (BOB, coordinator standing rule of 5 Nov 2026; the op-grades-folds pattern), to bring `build/requirements/acquisition.md` under the requirements cap. The requirements below are copied from `build/requirements/acquisition.md` as it stands on `job/acquisition` verbatim, each line, sub-bullet and mark unchanged, under the same numbers, so a test that names "R96: ..." stays true. They are owed work and the member's wait (open captures, the whole keep, renders pending and failed, parked fetches, deliveries, the registrar, the profile's range reads, one text rule and decode, links, a site that asks for a person), as the job proposed (`docs/requirements-notes/acquisition.md` on `job/acquisition`); the act, its arms, what it records and the Invariants R25 to R30 stay in `acquisition`, whose terms these requirements use. Their change history is the one kept in `docs/requirements-notes/acquisition.md`. Until a job of this module moves the code they rule, it lives in `bio-plane/src/acquisition/`.

## Public

- **R96** (fix round 3, C5; K4356; amends R10, R11, R95; `extraction` R80) **A capture of several parts is kept whole, once, with no read-back.** A capture R10 stores in parts is also kept whole as one object under the digest of the whole document, put once when the body ends from the bytes this call already holds: no part is read back from the object store and the parts are not hashed a second time. A body too large to hold in memory is written to the object store as one whole object by the store's own multipart upload, each part sent once as it arrives and none read back. No size is a reason to keep a capture in parts only or to refuse it: a capture R10 admits (at most `CAPTURE_MAX`) is kept whole, and one the store itself cannot hold as one object is refused in words that say so, with nothing filed. The time spent keeping the whole is stage `keep` (R95), apart from `read`. A second press of the same capture while the first is under way leaves one whole object. Test: a 16 MiB capture in three parts, against a store whose `put` takes 1.5 s, is answered inside the door's bound with one whole object under the whole digest and each part under its own; `keep` is timed apart from `read`; a second `acquire` begun while the first runs leaves one object; a capture too large for memory is kept whole and `op=reading` reads it. Control: a single-part capture is kept as before and states no `keep` stage; a capture the store cannot hold is refused in words and files nothing. (fix round 4, C5c; K4429; `store-assembly` R62) **The keep is awaited within the bound, else handed to the plane's alarm.** The put of the whole (and a body too large for memory, its multipart upload) is awaited by `acquire` within what is left of the door's bound (stage `keep`); a keep that will not end within it is registered as pending in the plane (`keeps_pending`, R75) and finished by the plane's alarm, and the answer says the capture is kept in parts and the whole follows (`keep_pending: true`); no keep relies on a promise that nothing keeps alive after the answer, and none relies on `opts.waitUntil`. Test, with the real `record-core` `putFromParts` and a request that ends before the keep: a 40 MB capture in three parts (past `MULTIPART_KEEP_MAX`) answers in time with `keep_pending`, and within a minute of the plane's alarm the whole is held and `readHeld` (`extraction` R80) reads it. Control: a capture whose keep ends within the bound answers without `keep_pending`. (fix round 7, G79; K4736) `keepWhole` takes `compose: true`: with no buffers the store is asked to make the whole from the stored parts (`putFromParts`), so no part is read back and this module puts no whole; a store that refuses or offers none is read back as before. `keepPast` takes `defer: true`: the keep is handed to the ledger at once (`keep_pending`) and not started; with no ledger, or one that refuses, it is awaited within the bound as before. Both are opt-in (capture passes them, `capture` R103). Test: `r7-t51.test.mjs`.

- **R46** (T50; N1094; DEC-240 (D3); K3317; amends R12, R19; fix round 6, G2: R105) **A member's open captures: `openCaptures(store, {by, now}) → {ok: true, captures, truncated}`** (`op=opencaptures`, a read), for Add's "Unfinished" (`capture.unfinished`, with "Finish it", which is `acquire` with `continue`, R12). `captures` lists, newest started first, at most `OPEN_CAPTURES_MAX` (50, exported) with `truncated`, each capture session (capture R22) that has not expired at `now` (an instant; default this instance's clock) and was begun by `by`: `{session, locator, primary_sha, started, updated, expires, outstanding}`, `session` the id R12 resumes, `primary_sha` the filed primary's digest (the screen titles the capture from that document), `outstanding` the number of supporting files still to fetch (at least 1: a complete walk parks no session, R19). A capture parked as a pending fetch (R105) is listed too, with `outstanding: 1`, `primary_sha: null` and the key `fetch_pending {since, declared_bytes, why}`; no other capture carries that key. A session is begun by the member whose member session called `acquire` when R19 parked it, and R19 records that member with the session; a session parked by any other caller, or before this requirement, has no member and is listed for no one, and a session whose state cannot be read (R12) is not listed. A `by` that is absent, not a string, blank or a machine identity answers `captures: []`. It lists nothing of another member's, writes nothing and never throws; a store that cannot answer gives `captures: []`. Test: a member session's walk that parks a session lists it for that member with `outstanding` equal to the files left, and for no other member; after expiry it is not listed; a complete walk lists nothing. Control: a daemon-parked session, a session whose state is unreadable and a `by` that is blank or `class:admin` list nothing; `acquire` with `continue` for a listed session still resumes it (R12). A refused fetch is listed one day after its failure (`FAILED_LISTED_MS`), and pressing it again replaces it (K4887).

- **R99** (fix round 4, C13b; K4429; amends R12) **A continuation with no live render starts the render afresh.** `acquire` with `continue: <session>` for a session whose render is no longer held by this call or this object (the object restarted, or the call that began the render ended) starts the render again from the session's primary, and answers the second document as a finished render does, or a refusal in words that says the page could not be rendered and closes the session; it never answers "already in the record, nothing new saved" for a session whose render was never finished. Test: render deferred, the in-memory render table reset, `continue` files the visitor copy or closes the session in words. Control: a continuation with its render live finishes it as before.

- **R100** (fix round 4b, F5, F11; K4487, K4488, K4489, K4492) **Owed capture work is never dropped.** A web capture's outstanding supporting files and its pending render are owed work: the session is parked for 30 days (`OWED_SESSION_TTL_MS`) and the plane's ledger holds `{kind: subresources, sha, session}` (hook `owedLater`, handed at composition); `owedRun(store, entry, opts)` continues the files and finishes the render (delivering its document through `opts.deliver`), answers `{ok, done, outstanding?, final?, reason?}`, and drops the session (`dropCaptureSession`) when the work is done or final, never at the session's hour. An automatic unpack that stopped for the call's share, on a daily budget or at a nested archive registers `{kind: unpack, sha}`; a tree or depth stop is not owed (a member's act, R40, stated by R41). `keepPast` is the `acquire` arm's keep, exported for capture. Test: 120 images over a 100-subrequest ceiling, 61 minutes later all fetched by `owedRun`, none twice, the session dropped. (fix round 7, G72; K4736, K4748) `owedRun` ends a visitor view whose render failed with `{ok: true, done: true, final: true, reason: "RENDER_FAILED", detail}` (`detail` the render's problem, at most 200 characters, the renderer's own words, no page text) and drops the session, so `opencaptures` no longer lists it; `acquire` writes one log line `{"event":"render_failed","problem","ms"}` where a render fails. The plane's ledger row keeps the reason alone (J31 answered (c)). Test: `r7-render-failed.test.mjs`.

- **R101** (T51 fix round 5, E1; amends R17; K4537) **A capture of any size is profiled from range reads of its head and its tail.** `profileOf` for a capture held in several parts, or held whole past the bytes read back, judges its `format` from range reads through `readRange` (R113) of its head and of its tail, never from a 1 KiB prefix alone (`format-registry.detectFormat` over those bytes, an image asked before a PDF or a web page, an office or OpenDocument file recognised by the directory at its tail, else the declared type with the absence stated). An office file past 8 MiB, in one part or several, is stored with its own `profile.format` (`docx`, not `undetermined`). A PDF or office file uploaded with no declared type is not `undetermined`; bytes of no known format stay `undetermined`; a first part that is not there is stated, never a failed profile. `profileOf` takes an optional `unread`, whose `reason` it sets when the store cannot read the range; the format is decided from the head's signatures, the end record and the central directory, read through the one range reader behind R113 (K4887).

- **R102** (T51 fix round 5, E5; K4537) **One rule for a member's file as text: `textTypeOf({bytes, type, name})`.** Answers the text content type to read the bytes as: the declared `type` when it is a text type, or, when none is declared, the type a name stands for in `csv-reader`'s table of delimited names and in `.txt` and `.md` (text/plain, text/markdown, text/csv, and text/tab-separated-values for `.tsv` and `.tab`, the names `csv-reader` reads as delimited text); only for bytes `ooxml.decodeText` decodes (R120; UTF-8, UTF-16, a declared or detected legacy charset) and within `PROFILE_TEXT_MAX`; else null. A declared type that is not text is never overridden by a name. Capture's upload and the doorbell's pull call it and keep no copy of the rule. (Fix round 10, cause 12; K5053) The text names are those of `csv-reader`'s delimited table, so a `.tsv` or `.tab` member of an archive is read as text as a standalone file is. Test: a ZIP holding `m01.txt`, `m02.tsv` and `m03.csv`: `textTypeOf` gives each its text type, each member reads whole with the cells of the standalone file; control: bytes with a NUL under a `.tsv` name answer null.

- **R103** (T51 fix round 5, E10; amends R95; K4537) **`read` is split.** `timings` carries `read_split: {download, hash, store}` once the body is being read: whole ms of the source's download, the hashing and the store's writes, within `stages.read` (their sum no more than it), on every outcome from then on (a throw and a cut call included), and no key before.

- **R104** (T51 fix round 5, E8; amends R12, R99, R100; K4537) **The visitor copy is owed until its document is filed.** When a member's `continue` completes a pending render and the plane's ledger was handed in, the capture session stays, owing the finished document (`render_done`, out of "Unfinished"), instead of being dropped; `owedRun` closes it once the register holds the copy's digest or after delivering it through `deliver`, a refused delivery leaving it owed. A second `continue` on such a session changes nothing and answers `render: {state: "owed"}`. A `continue` naming a session that is gone, with no address of its own, answers 404 `NO_SUCH_SESSION`; with an address of its own it is an ordinary capture (R12). New data only.

- **R105** (T51 fix round 6, G2; K4562; amends R9, R10, R12, R46, R100) **A large or slow web capture is owed work, not the member's wait.** For a member session's web capture (not the capture-request arm, the archive arm or the render arm), with the plane's ledger handed in: a response that declares a `Content-Length` over `LARGE_FETCH_BYTES` (8 MiB) is parked before any of its body is read; a body of no declared length read past 8 MiB, and any body read for longer than `FETCH_WAIT_MS` (10 s), is parked at that point and the reader cancelled (the parts stored so far are found again by the owed read). Parked means: a capture session (`opened_by` the member, `project` from the body, the body's own keys, `fetch_pending {since, declared_bytes (null with no length), why}` with `why` `LARGE_DECLARED`, `LARGE_BODY` or `SLOW_BODY`), registered in the ledger as `{kind: "fetch", session}`, and the answer is `200 {ok: true, fetch_pending: {session, since, declared_bytes, why}}` with no `document`; a second press by the same member for the same address answers the same session; `continue` on it answers its state; `openCaptures` (R46) lists it with `outstanding: 1`, `primary_sha: null` and `fetch_pending`. A declared length over `CAPTURE_MAX` is refused `TOO_LARGE` at once and nothing is parked; a ledger that refuses the entry, or none, reads the body in the call as before. `owedFetch(store, {kind: "fetch", session}, {deliver})` makes the capture as the member's own `acquire` would have (their stamps and body), reads it whole, keeps the document in the session and hands `{ok, existed, document}` to `deliver({answer, session})`; a refused delivery is delivered by the next run without fetching again; a busy source or a broken body goes again; any other refusal ends the entry final and leaves the session stating `fetch_pending.failed`; an accepted delivery drops the session. Test and controls as fix6-t51.test.mjs. New data only. (fix round 7, G85; K4748) The saved session of a made fetch keeps `fetch_pending {since, declared_bytes, why}` beside `fetch_done`, on the first delivery and on the retry after a refusal. Test: `r7-fetchdone.test.mjs`.

- **R106** (T51 fix round 6, G3; amends R12, R99, R104) **"Finish it" renders nowhere in the request.** With the plane's ledger handed in, `acquire` with `continue` for a session whose render is pending looks at the run it holds without waiting: a run that has returned is finished and its document answered; any other case registers the session's owed row again, due now, and answers at once `render: {state: "pending"}`; the alarm's run renders and delivers (R100, R104). With no ledger, or one that refuses the entry, the call waits and renders as R99 states. Test: the object restarted, the render taking 15 s: the press answers pending in under a second, starts no render and registers the row; the alarm's run files the visitor copy once. Controls as fix6-t51.test.mjs. New data only.

- **R108** (T51 fix round 6, G19; K4571 S10; amends R19, R6) **A capture states what of its supporting files was kept.** The answer of `acquire` (every continuation tick included) and the document's `capture` facts carry `supporting: {kept, skipped, total, reasons, not_walked?}` from the records the walk and the render keep: `reasons` the distinct reasons a file was not kept with their counts, a bound's figure as `limit`, the renderer's words as `detail`; `not_walked` the reason a walk asked for was not made. Absent when none was asked for. Test: a page of 450 supporting files answers kept 400, skipped 50, reason `CAP_REACHED`, limit 400. Controls as fix6-t51.test.mjs.

- **R109** (fix round 6, G27; K4608; amends R5, R12, R99, R106) **A render this call did not wait for is kept in the record when it returns.** Its page and each body it loaded are put in the evidence store and the renderer's reply is dropped: what the pending render holds is the page's digest, the bodies' digests and the reply without its bytes. "Finish it" and the alarm file from them the pair a waited render files (the same document, one render, one charge). A failed render is small and kept as it came; a record that refuses a piece leaves the reply held, so no render is lost; a kept page the record no longer holds is a lost render (R99). Test: `g27-g32-t51.test.mjs`; platform check `g27-workerd.probe.mjs`.

- **R110** (fix round 8, G103, G104; K4817; amends R46) **Open captures are matched by the member, until delivered.** `by` is matched against the session's member through `member-roster`'s one viewer predicate, so the viewer's form (`member:<id>`) and the bare id name the same member (the founder's `admin` as before); a fetch made but not yet delivered (queue empty, `fetch_done` present) is listed with `outstanding: 1`, `primary_sha: null` and `fetch_pending` marked `delivering: true` until its delivery drops it; a finished visitor copy (`render_done`, no `fetch_done`) and an expired session are listed for no one; a machine identity, blank or non-string `by` lists nothing. Test: a session parked with `opened_by: "m1"` is listed for `member:m1` and for `m1`, not for `member:m2`; a made, undelivered session is listed; through the door, an enrolled member's large capture is listed at every poll from the press to the delivery.

- **R111** (fix round 8, G105; K4817; amends R105) **A delivery refused for good ends in words.** In `owedFetch` and the two render-delivery paths, a delivery refused with a code in `DELIVERY_FINAL` (exported: `CAPTURE_HELD_BY_ANOTHER_BUNDLE`, `OVERSIZE_INLINE`, `TOO_LARGE`, `PROMOTE_NOT_A_PARTICIPANT`) ends the entry final (`{ok: true, done: true, final: true, reason: <code>}`) with that code as its `why`; `owedFetch` saves the session as a fetch that ended before it was made (`queue: [{fetch: true}]`, no `fetch_done`, `fetch_pending.failed` the code), so `opencaptures` lists it with `failed` and never as delivering, and the two render paths drop the session. Any other refusal still goes again with the ledger's back-off and keeps the reason it gave (`DELIVERY_FAILED` stays `DELIVERY_FAILED`; an unnamed refusal is `DELIVERY_REFUSED`). Replaces R105's "a refused delivery is delivered by the next run" for those codes. Test: `CAPTURE_HELD_BY_ANOTHER_BUNDLE` ends final with the code at each of the three sites, and each code of `DELIVERY_FINAL` does; `DELIVERY_FAILED` goes again with its reason kept; control: an accepted delivery drops the session and ends done.

- **R112** (fix round 8, G107; K4817; amends R5) **A render's keep fits the bar.** With `render: true`, keeping the render's loaded bodies is inside the render's share of the call's budget, so `acquire` answers within `ACQUIRE_BAR_MS` less `PROMOTE_RESERVE_MS`; for a member's `render: true` request under the bar (not the capture-request arm, not a caller that names `renderWaitMs`, not a render begun afresh) the page and its loaded bodies are kept and awaited for what the bar leaves for the render less `KEEP_FOLLOWING_MS` (1000 ms, at most a quarter of that share; `opts.postKeepMs` may hand another). A keep that has not ended leaves the render pending as R5 states, the keep going on from the record ("Finish it" or the alarm files the pair from it); a keep that ended is used in place, with no read-back. What is kept and read is unchanged. Test: a renderer returning at 8.8 s with 90 bodies over a store charging 270 ms a call answers within 11.5 s (the test's clock); controls: no bodies, as before; a caller naming `renderWaitMs`, and the alarm's finishing of a pending render, are not cut.

- **R115** (fix round 9, G124b; amends R96) **A multipart capture is not held twice.** While a body streams, the call holds in memory no more than the bytes it keeps (R114's `MULTIPART_KEEP_MAX`) plus the one part being read, counted by the byte ledger (`runtime-limits`), and it never forms a second copy of the whole: when the store offers no `compose` the whole is made from the stored parts as R96's keep states, not joined in memory. Test: `acquire` of a 20 MiB multipart capture against a store with no `compose` peaks, by the ledger, at no more than the kept parts plus one part (red today: a buffer of the whole's size beside the kept parts); a 24 MiB text capture stays under the object's cap with the ledger counting. A body that declares no length, or overruns the length it declares, holds no bytes once a second part shows it is several: the parts are already stored, and the whole is made from them by reading them back one at a time into the one buffer of the whole (the call holds the whole once plus one part, counted by the ledger), never joined from held parts; this amends R96's "no read-back" for such a body only (a body received into the one buffer its declared length asked for is read back by no one). A PDF's title (R98) is read only where the whole is held in one buffer (a single part, or a body received into the buffer its declared length asked for) and is otherwise `null` at capture, named by its reading. Test (also): the same 20 MiB capture served with no length, with a shorter and with a longer declared length peaks at no more than the kept bytes plus one part and the ledger counted the kept bytes (`peak() >= kept`, so a module that does not meter reads red). Control: a single-part capture is held and kept as before.

- **R116** (fix round 9, G126, G126f, G126g; K4835 (2); 2c item 1) **Work this module owes is one job row, written through the registrar.** `acquire` and `unpack` register the work they owe through the callback `registerJob`, read as `keepLater` is (the store handed in's `registerJob` when it is a function, else `opts.registerJob`, else the call goes on without a row and says so in its answer, never silently), and import nothing from a later module. Kinds: `keep` (R96's keep that did not end in the bound, keyed by the whole sha), `fetch` (R105's parked fetch, keyed by its capture session), `subresources` (R100's outstanding supporting files, keyed by the session) and `unpack` (the open of an archive: an `acquire` whose profile names `zip`, R40, registers it with the archive's whole sha before it answers; a continuation registers none). The registration is made inside the synchronous function the caller gives `core.transact`, so the row commits or rolls back with the write that files the capture; arming the plane's alarm is a separate step after the commit. A `registerJob` that answers `{ok: false}` is stated in the answer as a refusal, or thrown inside the transaction so that it rolls back, never swallowed. Registration is idempotent: a second press of the same capture leaves one row. The "ledger" of R96, R100, R105 and R106 is this job table, and `owedRun` and `owedFetch` read and finish its rows. Test, on test-support's `jobRegistrarStub`: an `acquire` of a large web page, of a capture whose keep passes the bound, of a page with outstanding supporting files and of a ZIP each leaves exactly one row of its kind and sha; a kill between the put and the transaction leaves one row and no inline read; a refusing registrar is stated. Control: a small capture that owes nothing registers nothing. A registration the registrar refuses is counted in the answer's `jobs.not_registered` (K4887). **Amended (fix round 9, G160; K4980):** `owedFetch`'s final answers carry the refusal's facts as the refusing module gave them (`kind`, `capture_sha`, `holder`), not `reason` alone. The facts are taken from the refusal's `detail` when it is a plain object, else from its top level, and a refusal with none adds no `detail`. Test: `r123-batch-d.test.mjs`. **Amended (fix round 9, G175; K5005):** every row `registerJob` accepts is followed, after the commit, by the plane's `armJobs` (the store handed in's, else `opts.armJobs`), as capture and extraction arm theirs, so owed work is due at once and never waits on an alarm set for later. Test: right after `acquire` answers `fetch_pending`, the founder's `owedwork` shows `alarm_at` within 60 s of the row; control-plane's `t51-round7-seam` "G61 twin" green alone.

- **R117** (fix round 9, G126g, X34; amends R99, R100, R106) **A pending render survives a restart.** A render this call did not wait for is held as a row of the job table (R116), not in memory: the object restarted between the render's start and its delivery still finds it, finishes it and delivers the document once. R99's afresh start is for a render the row no longer holds. Test: DOCS V4 and V7 with `restartAt` between the render's start and its delivery: the row survives and delivers; control: a render that ends within the bound leaves no row. The run's answer (`keepReply`'s summary, or a failed render's `{ok: false, error, failure_class}`) is written into the capture session the row names as `render_pending.answer`, never past `RENDER_ANSWER_MAX` 2 MiB of JSON, so "Finish it" after a restart files the pair at once with no second render, reservation or charge; a session gone or no longer pending is never written (K4887).

- **R118** (fix round 9, G137c, X23; K4831 (10); amends R5, R100) **A failed visitor render is charged its real time and judged by what came back.** The reservation of a failed render is released with its real elapsed time (and the close), as every other path's is (R5), so the day's allowance counts the time spent and not the whole reservation, and a day of failed renders does not switch visitor views off. A render whose page answered a status of 400 or more, whose location is a `chrome-error://` page or whose page is an empty shell is `RENDER_FAILED` stated as "the page did not render", and nothing is filed in its place as the document. A failure is retried by its class: `transient` (a session refused, three a second, the open bound) up to three times with back-off, `permanent` final with its detail (R100's `final`), never one `final` for every cause. The copy states what it is: the first load, not scrolled, banners not dismissed, no frames or shadow content; scrolling, shadow DOM and frames are stated limits (`capture-sources` R69, R71). A page whose serialised DOM is over the render-DOM bound (`READ_BOUNDS`, proposed 25 MiB, BOB records) is not filed: `RENDER_FAILED` stated with its size and the bound. Test: DOCS V4–V7 on an offline origin: a failed render charged its elapsed time; a 403 and a `chrome-error://` page not filed as the document; a transient failure retried and a permanent one final; the 12 s page delivered by the owed path. Control: a render that succeeds is charged as before.

- **R119** (fix round 9, G138, S15; amends R108) **Web pictures kept as bytes are said to be unread.** The answer's `supporting` (R108) and the document's `capture` facts also carry `pictures_unread: {count, bytes}`: the number and total size of the picture bodies kept and not read, so the document says "N pictures kept, not read"; absent when the page has none. Control: a page with no pictures carries no such key. `pictures_unread` is a field of `supporting` (K4887).

- **R120** (fix round 9, G131g, X9; BOB's under K4628) **One decode for every text this module reads.** Every decode of a capture's bytes as text here (the profile's text, R102's text test, the title R98 reads) is `ooxml.decodeText`'s, so an upload, a fetch and a part agree: UTF-8 (BOM or not), UTF-16 with or without BOM, the declared or detected legacy charset (windows-1252, ISO-8859-x); the `charset` of a header or a meta tag is the declaration it is given. A text it cannot decode is `ENCODING_UNSUPPORTED {name}` in the profile, never replacement characters with no failure. Test: the encoding matrix at 100 KB, 3 MiB and 9 MiB, fetched: the title and the profile's text equal the truth or state the refusal. Control: UTF-8 is decoded as before. The encoding found is stated as `profile.text_encoding` (K4887).

- **R123** (K5157; walk-T51-final 06 §9) Every HTML capture files its page's own links (capture R27) with no fetching, whether or not supporting files are asked; `subresources: true` adds only the walk of supporting files (R19).

- **R140** (T53; K5574, K5575; K3656; R9) **A site that asks for a person.** A fetch acquisition makes (for `acquire`, a capture request, a register or a keyed read) that is answered by a challenge or a CAPTCHA in place of the document (a `cf-mitigated: challenge` header, or a challenge or CAPTCHA page as the body) is never filed as the document: nothing is written but the count below, and the fetch is refused `SITE_ASKS_FOR_PERSON` (acquisition's own family), naming the address so the caller can hand it to a member. The check is never worked around and no member's pass is carried to the server (K3656). Each such answer adds one to its host's count, kept by acquisition with the time of the last; `personChecksFor(store, {host})` answers `{host, count, last_at}` (module-only; Bob decides a browser extension from these counts, K5575). Test: a fetch answered with `cf-mitigated: challenge`, and one answered with a CAPTCHA page, each refuse `SITE_ASKS_FOR_PERSON` with the address, file no capture, and raise the host's count by one. Control: an ordinary answer from the same host is filed and leaves the count unchanged.
