## Public

- **R88** (amends R24, R28; T47; N971; `setup-page` R5, retired with no home at T47's L11 fold, K3100) `COUNTERPARTY_LEVELS` is exported: `state`, `county`, `city`, `district`, in that order, frozen, the one vocabulary of an office's level (R24), which `validate` reads (a counterparty `level` outside it is `LEVEL_UNKNOWN`) and a caller offering an office's level imports and repeats none of. It is not a law's level (R31).

- **R89** (T48; N847; DEC-227 (3)) `pointers`: `[{kind, name, does, contacts, hours?, languages?, source, status, basis}]`, the places outside Civicsmith a member is sent to when what she needs is a matter hers alone (`wizard-scripts`' `elsewhere` route), researched per place like every other fact and never a member's data. `kind` is one of the frozen exported `POINTER_KINDS`, in this order: `legalaid`, `selfhelp`, `service`, `agency`, `representative`, `tipline` (a new kind is a requirement change). `name` is the body's own name, one line of 1 to 200 characters. `does` says what the body does and for whom, in the profile's words, 1 to 500 characters. `contacts` is a non-empty list of `{how, value}` as R32 has them, `how` one of `web`, `email`, `phone`, `mail` and `address` (a street address, for a pointer only). `hours` is R42's form. `languages` is a non-empty list of tags `isLocale` accepts (R37). `source` is the address or citation read, non-empty. `status` is `researched` and `basis` a dated measurement entry (`YYYY-MM-DD`, optionally qualified, R2), so the date read can be shown with the pointer; in a test profile, `TEST`. R44's rule holds: a pointer without a source is not written, and `UNMEASURED`, a `ruled` status or a basis with no date is `BASIS_INVALID`. A profile may hold several pointers of one kind and none of another. `pointers` is a known section, never `UNKNOWN_SECTION`.

- **R90** (T48; N847; R89; amends R14, R28, R35) `validate` names a malformed pointer by its field: a `kind` outside `POINTER_KINDS` is `KIND_INVALID`; an empty or over-long `name` or `does` is `VALUE_INVALID`; an empty `contacts`, a `how` outside R89's list or an empty `value` is `CONTACT_INVALID`; a malformed `hours` is `HOURS_INVALID`; a language `isLocale` refuses, or an empty `languages`, is `LOCALE_INVALID`; no `source` is `SOURCE_MISSING`; a `status` or `basis` outside R89 is `BASIS_INVALID`. In `combine`, `pointers` are unioned in the order given (R14): an entry equal to an earlier one in everything but its basis and `profile` is kept once, carrying both.

- **R95** (T51 fix round 3, C64; DEC-402; ENTITIES #18's finding (a); K4407; amends R7, R76) **The federal records law.** The first profile's `records_laws` also holds the federal Freedom of Information Act (`level: federal`, citation 5 U.S.C. § 552), with its stages (R76) and requesters, each `researched` with its basis, so a member naming a federal office from a request (`entities` R63) can name it as the office's law. Test: `governingLawsOf` and the profile check accept it; control: an unlisted law is still refused.

- **R96** (fix round 4, C64-law; K4438; amends R7, R76) **A law names its own first-request window.** A `records_laws` entry may carry `response`: the `rule` of the `deadlines` entry that is that law's window for a first request, which must exist and apply to `records_request`, else `VALUE_INVALID`; one value per key under the law's `name` (R15). The first profile sets it: the California Public Records Act `records_response`, the Sunshine Ordinance `immediate_disclosure`, the federal Freedom of Information Act `foia_response` (5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(6)(A)(i)). A screen draws a law's window from its `response` row and never guesses. Test: the first profile's FOIA entry names `foia_response`, whose citation is (A)(i); control: a `response` naming no deadline is refused.

- **R97** (fix round 4, C64-law; K4438; amends R26) **A deadline has words.** A `deadlines` entry may carry `label`, the rule's name in the profile's words (FOIA: "answer to a first request" for `foia_response`, "decision on an appeal" for `foia_appeal_response`), read from the same statute text as R95; one value per key under `rule` and `applies_to` (R15); absent, a screen says it has no words for it (R27). Test: the first profile's FOIA deadlines carry their labels and combine; control: a label on no rule is refused.

- **R98** (T51 fix round 10; K5292; local-facts R22) `governingZone(profileIds, teamZone)`, exported: a pure function answering the team's governing time zone: `teamZone` when it is an R41 value (an IANA name or `UTC`); else the `time_zone` value of `combine(profileIds)`'s view when `profileIds` is a non-empty list that combines; else `null`. It never throws: a list that does not combine, or any other input, answers by these rules.

- **R99** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; K5452; amends R1, R10, R11) **A profile may be a layer.** A profile may carry `layer: {level, place, within?, origin}`: `level` one of `LAW_LEVELS` (R31), `place` the place's name (1 to 200 characters, one line), `within` the `id` of the layer it sits in, a layer of a higher level (absent on a federal layer; a city may sit within a county, within a state, or within the federal layer when its state is not held), `origin` `civicsmith` or `team`; `level` and `place` are absent on the team layer alone (R115). Several Civicsmith layers of one level are valid (several counties); a team holds the one team layer. Names a layer gives to a rule, list, law or office are unique within the layer (in the team layer, within the government an entry is about, R115) and addressed across layers as `<layer id>:<name>`; an unqualified name resolves to the asking layer's own, else to one in its branch, else to the one active layer that gives it, and when two active layers give it `NAME_AMBIGUOUS` (never a guess). A profile with no `layer` is valid and read as today (the test profile, a whole-place profile). A malformed `layer` is `LAYER_INVALID`, naming the field. Test: a layer of each level validates; a level outside `LAW_LEVELS`, an empty `place`, an `origin` outside the two, a federal layer with `within`, and a city `within` a layer of its own or a lower level are each `LAYER_INVALID`; two city layers each giving a list named `city` are addressed `a:city` and `b:city`, and the bare `city` from a third layer is `NAME_AMBIGUOUS`. Control: a profile with no `layer` validates exactly as before.

- **R100** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; amends R31, R88, R11) **A layer holds only its own level.** In a layer of level L, every `records_laws` and `standard_sources` entry that carries a level (R31) carries L, and every counterparty's `level` (R24, R88) is L, or `district` in a city or county layer. Otherwise `LAYER_LEVEL_MISMATCH`, naming the entry. A fact that carries no level of its own (a deadline, a holiday year, a pointer) is placed by its citation and is judged by R113's test, not by `validate`. The team layer has no level and is not judged by level as a layer; each of its entries carries `government: {level, place}` (R115) and the same test is made against that `level`. Test: a state law inside a city layer is `LAYER_LEVEL_MISMATCH`; a team entry carrying a city law with `government.level` `county` is the same. Control: the same law in a state layer validates.

- **R101** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; K5452; amends R12) **The forest is checked by `combine`, branch by branch.** When any profile combined carries a `layer`, `combine` also checks: each layer id appears once (`LAYER_ID_TWICE`); at most one federal layer (`LAYER_FEDERAL_TWICE`); each layer's `within`, when it has one, names a layer in the list (`LAYER_PARENT_MISSING`) of a higher level (`LAYER_PARENT_LEVEL`), and following `within` reaches the federal layer without a loop (`LAYER_CYCLE`); a federal layer is present (`LAYER_NO_FEDERAL`, a refusal only when a state, county or city layer is present). Any number of layers of one level is valid (several counties, several cities, several states); a state layer with no county or city layer below it is valid (a state-wide footprint). Each branch is checked and a refusal names the branch (the layer ids from the failing layer up). The team layer (R115) is the one layer with no level or place: a second is `LAYER_TEAM_TWICE`; it is `within` the federal layer when that is active, else a root (`LAYER_NO_FEDERAL` does not apply to it); it is listed last. `view.layers` lists `{id, level, place, origin, within}` in level order, then by place. Layers combine side by side; none overrides another. Test: federal, California and Oakland with Oakland `within` a county layer not in the list is `LAYER_PARENT_MISSING` naming the branch; a county `within` a city is `LAYER_PARENT_LEVEL`; two `within` pointing at each other are `LAYER_CYCLE`; federal, California, two county layers and three city layers in any order give one view, `layers` in level order. Control: the four shipped layers in any list order give one view with `layers` in level order; federal and California alone (state-wide) is valid.

- **R102** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; K5452; amends R15, R29, R55) **One key, two layers, two answers.** Where two layers give different values for one key (R15), the fact stays withheld and reported in `conflicts`, each conflict now carrying each giver's `layer` level, `origin` and id, so a screen can say which layers disagree. `combine` chooses nothing. Two layers on different branches (neither sits in the other) never conflict on a fact addressed by layer-qualified name (R99): their rules, lists, offices and laws stand side by side. A team-wide one-value fact (`time_zone`, `locale`, `weekend`) stated differently by layers on different branches is a conflict at team level, withheld; `fiscal_year` is not team-wide, it is **the fiscal year of one government** (R123), read by government and body, so two governments' years never conflict; a rule, office or law is still read in the value of its own branch (`governingZone`), so two counties in two zones each count in their own. Test: two layers giving different `locale` values give one conflict naming both levels and ids; two city layers on different branches each giving a list `city` give no conflict; two county layers in two zones give a team-level zone conflict and each county's clock reads its own zone; two cities with different fiscal years give no conflict and each reads its own. Control: two layers giving the same value give none.

- **R103** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; amends R46, R55) **Two layers may state the same weekend.** The weekend compares equal when its `days` are equal; the citations are then joined, as `time_zone` and `locale` already agree on a value whatever the basis (measured 2026-10-27). Today `weekend` is compared whole, its `citation` included (`index.mjs:2158-2165`), so a federal layer citing 5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(6)(A)(i) and a California layer citing CCP § 12a for the same Saturday and Sunday would withhold the weekend and make every business-day count undetermined. Test: two layers with equal `days` and different citations give one `weekend` carrying both citations and no conflict. Control: different `days` still give a conflict. The same check is made for every other one-value fact (R55) before the layers are cut (F).

- **R104** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; K5452; amends R26, R47; `civil-time`) **A rule may count on a closure list a layer above it holds.** A deadline's `closures` and `observed.closures` name a list held by its own layer or by a layer in its branch (its `within` chain up to the federal layer), resolved in the combined view by layer-qualified name; never a list of a layer on another branch. `validate` of a layer alone accepts a name it does not hold only when the layer has a `within` (today `CLOSURES_UNKNOWN`, `index.mjs:1049`, 1076: "no holidays entry of this profile"); `combine` refuses a name no layer of the branch holds, `CLOSURES_UNKNOWN`. A list name is unique within its layer; the same bare name in two layers of one level (two cities each with a list `city`) is two lists, `a:city` and `b:city`, each counted by the rules of its own layer. A second layer of one branch giving a list of the same name for the same year and offices is R102's conflict. Test: a California-layer deadline counting on a list the federal layer holds validates alone only with `within`, and combines; with the federal layer absent, `CLOSURES_UNKNOWN`; a city-layer rule naming `city` counts on its own city's list when a second city layer also holds `city`. Control: a layer's rule naming its own list validates alone.

- **R105** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; K5452; amends R93, R55) **Records-request help is one block per law, and each layer adds its own fields.** `records_help` becomes a list of blocks keyed by `law` (the law's layer-qualified name in `records_laws`); a block carries the fields R93 lists, and a block for the same `law` in two layers of one branch is merged field by field: the layer that holds the law gives `days`, `extra_days`, `extension_reasons` and `citations`; a city layer may add `portal` and `faster`. Two city layers (or two county layers) each adding `portal` for one state law are two blocks, one per layer, side by side, and give no conflict; two layers of one branch giving different values of one field are R102's conflict. Today the single block conflicts when two profiles give different blocks (`index.mjs:1883-1886`). Test: a California layer's CPRA block and an Oakland layer's block holding only `portal` and `faster` combine to the block the unsplit profile gives today, field for field; a federal block for FOIA appears beside it; a second city layer's `portal` for the same law appears as its own block. Control: two layers of one branch giving different `days` for one law conflict.

- **R106** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; amends R7, R76, R96) **A records law says whose records it governs.** A `records_laws` entry carries `governs`: a non-empty list of levels of office (`federal`, `state`, `county`, `city`, `district`), each from the statute's own definition of the bodies it binds (`citation` shows it). FOIA governs `federal`; the Public Records Act governs `state`, `county`, `city`, `district`; the Sunshine Ordinance governs `city`. A screen offering the laws a request may go under reads the office's level and this field, and offers no law that does not govern it. Missing `governs` reads undetermined (R27), and every law of the layer is then offered, labelled so. Test: a city office is offered the Public Records Act and the Sunshine Ordinance and not FOIA; a federal office is offered FOIA alone. Control: a law with no `governs` is offered with the label.

- **R107** (T53; N1256; DEC-401; DEC-404; amends R24, R88) **An office may be federal.** `COUNTERPARTY_LEVELS` gains `federal`, first: `federal`, `state`, `county`, `city`, `district` (frozen, in that order). DEC-404 says offices carry a level (R88), but R88's list today has no federal level (`state`, `county`, `city`, `district`), so a federal office cannot be stated. `LEVEL_UNKNOWN` still refuses any other. Test: a counterparty at `federal` validates. Control: `national` is `LEVEL_UNKNOWN`.

- **R108** (T53; N1257; DEC-404; amends R44) **A layer says how far its facts reach.** A layer carries `coverage: {as_of, holidays_through?}`: `as_of` the date its facts were last researched (`YYYY-MM-DD`), `holidays_through` the last calendar year every closure list it holds has a year for. `view.layers` gives both. A count reaching past a list's last year is undetermined (R33) and names the layer whose list ends. Test: a federal layer holding 2026 and 2027 gives `holidays_through: 2027`; a count into 2028 is undetermined and names it. Control: a layer with no holiday list gives no `holidays_through`.
