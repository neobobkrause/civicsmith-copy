# store-jobs — requirements

**Status** · New 4 Nov 2026: split from `store-assembly` for size (its requirement lines alone are over the 70,000-byte cap; the split proposed by `store-assembly`'s job, attempt 1, in `docs/requirements-notes/store-assembly.md` on `job/store-assembly`), accepted by the coordinator (BOB) 4 Nov 2026; K617, K624. A new module, layer 11, directly before `store-assembly`, split by copy with no `from`. R1–R26 are `store-assembly`'s R60, R61, R62, R63, R64, R65, R66, R67, R68, R69, R70, R71, R72, R73, R74, R75, R76, R77, R78, R79, R80, R81, R82, R102, R103, R104, copied verbatim with their marks and renumbered in the order of their old ids, meaning unchanged; each line says "moved from store-assembly:R<n>". `store-assembly`'s own restarted job removes and retires the moved lines there, naming their new ids, and never reuses them. A bare `R<n>` inside a moved line that names no module is a `store-assembly` id. The code this module owns today is `bio-plane/src/store-assembly/pending.mjs`, `casesweep.mjs`, `acquire-timings.mjs` and `stats.mjs`: it moves here only once `store-assembly`'s job has retired the lines; until then this module's job implements here and leaves `store-assembly`'s code to its job.

## Public

### Purpose

The owed-work ledger, the job table and the object alarm of the assembled store. `store-assembly` imports them; they never import `store-assembly`.

### Provides

- **R1** (moved from store-assembly:R60) (was `plane` R73) **An acquisition's stage times are readable while it runs and after, by its trace.** The door hands its `trace` to `captureOp`; the object reads capture's `x-civicsmith-trace` header (the Store sets it in-process for a POST `acquire` and strips a caller's own); `acquiretimings?trace=` answers `{ok, stageTimes}`: the stage running and the stages that ran, held per trace in a bounded holder (64 entries, 60 s; an ended acquire's entry stays until it expires, so a late ask still reads it). Test: the whole hop (door → header → the object's timings under the trace → the read), while running and after; controls: another trace, none, an address trace, the bound.
- **R2** (moved from store-assembly:R61) (was `plane` R74) The door's owner getters (`ownerOps`' `of`, R86) also hand `extraction` (`extractionOf(ctx)`, for `readHeld`, extraction R80) and `projectRoster` (`projectRosterOf(ctx)`, for `projectList`'s `sharedDraftsOf`, project-roster R42), each asked only when its op is served. Test: through the real store, the door's `readheld` arm reaches extraction's one instance and `projectlist` answers `shared_drafts` from review; control: neither getter is called for another op.
- **R3** (moved from store-assembly:R62) (was `plane` R75) **The object's alarm finishes what was left pending, and the stage record survives a restart.** The object keeps `keeps_pending` (captures kept in parts whose whole is not yet put, `acquisition` R96) and the digests whose reading was deferred (`control-plane` R120); `alarm()` (`plane` R4) finishes them, each once, within a minute of being registered, and writes the reading by `extraction.readHeld`; a pending entry is removed only when its work has ended, and a failure leaves it for the next alarm up to a stated attempt count. The stage record of an acquisition (R60) is held in the object's storage and not only in memory, so `acquiretimings?trace=` answers the stages that ran after the object restarted. Test: a keep left pending is finished by the alarm and its reading follows; after the object is restarted `acquiretimings?trace=` still answers the stages. Control: with nothing pending the alarm does nothing; R60's bound still holds.
- **R4** (moved from store-assembly:R63) (was `plane` R76, in part) `acquiretimings?trace=` answers `restarted: true` beside `stageTimes` when the stage record survived an object restart (R62 holds the record in the object's storage). Test: after the object is restarted over the same storage the read answers `restarted: true`; control: a record made by the running instance answers none.
- **R5** (moved from store-assembly:R64) (was `plane` R77) **The owed-work ledger never forgets.** `keeps_pending`, keyed by digest and kind, holds kinds `keep`, `reading`, `ocr` (pages left), `subresources` (keyed by capture session), `unpack`, `fetch` (R67), `scan`, `safe_view`, `grade` and `ingest`; R74 is its table. The lower modules register through the hooks handed at composition: `captureOf(ctx).keepLater({sha, parts})` and `captureOf(ctx).owedLater({kind, sha, session?, parts?, detail?})`. No count and no clock ends an entry: retries back off from 15 s, doubling to at most one hour, and continue; `NO_HOME`, a missing owner or a refusal leave it owed; a second registration re-arms it (attempts 0, due now, a final reason cleared). An entry ends only when its owner answers done (`read`, `done`, an `ocr` answer without `owed`) or a `final` reason, which stays on the row; `owed: true` (an `ocr` cursor, a transient reading) continues. A keep that succeeds becomes a `reading` entry read in the same tick; `reading` and `ocr` call extraction's `readHeld({sha, storeName, env, kind})`; `subresources` calls acquisition's `owedRun` with `{storeName, env, deliver}`, `deliver` being `control-plane`'s `deliverOwedRender` (handed in, R1); `unpack` runs the archive-unpack drain as the daemon, a tree or depth stop ending it stated, a daily budget leaving it owed. Op `owedwork` (read; administrator and member; `?sha=` lists a digest's entries, else counts) is declared. A ledger of the old shape is carried over at the next start, every entry re-armed. A row is stamped started and its try counted before its owner runs; a started row found by a later alarm, of an isolate no longer running it, is an interrupted run, counted in `interrupted` (kept across a re-registration), which does not end the row (R76's poison rule judges the unit that was running, and its no-progress rule ends a step that advances nothing). A tick runs the light kinds (`subresources`, `unpack`) and then at most one of `keep`, `reading`, `ocr`; the consumer's wake is the entry's own due instant, never raised to now; `owedwork&sha=` also answers a `subresources` row by its session key; each entry states `interrupted`, `running`, `last_ms`, `last_mem`. Test: a reading with no home for 20 minutes of alarms is then read; a 40 MB keep promoted at 90 s; re-arm after exhaustion and after a final stop; the owed render delivered through `deliver`.
- **R6** (moved from store-assembly:R65) (was `plane` R80) **`owedwork` shows the watchdog's fields to an administrator.** The `owedwork` read answers an administrator `last_run`, `alarm_at`, `stale`, `oldest_overdue`, `watchdog_seen` and, since fix round 9 (G94; `plane` R103), `watchdog_ran` and `running_since` beside its counts, from the scheduler's `health()`, and answers a member none of them; the administrator is judged by the `viewer` stamp through membership (`class:admin` or an active admin member), never by a caller's `cls`. `oldest_overdue` is the scheduler's value (the earliest wake of any consumer), not a ledger-only minimum. The read writes nothing. Test: an administrator's answer carries the seven fields; a member's carries none.
- **R7** (moved from store-assembly:R66) (was `plane` R90) **The founder's session is an administrator for `owedwork`.** `administersOf` judges the viewer the door stamps: `class:admin`, the founder's bare `admin`, `member:<id>` naming an active administrator; any other spelling or none is not one. An administrator whose `health` throws or answers no object is shown the seven fields (R65) as `null` and a `watchdog_why`; a member none. Test: the founder's session, through the door, is shown the seven fields.
- **R8** (moved from store-assembly:R67) (was `plane` R81) **A parked fetch is owed work.** The ledger holds the kind `fetch`, keyed by the capture session, heavy (one heavy row a tick, R64), run by `acquisition`'s `owedFetch(store, {kind: "fetch", session}, {deliver})` with the control-plane's `deliver`; the row ends when `owedFetch` answers `done` or a `final` reason, and continues on `done: false` with a transient `reason`; `owedwork&sha=` answers a `fetch` row by its session key. Without an owner the row stays owed (`NO_OWNER`). Test: the real `acquire` parks a 9 MiB declared source as a `fetch` row with no body byte pulled; the tick runs the real `owedFetch`, which pulls the body.
- **R9** (moved from store-assembly:R68) (was `plane` R82) **A reading row goes on by the cursor it was left.** A `reading` row whose owner answers `more` with a cursor in `detail` is not ended: `readHeld` is handed the row's `detail`, one heavy piece runs a tick, the row is due one millisecond on, and `interrupted` is cleared when the cursor moved. Test: a text read in pieces over successive ticks, each piece once, the row ended when `more` is false; control: a row without a cursor ends as before.
- **R10** (moved from store-assembly:R69) (was `plane` R83) **Extraction is handed the owed-work ledger.** At construction the composition hands `extraction` the ledger's `owedLater` once (`registerOwe`, `extraction` R89), as it hands `capture` its `owedLater`. Test: a reading `extraction` defers is registered in the ledger; control: an `extraction` without `registerOwe` builds as before.
- **R11** (moved from store-assembly:R70) (was `plane` R87) **The case-file sweep is a scheduler consumer.** The assembly registers `casefile-sweep` with the scheduler (`casesweep.mjs`, beside the other consumers in the assembly), `finished` counting the editions built; the object's own alarm builds a complete edition that has no case file, retries a failed one after its backoff, re-derives its alarm at a restart, and heals a manifest recorded over bytes never stored. Test: the alarm builds a complete edition that has no case file and heals a manifest recorded over bytes never stored (workerd for the restart).
- **R12** (moved from store-assembly:R71) (was `plane` R91) **A row's time is its own.** `started_at` is the row's own start; the row states `last_ms`, `own_ms` (the part that is not awaiting a worker; the 10 s bar's) and `wait_ms` (the union of its owner's calls through a service binding). Test: a row states `last_ms`, `own_ms` and `wait_ms` (workerd, under contention).
- **R13** (moved from store-assembly:R72) (was `plane` R92) **`last_mem` is NULL when unmeasured and a finished row leaves its last run.** `keeps_last` (the assembly's table, declared to purge with `keeps_pending`; R97 names it beside `keeps_pending` and `acquire_stages`) holds the last run of each digest-or-session and kind: `ledgerLast` and `owedwork&sha=`'s `last`. Test: a row never measured states `last_mem` NULL; a finished row leaves its last run.
- **R14** (moved from store-assembly:R73) (was `plane` R94) **A keep with no home owes nothing.** After a keep (the `keep` owner, `pending.mjs`), the reading is asked in the same tick; an answer `NO_HOME` ends the keep owing nothing (the entered `reading` row is deleted; the filing's promotion owes the reading through control-plane's `unreadDocuments`); a digest with a home and no reading owes its reading; every other answer settles as before, so a transient failure still leaves it owed. Test: a keep with no home ends owing nothing; a digest with a home and no reading owes its reading; a transient failure stays owed.
- **R15** (moved from store-assembly:R74) (was `plane` R97) **`keeps_pending` is the job table for the reading kinds.** One row for each (whole sha or capture session, kind), additive to R64's columns: `state`, one of `queued`, `fetching`, `reading`, `partial`, `refused`, `finished`, `final`; `verdict` (JSON `{ok, size, limit, bound_key}`, the plan step's verdict on the bound); `cursor` (JSON); the counters `written`, `skipped`, `owed`, `failed` (integers) with `failed_why` (JSON, by cause; a refusal's facts, `kind`, `capture_sha` and `holder`, are carried as the refusing module answered them, `holder` present only where `provenance` names it to that caller, else null); `last_progress_at`, `rate_per_s`, `carrier` (the R2 key or `snapshots/<name>`), `reader_version` and `priority` (`raised` is "Read it"). Beside it `job_unit_attempts(sha, kind, unit, tries, stamped_at)` (a unit's attempt is stamped before its reader runs, R76) and `job_batches(sha, kind, batch, landed_at)` (a batch lands once, idempotent by (sha, kind, batch)). The kinds are R64's. `PENDING_RENDERS`, a module-level map lost at a restart, moves into the table. A row ends only when the job's counters say nothing is owed (`owed` zero and no unit pending), never because a hook was called, and a picture's `ocr` row does not end `finished` while the picture is unread. A `reading` row whose owner answers that the capture has no home yet (`NO_HOME`) waits as the `ocr` row does and counts no failure; the counters the owner's finishing answer carries (`counters: {written, skipped}`) are kept as the larger of the row's and the answer's, so a scan read to its end by the door's read and the OCR pass ends `finished` with the units written. The table is read by `control-plane`'s `op=reading` and `owedwork` (its R135), by `extraction`'s `completeness` and by the screens. It is declared to `record-core`'s purge as R97 says; a ledger of the old shape is carried over at the next start (the columns are additive), each row keeping what it had. Test: a row registered for each kind reads back every column; a batch landed twice writes once; a ledger of the old shape opens with every row kept. Control: a row whose `owed` is above zero is not ended by the owner's hook being called; a picture whose OCR failed transiently keeps its `ocr` row; an owner's real failure is still counted.
- **R16** (moved from store-assembly:R75) (was `plane` R98) **The composition root wires one registrar, `registerJob`.** The assembly sets `capture.registerJob = (e) => pending.registerJob(e)` beside `capture.keepLater = (a) => pending.keepLater(a)`; `acquisition`, `capture` and `doorbell` read it as they read `keepLater` (the function on the capture, else `opts.registerJob`, else the caller proceeds without a row and says so in its answer, never silently); `extraction` is handed it as `opts.registerJob` and its `owe` is `registerJob` bound to a kind (R69's hook is the same hand-off and is retired as a separate one). `registerJob({kind, sha, session?, cursor?, owed?, carrier?, parts?, detail?, verdict?, priority?})` answers `{ok: true, existed, state}` or `{ok: false, reason}`; it is synchronous and never throws. Its key is (the whole sha or, for `fetch` and `subresources`, the capture session; kind); a second registration re-arms, updates `cursor`, `owed`, `detail` and `parts` only when given, and never resets a counter. It is a synchronous write on the object's own SQL storage, so a caller makes it inside the synchronous function it gives `core.transact(fn)` and the row commits or rolls back with the filing; arming the alarm is a separate asynchronous `armJobs()` after the commit. A caller whose `registerJob` answers `{ok: false}` throws inside its transaction or answers a stated refusal, never swallows it. The root also hands `notice-producers` its `deps.readingsEnded({after, limit})`, a read of the job table (a callback, as `deps.updateNotice`, R39: a lower module reaches a higher one only so), answering the rows that ended (`finished`, ended in part with the reason, `refused`, `final`) with the member the filing was made for. Test: a kill between the put and the transaction finds one job row or none, never a row without its filing, for each way in (upload, `acquire`, the doorbell's pull, the archive); a second registration re-arms and changes no counter.
- **R17** (moved from store-assembly:R76) (was `plane` R101) **A step that advances nothing ends the row stated; a unit that kills its reader is stated for itself.** (1) No-progress: a row whose owner answers a step that moved neither its cursor forward nor any counter is ended `final` with that reason stated, on that tick, and never retried; a file that needs a user password ends `final` "needs a password" in one tick, never "being read" after. (2) Poison: a unit's attempt is stamped (`job_unit_attempts`) before its reader runs; a unit found stamped by a run that did not finish counts one kill, and after three kills of one unit that unit is stated unread alone (`PAGE_KILLED_READER`) after one retry at half the budget while the row goes on with the rest; "three interruptions end the whole read" (`PENDING_INTERRUPTED_MAX`) is retired. A member's kill is not counted `transient`. (3) A failure on the file's own bytes is deterministic: the row ends `final` with its reason; a transient failure at tick two or later is retried and never ends the read `partial` with no reason. Test: a stub reader answering `count: 0` ends stated; the user-password file ends in one tick; a page killed three times is stated and the other 299 pages of a 300-page scan are read. Control: a row that advances by one unit each tick is never ended by this rule. A window its cursor names, killed three times, is handed to the reader as `killedCursor` and stepped past; only a read with no cursor ends stated.
- **R18** (moved from store-assembly:R77) (was `plane` R104) **Alarm handlers are idempotent on re-entry.** A completed long alarm can run again, and a code deploy cuts a running alarm and restarts it from the top on the new code, an outbound call dying silently. Every handler the assembly runs on the alarm (the scheduler's firing, `pending-work`, `casefile-sweep`, the money ingest) therefore writes nothing twice: a batch lands once by (sha, kind, batch) (R74), a unit once its attempt is stamped (R76), a row found running by a restarted alarm goes on by its cursor (R68) and is not started afresh. Test: a firing run twice over one storage, and one cut after its second consumer and restarted, leave the rows, facts and units of a single run; a call left in flight by the cut run is not trusted.
- **R19** (moved from store-assembly:R78) (was `plane` R105) **The object states what a tick cost.** Product code in the object keeps an event-loop lag meter for the duration of a tick (a 10 ms interval, `max_stretch_ms`, that holds no process: K5226), `rows_read`, `rows_written` and units for each tick, and, in each route's `Server-Timing` (`plane` R78, `plane` R96), the object-call counts; each is NULL when unmeasured, never zero, and none is a time read from the object's own clock (R-CLOCK). Test: the lag meter reports `max_stretch_ms` for a tick; the Queue's real read set, six at a time beside a 12 MiB CSV, derives at most once for each viewer. Control: an unmeasured figure is NULL. (Fix round 10, cause 28b; K5057; was `plane` R105) Beside the lag meter the object states a timer-free work figure for a reading's run, the most statements it ran between two yields (`max_run_statements`), non-NULL for any pass that ran a statement, and a row keeps the largest over all its runs, so a later and smaller pass does not lower it; the meter's `max_stretch_ms` remains NULL for a stretch that never yielded, and `last_mem` NULL where the platform measures no memory. Test: a 7.9 MB text read by the real alarm states a figure above 256 and at most 1,000 for the pass that wrote a tick's 4,096 units (extraction R111 yields between batches of 256 units, so a figure above 1,000 means a pass held the object; worded K5200), still there after the finishing pass; control: a pass that only waits states its few statements and an unmeasurable figure is NULL.
- **R20** (moved from store-assembly:R79) (was `plane` R107) **One job row, by a store-internal route.** `jobrow?sha=&kind=` answers `{ok: true, row}` with `holder.jobRow({sha, kind})`: every column of R74's row, a `finished` or `refused` row included, or `row: null` when none; `{ok: false, reason: "BAD_SHA"}` for a malformed digest or session, `{ok: false, reason: "BAD_KIND"}` for a kind outside R74's. It writes nothing and is no member op: `control-plane`'s door calls it and applies the sight check. Test: a finished row and a refused row are answered whole with `cursor`, `last_progress_at` and `verdict`; control: an absent row answers `row: null`.
- **R21** (moved from store-assembly:R80) (was `plane` R108) **A deferred job keeps its project.** `pendingadd` takes `project` beside `kind` and `sha` and writes it into the job row's `detail.project` (`holder.add`), so a member an unpack or continuation files lands in the project its archive was filed in; absent, the row carries none. Test: an archive filed in a project, its unpack deferred through `pendingadd` with the project, files its members in that project (seam §6); control: no project, no `detail.project`.
- **R22** (moved from store-assembly:R81) (was `plane` R109) **A registered row arms the object itself.** `registerJob` arranges, after the transaction that wrote the row commits, that the object's alarm is set no later than the row's due time (an earlier alarm kept, a later or spent one brought forward), so no caller has to call `armJobs` for its row to run; `armJobs` stays, idempotent. Test: acquisition's real `owedFetch` registration of a `fetch` row due now, with a daily alarm set hours ahead, runs within one tick; control: a row due later leaves an earlier alarm unchanged.
- **R23** (moved from store-assembly:R82) (was `plane` R110) **The unit in flight is stamped by the reader.** With a paged read the assembly hands the reader `stampUnit(unit)`, which records `unit` (`p<page>`, `c<cursor>`) in `job_unit_attempts` as the one in flight before that unit's member call, so a kill is charged to the unit that died (R74's `killedPages`, `halfBudgetPages`), never to `whole` while a page is known. Test: a scan whose page 5 kills its reader three times ends with `killedPages: [5]`, pages 0-4 and 6 read; control: a whole read with no unit stamped is charged to `whole` as before.
- **R24** (moved from store-assembly:R102) (T53; N824; C9 (2); amends R64, R74, R75, R81) **The job table gains the kind `member`, keyed by capture session.** `JOB_KINDS` (and `PENDING_KINDS`, `store-assembly/pending.mjs:99,100`) gains `member`, last; `SESSION_KEYED` (`pending.mjs:118`) is `["subresources", "fetch", "member"]`: a `member` registration without a `session` is `JOB_SESSION_MISSING`, one with a `sha` and no `session` is refused as `fetch`'s is. A tick runs `member` with the light kinds (`subresources`, `unpack`), before the at-most-one of `keep`, `reading`, `ocr`; its owner is `acquisition.fileMember` (R136); `owedwork&sha=` answers a `member` row by its session key as it answers a `subresources` row; it ends `finished` when R136 answers `filed`, `already_held` or `not_filed`, and `final` with that code stated when a refusal is deterministic (R76 (3)). R64's list of kinds and R74's "The kinds are R64's" read with `member` added. **The pinned tests to update,** in one commit with every place that lists the kinds or the session-keyed ones: `pending.mjs:99` and `:118`; `bio-plane/test/job-registrar-stub.mjs:13` (`JOB_KINDS`) and `:15` (`SESSION_KEYED`); `bio-plane/test/m/store-assembly/r9-jobs.test.mjs:22` (`SESSION_KEYED`), `:23` (so `keyOf("member", ...)` gives `{session: ...}`), `:41` (the expected kinds, `member` last) and the loop it feeds; `bio-plane/test/m/store-assembly/r10-fixtures-run.test.mjs:56` (`KINDS` and `SESSION`); `bio-plane/test/m/test-support/doubles.test.mjs:107` (the sorted list) and `:110` (the session-keyed case). Test: registering `{kind: "member", session: "<64 hex>!0", detail}` answers `{ok: true, existed: false, state: "queued"}`, a second leaves one row, `{kind: "member", sha}` is `JOB_SESSION_MISSING`, `{kind: "member", session: ""}` likewise; the table reads back every column R74 names for the kind; `owedwork` answers it; a source-text check that the three lists agree. Control: the nine other kinds register and read back as before.
- **R25** *(not yet met: T53)* (moved from store-assembly:R103) (T53; K5705, DEC-460; `file-safety` R1, R60, R66; owed line drafted by a worker for BOB, who reviews it) **The `safe_view` job is registered for photographs.** The registrar (R75) registers kind `safe_view` for a capture whose name `file-safety` R1's route rule reads as a baseline JPEG or an 8-bit PNG picture (`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`), as it does for the document routes R11 lists, so that `safeView` has its picture made and a photograph is not left answering `SAFE_VIEW_PENDING` for ever; the rule is `file-safety`'s and this module holds no copy of the name list, asking `file-safety` for the route.
