## Public

### Purpose

The acquisition act: fetches a document from its public address (directly, through a Google Drive export, from a web archive, or rendered as a visitor saw it), hashes and stores it as it arrives, records the fetch as a receipt, profiles what it is, captures a page's supporting files, requests co-attestation, and returns the provenance document a caller promotes. It writes no bundle. What capture keeps about sources and sites (reachability, site assets, links, sessions, the platform's ceiling, the render allowance, the event queue) is `capture`'s, reached through the capture store its caller hands in.

### Provides

Terms. The **document address** is what the record identifies the document by; the **retrieval locator** is what was actually fetched (they differ for an archive replay and a Drive export). A **public locator** is `https:` on a named public host (`isPublicHttpsLocator`: no bare IP, no `localhost`, no credentials, a dotted host). The **caller class** and whether the caller is a member session are the control plane's stamps. Every refusal names a `reason`; one with a catalogue row carries its `check` and `translation`. The **store handed in** is the capture store the caller passes as the first argument (`capture`'s instance: its R8 outcomes and reachability, R15 enqueue, R22 sessions, R23 ceiling, R24–R25 site assets, R27 links, R39–R40 render allowance, R55 measurement and the validators R22 reads; this module is earlier in the order than `capture` and imports none of it). (T50; N1094, N1117) The store handed in also answers capture R46's `liveCaptureSessions` and capture R94's `recordCapturedFor`, which R46 and R47 call.

**acquire(store, body, {cls, member, sessMember, storeName, captureRequest}) → answer** The one act that fetches and files (`capture`'s `acquire(body, opts)` hands itself in; `op=acquire`). `body`: `locator`, optional `via` (`"archive.org"`), `address` (archive arm), `render`, `subresources`, `continue`, `authority`, `file`, `matchedSweep`. Success answers `{ok: true, existed, existed_undetermined?, document, parts?, subresources?, snapshot?, files?, subresources_skipped?, note}`. **archiveLookup(store, {address})** (R3), **profileOf({ev, sha, ct, total, multipart, headers, locator, view, retrieved})** and **profileView(core)** (R17), **governedFetch(store, target, purpose, delegated, opts)** and **governedCall(store, purpose)** (R9), **userAgent(env, purpose, delegated)** (R9, R24), **substanceDigests**, **profilesAsText**, **ODF_DIGEST_MAX** (R17), **acquireGradeNote**, **ACQUIRE_GRADE_NOTE** (R18).

*Who may acquire, and from where*
- **R1** (was `capture` R1) A `daemon`-class caller is refused `NOT_PERMITTED` except on the archive arm and on the capture-request arm (K58); the archive arm is refused `NOT_PERMITTED` to any class but admin, probe and daemon.
- **R2** (was `capture` R2) The fetched address must be a public locator, else `BAD_LOCATOR`. Nothing a caller sends can supply a hop, a retrieval locator for the archive arm, an export address, an export format or a producer: every such fact is derived by this call at the moment it fetches.
- **R3** (was `capture` R3) **The archive arm** (`via: "archive.org"`). The body names the document's own public `address` (else `BAD_ADDRESS`). The arm runs only when the address is fallback-eligible (`capture` R8, through the store handed in), else `NOT_ELIGIBLE` with the reachability; it queries the CDX index through the host governor (`web.archive.org` held to 24 requests a minute), chooses a row by `capture-sources.selectCapture`, and fetches its replay. `ARCHIVE_UNREACHABLE`, `ARCHIVE_REFUSED`, `HOST_COOLING_OFF`, and a selection refusal with every row considered, are answered by name. The document address is the CDX `original`; the chain gains the archive's hop built from the CDX record this call fetched. `archiveLookup({address})` decides and reports the same without capturing.
- **R4** (was `capture` R4) **The Drive arm** (a Google Drive address, per `capture-sources.readDriveAddress`). A body carrying any hop fact (`export_address`, `export_format`, `producer`, …) is refused `DRIVE_HOP_FACT_SUPPLIED` (C-48.1) whatever its address. A folder, a file of undetermined kind and an unrecognised shape are refused `DRIVE_FOLDER_NOT_A_DOCUMENT`, `DRIVE_KIND_UNDETERMINED`, `DRIVE_SHAPE_UNRECOGNISED` (C-48.2–C-48.4). A harvestable address is fetched at its composed OpenDocument export; a non-2xx export is `DRIVE_EXPORT_UNREACHABLE` (C-48.6), an HTML content type is `DRIVE_EXPORT_IS_THE_SHELL` (C-48.5, body never read), and bytes detected as HTML are `DRIVE_EXPORT_BYTES_ARE_THE_SHELL` (C-48.7). The application shell is never filed in the document's place. The document address is the Drive link as given; the chain gains Google's hop (`capture-sources.driveHop`, `bound: false`).
- **R5** (was `capture` R5) **The render arm** (`render` present and not `false`). Any value but `true` is `RENDER_FLAG_MALFORMED` (C-83.1); with the archive arm, a Drive export or `continue` it is `RENDER_ARM_CONFLICT` (C-83.2); with no renderer bound, `RENDER_NO_RENDERER` (C-83.3, 501). Before any fetch it asks the host governor for the page's host (`RENDER_HOST_COOLING_OFF`, C-83.5) and the render allowance (`capture` R39, through the store handed in): over the concurrency cap it is `RENDER_AT_CAPACITY` (C-83.8, `waiting`, nothing reserved); with the day's allowance committed or unreadable, `RENDER_DEFERRED` (C-83.4). Served bytes that are not a single-part HTML page are `RENDER_NOT_A_PAGE` (C-83.6) and the reservation is released at no charge; a failed or oversized render is `RENDER_FAILED` (C-83.7) and nothing is filed, its reservation released with its real elapsed time (R118). Every other path releases the reservation with the render's elapsed time. (fix round 7, G79; K4736) **The render is waited only as long as the door's bound leaves.** The render is waited min(`RENDER_WAIT_MS`, `ACQUIRE_BAR_MS` less `PROMOTE_RESERVE_MS` less the time used so far) (`renderWaitFor`), 0 when nothing is left; a render not back is the page filed as served and the render pending. Test: `r7-t51.test.mjs`.
- **R6** (was `capture` R6) A filed render is a pair: the rendered document is the primary (`capture.method` `rendered`, its own digest), the served shell is held beside it under its own digest as `snapshots/<name>.shell.html`, and the document carries `pair`, `render` (`capture-sources.renderBlock`, each subresource body the renderer handed over hashed by this instance and kept) and `shell`. Content authority is `capture-sources.renderedAuthority`'s.
- **R7** (was `capture` R41) A render asks for the locale `capture-sources.renderLocaleFor` answers for R17's view; timezone stays UTC.
- **R8** (was `capture` R42) `acquire` does not read the document: its answer carries no `reading` or `text_units`. Reading from the stored capture is `extraction`'s (K49).

*The fetch*
- **R9** (was `capture` R7) Every outbound fetch goes through the host governor: admitted (after any wait it names) or refused `HOST_COOLING_OFF` (429, with `retry_in_ms`); each response's status and `Retry-After` is reported to it. The user agent names Civicsmith, the instance and the purpose (`acquire`), or the member-browser agent a request delegated.
- **R97** (fix round 3, C13, P4b; K4356; amends R5, R9; `host-governor` R29) **A host visit is booked once.** The render arm's admission for the page's host (R5) is asked without booking (`host-governor` R29's peek), and the fetch the visit then makes (R9) books the governor once: one token, one grant, one gap judged. A visit by any other arm books once, as before, and every refusal is unchanged (`RENDER_HOST_COOLING_OFF`, `HOST_COOLING_OFF`). Test: a render-arm `acquire` of one page books one grant for its host (`governorState` `granted` one higher), and a host in cool-off is refused on the render arm as before. Control: a plain fetch books one grant.
- **R10** (was `capture` R9) The body is hashed as it arrives and stored content-addressed in parts of 8 MiB, each part under its own SHA-256 with its checksum. Refusals: a thrown fetch is `FETCH_FAILED` (502); a non-2xx status is `SOURCE_REFUSED` with the status; no body is `NO_BODY`; more than `CAPTURE_MAX` is `TOO_LARGE` (413, with its size and the limit, R114) and the stream is cancelled; zero bytes is `EMPTY`. A single part whose digest differs from the running hash is `HASH_DISAGREEMENT` (500). The limit is exported once as `CAPTURE_MAX`, quoted from `READ_BOUNDS` (R114), which every other reader of it imports (K231; N825, K2453).
- **R11** (was `capture` R10) `existed`: for one part, whether that object was held before this call wrote it; for several, `true` exactly when `provenance.registerHolds` finds the whole digest registered under an existing bundle, otherwise `null` with `existed_undetermined` saying what was and was not asked and how many parts were already held. Several parts never answer `false`.

*Supporting files (`subresources: true`)*
- **R19** (was `capture` R19) Only a single-part HTML primary of at most 8 MiB is walked; otherwise `subresources_skipped` names `TOO_LARGE_TO_PARSE`, `NOT_HTML`, `PRIMARY_UNREADABLE`, `NO_SUCH_SESSION` or `SESSION_UNREADABLE`, and the capture is unaffected. *Co-attestation*
- **R20** (was `capture` R20; fix round 2, C12; fix round 3, C12, P4a; K4356) An acquisition requests, wherever the source permits, a trusted timestamp over the capture digest and a co-archive of the locator at capture time, recording each attempt and its outcome in `attestation_attempts`. The timestamp is requested at every capture; the co-archive at every capture R43 asks it for (the group's setting, on by default as K60 made it, or the member's choice for that capture; K1888), both through `attestation.attest` (its R1–R3; `provenance` R31–R33 before N512's split; K72). A capture for which the co-archive is not asked records `{kind: "co_archive", asked: false, by}` in `attestation_attempts` (`by` `group` or `member`, whichever decided), never as a failed attempt. A file cut out of an archive (R38, R39) requests neither: it carries its archive's (`attestation` R7). The timestamp and the co-archive are requested after the document is kept, never inside the bound that holds the fetch and the reading (control-plane's `ACQUIRE_TIMED_OUT` bound); each attempt is recorded in `attestation_attempts`, and a late or failed attempt never fails or un-keeps a capture. No set time is waited for either: `acquire` answers as soon as the document is kept, an attempt that has not ended recorded as pending in `attestation_attempts` and its outcome recorded when it ends (`capture` R68), so the `attest` stage (R95) covers starting the attempts and never a fixed wait. Test: with an attestation that answers after 2 s, `acquire` answers at once with the attempt pending and the attempt is recorded when it ends; with an attestation that takes longer than the bound, `acquire` answers the kept capture within the bound without waiting for it, and the attempt is recorded when it ends; with one that fails, the capture stays kept and the failure is recorded as an attempt. Control: a fast attestation is recorded as before.
- Errors: every refusal above is an answer, never a throw; a store or bookkeeping write that fails is stated or ignored as said, never turned into a failed capture.
