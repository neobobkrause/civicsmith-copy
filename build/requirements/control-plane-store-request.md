The requirements below are copied from `build/requirements/control-plane.md` on `tranche` verbatim, each line, sub-bullet and mark unchanged, under the same numbers, so a test that names "R136: ..." stays true; this change copies only: `control-plane.md` still holds them until control-plane's own job, in its next attempt, removes R29, R64, R136, R137 and R138 there and leaves a pointer here (coordinator ruling, 5 Nov 2026 17:20). Their subject is the door's request to the store: every store request made through `store-request`'s `innerRequest`, a secret's digest carried only in that request's body, and no stamp taken from the caller. The door itself, its envelope and the routes stay in `control-plane`, whose terms these requirements use (an R number without a module is `control-plane`'s). Their change history is the one kept in `docs/requirements-notes/control-plane.md`. Until a job of this module moves the code they rule, it lives in `bio-plane/src/control-plane/`.

## Public

- **R64** A secret's digest reaches the store only in the body of the store's internal request, never its query: the door hashes the secret a recipient presents on a review grant's or template grant's door (`reviewcopy`, `reviewcomment`, `statementack`, `templateread`, `templatecomments`, `templatereview`, `templatecomment`; R20, R44) and generates the secret of a new grant or credential (`reviewgrant`, `templatereviewgrant`, `aicredentialmint`), and hands only the digests to `innerRequest` (`secretSha`, `bySecret`, `mint`), which sets them in the body as `store-request` R23 states, after removing any a caller sent in the query or the body (`store-request` R3, R9); no digest of a secret is placed in the address of any request the plane makes. The owners read it there (`credentials` R53, `review` R29, `ratification` R32, `case-inputs` R5, `filing-templates` R27; `store-door` R9). A test drives each of these ops with a sentinel secret and finds its digest in the internal request's body only, in no address, and the owner admitting it as before.

- **R136** (`store-request` R1) The door makes every request it sends to the store with `store-request`'s `innerRequest`, handing it the op (after alias resolution), the request's address, method and body, the caller `admission.admit` admitted with the presented session token, `admission`'s `source` and `country`, the request's `User-Agent`, the origin of its address (`origin`) and its `Content-Length` (`length`), `resolving` (a knock resolved to `pulled` and re-routed as `inboxpull`, R36), the verdict on an asserted replay (R16), whether a `readheld` capture's reading is deferred (R120), and the digests it computed or generated (`secretSha`, `bySecret`, and `mint` for `aicredentialmint`; R64); it sends what the function answers (address, body, headers) and answers a `refusal` it returns as given. It makes no stamp, strip or credential header of its own: the rules that were R17, R37, R38, R73, R96 and R113 are `store-request`'s. Test: a spy on `innerRequest` sees one call for each store request of a door test and the store receives its answer; control: a stamp sent by the caller in the address or the body of any op reaches the store through neither.

- **R137** (K5052 (2); `store-request` R15, R17) A parity test: for one op of each row of `store-request` R6 to R12 and of each family of its R17 (a store-route read, an act whose body carries a stamp, a public relay, `claim`, `whoami`, `subscriptionsignin`, an op with a module's door arm), once through `makeFetch` over a world's store and once through the world's door, the status, the body and the store request made are equal. Control: an op the world has no arm for answers `WORLD_NO_DOOR_ARM` and is listed in the test, not compared.

- **R138** (K5180 (2); `store-request` R1, R23) The secret-presenting doors, `reviewcopy`, `reviewcomment`, `statementack` and the four template doors, make their store request with `innerRequest` too (R136): the request they send is its answer for the page's request, with a recipient's secret as its digest (`secretSha`, `bySecret`) and the reader's own sight set after it (`viewer`; for the template doors `author`, the positional identity), neither of which a declaration stamps for these public ops. A member's request carries the page's other parameters and body fields as every op's does; a forged stamp, a forged digest and the body's `secret` reach the store through neither the address nor the body. A recipient holds a secret and no standing, so the review arms take from a recipient's request only the keys they read (`draft` trimmed, `limit`, `reason`, `text`): the secret opens one copy (R20). Until `innerRequest` itself removes the body's `secret` and the stamps a caller names in the body (R23, R9; reported to BOB in J64), the door drops them before handing the body over. Test: each op's request equals `innerRequest`'s answer for the same inputs plus the reader's stamps; control: a recipient's request carries the review arms' keys only.

## Private

### Invariants

- **R29** No handler receives a caller-supplied value for any stamp (`store-request` R3, R4). This is tested for every op that declares a stamp, through `store-request`'s `innerRequest` (whose own test drives every declared op with every stamp as a sentinel) and once through the real door.
