The requirements below are copied from `build/requirements/capture.md` as it stands on `job/capture` verbatim, each line, sub-bullet and mark unchanged, under the same numbers, so a test that names "R77: ..." stays true. They are the held list and its acts, as the job proposed (`docs/requirements-notes/capture.md` on `job/capture`), kept under their `capture` numbers rather than renumbered; the evidence store, R94's `captured_for` table and the other rules stay in `capture`, whose terms these requirements use. Their change history is the one kept in `docs/requirements-notes/capture.md`. Until a job of this module moves the code they rule, it lives in `bio-plane/src/capture/`.

## Public

- **R77** (fix round 3, C9, C14, C16; K4356; Intake Doctrine §8; R100) `heldCaptures({member?, project?, bundle_id?, sort, dir, limit, after, viewer})` (`op=heldcaptures`) lists the Information documents at `collected` that are not set aside (R79, R81), showing only what the viewer may see (membership R43). `member` limits the list to the documents that member captured (the capture's `actor`, `acquisition` R16); `project` to that project's documents (`provenance.homeOf`) and to the documents seen there (R100): a seen document's row carries `also_here`, `{home}`, the project that is the document's home as `project` names it (a document whose home is the project carries none), and is a row only for a viewer who may see the document at its home (membership R43), so the list shows a document filed from another project and shows it to no one who cannot see where it lives; `bundle_id` limits the list to the one document of that bundle (the read for the document page). Each row gives the document, its source as provenance records it, its project, its age since it was collected, and its batch eligibility (R78). Each row also answers `captured_for`, read through R83's reader for the viewer: `questions`, one `{question, title, asker}` for each question the document was captured for that the viewer may see; and `withheld`, the sentence of the key `screen.held.withheld` (K3649) when the document was also, or only, captured for a question the viewer may not see, else null. A question the viewer may not see is withheld whole: no id, title or asker of it, and no count of such questions, appears in the row. A document captured for no question answers `questions: []` and `withheld: null`. With no reader registered, or one that fails or does not answer, `captured_for` is null with `captured_for_basis`, a sentence stating it undetermined (as R78's eligibility). It can be sorted by age, source or project. At most `limit` rows (default 200, clamped 1…1000), with `truncated` and `next` (N90's bound). It writes nothing, and nothing about held captures is notified. (DEC-97 (1); DEC-69, DEC-94; K1019; DEC-141 (1), K1618, K1645) (DEC-167 (8); N688) A held document whose capture is a file cut out of an archive (`acquisition.memberOf` answers an archive for it) is listed beside its archive, not as a row of its own, while its archive's document is a row of this list for the viewer: the archive's row carries `archive`, the summary `acquisition.archiveList` answers (`{sha256, entries, filed, already_held, not_filed, waiting}`), and `files`, its held files the viewer may see, each `{document, sha256, index, name, grade, eligible}` (`name` as the archive states it, `grade` the file's, which is the archive's, `eligible` R78's), in index order, at most `HELD_FILES_IN_ROW` (50) with `files_total` and `files_truncated`, the rest read through `archiveList`. `limit` counts rows, never files, and the `member` and `project` filters apply to an archive's row and its files alike. A file whose archive is not a row (set aside, released, or not seen by the viewer) is a row of its own, carrying `archive: {sha256}`. Picked files are set aside and restored (R79, R81) and vouched for (`ratification`'s `op=release`) by their own document ids, as any held document; setting an archive aside sets none of its files aside. (T47; N979) Each row, an archive's included, also answers `capture`: `{sha256, grade}`, `sha256` the digest of the capture the register files under the document (`provenance` R48; when it files more than one, the one first registered, ties by digest) and `grade` `provenance.captureGrade(sha256)`'s answer whole (`{grade, route, determined, basis}`, so a received or authored capture reads as stated there, never as measured), `null` when provenance does not answer; a document under which the register files no capture answers `capture: null`. (T50; N1117; amends R77) `captured_for` also names the questions a member captured the document for through `acquire`'s `for` (R94, by R83's reader), by the same sight rule as a question a request filed it for. (T51; N1235; K3771, K3774; amends R77) Each row, an archive's included, also answers `actor`, the capture's `actor` (`acquisition` R16; for an upload R86's `by`, the uploader's stamp), `null` when the capture names none; `retrieved`, the whole-second UTC instant the document records as retrieved (for an upload, the upload receipt's instant); and `bytes`, the size in bytes of the whole capture as held (all its parts; an archive row, the archive's), `null` when the record cannot say. The three are the capture's own facts, drawn as the viewer's sight of the document allows (membership R43): a document the viewer may not see is no row, so none of them is read from it. So `screens-intake` draws an upload's uploader, date and size without reading a fingerprint or the upload's address. (T51 fix round 2; K4331) `heldCaptures` grades a page of rows in one batch: `provenance.captureGrades` is asked once for the page's distinct capture digests, each row's `capture.grade` the answer `captureGrade` gives for it; an answer that is not an object, a batch that fails, or a provenance with no batch reads that grade null with the digest named (a provenance serving no batch is asked one digest at a time); an empty page asks nothing. The `picker` row carries `bundle_id`, `title`, `project`, `source`, `collected_since`, `capture: {sha256}`, `actor` and `standing`, and nothing heavier. Each full row also answers `whole`: true when the evidence store holds an object under the row's capture fingerprint, false when it holds none (a copy kept in parts only), null where that cannot be said (no capture, no evidence store, a failed read, or the page's wait `HELD_PAGE_WAIT_MS` ran out); a page asks the store once per distinct fingerprint, nothing for an empty page. (Fix round 3, C14, C16; K4356) The picker row answers `whole` from the size the register recorded when the capture was kept (true when it records a whole object, false when it records only parts, null when it records nothing) and asks the evidence store nothing for any row; the read for one document (`bundle_id`) answers `whole` from the store as a full row does. (T51 fix round 2; K4339) The full held row (not the picker's) also answers `format`: the register's format id for the row's capture, `null` when none. (T51 fix round 5, E13; K4541) Each full row answers `whole: true` without asking the evidence store when the register records the capture's size as held in one part (at most 8 MiB, `acquisition` R10's part); every other row (no size recorded, or a capture held in parts, whose whole object may be owed) is asked of the store once for each distinct fingerprint, `HELD_PAGE_LANES` at a time, and answers true, false or null as before; the answer is otherwise unchanged. (T51 fix round 6, G6, G12a, G12d; amends R77) **A held row's `capture` is the document's whole.** The row's `capture` (`sha256`, `bytes`, `actor`, `retrieved`, `grade`, `format`) is the document's whole: the register's `whole` row (`provenance` R72) when it has one, else the whole the document's own register document states for the part it holds, with the whole's size; `whole` is never taken from a part's size, and a row of 40 MB answers `whole` from the store under the whole's digest. Parts are never asked of the archive listing, and a digest `acquisition`'s `notArchivesAmong(store, shas)` answers decided to be no archive is not asked again; the picker row also names `format`, the format the register document profiles the capture as (null when none). New data and what `provenance`'s wake repair covers; a legacy bundle whose register document states no `parts` and which holds no whole row cannot be told apart. Test: a five-part upload whose parts sort below the whole is a row whose capture is the whole; a register holding only the parts is read as the whole its register document states; twenty captures profiled with no format drawn twice make no `archiveList` and no store call the second time. (fix round 7, G78; K4736, K4748) `heldCaptures` asks the evidence store no more for a capture whose object an earlier draw of this instance found held (it is immutable once it exists); an answer of not held, or none, is asked again at every draw, and the register is never the source of held. The memory holds at most `HELD_PROVEN_MAX` (5,000) digests, then is emptied. Each draw that asks the store logs `{capture: "heldcaptures", heads, head_ms, remembered}`. Test: `r7-t51.test.mjs`. (T53; JOURNEYS R-21, D1 capture R?1; changes R77) **A member's own testimony is in `heldcaptures` for that member.** The `member` filter lists a document `testify` wrote for that member, as it lists an uploaded one (the author's stamp `testify` writes is the capture's actor, `acquisition` R16). Test: `testify` as member M, then `heldCaptures({member: M})` lists it; another member's list does not. Control: an upload is listed as before.

- **R88** (T47; N969; K3027 doubt 6) `heldCaptures` takes `state`: `held` (absent or empty reads `held`) answers R77's list; `set_aside` answers instead the Information documents at `collected` whose latest act (R79, R81) is a set-aside, by the same sight, filters (`member`, `project`), sorts, bound (`limit`, `truncated`, `next`) and row fields as R77 (archive grouping included, an archive's row grouping its set-aside files), each row also carrying `set_aside: {reason, author, at}`, its latest set-aside as R79 recorded it. So a member reaches `heldrestore` (R81) for a document set aside in an earlier visit. Any other `state` is refused as an unknown `sort` is (the required-argument refusal, `REQUIRED_ARGUMENT_MISSING`, naming `state` and its two values), nothing read. (T51; N1235; K3771, K3774; amends R88) `state` also takes `held_and_vouched`: R77's list, and with it the Information documents the viewer may see that a member has vouched for (their state `verified`, released through `ratification`; `provenance.homeOf` for `project`), by the same filters, sorts, bound and row fields as R77; the required-argument refusal then names `state` and its three values. Each row carries `standing`, `collected` or `verified`; a vouched row also carries `vouched: {by, at}`, the author and instant of the release's entry in the document's own `state_history` (`null` for a field the entry does not give). A vouched row is not asked of R78's examination: `eligible` is `null` with `eligibility_basis` saying the document is already vouched for, and R91's wait is not spent on it. A vouched document is never set aside (R79 refuses it), so no vouched row carries `set_aside`, and `held` and `set_aside` answer as before. Vouched rows count toward `limit`, `truncated` and `next` with the collected ones; an archive's files beside it are listed as R77 lists them, each file's standing its own. So `screens-intake`'s document choice lists the project's vouched documents beside its collected ones in one bounded read. Through `op=heldcaptures` it is the query's `state`. It writes nothing and notifies nothing.

- **R91** (T49; N1080; K3295; K3324 (1); amends R77, R78, R88) **A page of held captures in bounded time.** For one page, `heldCaptures` (R77, R88) asks R78's registered examination at most once for each document the page shows. A document is shown as a row or as a file beside its archive. It also asks for the archive grouping (DEC-167 (8)) at most once for each document that is a row. Both are asked for the whole page under one wait of `HELD_PAGE_WAIT_MS`, a constant this module exports. Its value is set from M-T49-3 at this job's START and recorded by BOB. Nothing is asked again for each row in turn, and nothing waits without a bound. An answer that is not given within the wait is undetermined:
  - a row's or a file's eligibility reads `eligible: null` with `eligibility_basis`, a sentence saying the examination did not answer in time (R78);
  - a row whose archive grouping did not answer in time carries `archive: {sha256, grouping: null}` and `files: null`, with `files_basis`, a sentence saying that the files beside the archive are undetermined. Its files are neither dropped from the list without being named, nor counted as listed.
  Everything else in the answer is unchanged: the rows, their order, `limit`, `truncated`, `next` and every other field of R77 and R88.
  - Tests: at the plane's shape (M-T49-3), the walk's unfiltered `heldcaptures` answers within the bound the test states, with the time per row stated in the test. An examination that never settles leaves each row `eligible: null` with its basis, and the answer still comes within `HELD_PAGE_WAIT_MS`. An archive listing that never settles leaves that row `files: null` with `files_basis`. Controls: an examination that answers in time gives R78's `true` or `false` as today. The examination is called once for each shown document per page, counted at the registered reader.

- **R78** A later module may register, once at start, the batch-release examination of one document (`ratification` R34). R77 answers each row `eligible: true`, or `eligible: false` with the class it fails first and that class's reason (crucial, or contested: any open contradiction touching the document). With none registered, each row reads `eligible: null`, stated undetermined. A registered examination that throws, answers neither, or does not answer within R91's wait reads the same way, with a basis saying which. (DEC-97 (3); K1019; T49, R91)

- **R79** `setAside({ids, reason, author, viewer})` (`op=heldsetaside`) sets several held documents aside with one reason. Refusals in order: an empty or machine author `MACHINE_CANNOT_SET_ASIDE` (C-118.8); an empty reason, or one over 2,000 characters, `SET_ASIDE_NO_REASON` (C-118.9); no ids, or more than `PER_ITEM_MAX`; any id absent or invisible (one answer), not `information`, not at `collected`, or already set aside, named. The set is refused whole, never narrowed. Otherwise each is recorded as set aside with the reason, who and when. The reason is required of every set-aside (C-118.9), so a set-aside an open question waits on is never made without one. Each document's questions are read through R83's reader, once per act, before anything is written; each question the reader answers as waiting on the document (an open question, `waiting: true`), whether or not the viewer may see it, is recorded with that document's set-aside, so the act stands in that question's history (R84) with the reason, who and when; in a set of several documents the one reason applies to each waiting question of each. The answer names, per document, `captured_for` as R77 answers it and `waiting`: the waiting questions the viewer may see (`{question, title, asker}`), with R77's `withheld` sentence and no count when a question the viewer may not see waits. With no reader registered, or one that fails or does not answer, the set-aside is still made, no question is recorded with it, and the answer says, per document, `waiting: null` with a sentence stating the waiting questions undetermined. The document is unchanged, stays at `collected` and is never deleted; a set-aside document leaves R77's list. Append-only. (DEC-97 (2); K1019; DEC-141 (1), (2), K1618, K1645)

- **R81** `restoreHeld({ids, reason, author, viewer})` (`op=heldrestore`) undoes a set-aside (R79) by a reasoned act, for several documents with one reason. Refusals in order: R79's first three (C-118.8, C-118.9, the id count); any id absent or invisible (one answer), or not set aside now, named. The set is refused whole, never narrowed. Otherwise each is recorded as restored with the reason, who and when, appended beside its set-aside, which is never rewritten or removed; a document's latest such act decides whether it is set aside. The restore is recorded with every question its undone set-aside was recorded with (R79), so it stands in the same history (R84), in order after that set-aside, with its own reason, who and when. Any member who may see the document may restore it, the asker or another; no act waits on another member's consent, a vote or a count (K1618). A restored document at `collected` returns to R77's list and may be set aside again. (DEC-97 (2); K1019; DEC-141 (4), K1618, K1645)

- **R82** `heldCount({sweep})` answers the number of Information documents at `collected`, not set aside (R79, R81) and not released, whose register origin names `matched_sweep` equal to `sweep`. It counts the whole store, with no viewer, because it is read only by the daemon's hold (`link-sweep` R8). It writes nothing and never throws: when the store cannot be read it answers `null` (not known), never 0, so the hold fails closed (K1129). An unknown sweep answers 0. A register document held only as a blob (`files.content` NULL) is not counted; acquired and pulled register documents are inline (K1129). (K1036; Intake Doctrine §4, the backlog ceiling)

- **R83** (N587; DEC-141, K1618, K1645) A later module (`capture-requests`, its R48) may register, once at start, the reader of the questions a document was captured for, as a third slot beside doorbell R3's and R78's: `registerReader("captured-for", module, fn)`, a second or malformed registration refused as theirs are (`membership`'s `listenerRefusal`). `fn({document, captures, viewer})` (`document` the held document's bundle id, as R77 lists it; `captures` the capture digests the register files under it, provenance R48; K1770) answers synchronously `{questions: [{question, title, asker, visible, waiting}]}`: one entry per (question, asker) the document was captured for, `question` the question's id, `asker` the member who asked, `visible` whether `viewer` may see the question, `title` and `asker` null when it may not, and `waiting` true when that question is open and waits on the document. A throw, a promise, or any other answer is no answer (R77, R79 then state the questions undetermined). This module reads no other module's request rows, and never decides a question's sight, title, asker or openness itself. (T50; N1117; amends R83) `fn({document, captures, viewer, asks})`: `asks` is `[{question, asker}]`, the rows of R94's `captured_for` for those `captures`, and the reader answers one entry for each (question, asker) of its own requests and of `asks`, a pair in both once, with the same `visible`, `title`, `asker` and `waiting` rules. A reader that takes no `asks` leaves them unnamed; this module never names a question from `asks` itself.

- **R84** (N587; DEC-141 (3), (4)) `heldActsOf({question, viewer, limit, after})` answers the history of the documents R79 recorded `question` as waiting on: every set-aside and restore recorded with that question (R79, R81), oldest first, each `{document, act, reason, author, at}`, and per document `set_aside`, whether its latest act (R81) is a set-aside. An act on a document the viewer may not see (membership R43) is left out, unannounced and uncounted. It takes the question as given: the caller gates the question (`inquiry`, which reads the wait on the question and its project's list). A question with nothing recorded answers an empty history. At most `limit` acts (default 200, clamped 1…1000), with `truncated` and `next` (N90's bound). It writes nothing, notifies nothing and makes no queue item (DEC-94), and never throws.

- **R100** (fix round 3, C9; K4356; Intake Doctrine §8; `provenance` R2; amends R77) **A document seen in a project: `recordSeenIn({captureSha, project, by, at}) → {recorded}`**, the service `op=captureseenin` (R101) calls. It records, once for each (capture digest, project), at the first instant, that a member filed the held document in that project as a reference, in the table `seen_in` (`capture_sha`, `project`, `by`, `at`), declared to `record-core`'s purge as capture's other tables are (R74, R87) and a stated read contract as R57 states `links`. It makes no second home: the document keeps the one home `provenance` records (its R2), nothing moves, copies or re-homes it, and its sight is its home's. It answers `{recorded: false}` and writes nothing for a digest that is not 64 lowercase hex, a `project` that is not a non-blank string of at most 200 characters, or a `by` that is not a member's stamp (absent, blank, or a machine identity); it decides nothing about the project's existence or the viewer's sight of it, and never throws. `heldCaptures` (R77) reads the references. Test: `recordSeenIn` of a document whose home is project A, into project B, makes `heldCaptures` for B list that document with `also_here` naming A for a viewer who may see it at A, and not for one who may not; the same triple twice leaves one row; the document's home is still A. Control: a blank `project` and a machine `by` record nothing.

## Later requirements (T51, T53)

- **R101** (fix round 3, C9; K4370; amends R100) **A member files a held document in a project: `op=captureseenin`** (a mutating member act, `by` stamped from the session, body `{captureSha, project}`), declared in `DECLARED_OPS`, calls `recordSeenIn` (R100) for the member and answers `{ok: true, recorded, home}` (`home` the project the document is held under, as `heldCaptures`' `also_here` names it). It refuses, each by its own catalogue row, a digest no held capture has (`CAPTURE_NOT_HELD`), a project the member may not contribute to, and a document whose home the member cannot see (answered as one not held). A second press records nothing new and answers `recorded: false` with `home`. Test: a member files the acgov-type document held under another project and `heldCaptures({project})` then lists it with `also_here`; control: a non-member of the project is refused and nothing is recorded; a repeat changes nothing. (K4391) A filing into the document's own home records nothing and answers `recorded: false` with `home`; a failed write answers `SEEN_IN_NOT_RECORDED` (502). `CAPTURE_NOT_HELD` is check C-118.11 (R37).

- **R102** (fix round 4, P10; K4445; amends R77, R91) `heldCaptures` takes `bundle_ids`, at most 50 distinct document ids (a list, or a comma-separated string at the op); only the listed documents of the list are answered, in the list's own order and sight, with `limit`, `truncated` and `next` as for the page, `bundle_ids` selecting and not narrowing what the list holds (an archive named alone still groups its files). Absent or empty names none. A value that is not a list of ids, or names more than 50, is the required-argument refusal naming `bundle_ids`. The store's reads and the page's examinations and groupings are begun together, so a screen reads the picker rows for the page and the full fields for the rows it draws.
