# ux-design — requirements

**Status** · New 5 Nov 2026 (coordinator 15:31, on Bob's word 15:36): the UX design lane under BOB. Its purpose is that no job waits on wording or UX design. It works only within Bob's standing approval for the design stream (CLAUDE.md, "In the product's design stream (docs/development/ux-substrate) ..."): it may fix a generator so its output matches the product's code, stop committing a generated file over the process's size limit and have it built where it is needed, add a module's entries for words already in the stream, and set a component's measured value when a recorded decision of Bob's already fixes it. It never changes wording in the design stream and never makes a design decision; those stay with Bob's design stream until Bob widens that approval in CLAUDE.md himself. Its inventory of the words items jobs have raised is `docs/requirements-notes/ux-design.md`.
