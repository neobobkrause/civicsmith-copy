## Public

### Purpose

Holds the connections of the record, of two kinds. **Derived connections**: two captured documents that resolve to the same entity are connected (Framework §8), graded by the weaker of how each end resolved (§8.1). Each connection carries the reference that determined it on each side, a member's choice of the on-point mention, and what a cited part of a document may earn from it. **Edges between bundles**: the `references[]` of every document, projected so the record can answer who cites what, backlinks and dangling references. These edges include `links_to` edges, which the source asserted by a hyperlink the record resolved. **Themes** (Framework §8.4): a connection through an idea. A member declares a theme with its test, and documents and passages are placed in it, graded like any connection. A theme is a lens for finding material and never the basis of a claim. Grade here states how a connection was established, and never how credible a document is.

### Provides

Terms. A **connection** is `{a_capture_sha, b_capture_sha, entity_id, a_bundle_id, b_bundle_id, grade, a_grade, b_grade, established, needs_confirmation, asserted_by, basis, at, determining_pair}`, its ends in canonical order (`a < b`). The **determining pair** is `{a_ref, a_position, b_ref, b_position, positioned, why, selection}`, or null on a row derived before pairs were kept. A **position** is `text-chain`'s reading source; **covers** is `text-chain.readingPositionInExtent`. An **edge** is `{bundle_id, target_id, kind}` with `kind` a relation of the closed vocabulary. The **viewer** is the control plane's stamp, read through `membership.viewerPredicate`, failing closed when absent. Every refusal is `{ok: false, reason, detail}`; one with a catalogue row also carries `code`, `check` and `translation`.

**derive({entityId, assertedBy, limit?})** (`op=connect`)
- **R1** `NO_ENTITY` for an empty id, answered by `entities.noEntity` (its R37). It reads the entity's resolutions (`entities`), keeps per capture the strongest (ties: the first reference by sort), and writes one connection per unordered pair of captures, graded the weaker end, `established` only when both ends are `A` or `B`, `asserted_by` as given (`system` from the control plane and the sweep), a `basis` sentence naming both grades and the pair, each end's determining reference with the position of its first read (null where the reading could not say), and the pair rule `strongest-graded/first-reference-by-sort`. A held connection is updated in place, never duplicated. An unregistered entity id derives from whatever resolutions name it, `found: false`. `assertedBy` `member` or `source` is refused `CONNECTION_AUTHOR_NOT_DERIVED`, writing nothing.
- **R2** The pair bound is `limit` (default 500, maximum 5,000); the document bound is the largest k with k(k−1)/2 within it (32 at 500, 100 at 5,000); at most 5,000 resolution rows are read, a trailing partly-read capture dropped rather than graded weaker. The answer carries `documents`, `document_limit`, `resolution_rows`, `count`, `limit` and `truncated`; every connection written is true, and a truncated set is the first documents by capture digest.
- **R3** `onDerived(module, fn)`: a later module registers once; a malformed registration, or a second by the same module, is refused through `membership`'s `listenerRefusal` (its R81: `LISTENER_MALFORMED`, `LISTENER_DECLARED`), and the listeners run in the modules' total order (`membership`'s `MODULE_ORDER`, R83); after each derivation `fn` runs with `{entityId, count, documents, truncated, entityKnown, assertedBy}` (for `observation-log`).

**read({entityId | captureSha, limit, viewer})** (`op=connections&id=`, `&sha256=`)
- **R4** By entity or by capture, else `NO_KEY`; ordered by grade; at most `limit` (as R2's default and maximum) with `truncated` measured by reading one more. Each row carries the connection, and `on_point` naming each end's standing member choice when any.
- **R5** With an entity, the answer carries its derivation statement from the registered provider (`observation-log`), or the missing cause when none is recorded.
- **R6** `selection` states the method (strongest-graded) and the tie-break, or that the row predates the recorded rule. When a member's choice stands on an end, it names the choice, never "nobody has chosen". A choice that has lapsed is stated on every arm (`id=`, `sha256=`, `content=`).

**portionGrade({contentId, limit, viewer})** (`op=connections&content=`)
- **R7** An unknown content row is `CONNECTION_PAIR_NO_CONTENT` (C-49.3). For a `document` extent every connection of the capture reaches it, and says so.
- **R8** For a part, each connection of the capture is `reaching`, `undetermined` or `outside`: no pair is `CONNECTION_PAIR_NO_PAIR`, undetermined; a pair with no position on this end is C-49.2 (`CONNECTION_PAIR_UNPLACED`), undetermined; a pair read outside is C-49.1 (`CONNECTION_PAIR_OUTSIDE_EXTENT`), outside; a pair read inside reaches. An outside verdict is C-49.4 (`CONNECTION_PAIR_MENTION_UNCHOSEN`), undetermined, when another mention of the subject in the document is inside or unplaced, or the mentions were not all read (at most 5,000). A reaching verdict is C-49.4 when the pair won only a tie against a mention not inside. Each C-49.4 names the mentions.
- **R9** A standing member choice on the cited end answers instead. Its position is tested by the same predicate, and its grade is the weaker of the chosen mention's grade and the other end's (that end's choice when it has one). A choice whose mention the document no longer carries at that place, or a choice made before occurrences were recorded whose reference is now read at several places, has lapsed. The lapse is stated with the places, and the machine's pair is read as unchosen.
- **R10** `connection_grade` is the strongest reaching grade, else null; `established` and `needs_confirmation` follow it. The answer carries the three lists, their counts, `limit`, `truncated`, the row's `stale`. Its `why` sentence tells apart four cases: no connection, an unchosen mention, unplaced connections, and every connection outside. Undetermined is never read as none.
- **R11** No capture grade or testimony grade is mixed into a connection grade, and a null grade is never ranked.
- **R12** Every bundle id in the answer is withheld from a viewer who may not see it.
- **R13** `portionGrades(contentIds, viewer)` answers R10's grade for at most 200 rows in one set-based read, for `content`'s standings and the earned-basis registry (content R20).

**choose({capture, other, entity, ref, occurrence?, author, viewer})** (`op=connectionchoose`)
- **R14** Refusals, in order: C-74.1 (`CONNECTION_CHOICE_NOT_A_MEMBER`: no author, or a machine identity); C-74.2 (`CONNECTION_CHOICE_NO_CONNECTION`: no such connection, or the chosen end's bundle hidden from the viewer, the same answer); C-74.3 (`CONNECTION_CHOICE_NOT_A_MENTION`: `ref` is not a resolution of the entity in that capture, or the named occurrence is not read there, listing the places); C-74.4 (`CONNECTION_CHOICE_OCCURRENCE_UNNAMED`: the reference is read at several places and none is named). Listed occurrences are at most 256, with `truncated`.
- **R15** An occurrence is named by its key, or by its place when exactly one occurrence is read there. An empty `occurrence` names the read that carries the empty key, the one with no recorded place.
- **R16** Append-only: a new choice supersedes the current one on that end in the same transaction; at most one choice per end is current; choosing the current one again writes nothing (`wrote: false`). The answer names the choice, the superseded one, the machine's pair reference and a sentence. A choice never rewrites the connection's own pair.
- **R70** (T51; C28; K4165; amends R4, R14) A member can choose a mention other than the one the record chose. A connection row read by capture (`op=connections&sha256=`) carries `mentions`, the mentions of the connection's entity in that document, each `{ref, occurrence, position, grade}`: `occurrence` the key `connectionchoose` takes (null where the reading kept none), `position` where it was read (null where unplaced), ordered by reference and then in the order the reading kept them. A name mentioned in three places lists the three. The list is cut at 256 (R14's bound on listed occurrences) and `mentions_truncated` says so. A row read by entity or by content id carries neither field, and a row whose document the viewer may not see carries none (R33). `connectionchoose` accepts any listed mention by its `ref` (and `occurrence`) and writes it; the mention already chosen writes nothing (R16).

**The dirty set: markDirty(entityId), wake(now), sweep()**
- **R17** Registered on `entities`' `onResolved` (its R13): an inserted or raised resolution marks its entity once, inside the resolving transaction; many marks of one entity are one row.
- **R18** `sweep` derives at most a batch of the oldest marked entities (default 100; a binding may set it), each at R2's default bound with `asserted_by: system`, and clears each only after its derivation (a crash leaves it marked). It answers `{entities, remaining, swept}`. `wake(now)` is null when nothing is marked, else `now` plus the delay (default 60 s; a binding may set it). The scheduler calls both; this module never arms an alarm.

**Edges between bundles: onPromote (registered with promotion), backlinks({target, viewer}), dangling(viewer), citesInto(id), edgeSevered(citingId, targetId, rel?), the fact `citedBy`** (`op=backlinks`, `op=dangling`)
- **R19** Registered with `promotion` as a projection (its R39, K31): in the promotion's transaction, the bundle's edges are replaced by the `references[]` entries of its document, one per (target, relation). The inquiry's `supersedes` reverse index is `inquiry`'s and reads these edges. The edges the projection replaced are left on the promotion's context as `refsReplaced` (`[{target_id, kind}]`), read with `refsReplacedOf(c, kind)`, for a later step that must un-tell what a revision dropped (inquiry's `supersedes`).
- **R20** `backlinks`: `NO_TARGET`; a target the viewer cannot see answers `NO_SUCH_DOCUMENT` (R72) like an absent one. Otherwise every citing bundle the viewer can see, with its relation, type, title and state, and the edge's status read from the citing document (unreadable or unrecorded is `confirmed`). A citing bundle the viewer may not see is withheld whole (no id, title, type, state, relation or count), and the answer states `out_of_view: true` when one was, and only that (DEC-36, K903 (4); N459, K935).
- **R21** `dangling` (C-6.2): every edge whose target no bundle holds, a hidden citing bundle's edge withheld whole.
- **R22** `edgeSevered` is true only when the citing document records that entry `severed`; an unreadable document or an unrecorded entry is live. `citesInto` partitions the `cites` edges into `confirmed` and `severed`, sorted.
- **R23** Registered with `promotion` as the fact `citedBy(id)` (its R16, R40): the confirmed citers of `id`.

**projectLinks({sourceCapture, sourceBundle?, viewer})** (`op=linkproject`)
- **R24** The capture's links are resolved by `capture` (its R27). Only a `linked` link whose target a bundle has registered becomes an edge, as `links_to`, never `cites`, asserted by the source. A self-edge is dropped. A target whose bytes no bundle claims is counted `skipped_unregistered`. The answer lists each edge with address, verdict and basis.
- **R25** A capture not registered to a bundle writes nothing and says so.
- **R26** Through the viewer: a source capture the viewer cannot see answers as one the record does not hold and writes nothing; a hidden target is neither listed nor counted; an absent viewer fails closed.
- **R27** It writes exactly the edges its answer names. A `bundle` the viewer cannot see, or one the record does not hold, is refused as not held.
- **R28** A projected `links_to` edge survives the source bundle's later promotions: the resolved link becomes a `references[]` entry of the source document (LINK-FIDELITY, "Links and citations").
- **R29** When a target is promoted, the resolved links that point at it become edges (LINK-FIDELITY, step 8).

**Item-to-file membership**
- **R30** An agenda item's membership in a file is derived from containment on positional text and served beside the links, never inside them. It is labelled `derived: containment, work: machine, asserted_by: system, grade: C, standing: inferred, established: false`, and never presented as the publisher's link. A file link above the first item is `unplaced`, never assigned. Item and file addresses are told apart by shapes the active profiles' systems supply (K1).

**Authors other than the system**
- **R31** A member may assert a connection between two documents directly, with a stated basis, `asserted_by: member`, grade `D`. It is kept apart from derived rows and never rewritten by a derivation (Framework §8.1, "The connection table" 1).
- **R32** A source's own link between two held documents counts as an `A` connection (§8.1's grade-A example), `asserted_by: source`, kept apart from the system's derived connections; a link whose timing is undetermined is labelled so (K102). R32's rows are written by `projectLinks` for each edge it writes, source → target, grade `A`, established, `timing` `contemporaneous` only on that verdict and otherwise `undetermined`.
- **R49** An agenda item's membership in a file (R30) is stored, once both the item's document and the file's are held, as a system-asserted connection graded `C` (`asserted_by: system`, never established); it is re-derived when either document is re-read, and a member may confirm or reject it, both acts kept with who, when and why. Until it is stored it is served only at the read (R30) (K102). Registered on `extraction.onReading` (its R24): a reading of a capture re-derives every pending pair naming one of that capture's addresses, and restamps the stored containments of its document.

**Themes: declareTheme, placeInTheme, proposeForTheme, withdrawFromTheme, readThemes** (`op=themedeclare`, `op=themeplace`, `op=themepropose`, `op=themeread`; Framework §8.4)

Terms. A **theme** is `{theme_id, name, test, at}` and its declarer, `theme_id` of the shape `THEME-YYYY-MMDD-<hex>`. A **placement** is one (theme, target): `target` is a held bundle id (`target_kind: document`) or a held content id (`target_kind: content`, a passage), and `document` is the bundle either way. Its `state` is `member` (grade `D`) or `hunch` (grade `C`). **Membership** is `state: member` alone. The **cap** on a name, a test or a note is 131,072 bytes (UTF-8), what one passage is stored to; over it is refused, never cut. **Who may act** is the control plane's (K3): declare and place take the `admin` and `member` classes and the `contribute` capability; propose takes `admin`, `member` and `probe`, and `ai` when its minted writes name it, with `contribute`; the read takes `admin`, `member` and `probe` and no capability. The **declarer**, **placer** and **proposer** are the control plane's stamps: a session's member id, else `class:<cls>` for a machine (`class:ai/<tokenId>` for an assistant's proposal). Every answer carries `evidence: false`.

- **R39** `declareTheme({name, test, declarer, administer})`. Refusals, in order, each writing nothing: C-81.2 (`THEME_NOT_A_MEMBER`: no declarer, or a machine identity); C-81.3 (`THEME_NO_TEST`: the test empty or blank); C-81.4 (`THEME_NO_NAME`: the name empty or blank); C-81.5 (`THEME_TOO_LONG`: name or test over the cap, with `limit`). Otherwise it records a new theme under a new id, name and test as written, and answers `{theme_id, name, test, at}`, the declarer as R45 projects it, and a `says` sentence that the theme is a lens and never the basis of a claim.
- **R40** `placeInTheme({theme, target, note, placer, viewer, administer})`. Refusals, in order, each writing nothing: C-81.7 (`THEME_PLACEMENT_NOT_A_MEMBER`: no placer, or a machine identity); C-81.6 (`THEME_NOT_FOUND`: no such theme, or a viewer the gate does not recognise, the same answer); C-81.8 (`THEME_TARGET_NOT_FOUND`: the target is neither a held bundle id nor a held content id, or its document is hidden from the viewer, the same answer); C-81.9 (`THEME_REASON_TOO_LONG`: the note over the cap, with `limit`). With nothing standing at the target it records membership, grade `D`, with placer, time and note. At a standing hunch it confirms it: membership, grade `D`, placer, time and note, the proposer, proposal time and proposal note kept (`confirmed_hunch: true`). At standing membership it writes nothing (`already: true`) and the first placer stands. The answer is the placement (R44's view), `confirmed_hunch`, `already` and `says`.
- **R41** `proposeForTheme({theme, target, note, proposer, viewer, administer})`. Refusals, in order: C-81.10 (`THEME_NO_PROPOSER`: no proposer), then C-81.6, C-81.8, C-81.9 as R40. With nothing standing at the target it records a hunch, grade `C`, with proposer, time and note. A proposal where a hunch or membership stands writes nothing (`already: true`) and never demotes membership. The answer is the placement (R44's view), `already` and `says`.
- **R42** `readThemes({q, limit, viewer, administer})` without an id: every theme, for any recognised viewer (a theme is not existence-private; its placements are gated, R44). `q` narrows to themes whose name or test contains the phrase, ignoring case. Newest first, ties by id; at most `limit` (default 200, maximum 2,000; zero or a non-number reads as the default, a negative value as 1), `truncated` measured by reading one more. An unrecognised or absent viewer reads an empty list, `truncated: false`. The answer is `{q, limit, truncated, themes: [{theme_id, name, test, at}, with the declarer as R45 projects it]}`.
- **R43** `withdrawFromTheme({theme, target, reason, actor, viewer, administer})`: the placer (or an administrator) withdraws a membership, and any member rejects a hunch, with a reason under the cap. Refusals: a machine or absent actor; C-81.6 and C-81.8 as R40; no placement standing at the target; a membership the actor did not place, for an actor who does not administer. The placement then reads `withdrawn` or `rejected` with who, when and why, is neither membership nor a hunch, and the acts before it are kept. A later placement at the same target is recorded afresh beside it. `withdrawFromTheme` is `op=themewithdraw`, `actor` and `administer` the control plane's stamps; its own refusals are C-81.11 `THEME_WITHDRAW_NOT_A_MEMBER`, C-81.12 `THEME_WITHDRAW_NO_REASON`, C-81.13 `THEME_WITHDRAW_NOTHING_STANDING`, C-81.14 `THEME_WITHDRAW_NOT_THE_PLACER`, in the order: actor, C-81.6, C-81.8 and C-81.9 (the reason as a note), no reason, nothing standing, not the placer.
- **R44** `readThemes({id, limit, viewer, administer})`: an unknown id, or an unrecognised viewer, is C-81.6. Otherwise the theme, its declarer (R45), and its `members` and `hunches` apart, never mixed: each ordered by target and bounded by `limit` on its own, with `members_truncated` and `hunches_truncated`. Each placement is gated by its document through the viewer: one the viewer cannot see is omitted, not counted and not labelled, and `truncated` counts only visible rows. A placement's view is `{target, target_kind, document, state, membership, hunch, grade, placed_at, note, proposed_at, proposal_note}` with placer and proposer as R45 projects them; `membership` is true for `member` alone. A placement whose `target_kind` is `content` also carries `extent`, the content id's canonical extent as `content` states it (`canonicalExtent`), and `page`, that extent's page counted from 0, when the extent has one; a whole-document placement carries neither (T51 fix round 10; K5201 C3, K5202: amends R44; screens-inquiry R56, R78 draw it). `says` states the visible counts, that hunches are not membership, and that the theme is never the basis of a claim. The taken-back placements are carried apart, as `withdrawn` (with `withdrawn_truncated`), each `{…placement's view, ended_by(_handle), ended_at, reason}`.
- **R45** Who is shown (Membership v2 §3): every reading and every `says` sentence that names a declarer, placer or proposer shows its handle (`<role>_handle`, null when none is recorded) and never the member id or cover. Only with the control plane's affirmative administer stamp does the answer also carry the member id (`<role>`) and the cover (`<role>_cover`); any other value yields handles only. A machine stamp is shown as itself to every reader, with a null handle.

- **R46** `themeLegFindings(label, leg, findings)` (C-81.1, `THEME_NOT_EVIDENCE`): a leg whose `target` or `content_id` names a theme id, or membership in one (the id followed by `#`, `/`, `:` or `?`), or that carries a non-empty `theme` or `themes` key, gets one error finding naming the theme, with the repairs (cite the document or passage itself), and the answer is true. Any other leg, including one citing a document that is placed in a theme, gets no finding and the answer is false. Never throws.
- **R50** `weakerGrade(a, b) → grade` (K147; a module-level function): the weaker of two §8.1 grades by entities' `gradeRank` (R34's rule; equal grades answer the first). Never throws. Read by `progressions` for an instance's weakest-link grade.
- **R51** `registerDerivationProvider(module, fn)` (R5's provider): one module registers `fn(entityId, {hasArtifact, enteredAt}) → statement`, this module supplying the second argument; a second is refused `PROVIDER_DECLARED`. With none registered R5 answers `{recorded: false, cause: "NO_PROVIDER", says}`; a provider that throws answers `{recorded: false, cause: "PROVIDER_FAILED", says}`.
- **R52** `portionAxes(contentIds, {entityId})` (R13 for a caller that names no viewer and shows no bundle id: the earned-basis registry): each held row's axis, `{determined: true, grain: "portion", mode: "value", grade, established, why}` for a reaching grade, else `{determined: false, grain: "portion", grade: null, undetermined_because, empty_level, why}` with `undetermined_because` one of `NO_CONNECTION`, `CONNECTION_OUTSIDE_PORTION`, `CONNECTION_PORTION_UNDETERMINED`, never read as none; `entityId` narrows to one subject's connections (also accepted by `portionGrades`).
- **R53** `assert({a, b, basis, member, viewer})` (`op=connectionassert`; R31's service): refusals in order: `CONNECTION_ASSERT_NOT_A_MEMBER` (no member, or a machine identity), `CONNECTION_ASSERT_NO_BASIS`, `CONNECTION_ASSERT_BASIS_TOO_LONG` (over 4,000 characters, with `limit`), `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE` (either end not held or not visible, the same answer), `CONNECTION_ASSERT_SELF`. Otherwise one row per (member, pair), ends in canonical order, grade `D`, never established, `asserted_by: member`, `author` the stamp; the same member asserting the same pair again writes nothing (`wrote: false`). `member` is the control plane's stamp.
- **R54** `asserted({bundleId, viewer, limit})` (`op=connectionsasserted`): a held, visible document's connections asserted apart from derivation, as three lists `member`, `source`, `containment`, each row `{connection_id, kind, a_bundle_id, b_bundle_id, grade, established, needs_confirmation, asserted_by, author?, basis, at}` with, for a link, `timing`, `timing_says`, `address`, `source_capture`, and for a containment R30's label, `item_address`, `file_address`, `agenda_capture`, `judgements` and `standing` (`inferred`, `confirmed` or `rejected`); a row whose other end the viewer cannot see is omitted; at most `limit` (default 200, maximum 2,000), `truncated`. Absent or invisible: `NO_SUCH_BUNDLE`.
- **R55** `storeFileMembership({captureSha, viewer})` (`op=filemembershipstore`; R49's store): an agenda capture the viewer cannot see, or not held, is `NO_SUCH_CAPTURE`. The membership is extraction's R52 derivation over the capture (`extraction.pdfStructure`); null answers `membership: null` with its reason. Each pair whose item and file documents are both held (an address's held document: its earliest direct receipt's capture's home) is stored as a containment connection (item → file, grade `C`, never established, `asserted_by: system`); any other pair is kept pending. The answer carries `stored`, `pending`, R30's label and the derivation's `unplaced`, which are never stored.
- **R56** `fileMembership({captureSha, viewer, limit})` (`op=filemembership`; R30 at this module): the capture's stored pairs (with their judgements) and its pending ones, each with R30's label and `stored` true or false; `NO_SUCH_CAPTURE` as in R55. Each list at most `limit` (default 200, maximum 2,000), with `truncated`, `stored_truncated` and `pending_truncated` (K155).
- **R57** `judgeFileMembership({id, verdict, reason, member, viewer})` (`op=filemembershipjudge`): refusals in order: `FILE_MEMBERSHIP_NOT_A_MEMBER`, `FILE_MEMBERSHIP_BAD_VERDICT` (not confirm or reject), `FILE_MEMBERSHIP_NO_REASON`, `FILE_MEMBERSHIP_NO_SUCH_CONNECTION` (no stored containment by that id, or an end hidden). Otherwise appends the judgement with who, when and why; the latest stands; the grade does not move.

**The `refs` read contract** (N213)
- **R58** (N213) The table `refs` with its columns `bundle_id` (the citing bundle), `target_id` and `kind` (the relation: `cites`, `links_to`, `supersedes` and the others R19 projects) is a stated read contract. A later module may join it in its own SQL: `inquiry` (its R39), `publication` and `ratification`. On the terms of record-core R37, this module changes none of those columns' names or meaning without a change to this requirement. Whether an edge is severed is not a column of it: a reader asks R22's `edgeSevered`. Every write to it stays this module's (R19, R24).

**The `connections` read contract** (N288)
- **R59** The table `connections`, with its column `entity_id`, is a stated read contract. `entity_id` is the entity id a connection was derived through, exactly as R1's `derive` was given it, registered or not, with one row per unordered pair of captures. A later module may probe it in its own SQL: `retrieval`'s missing-row probe asks whether any row names an id (`SELECT 1 … WHERE entity_id = ? LIMIT 1`). On the terms of record-core R37, this module changes neither the table's name nor that column's name or meaning without a change to this requirement. A row, once written, stays until R36's purge.
