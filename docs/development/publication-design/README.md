# The publication design lane

This folder belongs to the `publication` design lane (`process/config.json` `lanes`; product decision BD-2, DECISIONS.md, 6 Nov 2026). The lane decides design and wording here, within the product's principles. Its area is publication, ratification, docket, case making and the actions around them (ACTIONS-DESIGN's "Publication & action" area; K2433; Bob, 6 Nov).

The folder holds only what the product reads or publishes. Sources and generators go here. Research, working notes and hand-offs never do: research lives in `docs/development/publication-design-research/` (read its `index.md` first), and design documents are claude.ai artifacts. Screens and their words are the ux lane's (BD-3).
