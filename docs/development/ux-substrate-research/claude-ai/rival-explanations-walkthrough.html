    How it works (the investigation lane's model)

    • A mark is one member's judgment. Each member holds one mark per piece and explanation, and changing it keeps the earlier one in the history. No mark ever changes a grade, and Civicsmith never marks.
    • When members agree, the box shows the mark. When they differ, it shows “Disputed” and opens to who marked what.
    • An explanation stays until a member sets it aside, and the reason must name evidence marked against it. Anyone can bring it back.
    • “Doesn't tell them apart” is Civicsmith's own signal, drawn with the dotted edge for machine work. It shows only when every explanation is marked “fits” and none is disputed. The second way has no such signal.
    • In the second way, “rules out” is the same record as “cuts against” in the first, so switching later loses nothing.
