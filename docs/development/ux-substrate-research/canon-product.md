## 1. The DEC register (DEC-96 onward)

### 1.1 What the register is, and the rules around it

- DECISIONS.md is "the return channel": questions go up, and the BOB session writes `response:` and `decided:`. "An entry is never deleted. This file is append-only" (DECISIONS.md:3-27). An open entry must carry a `provisional:` line, because "AN OPEN DECISION NEVER BLOCKS WORK" (DECISIONS.md:29-33).
- What is Bob's: "**doctrine** …, **risk carrying his name** …, **effects on people outside the project**, and the gated mechanical acts" (DECISIONS.md:42-46). The process rulebooks restate P17: Bob defines "values, policies, mission, audiences, requirements, principles, high-level architecture, UX, doctrine" (`.process/rulebooks/bob.md`:15; `.process/rulebooks/root.md`:16).
- The UX stream "mints **DEC** ids (DEC-96 onward) for Bob's UX rulings, and amends canon documents under `docs/architecture/`" (`build/manifest.md`:57; K945). "No session of this process edits, reverts or re-words `docs/development/ux-substrate/` or that stream's canon amendments, and a merge conflict with them is resolved by keeping its text" (`build/manifest.md`:59).
- Bob delegated detail on 2026-10-06: "Bring me only the questions that are mine … and decide the rest yourself, recording it and reporting it as done" (quoted in DEC-138, DECISIONS.md:2208). This is why 313 of the 394 entries are the design session's own calls (P17).
- Earlier DECs are rolled to `docs/archive/ledgers/DECISIONS-2026-08.md`; `node tools/decided.mjs "<subject>"` searches every rolled ruling (DECISIONS.md:91-98).

### 2.1 Mission, values, operational principles (the Roadmap)
